## 3. Key Metrics to Watch — Ranked

**1. PMT revenue and the semiconductor wafer-fab trajectory (highest importance).**
At the Lytham Partners Fall 2026 conference (early Oct), management said FY27 wafer-fab revenue should **surpass $40M vs. ~$32M in FY26** (+25%) — back toward FY23's peak. They are **adding second-shift capacity**, with a third shift possible. PMT was 70% of FY26 sales; a $39M → $43M+ quarter is the difference between a beat and a miss on its own. Watch for any softening of the "ongoing optimism into calendar 2027" language — management has repeatedly flagged that quarter-to-quarter visibility is *limited*.

**2. Backlog and book-to-bill.** $164.4M was the highest in ~3 years. Q1 is seasonally softer and Q4 likely pulled shipments forward, so a modest sequential decline is tolerable; a drop below ~$155M would undercut the entire FY27 growth narrative and is the main bear trigger.

**3. Canvys durability.** Q4's $12.3M was an outlier (+29.5% y/y) on a record backlog and **1.3 book-to-bill**. This segment is project-lumpy — it printed $8.0M just two quarters earlier. Holding ~$10M+ validates the mix; reverting to $8.3M removes ~$2M of revenue and roughly $0.04 of EPS versus my base.

**4. GES / battery energy storage — expectations management.** The multimillion-dollar BESS order management promised for Q1 **did land**: 18 customized systems, ~7 MWh, across seven remote Alaskan communities (announced Aug 20, 2026). But management has explicitly deferred the payoff: FY27 is a **pipeline-building** year (~50 active opportunities, commercial/industrial and municipal — *not* mega data centers), with "meaningful revenue growth expected in fiscal 2028." Also watch ULTRA3000/ULTRAPEM wind: ~15% penetration of applicable GE turbines, with **Suzlon** the first non-GE OEM opening.

**5. Gross margin and opex leverage.** The FY26 story was operating leverage — opex fell to 26.6% of sales from 30.0% — while gross margin compressed slightly on PMT/GES mix. Engineered and proprietary products were **55% of FY26 sales**; any move up in that mix is the credible path to the "margin improvement" management promised for FY27. Tariffs, sourcing and pricing discipline remain named headwinds.

## 5. Catalyst Checklist

1. **PMT revenue vs. ~$43M (my whisper)** — consensus revenue of $58.98M is effectively a bet on PMT. The street's published number looks beatable given $164.4M backlog; the real bar is the unpublished buy-side number closer to $60–61M after two consecutive large beats.
2. **Backlog print vs. $164.4M** — the only hard forward-looking datapoint the company gives. Treat $155M as the line in the sand.
3. **Wafer-fab FY27 framing** — does management reiterate ">$40M" on the call? Any hedge here outweighs a Q1 revenue beat.
4. **Opex % of sales vs. 26.6% (Q4) / 29.2% (Q1 FY26)** — second-shift labor and facility expansion are new costs. Operating leverage, not revenue, has driven the 2x stock move; a step-up in opex ahead of revenue is the quiet bear case.
5. **BESS revenue recognition and FY28 language** — if management pulls the "transformational" BESS framing forward from FY28 into FY27 with numbers, it re-rates the multiple. If they push it out, the growth story narrows back to semis.
6. **Narrative/capital allocation** — M&A was explicitly *not* a near-term priority as of July; capital is pointed at capacity and facility expansion. Any change (acquisition, buyback, or raise at these levels) is a narrative event.
