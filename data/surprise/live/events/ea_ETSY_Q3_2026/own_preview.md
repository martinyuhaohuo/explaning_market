## Context from Q2 2026 and Guidance

- **Q2 GMS:** $2.6B, +7.5% Y/Y on a marketplace basis, but only +1.0% on a continuing-operations basis. The gap reflects the Depop divestiture, which closed July 30, 2026. **Comparability is the main analytical trap this quarter.**
- **Q2 revenue:** $668M, +9.3% marketplace / +6.2% continuing operations.
- **Take rate:** 25.9%. Q3 guidance is ~26%.
- **Active buyers:** ~87M, roughly flat Y/Y. GMS per buyer (TTM) rose 2.8% to $124. Purchase frequency is still below prior year, though the decline moderated.
- **Adj. EBITDA:** $195M, 29.2% margin. Q3 guide is 28%–30%.
- **FY2026 outlook (raised):** GMS +4% to 6% Y/Y, take rate ~26%, adj. EBITDA margin 29%–30% (up from 28%–30%). The MarketBeat and 247wallst conflict is resolved: the shareholder letter says mid-single-digit.
- **Capital allocation:** $250M buyback in Q2. New $2B authorization on Aug 3. ~$1.4B of Depop proceeds arriving in Q3, which management says will accelerate repurchases.
- **Restructuring:** ~220 roles cut (~12%), ~$35M charges, mostly complete by end of Q3. Management frames it as reinvestment in product and engineering.

## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Continuing-ops GMS vs. $2.53B–$2.58B guide.** Confirms whether the marketplace is holding up without Depop.
2. **Active buyers and buyer frequency.** Whether the flat buyer count is stabilizing. This is the core bull/bear debate.
3. **Take rate vs. ~26%.** Ads and payments contribution.
4. **Adj. EBITDA margin vs. 28%–30%**, and whether the restructuring savings show up.
5. **Q4 and FY GMS commentary.** Whether management holds the 4%–6% full-year range.
6. **Buyback pace post-Depop.** Share count reduction and capital return messaging.
