## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Adj. EPS vs. consensus, and how much came from gains on sale.** Q2 beat by roughly $1.00 against an estimate of about $4.14. Since guidance excludes future gains, the quality of the beat matters.
2. **Full-year guidance.** The current ~$16.80 excludes further gains. Watch for a raise. The implied H2 run rate looks low against H1 (see below), so a raise is plausible if gains continue.
3. **Lease rates, utilization, and fleet values.** These drive core lease margins and are the main operating signals for aircraft lessors.
4. **Asset sales and gain pipeline.** Pace of disposals and the size of expected gains.
5. **Capital allocation.** Buyback pace (over $1.4B YTD), leverage, and cost of funds.

## Guidance Math (my derivation)

- Q1 adj. EPS was reported at $5.39 (one source), Q2 at $5.14. H1 ≈ **$10.53**.
