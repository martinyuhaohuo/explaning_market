## 1. The Setup in One Paragraph

CMS enters this print with a **back-end-loaded 2026 guide and a high bar**. H1 adjusted EPS was **$1.50 vs. $1.73 in H1 2025 (-13%)** — Q1 beat ($1.13 vs. $1.02) but Q2 collapsed to **$0.37 from $0.71**, hit by weather and storm O&M. To land inside the reaffirmed **$3.83–$3.90** FY guide, H2 must deliver **$2.33–$2.40 vs. $1.88 last year — +24% to +28% YoY**. Q3 is the first of only two proof points. The good news: the two mechanical tailwinds are identifiable and largely already banked — the **$276.6m electric rate increase effective May 1, 2026** (first full summer quarter of new rates) and a **hot Michigan summer** (June CDDs ~13% above normal, July ~18% above normal). The bad news: if Q3 lands light *with* that help, the FY guide — and management's repeated "confidence toward the high end" — breaks, and 2027's $4.08–$4.17 gets re-underwritten. The stock has already de-rated: **-11% over 52 weeks, ~30pts behind the S&P 500** and modestly behind the XLU.

## 3. Key Metrics to Watch — Ranked

**1. Implied Q4, not reported Q3 (highest signal).** Q3 is half-known already — rates are in, weather was hot. The number that matters is what's left for Q4 after Q3 prints. A $1.16 Q3 with a reaffirmed high-end guide is clean. A $1.16 Q3 *paired with softened guide language* says Q3 was pulled forward and Q4 is in trouble.

**2. FY2026 guidance verb.** Management has said "confidence toward the high end" at both Q1 and Q2. Watch for: **narrowed to $3.87–$3.90** (bullish), **reaffirmed with "high end" retained** (neutral/base), **reaffirmed with "high end" dropped** (bearish — this is the tell), **trimmed** (very bearish).

**3. Weather-normalized vs. actual.** Michigan CDDs ran well above normal in June and July. Management will quantify the weather benefit. If the *weather-normalized* result is soft and the beat is all heat, the quality of the quarter is poor and Q4 (gas-heavy, weather-dependent again) has no cushion.

**4. U-22070 electric rate case.** Filed June 2, 2026: **$456m request at 10.25% ROE** — which would be the highest authorized ROE in Michigan. The AG filed testimony on **October 6, 2026 arguing to cut the request roughly in half**. Order expected ~April 2027 under Michigan's 10-month clock, rates effective ~May 2027 — i.e. this case *is* the 2027 guide. Any commentary on settlement posture or staff position is high-value.

**5. Data center conversion rate.** The **~9 GW pipeline** (1.5–2 GW qualified / 4–5 GW advanced / **1–2 GW in final negotiation**) is the entire long-term capex story at **$2–5bn of capital per GW**. But only **~110 MW was signed YTD as of Q2** — the gap between pipeline and signatures is the credibility issue. One hyperscale **1 GW** agreement is in hand.

**6. Financing and the capital plan.** $24bn+ five-year plan; rate base **$28.4bn (2025) → $46.8bn (2030), 10.5% CAGR**. As of Q2, ~**$495m of ~$700m** 2026 equity was done (~$205m to go). The **September 3 IRP** (14.1 GW in 2028 → 33.8 GW by 2040; +19 GW solar/wind/storage; two fast-start gas plants totaling 1.4 GW in Bay and Genesee counties) sets up a plan upsize — most likely at the Q4/February update, but a preview here would be a positive surprise.

**7. NorthStar exit execution.** Redirecting ~$1.7bn of capital away from NorthStar by 2030, cutting parent funding needs by **>$500m**, moving toward ~100% utility earnings post-2027. Retaining DIG, gas peakers and ~0.5 GW of solar. Watch for proceeds, timing, and dis-synergy drag.

### What each requires operationally

**Bull** — needs the weather tailwind to land *on top of* rate relief rather than being offset by O&M, plus a named catalyst. The cleanest bull trigger isn't EPS at all: it's a **signed data center contract out of the 1–2 GW final-stage bucket**, which converts the 9 GW pipeline from a slide into a capex commitment and re-rates the 2027+ growth runway. *Signal language:* "narrowing," "ahead of plan," "executed a definitive agreement."

**Base** — the modal outcome for a Michigan regulated utility with 7 of 8 quarters of beats and a 1.4% average surprise. CMS beats by a penny or two and says nothing new. *Signal language:* "reaffirm," "confident toward the high end," "momentum." Historically this prints a non-event: **Q2 2026 moved +0.1% the day after** despite a revenue miss and underwhelming 2027 guide.

**Bear** — the real bear case is not a Q3 miss, it's a **guidance-language downgrade**. With H2 needing +24–28% YoY, the market has no tolerance for hedging. Secondary trigger: equity. CMS is funding a $24bn plan with ~$205m of 2026 equity still to issue and a 10.5% rate base CAGR; any hint of upsized equity against a $69 stock is dilution at a bad price. *Signal language:* "within our range" (dropping "high end"), "evaluating financing alternatives," "timing of regulatory outcomes."

### Historical calibration

- **Beat 7 of last 8 quarters**; met or beat in each of the last four. Average surprise ~1.4% over the last two quarters.
- **Q2 2026 (Jul 28):** EPS $0.37 vs. $0.36, revenue miss, soft 2027 guide → **+0.1%** next day, but **-5.7% over the following 90 days**. The lesson: CMS doesn't gap on the print, it bleeds on the thesis. Post-earnings drift has been the bigger P&L than the day-one move.
- **Q3 2025 (Oct 30):** $0.93 vs. $0.87, guidance raised, 2026 initiated — a textbook beat-and-raise. That is the comp the bull case is being measured against, and it sets a high narrative bar.

## 5. Catalyst Checklist

1. **Adj. EPS vs. $1.16** — and immediately solve for implied Q4. Q3 carries both the May 1 rate increase and above-normal CDDs; anything below ~$1.10 with that help is a genuine problem for the FY guide.
2. **FY2026 guide language** — "confidence toward the high end" retained, narrowed, or dropped. The buy-side is positioned for *retained or narrowed*; dropping it is the single highest-beta outcome in the release.
3. **U-22070** ($456m, 10.25% ROE) — settlement posture and any read on staff position after the AG's October 6 push to halve it. This case underwrites the $4.08–$4.17 2027 guide; the requested ROE is aggressive for Michigan and a trim is widely assumed.
4. **Data center signatures** — MW signed vs. ~110 MW YTD, and whether any of the 1–2 GW final-stage tranche closed. At $2–5bn of capex per GW this is the only lever that meaningfully changes the long-term growth algorithm.
5. **Capital plan / financing** — any pre-announcement of a plan upsize off the September 3 IRP, residual 2026 equity, and NorthStar exit proceeds. A plan increase funded with equity at $69 is not the same news as one funded with retained cash and asset sales.
