## Guidance Context

- **FY2026 adjusted EPS:** $3.83–$3.90, reaffirmed on July 28, with management signaling confidence toward the high end.
- **YTD adjusted EPS through Q2:** $1.50 (vs. $1.73 in 2025). Q2 was $0.37, in line with consensus. Q2 revenue was $1.83B, a 4.2% miss.
- **Implied H2 2026:** Roughly $2.33–$2.40 from guidance minus YTD. Q3 is typically the largest quarter for a Michigan utility with summer demand, so Q3 should carry much of that. Check this against Q3 2025 actuals, which I did not verify.
- **FY2027 guidance (new in Q2):** $4.08–$4.17. Zacks' FY2027 consensus is $4.16, at the top of the range.
- **Long-term:** 6–8% adjusted EPS growth, reaffirmed.
- **Strategic change:** CMS completed a review of NorthStar Clean Energy. It is exiting non-utility renewables development and keeping Michigan assets, including Dearborn Industrial Generation. Management says this reduces financing needs.
- **Unverified:** A third-party summary cites a $24B capital plan through 2030 and 10.5% rate base growth. I could not confirm either in the release.

## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Q3 adjusted EPS vs. consensus.** Sources disagree on the bar, so settle the number first. A beat on the $1.30 bar is different from a beat on $1.13.
2. **FY2026 guidance.** Does management keep $3.83–$3.90, or move toward the high end? Narrowing upward is the bullish signal; a cut to the low end is the bearish one.
3. **FY2027 guidance reaffirmation.** The $4.08–$4.17 range sits at the high end of the 6–8% long-term growth path. Any hint of a shortfall matters most here.
4. **Rate base growth and capital plan.** Confirm the capital plan and rate base CAGR. These drive the long-term EPS growth argument.
5. **Regulatory items.** Michigan rate case timing and outcomes, and any constructive-equity or settlement commentary.
6. **Revenue and margins.** Revenue missed in Q2. Check weather-driven electric sales and gas margin.
7. **NorthStar exit costs and cash.** Look for impairments, one-time charges, and proceeds, plus whether they change adjusted vs. GAAP EPS.
