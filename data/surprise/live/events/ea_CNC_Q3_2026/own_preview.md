## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Consolidated HBR vs. the 90.5%–91.3% FY range.** Q2 was 89.6%, which is well below the full-year band. Q3 HBR at or below ~90% supports the guide. A move back above 91% would be a negative signal.
2. **Medicaid HBR (Q2: 93.9%).** This is the main debate, per RBC. Look for evidence of rate adequacy and margin recovery, and whether management still describes progress as "tangible."
3. **Guidance change.** Does management hold, raise, or cut the >$4.80 adj. EPS floor? Given the H1 total, the sign of any implied H2 decline matters most.
4. **Marketplace and ACA dynamics.** Membership is down ~40% YoY. Watch the 2027 rate and risk-adjustment commentary, and whether 4.5%–5% margins hold.
5. **Commercial HBR durability (Q2: 79.2%).** The large YoY drop may include one-time items. Check whether management calls it sustainable.
6. **Medicare membership and HBR** (Q2: 89.5%) ahead of the 2027 bid cycle.
