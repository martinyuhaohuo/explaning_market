## 2. Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

**1. Product gross margin (most important).** Product GM landed in the **low-60s%** in Q2 on mix and supply-chain cost pressure. Q3 guidance mixes *toward* the lower-margin line: product +14% q/q to $113M while royalties (~100% margin) fall 14% q/q to $72M. My bridge puts blended GM at **~79–80% vs ~81% in Q2** — i.e., record revenue with *negative* margin optics. TTM net margin has already compressed from 35.5% to 31.7%. This is the single most likely source of a "good quarter, bad stock" outcome.

**2. The royalty step-down — real or conservatism?** Royalties guided $69–75M after $84.2M in Q2 (and $84.1M licensing billings). Fixed-fee license revenue under ASC 606 is lumpy; if Q2 carried a true-up, $72M is the clean run-rate. If royalties come in *at/above* $78M with billings holding, the Street will mark up the annuity. If they come in at $69M **and** Q4 billings guide down, the bear case on "the IP annuity is flattening while the lower-margin chip business carries growth" gets real traction.

**3. Q4'26 guide — the actual swing factor.** Against a $190.2M Q4'25 base, my bar is **~$222–230M revenue (+17–21% YoY) and ~$0.84–0.88 non-GAAP EPS**. Below ~$218M reads as a product-ramp stall; above ~$230M breaks the sell-the-news pattern.

**4. Inventory and supply commentary.** Inventory jumped to **$74.8M from $44.1M at YE25 (+69%)** — management framed it as a deliberate build for ramping products with "no customer-side buildup," and flagged industry supply constraints persisting into 2027. A second consecutive outsized build without matching product revenue would be a genuine quality-of-earnings issue. Bears also cite ~$22.8M of net insider selling over 12 months with no buys.

**5. Cash generation.** OCF: $88.4M (Q3'25) → $83.2M (Q1'26) → **$61.2M (Q2'26)**, FCF $49M, cash + securities $824.9M. The OCF decline tracks the inventory build and lower billings linearity; a rebound toward $80M+ would defuse point 4.

**6. 2027 framing / AI design wins.** CEO Luc Seraphin: *"the seeds we've planted in the last two years should manifest in 2027."* Watch for monetization timing on: Tier-1 US hyperscaler **next-gen HBM IP win**; **LPDDR6 SOCAMM2 PMIC** (announced Oct 13, sampling to lead customers); **DDR5-9600 client + server chipsets**; **MRDIMM** (immaterial near-term, material in 2027); silicon IP guided to **10–15% annual growth**. CXL remains fragmented/ASIC-like — management has explicitly de-prioritized standardized CXL product investment.

**Management tells.** Bullish: quantified HBM/PMIC revenue timing, product GM "normalizing," billings guided up. Bearish: "mix headwinds," "supply constraints into 2027," reiterating silicon IP at the 10% low end, or declining to frame 2027.

## 4. Catalyst Checklist

1. **Product gross margin vs. low-60s%** — the mix shift from ~100%-margin royalties to ~62%-margin silicon is the structural bear thesis; one more leg down and the multiple re-rates regardless of the revenue line.
2. **Royalties ≥$75M vs. the $69–75M guide** — tests whether Q2's $84.2M was a true-up or a new annuity level.
3. **Q4'26 revenue guide vs. ~$222–230M / EPS ~$0.84–0.88** — the forward number, not the print, has driven all three 2026 reactions.
4. **Inventory and OCF** — $74.8M / $61.2M need to move the right way to kill the quality-of-earnings narrative.
5. **2027 quantification on HBM IP + LPDDR6 PMIC + MRDIMM** — the entire "seeds manifest in 2027" bull case is currently unpriced because it's undated.
