**Data conflicts to resolve:**
- One aggregator shows Q3 GAAP sales guidance of $204M–$222M and a non-GAAP revenue range of $389M–$407M. The $389M–$407M figure does not fit a quarterly revenue guide and is likely an error or a different basis. Treat the $210M–$216M company guide as the reference.
- The CFO said the company will now focus its results and guidance on an ASC 606 revenue basis. Confirm whether the Q3 guide and consensus are on the same basis, since a basis mismatch could distort the beat/miss read.

## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Total revenue vs. the $216M top end.** Q2 beat its guide range ($207.4M vs. $192M–$198M), so a clean beat is expected. A print at or below the midpoint would likely be read as a miss.
2. **Royalty line ($69M–$75M guide vs. $84.2M in Q2).** A sharp sequential decline needs an explanation. It may reflect timing, licensing mix, or the ASC 606 change. Management's explanation will matter as much as the number.
3. **Product revenue ($110M–$116M).** This is the growth driver for the AI/data-center memory interface story. Q2 product revenue was a record $99.2M.
4. **Q4 revenue and EPS guidance.** Management said 2H26 should be stronger than 1H26 and that favorable demand could extend into 2027. The buy-side will want that to hold.
5. **Non-GAAP EPS vs. $0.75–$0.78.** Q2 non-GAAP EPS was $0.77 against a $0.72 consensus, so the company has been beating. EPS is a secondary driver.
6. **Customer agreement signings.** Management said the revenue range depends on signing customer agreements for product sales and solutions licensing. Any commentary on timing or slippage is a risk.

**Operational signals:**
- *Bull:* Management cites specific new design wins or large product agreements, and a Q4 guide above consensus with product growth continuing.
- *Base:* Guidance reiterated with "favorable demand" language but no new specifics.
- *Bear:* Management pushes agreement timing into Q4 or later, or softens the 2027 outlook.

**Historical reactions (small sample, treat as directional):**
- **Q1 2026 (Apr 27):** Results in line with guidance. Stock fell about 10.8% on Day 0 and about 21% by Day +1, according to a Webull dataset.
- **Q2 2026 (Jul 27):** Beat on revenue and EPS. Shares were down about 9% from the prior close around $96 by Jul 29, per Simply Wall St.
- **Longer-run:** Webull reports an average absolute Day 0 move of about 4.2% and a Day +1 move of about 12% across four releases from 2024–2025.

The pattern is that results have been good but the stock has sold off on the last two prints, so expectations are high. The bar for a positive reaction is probably a clean beat plus a Q4 guide above consensus.

## Catalyst Checklist

1. **Revenue vs. $213M consensus / $216M top end.** A beat above the top of the range is needed to move the stock up.
2. **Royalty trajectory ($69M–$75M).** Investors need to understand why royalties are down from $84.2M.
3. **Product revenue growth (+11% to +17% QoQ at guide).** This is the AI-memory narrative. Confirm it is on track.
4. **Q4 / FY2026 guidance.** The buy-side wants confirmation of 2H strength and a view into 2027.
5. **ASC 606 basis change and any restructuring or M&A.** Clarification of the new reporting basis and any strategic news could shift the narrative.
