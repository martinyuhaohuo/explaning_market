## 1. The Setup in One Paragraph

This is an unusual print: the quarter being reported ended **July 31, 2026 — nearly three months ago** — and essentially every event that matters to the equity happened *after* the quarter closed. Since July 31: a **$7.675M insider-led private placement** (Sept, led by Vice Chairman Howard Jonas via Chartwell), a **CEO change** (Morris Berger in, effective Oct 1; Jonathan Reich to President/COO), **buybacks suspended**, the **first commercial DataSeeds "Evals" engagement with Bria.AI** (Oct 19), and an LD Micro presentation (Oct 21) explicitly repositioning Zedge as an **AI data company**. The P&L for Q4 FY26 is therefore a backward-looking cash-generation checkpoint; **the stock will trade on the 4:30pm call**, specifically on whether the new CEO quantifies DataSeeds. Published consensus of **$7.25M / $0.04** looks stale and low — my base case is **$7.95M / $0.06**.

## 4. Key Metrics to Watch — Ranked

**1. Any quantification of DataSeeds.AI (highest importance, lowest visibility).**
This is now the entire equity story. Known facts: first six-figure order fulfilled, a **repeat order >$100K in Q3**, "multiple six-figure opportunities" in pipeline, Evals service launched and validated with Bria.AI, video annotation targeted for **H1 2027** to complete the "full stack." Unknown: DataSeeds revenue in isolation. It is currently buried in "Other revenue" (~$0.5M/qtr, *including* GuruShots and licensing). **The single highest-value disclosure on this call would be a DataSeeds revenue line, a contracted-backlog figure, or a named customer count.** Management has repeatedly pre-warned revenue will be "lumpy" — read that as expectation management.

**2. Advertising revenue and the Emojipedia drag.**
Advertising is ~65% of revenue and the swing factor. Core marketplace advertising was described as "essentially flat" in Q3 against a prior-year quarter that carried a one-time $450K partner bonus — i.e., underlying trend is better than the reported -4%. **iOS advertising grew 35% YoY.** Emojipedia continues to decay on structural Google search changes (AI overviews / zero-click), and has already been written down $3.7M. Watch whether management sizes the remaining Emojipedia contribution; if it is now de minimis, the drag annualizes away in FY27.

**3. MAU: five straight quarters of double-digit declines.**
23.2M → 22.2M → 20.4M → 19.6M. This is the bear case's load-bearing metric. Management's counter is quality-over-quantity: **ARPMAU +21% to a record $0.119**. The October investor deck rounds to "~20 million MAU," which hints at stabilization. **A Q4 MAU print above ~19.5M with YoY decline narrowing inside -10% would be a genuine positive surprise.** Below 19.0M and the monetization-offsets-decline argument gets harder to sustain.

**4. Subscription momentum and deferred revenue.**
Nine consecutive quarters of growth; 1.26M active subs (+40.6%); **deferred revenue $6.2M (+25.7%)** at near-100% gross margin — the cleanest forward-revenue visibility in the model. But growth is decelerating: +54% → +48.5% → +40.6%. Sequential adds: +91K, +100K, +85K. **Watch sequential adds: >90K is on-trend, <75K signals saturation.**

**5. FY-end impairment risk and margin integrity.**
Q4 is the annual goodwill/intangible testing quarter and Zedge has a history of Q4 one-timers (Q4 FY25 carried ~$0.6M of restructuring; Q2 FY26 the $3.7M Emojipedia charge). Gross margin has been running **93%** and SG&A **declined 1.7% to $6.2M** even while funding growth. Any SG&A step-up above ~$6.5M in Q4 would be worth probing — though note the Berger comp package (**$450K base, $50K in bonuses, options on 3% of shares vesting quarterly over 5 years**) begins Oct 1 and hits **FY27**, not this quarter.

## 6. Catalyst Checklist

1. **DataSeeds.AI disclosure granularity** — a standalone revenue line, backlog, or customer count vs. another quarter of adjectives. *Why it matters:* at ~$16M pro-forma EV, essentially none of the AI-data optionality is priced in; this is the only disclosure that can re-rate the multiple. Conversely, a third consecutive quarter of "lumpy, early-stage" after a CEO change and a $7.7M raise reads as a stall.
2. **Revenue vs. the $7.25M bar / ~$7.9M whisper** — *Why:* the published bar is low enough that an in-line-looking print may still disappoint the real buy-side number. Watch "Other revenue" specifically.
3. **Morris Berger's first public remarks** — *Why:* he is three weeks into the role with an option package on 3% of the company struck near current levels. The buy-side wants a FY27 framework: DataSeeds investment pace, headcount plan, whether the consumer marketplace is a cash engine or a divestiture candidate. Note Reich — not Berger — presented at LD Micro on Oct 21, so this may be Berger's first substantive investor appearance.
4. **MAU stabilization** — *Why:* the bear thesis's single metric. Five quarters of double-digit declines; monetization has papered over it, but ARPMAU cannot compound forever against a shrinking base.
5. **Capital allocation reconfirmation** — *Why:* buybacks are **suspended** (~$2.2M of authorization unused) to fund DataSeeds; the **$0.02/qtr dividend (~2.9% yield)** is intended to continue and grow. Any dividend wobble would hit the value-oriented holder base hard. Also watch for the **proxy/stockholder-approval timeline** for warrant exercisability under NYSE American rules.

**Liquidity:** Float 10.53M shares; 20-day average volume **~49,000 shares/day** (~$135K notional). Insider ownership 32.5%, institutional 17.3%. Short interest is negligible (37,971 shares, 0.24% of shares out, 0.35 days to cover) — **there is no short base to squeeze; upside has to come from real buyers in a very thin book.** Position sizing, not thesis, is the binding constraint on this name.

## 8. Bottom Line

The published bar ($7.25M / $0.04) is low relative to the FY26 run-rate, and Zedge has beaten revenue in each of the last three quarters. **A fundamental beat is the likely outcome and is largely why I'd lean long into the print — but it is not the variable that determines the reaction.** A stale July quarter plus a 2.9% dividend plus 0.5x EV/sales is a floor, not a catalyst. What re-rates this stock is a new CEO putting a number on DataSeeds.AI. Absent that, expect a modest relief pop that fades into the $3.22–3.28 warrant strikes. The risk/reward is attractive at $2.76 with ~$27M of net cash behind it; the risk is time, not capital impairment — and in a 49K-share-a-day market, the exit is narrower than the entry.
