## 2. Recent Trend (Q3 FY2026, reported June 11, 2026)

- **Revenue:** $8.0M (+3% YoY), against a $7.0M estimate at the time
- **Revenue mix:** Advertising $5.4M (-4%, entirely from Emojipedia; Marketplace ads roughly flat), Subscription $1.7M (+32%), Digital goods $0.5M (+7%), Other $0.5M (+5%)
- **Operating income:** $1.1M (13.4% margin); gross margin about 93% (calculated)
- **Net income / EPS:** GAAP $0.9M / $0.07 diluted; non-GAAP $1.0M / $0.07
- **Adj. EBITDA:** $1.3M
- **Free cash flow:** $1.2M (+55% YoY)
- **Balance sheet:** $19.7M cash, no debt
- **Capital return:** Quarterly dividend raised 25% to $0.02. Buyback authorization increased by $2M after quarter-end (about $2.2M capacity).
- **Subscriptions:** Active subscriptions up 41% to about 1.3M
- **Guidance:** None formal. Management cited six "alpha launches" for the year, and said DataSeeds.AI revenue "remains lumpy."

Q2 FY2026 missed on EPS ($0.06 vs. $0.07) but beat on revenue. Q1 FY2026 sources conflict on the EPS result, so I have not relied on them.

## 3. Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Advertising revenue, especially Emojipedia.** Ads are about 2/3 of revenue, and the decline is concentrated there. Whether the drop continues determines the top-line trajectory.
2. **Subscription growth and active subscriptions.** Growth has been about 30%+ and is the main bright spot. Watch whether subscription revenue keeps compounding and whether it offsets ad weakness.
3. **Marketplace monetization.** Management named this as the priority. Flat ad revenue here is a concern.
4. **Margins and EPS quality.** Operating margin was 13% in Q3. Check whether Q4 holds that level and whether EPS is driven by operations or by one-time items.
5. **Full-year FY2026 picture and FY2027 commentary.** No formal guidance exists, so the buy-side will look for qualitative direction on the Marketplace, DataSeeds.AI, and the launch pipeline.

## 5. Catalyst Checklist

1. **Revenue vs. ~$7.25M consensus (low-confidence) and vs. the $8.0M Q3 run rate.** A reported number near $8M would look like a beat against the stale consensus but may be seen as flat sequentially.
2. **Advertising trajectory (Emojipedia vs. Marketplace).** The buy-side wants to see the ad decline stabilize.
3. **Subscription growth.** Confirming the ~30%+ pace is the main support for the thesis.
