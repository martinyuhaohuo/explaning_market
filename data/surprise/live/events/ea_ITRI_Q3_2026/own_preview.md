## Prior Quarter Context (Q2 2026, reported Jul 28)

- Revenue $563M, down 7% y/y. Networked Solutions fell 17% on project-deployment timing.
- Non-GAAP EPS $1.59 vs ~$1.29 consensus. Revenue was a slight miss against ~$566M.
- Record adjusted gross margin of 41.4%. Free cash flow $81M.
- Annual recurring revenue $417M, up 21%. Outcomes revenue up 13%.
- Total backlog $4.4B, down from $4.5B a year earlier.
- Full-year guidance raised on EPS, with revenue held.

Sources: [Q2 results summary](https://finviz.com/news/373519/itron-reports-q2-earnings-and-raises-full-year-2026-eps-outlook), [Q2 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000780571/000117184326004912/exh_991.htm)

## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Revenue vs. consensus and Networked Solutions timing.** Q2 was pushed by deployment timing. Does Q3 recover toward $600M, or does the slip repeat?
2. **Full-year revenue guidance ($2.37B–$2.41B).** Reaffirmed, narrowed, or cut? This matters more than the Q3 print.
3. **Full-year EPS guidance ($6.30–$6.50).** Another raise would confirm margin strength. The gap to consensus is large, so a hold could disappoint.
4. **Gross margin.** Can it stay near the 41.4% record? Sustained margin expansion is the core of the bull case.
5. **ARR growth (~21% in Q2) and backlog.** Is recurring growth holding, and does backlog stabilize against last year's $4.5B?

## Scenarios

| **Bull** | ≥$600M | ≥$1.60 | Deployments catch up, margins hold at ~41%, FY EPS raised above $6.50 | Gap up and hold; sustained if ARR and backlog also improve |

| **Bear** | <$590M | ~$1.50 or below | Deployment slip repeats, FY revenue trimmed toward the low end | Sharp drop, especially if FY revenue is cut |

**What would signal each scenario:**
- **Bull:** Management cites backlog conversion and "stronger operating model" language, and raises FY EPS again.
- **Base:** Reaffirmed ranges, with commentary that Networked Solutions timing is "shifting" rather than lost.
- **Bear:** Language about "customer-driven delays," lower Outcomes or recurring growth, or a narrowed FY revenue range at the bottom of the band.

**Historical context:**
- Q2 2026: EPS beat, and shares gapped up on the day ([MarketBeat](https://www.marketbeat.com/instant-alerts/itron-nasdaqitri-shares-gap-up-after-earnings-beat-2026-07-28/)). Good EPS with a revenue miss still drew a positive reaction.
- Q4 2025 and Q1 2026: EPS beats, but shares posted only mild declines. A beat alone has not reliably driven the stock higher.

## Catalyst Checklist

1. **Q3 revenue vs. $597.6M / $607.4M.** The Street number is unclear. Revenue, not EPS, drove the Q2 debate. Clear revenue above ~$600M is the cleanest positive signal.
2. **FY26 revenue guidance.** The buy-side will want the Networked Solutions recovery quantified. A cut here is the most likely source of a negative reaction.
3. **FY26 EPS guidance vs. ~$5.98 consensus.** Expectations for another raise are probably modest, given the gap. Confirm the Street's current number before the call.
4. **Gross margin and free cash flow.** Sustained ~41% margin and FCF near Q2's $81M support the margin-expansion thesis.
5. **Strategic items.** I found no reported M&A or restructuring news. Check for any announcement on the call.
