## 2. The Setup: Two Things Consensus Isn't Capturing

### (a) The EPS bar is artificially low — a beat is the base case, not the bull case

Itron has beaten in 8 of 8 quarters, averaging ~+24¢. More tellingly, it has blown through *its own guidance ceiling*:

| Quarter | EPS Guide | Actual | vs. High End |
|---|---|---|---|
| Q1 FY26 | — (cons. $1.26) | $1.49 | +23¢ vs. cons. |
| Q2 FY26 | $1.25–1.35 | **$1.59** | **+24¢ above high end** |

Mechanically: Q2 did $1.59 on $562.9M at a record 41.4% adj. gross margin. Add the ~$32M of guided sequential revenue at ~40% incremental margin and hold opex, and you get ~$1.78–1.80. The $1.50–1.60 guide therefore embeds **~200bp+ of gross margin give-back**. My estimate for the real buy-side whisper is **$1.70–1.75**, not $1.56. An in-line $1.55 print would read as a *miss*.

### (b) The Q4 implied number is the whole story — and it requires an all-time record quarter

| Build | Figure |
|---|---|
| H1 FY26 actual revenue | $1,149.9M |
| FY26 guide | $2,370–2,410M |
| Implied H2 | $1,220–1,260M (+6% to +9% y/y) |
| Less Q3 guide (mid) | $595M |
| **= Implied Q4 FY26 revenue** | **~$645M** (range $620–670M) |
| Q4 FY25 actual | $571.7M → **+12.8% y/y** |
| Prior all-time record quarter | $615.5M (Q3 FY24) → **+4.8% above the record** |
| Q3→Q4 sequential step | **+8.4%** |

Itron has not grown revenue y/y in any quarter of FY26 on an organic basis, yet the guide requires a record-breaking Q4. **This is the number that trades the stock**, not Q3 EPS.

**Also worth noting:** the +2.4% headline Q3 growth is essentially all inorganic. Resiliency Solutions (Urbint/Locusview) contributed $16M in Q2 and has no prior-year comparable; backing out ~$17M leaves organic revenue of ~$578M, or **-0.7% y/y**. Segment y/y comparisons this quarter are not apples-to-apples.

**What each requires operationally:**

- **Bull:** Networked deployments actually inflect (≥$370M) *and* regulatory approvals land to push bookings above parity. The tell on the call: management giving a *specific* Q4 revenue number at or above $640M without hedging language, plus any quantified 2027 bracket.
- **Base:** The familiar Itron print — margin-driven beat, revenue in-line, forward guide technically intact but conservative. Tell: "lumpy bookings," "nothing has changed in the marketplace," no 2027 numbers.
- **Bear:** Any FY26 revenue trim. Tell: shifting the Q4 ramp into 2027, or framing backlog conversion as "customer-paced."

**Historical calibration — this stock moves violently, and *forward guide* dominates the print:**

| Date | Result | Reaction |
|---|---|---|
| Jul 28, 2026 (Q2 FY26) | EPS $1.59 vs. $1.29; revenue **missed**; FY raised | **+19% to +20%** ($84.78 → ~$101) |

The Q1 FY26 precedent is the key warning: **a double-digit EPS beat with a soft forward guide was worth -7%.** Conversely, the entire +19% Q2 move has since round-tripped — the stock is now ~14% *below* its post-Q2 close.

## 5. Catalyst Checklist

1. **Q4 revenue guide vs. ~$645M implied.** The single determinant. A guide in the $620s with FY26 revenue trimmed to $2.37B is a cut dressed as a narrow.
2. **Bookings >$600M / book-to-bill >1.0x.** Three straight sub-parity quarters have driven the derating. Parity breaks the "backlog is melting" narrative; another sub-0.9x quarter confirms it.
3. **Gross margin sustainability as volume returns.** The bull case rests on 41%+ being structural ("smaller factory footprint, operational efficiency"). If Networked revenue recovers +9% q/q and GM falls, Q2 was mix and FY27 estimates come down.
4. **FY2027 framing.** With the ~$0.38 Urbint/Locusview interest-income drag annualizing and FY26 EPS down ~10% y/y, the buy-side needs a path back to $7+. Zacks and Raymond James are already modeling below consensus.
5. **Narrative/strategic.** Any commentary on AMI 2.0 order timing, data-center load vs. distribution capex crowd-out (management pushed back on this in Q2), capital allocation at 2.3x net leverage, or buyback at a 13x multiple.
