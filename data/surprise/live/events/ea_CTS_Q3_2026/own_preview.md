## Prior quarter and guidance (Q2 2026, reported July 28)

- Sales of $144.8M, up 7% year over year
- Adjusted diluted EPS of $0.74, versus $0.57 a year earlier. Management attributed about $0.07 to FX and customer reimbursement, so the underlying beat is smaller than it looks.
- Adjusted gross margin of 41.5%; adjusted EBITDA margin of 25.4%
- **FY2026 guidance raised:** sales to $565–585M (from $560–580M); adjusted EPS to $2.55–2.70 (from $2.35–2.45)
- No explicit Q3 guidance was provided. Management said diversified end markets should keep growing, transportation volumes should be flat to slightly down, and launches of new transportation programs could pressure gross margin.
- Management expects no material tariff impact.

## What to watch (ranked)

1. **Adjusted EPS vs. ~$0.65–0.67.** A repeat of Q2's $0.74 is unlikely to be the base case, since Q2 included one-time items. Watch how much of the result is operational.
2. **Revenue vs. ~$146–147M.** Q2 was $144.8M, so the consensus implies modest sequential growth.
3. **Gross margin trajectory.** Management flagged launch-related margin pressure, so any decline from Q2's 41.5% will matter.
4. **Implied H2 and FY guidance.** Holding or raising the $565–585M and $2.55–2.70 ranges is the minimum expectation after the July raise.
5. **Transportation vs. diversified mix.** Transportation is guided flat to down. Commercial-vehicle pre-buy ahead of 2027 emissions rules is a potential upside driver.

## Catalyst checklist

1. **EPS quality:** Adjusted EPS vs. ~$0.66, with the split between operations and FX/reimbursement. Why it matters: Q2's beat was partly non-operational.
2. **FY guidance:** Whether the $565–585M and $2.55–2.70 ranges are held, raised, or cut. The buy-side will expect at least a reaffirmation after July's raise.
3. **Gross margin:** Whether launch costs reverse or persist. This is the most likely source of an H2 margin miss.
4. **Transportation outlook:** Confirmation of the flat-to-down view, and whether pre-buy demand is visible.
5. **Narrative:** Any M&A, portfolio, or restructuring commentary. None was found in the sources I reviewed.
