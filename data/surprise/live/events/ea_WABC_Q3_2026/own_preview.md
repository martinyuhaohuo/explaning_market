## What to watch (ranked)

1. **NIM and net interest income.** The margin is the key driver for a bank this size. Watch whether it holds near 3.7–3.8% and whether asset yields keep rising faster than funding costs.
2. **EPS versus $1.17.** A small beat or miss will move the stock less than the margin trend will.
3. **Credit quality.** Provision expense, net charge-offs, and nonperforming loans. Any reserve build would be read negatively.
4. **Deposit trends and funding cost.** Cost of funds has been very low at 0.24%. Any rise would compress the margin.
5. **Fee income and expense run-rate.** Trends in noninterest income and noninterest expense, and any efficiency commentary.
