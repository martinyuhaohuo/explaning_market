## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Adjusted net revenue vs. ~$1.24B midpoint.** Revenue is the headline number, and the FY raise implies continued growth through H2.
2. **Adjusted EPS vs. $0.17.** Small absolute numbers mean a few cents moves the percentage surprise.
3. **FY2026 guidance.** Whether management raises the $4.75B–$4.85B revenue range or only reaffirms it, and whether the ~$0.60 EPS guide holds.
4. **Member and product growth.** Net member adds near the ~1.1M Q2 pace, and products added (2.2M in Q2). Ratio of products to members.
5. **Net interest income and credit provisioning.** Loan growth, NIM, and charge-offs in the lending book. Not verified for this quarter.
6. **Adjusted EBITDA margin.** Q2 showed operating leverage (EBITDA +44% vs. revenue +40%). Watch whether that holds.
7. **Non-lending revenue mix.** Products are 87% non-lending. Check fee and technology platform trends.
