# SoFi Technologies (SOFI) — Q3 FY2026 Earnings Preview

**Report date:** October 26, 2026 · **Spot:** $14.48 · **Market cap:** $18.7B
**Event:** Tuesday, October 27, 2026 — press release ~7:00 AM ET (11:00 UTC), conference call 8:00 AM ET. **Pre-market print.**

---

## 1. The Setup in One Paragraph

Revenue is not the question. SoFi has beaten EPS 11 straight quarters and topped revenue estimates for at least seven. The question is **the implied second-half EBITDA ramp into a rising-rate quarter.** Management's reaffirmed FY26 adjusted EBITDA of ~$1.6B against $697.7M booked in H1 requires roughly **$450M per quarter in H2 versus $357.8M actual in Q2 — a ~26% sequential step-up** — at the same time fair-value marks on the loan book are compressing and KBW flags a ">$1B pre-hedge rate headwind" in the quarter. The stock is down ~44% YTD and sits 4.3% above its 52-week low. Consensus revenue of $1.25B looks beatable; the EBITDA math and the FY guide are where the stock gets decided.

### Implied guidance math (do this before the print)

- **Revenue:** FY guide $4.75–4.85B less H1 actual $2.292B → **H2 implied $2.458–2.558B.** A $1.25B Q3 leaves Q4 at $1.21–1.31B. Consensus is not front-loading; Q3 at $1.25B is simply on-trend.
- **EBITDA:** ~$1.6B less $697.7M → **~$900M implied H2, ~$450M/qtr.** This is the stress point. Anything under ~$400M in Q3 makes the FY number arithmetically very hard and invites a guide-down.
- **EPS:** ~$0.60 FY less ~$0.24 H1 → **$0.36 H2**, i.e. $0.17 Q3 + ~$0.19 Q4. Internally consistent.

## 3. Key Metrics to Watch — Ranked

**1. Adjusted EBITDA and the FY $1.6B reaffirmation (highest signal).**
The Q2 stock reaction is the tell: SoFi *beat* on both lines ($1.2B vs $1.11B; $0.12 vs $0.11) and fell 5.9% pre-market because management held EBITDA flat while funding SoFi Coach, crypto, big-business banking and SoFi Plus. CFO Chris Lapointe: "there are just too many large attractive growth areas for us to invest versus adding even more profitability." The buy-side has already punished reinvestment once. A second quarter of revenue upside funding opex, with the FY EBITDA bar untouched, is the most likely path to another down print.

**2. Fair-value marks on personal and student loans.**
Q2: personal loans marked at **104.7% (−71bps QoQ)**, student loans **104.4% (−85bps)**, both compressing on higher discount rates. Management moved its FY rate assumption from *two cuts* to *one to two hikes*. Watch for another 50–100bps of mark erosion and, critically, how much the hedge book absorbs. KBW's ">$1B headwind" figure is explicitly **before hedging** — that qualifier is doing enormous work and is where the bull/bear gap actually lives.

**3. Total capital ratio.**
18.8% at Q2 against management's own stated glide path to "low-to-mid teens." The bear case is capital erosion from rate marks plus record loan growth; the counter is that SoFi starts with ~500bps of cushion above its target and is *intentionally* normalizing. A drop to 16–17% is on-plan, not distress. Below ~15% with originations still accelerating changes the story.

**4. Personal loan credit.**
Q2 net charge-offs **2.62%** (−41bps QoQ, −21bps YoY), 90-day delinquencies 40bps. Credit has been *improving*, the most underappreciated leg of the bull case. A reversal above ~3.0% (Q1 was 3.03%) would validate the "multiple compresses to 8–10x like a traditional lender" bear thesis.

**5. Fee-based revenue and the loan platform business.**
Q2 fee revenue $472.3M, 39% of total. Capital-light LPB added a $3B/3-year BasePoint SMB agreement plus home equity, with $100–200M monthly capacity targets. This segment justifies a fintech multiple rather than a lender multiple. Also watch Technology Platform, which has declined two straight quarters (−23% YoY in Q2, −27% in Q1) — a third decline undercuts the platform narrative.

**6. SoFi Plus and tokenized deposits (narrative, not numbers).**
Plus hit 200k paid members in its first quarter (~$24M annualized); Noto: "I'd be disappointed if we are not at 1 million SoFi Plus members generating annual revenue of $120 million a year from now." SoFiUSD/SoFi Earn and the SoFi Exchange Network are optionality the market currently assigns roughly zero value. Won't move the print; shapes the 2027 bridge.

**Why the skew is negative despite a cheap-looking stock:** the base case is a revenue beat that still doesn't clear the EBITDA bar — exactly the Q2 configuration, which produced a 5.9% decline. A beat-and-hold is not a positive catalyst here. SoFi has to beat *and* raise EBITDA to get paid.

**Historical calibration:** last four post-earnings moves averaged **9.08% absolute**, with declines in three of four (range −6.36% to −15.44%) and a single +5.53% after the October 2025 print. The market has punished this stock on good numbers repeatedly.

## 5. Catalyst Checklist

1. **Adj. EBITDA vs. ~$430M whisper** — the real number that matters. Published consensus centers on revenue/EPS, but the buy-side is modeling backward from the $1.6B FY commitment. A $390M print with $1.27B revenue is a *down* day.
2. **FY26 EBITDA guidance language** — reaffirmed at ~$1.6B, trimmed, or raised. Any softening ("approximately" → "at least $1.55B") is the bear trigger.
3. **Net fair-value impact after hedging** — the explicit reconciliation of the rate move. This is the direct rebuttal (or confirmation) of KBW's >$1B figure and the most likely source of a violent move in either direction.
4. **Total capital ratio trajectory** — needs framing as deliberate normalization from 18.8%, not erosion. Management's handling of this on the call matters more than the number.
5. **Personal loan NCO rate vs. 2.62%** — the improving-credit leg of the bull case. Breaking back above 3% re-rates the stock as a subprime lender.
6. **Member adds vs. ~900k required run-rate** — FY "at least 30%" growth needs ~+1.8M over H2 from 15.8M. Sub-850k adds signals the growth engine is throttling.

**Vol assessment:** 9.7% implied against 9.08% realized is roughly fair — a modest premium with no obvious straddle edge. The asymmetry is directional, not volatility-based. Two considerations:

- **Downside gaps are unprotected.** The 52-week low at $13.86 is only 4.3% below spot. A bear print doesn't find technical support; it breaks to new lows immediately, and the measured move (−12% to −20%) targets $11.60–12.75. The stock already broke $15–16 support on Oct 14 alongside Form 144 insider selling.
- **Upside is crowded with sellers.** Two $15 targets from KBW and Goldman sit right at spot. Even a good print runs into analysts whose published fair value is below the current price, capping the relief rally short of the $20 consensus target.

**Expressing a view:** if you believe the hedge book works and management raises EBITDA, upside calls are the clean expression given the negative skew in the options surface. If you believe the base case (beat, no EBITDA raise), the Q2 analogue argues for a *fade* — this stock has sold off on beats twice running. Selling premium is unattractive: the tails are genuinely fat in both directions because the fair-value-mark disclosure is binary and poorly forecast by the Street.
