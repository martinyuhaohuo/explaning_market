## Q2 2026 Baseline (reported 2026-07-29)
- Net sales $3.27B, up 24% YoY (18 pts organic). Revenue missed the ~$3.38B estimate.
- Adj. EPS $1.52 vs $1.43 consensus. Adj. operating margin 22.6%.
- Adj. FCF $925M. Net cash position, $5.6B liquidity.
- Management cited supply-chain congestion and multi-phase project timing as minor revenue shifts.
- Stock fell ~14% to ~$230.91 on the revenue miss and valuation concerns.

## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)
1. **Revenue vs. the $3.7–3.8B zone, and whether Q2 timing slips were recovered.** A Q2 miss partly from timing makes a clean Q3 beat important.
2. **Orders and backlog.** Not retrieved. Book-to-bill and backlog growth are the main test of whether the 31% organic FY guide is credible.
3. **Adj. operating margin vs. Q2's 22.6%.** Expansion supports the EPS guide; compression would be read negatively.
4. **FY26 guide.** Holding or raising the $6.65–6.75 EPS range is the baseline. A narrowing toward the low end would likely be punished given the valuation.
5. **Free cash flow conversion** after the Q2 $925M adj. FCF.
