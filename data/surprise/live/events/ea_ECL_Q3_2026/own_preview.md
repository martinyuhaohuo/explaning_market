1. **Organic sales growth vs. ~5% run-rate.** This is the main test of the acceleration thesis. A decel below ~4% would undercut the narrative.
2. **Pricing.** Management guided 2H total pricing to 5–6% to offset commodity inflation. Watch whether pricing holds at or above the top of that range.
3. **Adj. operating margin and EPS vs. $2.18.** Commodity costs and growth investments are the margin pressure. Any beat needs to come from price/cost, not volume.
4. **FY26 EPS guide.** The buy-side will look for a raise, reaffirm, or cut. The bar is set by the existing $8.05–8.25 range and whether consensus (~$8.37 per one source) is stale.

## Catalyst Checklist

1. **Organic growth (~5%) vs. the acceleration narrative.** A miss here matters more than the EPS line.
2. **Q3 EPS vs. the ~$2.18 midpoint.** Consensus is at the midpoint, so a beat needs to be meaningful to move the stock.
3. **FY26 EPS guide vs. ~$8.37 consensus.** If consensus is stale, the guide will look like a cut even if it is raised. Confirm the consensus source before the print.
4. **H2 pricing 5–6% vs. commodity inflation.** The spread drives margin, and margin drives the multiple.
5. **Narrative shift:** any M&A, portfolio changes, or restructuring commentary on the call.
