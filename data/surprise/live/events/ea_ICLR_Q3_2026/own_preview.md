## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Guidance revision.** Whether management narrows, raises, or cuts the FY revenue and EPS ranges. For the stock, this matters more than the quarter itself.
2. **Net book-to-bill and direct-fee book-to-bill.** Q2 headline net book-to-bill was 1.51x (net wins $3,120M, backlog $23.4B), but that figure includes pass-through. Direct-fee book-to-bill was 1.2x in Q2 and 1.3x in Q1. Direct-fee is the cleaner demand signal. Watch whether it holds above 1.0x.
3. **Revenue vs. ~$2.01B** and the pass-through share of revenue. Pass-through inflates revenue without adding margin.
4. **Adjusted EPS vs. $2.44–$2.68**, plus operating margin trend. Q2 EPS fell about 27% y/y, so margin recovery is the central question.
5. **Cancellations and backlog conversion.** Q2 cancellations were $562M. Any increase is a negative signal.
