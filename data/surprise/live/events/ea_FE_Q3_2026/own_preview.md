## Context from Prior Quarter (Q2 2026, reported July 28)

- Core EPS was $0.50, against a $0.49 consensus. The company called this in line with plan. It was down from $0.52 a year earlier.
- H1 2026 Core EPS was $1.22, against $1.19 in H1 2025.
- The company reaffirmed FY2026 Core EPS guidance of $2.62–$2.82.
- It reaffirmed long-term Core EPS CAGR near the top of 6–8% for 2026–2030.
- Data center demand rose about 30% since Q1. Contracted demand is 6.4 GW.

**Implied H2 math:** Guidance less H1 ($1.22) leaves $1.40–$1.60 for H2. Q3 consensus of about $0.93 implies Q4 of roughly $0.47–$0.67. A Q3 beat or a guidance reaffirmation at the high end would support the bull case.

## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Core EPS vs. $0.93 consensus.** The quarter matters most for the FY number.
2. **FY2026 guidance ($2.62–$2.82).** Watch for a raise, a reaffirmation, or a move toward the low end.
3. **Data center pipeline and contracted GW.** The growth thesis depends on it. Watch for an update above the 6.4 GW contracted figure.
4. **Long-term EPS growth (6–8% CAGR, top end).** Watch whether management keeps the top-end framing for 2026–2030.
5. **Rate case outcomes and capital plan.** Rate base growth drives the regulated earnings path. Not verified in this preview.
6. **Revenue vs. ~$4.3–4.4B.** Lower priority for a utility. Revenue is mostly pass-through.

1. **Q3 Core EPS vs. $0.93.** A beat with a reaffirmed or raised FY guide is the key positive.
2. **FY2026 guidance ($2.62–$2.82).** Buy-side expects a reaffirmation at minimum and likely a bias toward the upper half.
