## Key Metrics to Watch (Ranked)

1. **Organic revenue growth.** Q2 was +5%, and FY guidance is "mid-single-digit or greater." A print at or above 5% supports the guide. A print below 4% would raise doubts about the full year.
2. **Adj. EPS vs. ~$3.35–$3.40.** H1 adj. EPS was $10.29, up 12%. Management has reaffirmed "strong" EPS growth.
3. **Adj. operating margin.** Q2 was 28.9%, up from 28.2%. FY guidance is +70–80 bps of expansion. Watch whether Q3 margin expansion stays on pace.
4. **Free cash flow.** H1 FCF was $846M, up 4%. Q2 FCF fell 34% to $483M. FY guidance is double-digit growth, so the back half needs to deliver.
5. **FY guidance and FX.** Management has guided FX to roughly neutral for Q3 EPS and about +$0.42 for the full year. A reaffirmation is the base case. A raise would be a positive surprise.
6. **Capital return.** $600M in Q2 buybacks. Watch for any step-up in pace.
