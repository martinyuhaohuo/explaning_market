## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Revenue vs. $4.3B.** Q2 missed on the top line while beating on EPS. Repeating that pattern would be read as lower-quality beat.
2. **EPS vs. ~$2.50.** Check whether the beat is driven by one-offs or underlying earnings.
3. **Asset management performance fees and net flows.** Management said performance fees would be roughly flat with 2025. Weak flows would be the main negative.
4. **Benefits & Protection loss ratio.** Management guided it below the 60–64% range. Beating that range supports margins.
5. **Specialty Benefits growth.** Management expects 2027 growth at or above the top of its 5–9% target range. Any update matters for the multi-year story.
6. **Capital deployment and buybacks.** The $1.5–1.8B FY2026 target is the key capital-return signal.
7. **Full-year EPS guide.** The company reaffirmed $9.01–9.26. A raise is upside, a cut is the largest downside risk.

## Catalyst Checklist

1. **Revenue vs. ~$4.3B:** Q2 showed the top line can lag while EPS beats. A second miss would dominate the reaction.
2. **Asset management flows and performance fees:** The buy-side will focus on whether fee growth is sustainable after the flat-fee outlook.
3. **FY2026 EPS guidance ($9.01–9.26):** A raise is upside. Any language implying the low end is the most likely negative catalyst.
4. **Benefits loss ratio and Specialty Benefits trajectory:** This is the main margin driver and supports the 2027 growth narrative.
5. **Beam Benefits integration and capital deployment:** Any change to the $1.5–1.8B capital target or new M&A would change the narrative.
