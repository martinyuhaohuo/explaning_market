## The setup in one paragraph

Published consensus is stale and almost certainly too high. Zacks shows **$2.46** (+17% YoY), but estimates have been cut 2.8% over 60 days and Wells Fargo went to **$2.30 on Oct 20** — ~6% below consensus — on two specific, identifiable drags: Chilean **encaje** (~10c) and alternatives/variable investment income running below long-term expectations. Critically, Wells flags **both as "significant variances."** That matters more than the headline, because PFG's investor base trades the ex-variance number. The direct analog is **Q3 2025: PFG printed $2.10 vs. $2.20 consensus — a headline miss — and the stock rose 4.5%**, because ex-variance EPS was $2.32 (+13%) and positioning was washed out. Today the stock is again ~9% off its Sept 3 high with five October target cuts behind it. A headline miss is the base case; whether it's a *bad* print depends entirely on the bridge.

## Key metrics to watch — ranked

1. **Ex-significant-variance EPS vs. ~$2.45, and the size of the bridge.** The only number that matters. If encaje + alts are isolated at 10–20c and the underlying is $2.45+, a $2.30 headline is a non-event. If the bridge is thin and the shortfall is operating, it's a problem.
2. **Principal Asset Management net cash flow.** Q2 was −$11.1B at the enterprise level with IM AUM now down YoY. Management guided to continued pressure but cited a **$10B committed-but-not-funded pipeline**. Did any of it fund? This is the structural bear case, not a variance.
3. **Specialty Benefits loss ratio vs. 60–64% FY guidance.** Management said on the Q2 call it should land **below** the range on dental network optimization and favorable incidence. A print in the 58–60s validates the B&P margin story (+300bps in Q2); back above 64% breaks it.
4. **RIS margin and transfer deposits.** 41.5% in Q2 (+60bps). Guided to strong transfer/recurring deposits. The highest-quality earnings stream in the model — any margin give-back gets punished.
5. **Capital deployment vs. the $1.5–1.8B FY target.** Explicitly back-half weighted and dependent on Chile annuity proceeds (deal closed September). Needs a large Q3/Q4; watch buyback pace above $250M/qtr and the Q4 dividend declaration (Q3 was $0.84, +8% YoY).
6. **Beam Benefits.** Closed Sept 1 (~$175M of 2025 premiums, 25k small businesses). Drives the +21.6% premium growth estimate. Watch integration costs and whether B&P premium/fee acceleration actually showed up as promised.

**Not coming on this call:** formal 2027 guidance. PFG combines its outlook call with Q4 (February). *(One IR event page is titled "3Q 2026 … and 2026 Outlook Conference Call," but the Oct 1 announcement describes a standard Q3 call — I read the title as a stale CMS artifact. Qualitative 2027 framing is still plausible; management already pre-announced Specialty Benefits growth at/above the high end of its 5–9% range for 2027 on the Q2 call.)*
