## Context From the Prior Quarter (Q4 FY2026, reported Jul 27, 2026)

- **EPS:** $4.05 vs. ~$3.40 consensus (+19%). Full-year EPS was $12.74.
- **Net income:** $34.3M in Q4.
- **NIM:** 4.8%, down from 5.15% in the prior quarter. The drop came mainly from lower accelerated accretion on purchased loans.
- **Net interest income before provision:** $60.3M, down from $63.1M.
- **Revenue:** Sources conflict. Earnings Whispers shows $104.7M; Quiver and Benzinga show ~$65M (a miss). Check the release.
- **Loans:** $4.59B total, including loans held for sale. Record $257.3M of National Lending originations.
- **Guidance:** Management expects NIM to stay relatively steady, excluding transactional income. The CFO did not expect much further funding-cost relief. Expenses are expected to rise about $1M next quarter, then stabilize. FY27 tax rate guided at 28–29%.

Sources conflict on the FY26 NIM (4.77% vs. 4.8%). The company's own figure is 4.8%.

## Key Metrics to Watch (Ranked)

1. **NIM and accretion.** Does the margin hold near 4.8% now that the accelerated accretion tailwind has faded? The sequential EPS drop from $4.05 to ~$3.40 consensus is mostly this effect, so the margin trend matters more than the headline.
2. **EPS vs. $3.35–$3.50.** A beat is likely if the Q4 pattern holds, but the Q4 beat was partly transactional income, so look at core versus one-time items.
3. **Credit quality.** Provision, charge-offs, and non-performing loans on the $4.6B book. Watch the purchased-loan and National Lending portfolios.
4. **Loan growth and National Lending originations.** Can originations stay near the Q4 record? Net growth matters as much as gross volume.
5. **Expense trajectory.** Confirm the ~$1M step-up and whether the efficiency ratio is stable.
6. **FY27 commentary.** Any update to NIM, tax rate, or capital return. The dividend is $0.01 per quarter, so watch for changes in the capital return plan.
7. **Revenue definition.** Sources disagree on Q4 revenue, so compare the reported net interest income plus non-interest income to the consensus basis.

## Catalyst Checklist

1. **NIM vs. the ~4.8% run-rate.** A sustained margin below ~4.7% would likely be read as the accretion tailwind fading faster than expected.
2. **EPS vs. $3.35–$3.50 consensus.** The margin of the beat matters more than the headline, and buy-side will look for core versus one-time items.
3. **Provision and credit trends** on the purchased-loan and National Lending books.
4. **Guidance on FY27 margin, expenses, and tax (28–29%).** This is the item the buy-side is most likely to focus on for the rest of the year.
5. **Narrative shift.** Any change to the National Lending strategy, capital return, or dividend.
