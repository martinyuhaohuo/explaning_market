## Recent Context (Q2 2026, reported July 29)

- Revenue rose 2% to $2.1B. Domestic residential equipment sales grew 5%, with units up 2% and ASPs up 2%.
- EPS was $4.00 vs. $4.35 consensus, a miss. Gross margin fell to 27.5%, and operating income fell 12% to $238M.
- Management said 2025 OEM pricing, which captured inflation and tariffs, is not repeating in 2026. The A2L transition is "largely behind" the company, and the focus is now on inventory optimization.
- Watsco has missed EPS in three of the last four quarters ([Benzinga](https://it.benzinga.com/quote/WSO/earnings-forecasts)).
- I did not find explicit FY2026 guidance. Verify it on [Watsco IR](https://investors.watsco.com/node/19461/html).

## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Gross margin** vs. the 27.5% in Q2 and the prior-year level. Pricing tailwinds from 2025 are fading, so this is the biggest swing factor.
2. **Revenue vs. ~$2.20B**, with a focus on residential equipment units vs. ASP. A unit-led beat matters more than price-led growth.
3. **EPS vs. ~$4.13** and whether operating leverage holds.
4. **Inventory reduction** and free cash flow. Management has signaled normalizing inventory after the post-A2L peak of over $2B.
5. **Q4 outlook.** Q4 2026 consensus is ~$1.99 EPS on ~$1.69B revenue ([Benzinga](https://it.benzinga.com/quote/WSO/earnings-forecasts)). The buy side will read the full-year tone from this.

- **Bull signals:** Management cites unit share gains, stronger e-commerce or new-customer wins, and margin stability.
- **Base signals:** Commentary that inventory is "normalizing" and pricing is "stabilizing," with no new guidance.
- **Bear signals:** Words like "pressure," "competitive pricing," or "channel destocking." A weaker Q4 outlook would be the most damaging item.

## Catalyst Checklist

1. **Gross margin vs. 27.5%.** The market is watching whether the 2025 pricing benefit is gone and what replaces it.
2. **Revenue vs. ~$2.20B.** A miss would extend the pattern of recent misses. Look at unit vs. price split.
3. **Q4 2026 EPS vs. ~$1.99 consensus.** This is what buy-side investors will weigh most.
4. **Inventory and free cash flow.** A clear reduction would support the cash-return story.
5. **Narrative:** any change to A2L commentary, pricing outlook, or M&A/capital allocation.
