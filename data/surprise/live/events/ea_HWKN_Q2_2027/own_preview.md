- Revenue: record $315.7M, up 8%. Organic growth about 5%, which management tied to a return to historical organic growth rates in FY2027.
- Diluted EPS: $1.35, down 4%. This was a miss against the $1.41–$1.42 consensus. Revenue was in line with the ~$313M–$320M estimates.
- Margins: pressured by raw material volatility. The LIFO reserve increase cost $0.8M of gross profit, $0.6M worse than a year earlier.
- No formal FY2027 guidance was given in the press release.
- Leverage: 1.36x adjusted EBITDA, with continued debt paydown planned.
- Quarterly dividend raised 5% to $0.20.

Q4 FY2026 (reported May 2026): EPS $0.74 vs. $0.76 consensus (miss); revenue $265.9M, beat by about 4%. Q3 FY2026 (Jan 2026): EPS $0.69 vs. $0.73 (miss).
