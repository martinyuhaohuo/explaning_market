# Teradyne (TER) Q3 FY2026 Earnings Preview

**Report:** Q3 FY2026, scheduled 2026-10-27 at 20:30 UTC (4:30 PM ET). MarketBeat reports the release after the close, with the call on 2026-10-28 at 8:30 AM ET. No results were found, so this is pre-release.

## Consensus Estimates

Sources disagree on consensus, so the table gives the range.

| Metric | Q3 Guidance (Jul 28) | Consensus (Oct 20 prior) | Q2 FY26 Actual |
|---|---|---|---|
| Revenue | $1.20B – $1.30B | ~$1.26B | $1.329B |
| Non-GAAP EPS | $1.85 – $2.15 | $2.05 – $2.08 | $2.47 |
| GAAP EPS | $1.79 – $2.09 | n/a | n/a |

- Consensus figures are as of about 2026-10-20 and come from MarketBeat and Benzinga. Some older sources show a much lower pre-guide consensus (about $1.43 EPS and $1.0B revenue). Those figures look stale, so treat them as unreliable.
- Q2 beat both revenue ($1.33B vs. ~$1.22B) and EPS ($2.47 vs. ~$2.06). The Q3 guide sits well above the prior consensus, which is why the consensus moved up.

## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Semiconductor Test revenue.** It was about $1.12B in Q2, so it drives the total. Watch for AI-related demand.
2. **Q4 revenue and EPS guidance.** The buy-side will focus on the next-quarter outlook more than the print itself.
3. **Non-GAAP EPS vs. ~$2.05–2.08.** A beat is likely given Q2's pattern, but the bar has risen.
4. **Gross and operating margin.** Check whether they hold or expand from Q2 levels.
5. **Robotics and Product Test.** These are smaller contributors, but commentary on them shapes the narrative.
6. **Wafer fab equipment and 2027 commentary.** The CEO has pointed to WFE investment as a 2027 growth driver.

## Catalyst Checklist

1. **Q3 EPS vs. ~$2.05–2.08 consensus.** A beat is expected, so the reaction will depend more on the guide.
2. **Q4 revenue guide vs. the Q3 run-rate.** This is the main driver. Watch for an implied step-up or a flattening.
3. **AI and Semiconductor Test demand.** Management's comments on AI orders and visibility will set the 2027 narrative.
4. **Margin trajectory.** Expansion supports the bull case; compression would weigh on it.
5. **Capital allocation and strategic items.** Any update on the Technoprobe investment or M&A would be a narrative shift.
