## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Weather-normalized vs. actual load and the weather impact on Q3 EPS.** Q3 is peak cooling season, so weather drives the quarter. Watch whether management says the full-year guide still assumes normal weather.
2. **FY2026 EPS guidance reaffirmation or change.** Reaffirming $2.38 to $2.48 is the base case. A move toward the low end would be a negative surprise.
3. **Revenue vs. consensus.** Q2 missed badly on revenue, so the revenue line is a credibility question even if EPS is in line.
4. **Holding company drag.** Higher interest expense weighed in Q2. Check whether the drag is widening.
5. **Capital plan and rate case timing.** Rate base growth and recovery mechanisms drive the long-term EPS growth rate. Any update to the capital plan or rate filings matters for the 2027 outlook.
