## Prior Quarter (Q2 2026, reported Aug. 4, 2026)

- **Net sales:** $544.6M, up ~1% y/y, versus consensus near $582M (a miss).
- **Adjusted EPS:** $3.08 reported, including a $1.18/share IEEPA tariff refund. Excluding the refund, comparable EPS was $1.90, versus consensus of about $1.87–1.89.
- **Guidance:** Sales growth cut to 3–5% (from 7–9%) as tariff-related price cuts are passed through. Adjusted FY26 EPS raised to $8.50–8.80, but **$8.20–8.50 excluding the refund**. The guide covers tariffs enacted as of Aug. 3.

## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Revenue vs. consensus, and the sales-growth trajectory.** The Q2 miss plus the cut to 3–5% growth makes top-line the main risk. Watch whether the price pass-through is cutting into volume.
2. **Underlying EPS ex-tariff-refund.** Any Q3 EPS beat that relies on tariff recovery is lower quality. Look for the clean number against ~$1.99.
3. **Gross margin.** Tariff cost relief from the shift from IEEPA to Section 301 duties should help, but price cuts can offset it. Check whether margins expand sequentially.
4. **FY26 guidance.** Does management hold the $8.20–8.50 ex-refund range, or narrow it? A raise on clean EPS would be a positive surprise. A cut to sales growth below 3% would be a negative one.
5. **Free cash flow and inventory.** Tariff refunds helped cash flow in Q2. Check whether Q3 cash generation holds up without them.
