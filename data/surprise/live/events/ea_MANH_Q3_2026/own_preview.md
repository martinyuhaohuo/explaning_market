**Prior quarter (Q2 FY2026, reported Jul 28, 2026):**
- Revenue $297.8M, up from $272.4M a year earlier.
- Cloud subscription revenue $126.7M, up from $100.4M (about 26%).
- RPO $2.47B, up 23%. Management expects year-end RPO at the high end of $2.62–$2.68B.
- GAAP EPS $0.85 (vs. $0.93 prior year), including $8M in restructuring expense.
- Record bookings for a third straight quarter. Over 40% of new cloud bookings came from on-prem conversions to Manhattan Active.
- Management flagged roughly a 1-point FX headwind in H2.

## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Cloud subscription revenue growth.** This drives the thesis. Q2 was about 26%, and the FY cloud midpoint of $505.5M implies about 24% growth. Any deceleration below that would be read negatively.
2. **Bookings and RPO.** Watch the q/q RPO trajectory toward the $2.62–$2.68B year-end target. A fourth record-bookings quarter would support the narrative.
3. **Full-year guidance revision.** Buy-side will focus on whether the $5.44–$5.50 EPS and $1.16–$1.166B revenue ranges are raised again, held, or narrowed to the low end.
4. **Conversion pace.** The share of new cloud bookings from on-prem conversions (40%+ in Q2) and the attrition in license/maintenance revenue.
5. **Margins and GAAP vs. adjusted gap.** Restructuring charges are recurring-ish; check whether they continue and whether adjusted operating margin holds.
6. **FX and H2 commentary.** The 1-point FX headwind is a known item; any change in the outlook matters more than the number itself.

## Catalyst Checklist

1. **Cloud revenue vs. ~24–26% growth run-rate.** This is the main growth signal. Buy-side will compare against the implied H2 cloud trajectory.
2. **RPO and bookings vs. the $2.62–$2.68B year-end target.** Confirmation that the high end is achievable.
3. **FY2026 guidance.** Expectation is a hold or modest raise after the Q2 raise. A cut would be the largest negative.
4. **Conversion and attrition commentary.** Evidence that on-prem conversions are sustaining cloud growth as license revenue declines.
5. **Restructuring and margins.** $8M charge in Q2; any follow-on charges or margin pressure would be a narrative negative.
