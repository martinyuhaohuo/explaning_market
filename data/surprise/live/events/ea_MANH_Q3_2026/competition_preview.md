## 2. Key Metrics to Watch — Ranked

1. **RPO (highest signal).** Q2 exited at $2.47B, +23% y/y and +5% sequentially, after **three consecutive record bookings quarters**. RPO is the cleanest read on bookings durability and the entire reason the stock doubled. A print below ~$2.55B (sub-20% y/y) breaks the "accelerating bookings" narrative that Morgan Stanley and Citi upgraded on. **Watch the sequential rate, not just y/y** — easier comps flatter the y/y line.
2. **Cloud subscription revenue and the 20% floor.** $126.7M in Q2 (+26.2%). The guide is ~$130M. Cloud growth has been decelerating on an absolute basis as the base scales; the market has anchored on "above 20%." ~$132M+ (≈+25%) is a clear positive; $128M or below is a problem even if total revenue holds.
3. **FY revenue and adj. EPS guidance revision.** Management raised at both Q1 and Q2 and has explicitly run a "conservative guidance" playbook all year (the CFO said as much in April). The buy side now *expects* the raise, which means an in-line reiteration functions as a cut. See the Q4 implied math above.
4. **Adj. operating margin vs. the 36.9% guide.** This is a 200bp sequential step-up off 34.9% in Q2. It is achievable seasonally but it is a high bar, and margin is where cloud mix shift, AI/agent R&D spend, and go-to-market investment for Editions all collide. Note the persistent GAAP/non-GAAP gap (22.3% vs. 34.9% in Q2) — mostly stock comp; GAAP EPS was *down* y/y in both Q1 and Q2.
5. **AI monetization — Active Agents.** As of Q2, Active Agents touched **>10% of the Active install base** via pilots or subscriptions, with **100% pilot-to-subscription conversion**, but management called revenue contribution "still too early to quantify" for 2026. The ask tonight is a quantified attach rate, pricing construct, or a 2027 framing. This is the single most likely source of a *narrative* upgrade.
6. **Editions traction.** The three-tier packaging (Essentials / Enterprise / Enterprise Premier across ActiveWarehouse, ActiveTransportation, ActiveStore, ActiveOrder) launched **September 22, 2026** — only ~1 week of Q3. Expect pipeline color, not revenue. Management framed it as "a packaging motion, not a new product line." Any evidence of mid-market logo wins is incremental upside; any hint of ASP cannibalization in the enterprise base is a red flag.
7. **Services and maintenance (the known drags).** FY services guided to just **+2% to $513.5M** on FX and European implementation timing; maintenance guided to **-12% to ~$114M** on cloud migration. These are structural and understood — but services is the leading indicator for future cloud go-lives, so a *further* services cut would be read as a forward demand signal, not just FX.
8. **Cash generation and buyback.** Q2 operating cash flow $90.7M, cash $186.1M, DSO 67 days. The company repurchased $275M in 1H'26 (~$225M authorization remaining). An authorization increase at this price would be a notable statement of management's own valuation view.

## 4. Catalyst Checklist

1. **RPO ≥ ~$2.58B / +20%+ y/y** — the bookings-durability proof point. This, more than revenue, is what the 70% rally is collateralized against. Below 20% y/y and the multiple is indefensible.
2. **Cloud subscription ≥ $130M with growth ≥ 24%** — the explicit threshold the sell side has published. A deceleration through 20% is the thesis-breaker.
3. **FY2026 guidance raise with a lifted Q4 bar** — not just a raise. The buy side wants FY revenue ~$1,170M+ and adj. EPS ~$5.55+, i.e., Q4 implied revenue growth above the ~6% currently embedded.
4. **A quantified Active Agents / ActivePlatform number** — attach rate, ARR contribution, or a 2027 framing. "Too early to quantify" for a second straight quarter, with the stock at 36x forward EPS, is a sell-the-news setup.
5. **Adj. operating margin delivery at ~36.9%** plus any 2027 investment-year language. Early signaling of elevated AI/go-to-market spend for 2027 is the under-discussed risk on tonight's call.
6. **Editions commentary** — mid-market pipeline evidence vs. any sign of enterprise ASP cannibalization.

- **Sell-side:** 9 of 11 Buy. Consensus PT clusters **$222–$236** depending on panel (S&P Global ~$223.70 on 11 analysts; ~$235.90 on a more recent 11-analyst poll; ~$222.91 on 14). **Asymmetry:** skewed to the downside. The bull case requires a *narrative* catalyst — a quantified AI number or a step-up in RPO — not just a beat-and-raise.
