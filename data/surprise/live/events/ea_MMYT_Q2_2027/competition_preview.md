**The definitional trap.** Three EPS conventions are circulating. Zacks-style consensus runs on *adjusted* diluted EPS (Q3 FY26 actual $0.52, Q1 FY27 actual $0.53). TipRanks runs a GAAP-ish series (Q1 FY27 actual $0.15 vs. $0.09). MarketBeat's $0.0356 for this quarter is a GAAP-basis number — it is not comparable to the $0.53 printed in August. Expect at least one headline on Monday morning comparing the wrong two numbers.

**The revenue math doesn't work.** For IFRS revenue of $267M you need ~+16.6% reported growth off last year's $229.3M. With the rupee down ~8–9% YoY (USD/INR ~95–96 in Jul–Sep 2026 vs. ~88 a year ago), that implies **~+26–28% constant-currency** growth — well above the +16.1% cc MMYT printed in Q1 FY27 and above anything in the last eight quarters. My base case is $243–252M. Note the precedent: at this same quarter last year consensus was ~$264M against a $229.3M print, a 13% miss, and the stock fell 10.25%.

### Key metrics to watch (ranked)

1. **Hotels & packages adjusted margin, cc growth.** Ran +21.6% cc in Q2 FY26 and +21.3% cc ($134.5M) in Q1 FY27. BofA models ~+20%. This segment is carrying the entire growth story while air stalls — anything with a 1-handle is a problem.
2. **Air ticketing adjusted margin, cc growth.** The crux. Indian carriers flew 11.82M domestic passengers in September 2026, **–6.5% YoY** and the weakest month since Nov 2022; Air India and IndiGo cut ~22,000 domestic flights (–6%) across Jun–Sep. Air adjusted margin still grew +10.6% cc (Q2 FY26) and +10.8% cc (Q1 FY27) on fare inflation (+20.5% on 72 routes vs. Mar 2025) and international mix. Holding double-digit cc with volumes down 6.5% would be a genuine positive surprise.
3. **Adjusted operating margin as % of gross bookings.** Management has anchored on 1.8% ($44.2M in Q2 FY26, $51.4M in Q1 FY27) and explicitly said it is prioritising share over margin. Watch marketing & sales promotion: 5.2% of GB in Q2 FY26, 5.4% in Q1 FY27.
4. **Bus ticketing cc growth.** +44.1% cc in Q2 FY26, decelerating to +32.4% in Q1 FY27; consensus ~+26%. Beneficiary of air-to-ground substitution.
5. **MMT India IPO.** Confidential pre-filed DRHP with SEBI on 17 Jul 2026; >$1bn offer-for-sale. Any timeline, size, or fungibility-structure detail is a live re-rating catalyst independent of the quarter.

### Catalyst checklist

1. **Air ticketing cc adjusted margin vs. ~+10–11% run-rate** — tests whether fare inflation and international mix can offset a 6.5% volume decline. The single biggest swing factor.
2. **Hotels & packages cc growth vs. ~20% whisper** — the bull case is entirely "hotels and bus outgrow air weakness." Sub-18% breaks the thesis.
3. **Full-year adjusted margin growth framing** — reaffirm "~20%" or walk it back.
4. **MMT India IPO timing and structure** — size, OFS mechanics, and the fungibility/ADR path management said it would only discuss post-IPO. Also how the $794M cash balance plus IPO proceeds handle the convertible notes (the $200M 2028 bonds are in the money).
5. **Capital return** — $7.8M repurchased in Q1 FY27; with the stock down ~46% YTD, an upsized buyback would read as a confidence signal.
