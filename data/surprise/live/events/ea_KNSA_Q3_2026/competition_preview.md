## 1. Setup in One Paragraph

KNSA is a single-product, newly-profitable commercial story: ARCALYST (rilonacept) in recurrent pericarditis is compounding ~50–60% YoY, and management has raised FY26 guidance at each of the last two prints ($900–920M → $930–945M → $980–995M). The stock has nearly doubled YTD (+73%), hit a 52-week high of $84.06 on ~Oct 21, then sold off ~15% in three sessions into the print (-6.8% today to ~$71.45) with no company-specific news — a meaningful de-risking. **The key asymmetry: this name trades on the guidance raise and the revenue line, but it has fallen on two of its last four prints, and both down-prints were driven by EPS, not revenue.** Q3'25 beat revenue by ~9% and still fell because EPS came in $0.23 vs. $0.33 consensus. Opex modeling — specifically collaboration expense and a lumpy tax rate — is where the sell-side keeps getting it wrong, and R&D is now ramping for a Phase 3.

**What each would need operationally:**
- **Bull:** The Q2 enrollment surge was structural (prescriber-base widening), not a one-off. Management volunteers 2027 color, which they have avoided — that alone would be the signal.
- **Base:** Normal quarter. Note the setup matters more than the print: with the stock already -15% from its high, an in-line-plus-raise could still rally. The pre-print drawdown has done the work a miss usually does.
- **Bear:** Watch for the *shape* of the raise. A narrow raise to $1.000–1.005B implies Q4 of only ~$275M after a $267M Q3 — management signaling deceleration. Pair that with a sub-$0.32 EPS and this is a Q3'25 repeat.

## 5. Catalyst Checklist

1. **FY26 guidance ≥$1.00B** — mechanically required; the magnitude and the implied Q4 are the real tell.
2. **Revenue >$265M** vs. ~$260M consensus — I'd call ~$265–267M the whisper, given the Q2 enrollment commentary.
3. **EPS ≥$0.35** — the historical trip-wire. Collaboration expense % of sales and the tax rate are the two lines to read first in the release.
4. **Penetration ≥22%** of the 14,000-patient multiple-recurrence pool — the durability of the 2027–2028 growth narrative.
5. **Narrative/strategic:** Ross Moat was appointed President & COO on Oct 22 — four days ahead of the print. Watch for any commercial-strategy reframing or organizational commentary on the call. Separately, insiders sold ~$57.9M over twelve months with no purchases, which the bears will keep citing.
