## Prior Quarter (Q2 2026, reported ~July 29)

- Net sales $8.03B, slightly above estimates (~$7.89B).
- Adjusted EPS $0.35 vs. $0.42 consensus, a miss.
- Adj. EBITDA $1.14B, margin 14.2% (down from 15.3% a year earlier).
- Freight headwind raised to ~$300M YoY, up from ~$50M in April. Drivers: fuel, Middle East-related shipping rates, and European/North American domestic transport costs.
- A newly announced $100/ton North American containerboard increase is **not** in 2026 guidance. Management frames it as groundwork for 2027, with price recovery expected late 2026 into 2027.
- Liquidity $5.24B.

## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Q3 Adj. EBITDA vs. ~$1.3B guide.** This implies a sequential jump from $1.14B. It is the single most important number.
2. **Freight cost trajectory.** Does the ~$300M headwind widen again? Any upward revision is a negative signal.
3. **Price realization.** Containerboard and box pricing, plus the timing of the $100/ton increase.
4. **FY26 EBITDA guide ($4.9–5.1B).** Watch for narrowing toward the low end.
5. **Volume trends** in North America and Europe, and any read on 2027 demand.
6. **EPS vs. $0.67.** Adjusted EPS has missed in recent quarters, so a miss is a live risk even if EBITDA holds.

## Catalyst Checklist

1. **Q3 Adj. EBITDA vs. ~$1.3B guide.** The guide implies a large sequential step-up, so the buy-side will look for evidence the pricing and cost picture supports it.
2. **Freight headwind update.** Whether the $300M figure holds or grows is the main swing factor for the FY guide.
3. **FY26 EBITDA guide ($4.9–5.1B).** The buy-side expects reaffirmation at minimum. A cut would likely dominate the reaction.
4. **2027 pricing commentary.** The $100/ton increase sits outside 2026 guidance. Any detail on acceptance or timing is the main narrative driver.
5. **Adjusted EPS vs. $0.67.** A miss has been the pattern recently, so the market may discount it unless EBITDA beats.
