## Prior Quarter and Guidance (Q2 FY2026, reported July 28)

- **Revenue:** $118.4M, up 12% y/y, against a ~$110.1M consensus.
- **EPS:** $1.83 diluted GAAP ($1.90 adjusted), against a $0.35 consensus. This beat was driven by tariff refunds.
- **Tariff effect:** IEEPA refunds cut COGS by about $15.0M. Gross margin was 51.4% reported and 38.7% ex-refunds.
- **Full-year guidance (raised):** Revenue growth of ~8.5% over 2025. EPS of ~$5 reported and ~$4 ex-refunds. Q4 revenue growth is expected to exceed Q3.
- **Margin path:** Ex-refund gross margin of ~40% for the year, with Q3 and Q4 in the low-40s.
- **Headwinds:** Inbound freight rates, oil-linked component costs, and higher SG&A from freight, logistics, and digital advertising.

Sources: [SEC 8-K exhibit](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000895456/000143774926024672/ex_973045.htm), [Business Wire release](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260728653649/en/), [Nasdaq call highlights](https://www.nasdaq.com/articles/rocky-brands-q2-earnings-call-highlights).

## Key Metrics to Watch (Ranked)

1. **Ex-refund gross margin.** Does Q3 land in the low-40s? Q2 ex-refund was 38.7%, so this is the main test of underlying profitability.
2. **Revenue vs. ~$130M.** Management implied Q3 growth below the full-year 8.5%, so a beat here would be a positive surprise.
3. **Any residual tariff refund in Q3.** Check whether more refund income was booked. If so, EPS will look better than the underlying business.
4. **Freight and SG&A.** Watch whether the freight and digital-advertising spend pushes SG&A/sales up more than guided.
5. **Wholesale vs. retail (DTC) growth.** Both segments grew in Q2. Any divergence matters for the mix story.
6. **Full-year guidance.** Watch whether the ~$5 reported / ~$4 ex-refund EPS guide is held or raised.
