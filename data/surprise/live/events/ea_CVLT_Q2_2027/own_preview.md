## Where Commvault stands (Q1 FY27, reported 2026-07-28)

- **Total revenue:** $314.1M, up 11% YoY. Benzinga cited a $310.4M estimate, so this was a beat.
- **EPS:** $1.42 vs. $1.16 consensus per Benzinga.
- **Subscription revenue:** $267M, up 16%, above the $265M high end of guidance.
- **Subscription ARR:** $1,054.3M, up 22% YoY.
- **SaaS ARR:** $424.3M, up from $306.9M a year earlier. SaaS revenue was $100.6M, up 39%.
- **Free cash flow:** $51M, up 71%. Non-GAAP EBIT margin was 22.8%.

**Implied H2 ARR build:** Reaching the $1.200B low end from $1.054B in Q1 requires about $146M of net subscription ARR adds over Q2–Q4, roughly $49M per quarter. Reaching the high end needs about $52M per quarter. This is the key number to check against the Q2 print.

## Metrics to watch, ranked

1. **Subscription ARR and net new ARR.** Compare against the ~$49–52M/quarter pace the FY guide implies.
2. **Subscription revenue vs. the $264–268M guide.** Q1 landed at the high end of its range, so a beat above $268M would be consistent with the trend.
3. **SaaS ARR growth and SaaS revenue.** The 39% SaaS growth rate is the main growth driver.
4. **Non-GAAP EBIT margin vs. the ~20% Q2 guide and ~21% FY guide.** Margin misses have drawn sharp selloffs in past quarters.
5. **FY27 guide changes.** Watch for a raise on subscription revenue and whether total revenue and FCF guidance hold.
