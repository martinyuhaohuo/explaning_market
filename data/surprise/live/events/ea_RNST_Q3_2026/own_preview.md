## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Net interest margin.** Q2 reported NIM was 3.83%, down 4 bps linked quarter. Adjusted NIM was flat at 3.61%. Management guided to a *stable* margin, with help from loan repricing and securities roll-off. Any compression below guidance would hit the EPS beat/miss most directly.
2. **Loan growth.** Q2 loans grew $220.9M (4.7% annualized). Management targets mid-single-digit growth in 2H. The pipeline is up 6–10% since the start of Q2, but CRE payoffs and pricing competition are headwinds.
3. **Deposits.** Q2 deposits fell $398.4M, mainly from seasonal public-fund outflows. Management expects a 2H rebound. Watch deposit costs and any change to that rebound call.
4. **Fee income and expense run-rate.** Not researched. Check for any change in efficiency ratio or noninterest expense guidance.
5. **Credit quality.** Not researched. Watch NCOs, provisions, and criticized loans, particularly CRE.

## Catalyst Checklist

1. **NIM vs. the "stable" guide (3.83% reported).** A decline of more than a few bps would signal that the repricing tailwind is weaker than management said.
2. **Loan growth vs. mid-single-digit 2H target.** The pipeline is up 6–10%, so the buy side will test whether that converts into Q3 balances.
3. **Deposit trajectory.** Whether the public-fund outflow reverses in 2H is a key test of the funding story.
4. **Any change to 2H or full-year commentary.** Buy-side expectations are set by the qualitative guidance from the Q2 call, so any shift in tone will matter.
