## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Full-year guide, especially the H2 implied number.** The Q2 cut was the main story. The question is whether the Q3 print and the Q4 outlook hold, or whether there is a second cut.
2. **WATCHMAN.** Management guided full-year growth to flat to low-single digits. U.S. standalone procedures fell in the low teens year over year in Q2. Watch for procedure volume and any commentary on referral patterns and clinical evidence.
3. **U.S. electrophysiology (EP) share.** Management cited significant competitive share loss to pulsed field ablation (PFA) rivals. Watch EP revenue growth and any share-recovery timeline.
4. **Interventional cardiology and other growth segments.** Q2 showed strength here, which offset the EP and WATCHMAN weakness. Check whether it continues.
5. **Margin and tax quality.** The Q2 beat was helped by a low adjusted tax rate (10.4%). Look at the underlying operating margin and whether the tax benefit repeats.

## Catalyst Checklist

1. **Q3 EPS vs. $0.80–0.84.** A print at the top of the range or above the higher consensus would be a positive signal. A print at the bottom would reinforce the guidance-cut narrative.
2. **FY2026 guidance (organic 5–6%, EPS $3.28–3.32).** The buy-side will expect a reiteration. Any cut would be the dominant negative.
