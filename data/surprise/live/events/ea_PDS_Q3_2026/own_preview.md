## Last Quarter (Q2 2026) Baseline

From the [Q2 2026 release](https://www.bnnbloomberg.ca/press-releases/2026/07/29/precision-drilling-announces-2026-second-quarter-unaudited-financial-statements/):
- **Revenue:** C$452.8M, up 11.4% YoY
- **Adjusted EBITDA:** C$97.1M, down 10.2% YoY (US rig reactivation costs and lower international margins)
- **Net loss attributable to shareholders:** C$(1.2)M
- **Utilization days:** Canada 5,510; US 3,216; International 637
- **Capital returns:** Repaid $50M of debt and repurchased $12M of shares. Operating cash flow was $146M.

Another [Q2 summary](https://www.barchart.com/story/news/3514161/precision-drilling-announces-2026-second-quarter-unaudited-financial-statements) gave EPS of $(0.38) against a $0.42 estimate and revenue of US$327.1M. The per-share figure conflicts with the release, so use the release figures.

## Forward Guidance (from Q2 release and call)

- **Q3 Canada margins:** C$12,000–13,000 per utilization day
- **Q3 US margins:** US$7,000–8,000 per utilization day
- **Q4 margins:** Expected to approach US$10,000 per utilization day (from call commentary, per [call summary](https://statementdog.com/analysis/PDS/earnings_calls/315791))
- **2026 capex:** C$265M, unchanged
- **Canada:** H2 activity expected above prior-year levels
- **US:** Active rigs rising, 43 as of the Q2 call

## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Canadian and US margin per utilization day vs. the Q3 guidance ranges.** This is the main operating driver. Results inside C$12–13k (Canada) and US$7–8k (US) are in line. Above-range results would be the upside signal.
2. **US rig count and utilization trajectory.** The reactivation story matters most. Watch whether management confirms the rig count is still rising and what it says about Q4 demand.
3. **Q4 margin outlook (~US$10k/day).** The buy-side will test whether this holds, since it implies a step-up.
4. **Adjusted EBITDA and margin trend.** Q2 EBITDA fell YoY, so the comparison matters.
5. **Free cash flow, debt reduction, and buybacks.** Q2 showed debt paydown and buybacks. Continued deleveraging supports the capital return narrative.
