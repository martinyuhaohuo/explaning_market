## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Retail revenue and same-store trends.** SNDL's core is the liquor and cannabis retail network in Canada (Ace Liquor, Wine and Beyond, Liquor Depot, Value Buds, Spiritleaf). Revenue has been running above consensus, so the question is whether the beat is organic or acquisition-driven.
2. **Gross and operating margin.** Q2 revenue beat but EPS missed, so watch for margin compression from retail mix, pricing, or cannabis wholesale.
3. **Net loss versus the -US$0.02 consensus.** Pay attention to non-GAAP versus GAAP differences and any one-time items.
4. **Free cash flow and balance sheet.** Cash generation and the investment portfolio (SNDL also deploys capital across North American cannabis).
5. **FY2026 outlook.** Whether management reaffirms, narrows, or moves the full-year revenue and EPS view relative to the CA$909M / -CA$0.085 consensus.
