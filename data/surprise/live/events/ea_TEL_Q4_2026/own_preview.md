# TE Connectivity (TEL): Q4 FY2026 Earnings Preview

**Company:** TE Connectivity plc (NYSE: TEL)
**Quarter:** Fiscal Q4 2026
**Release:** 2026-10-28, 10:00 UTC (about 6:00 a.m. ET, pre-market). Note that one source gave a Nov 4 date as an estimate, so confirm against the company's announcement.

Consensus figures come from a single preview and Benzinga's table, both dated around the July Q3 print. I could not confirm a current consensus as of Oct 27, so treat these as approximate. Also, the YoY growth rates differ across sources, so verify them.

**Prior quarter (Q3 FY2026, reported Jul 22):** Net sales were a record $5.16B (+14% reported, +12% organic). Adj. EPS was a record $2.94 (+22%), and management cited AI-related demand as a driver.

## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Organic sales growth vs. the ~11% guide.** Management has guided conservatively, so the question is whether the beat-and-raise pattern holds.
2. **Adj. EPS vs. ~$3.05, and margin trajectory.** Watch for incremental margin expansion.
3. **FY2027 outlook.** The buy-side will focus on the first full-year guide. Sources suggest full-year 2026 implies roughly 15% sales growth and 23% adj. EPS growth, but I could not verify this against the release.
4. **Segment performance.** Look at the Transportation and Industrial/Communications segments, particularly AI/data-center connectivity commentary, which has been the core growth narrative.
5. **Free cash flow and capital return.** Confirm FCF conversion and buyback/dividend pace.

## Scenario Analysis

These scenarios are my own estimates built from the guidance. They are not sourced.

| Scenario | Revenue | Adj. EPS | Key driver | Stock reaction (estimate) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Bull** | ~$5.35B (+13%) | ~$3.15+ | AI/data-center demand drives a beat, plus an FY27 outlook above the street | +5% to +8% |
| **Base** | ~$5.27–5.30B | ~$3.05–3.10 | In line to slight beat; FY27 guide roughly in line | −2% to +3% |
| **Bear** | ~$5.18–5.22B | ~$2.95–3.00 | Industrial/transport softness; soft FY27 commentary | −5% to −8% |

**What would signal each scenario:**
- **Bull:** Management raises the FY27 outlook above consensus, cites order or backlog growth in AI-related connectivity, and reports margin expansion above the guide.
- **Base:** Results land near consensus, with a measured FY27 framework and no major new narrative.
- **Bear:** Organic growth decelerates toward single digits, management trims FY27 expectations, or margins flatten despite the volume.

**Historical context:** On July 22, TEL beat both revenue and EPS but fell roughly 4–5% in the regular session. Sources conflict on the premarket move (one reports +5.75%, another −7%). The pattern suggests a high bar: the stock had run up into the print, and the guide offered no new catalyst. TEL has beaten EPS consensus in each of the past four quarters, so a "beat but fall" outcome is a live risk.

## Catalyst Checklist

1. **Organic sales vs. ~$5.25B guide / ~$5.28B consensus.** A beat that is driven by AI-related demand is the bull case. A beat driven by pricing or FX would carry less weight.
2. **FY2027 guidance vs. buy-side expectations.** This is the most important item. The buy-side will want to hear a growth framework above roughly low-double-digit sales growth.
3. **Adj. EPS vs. $3.05 / $3.07 and incremental margins.** Margin expansion is needed to justify the premium.
4. **AI/data-center narrative.** Look for quantified commentary on orders, backlog, or capacity additions.
5. **Capital allocation.** Buyback and dividend updates, plus any M&A or portfolio changes.
