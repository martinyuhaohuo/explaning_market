## Recent Context (Q2 FY2026, reported 2026-07-28)

- Net sales $4.5B, +7% YoY; organic +4%
- Adj. EPS $2.23, flat YoY; reported EPS $1.96
- Bright spots: aerospace, Latin American architectural coatings. Weak: automotive refinish
- FY2026 adj. EPS guidance of $7.70–$8.10 **reaffirmed**
- Capex guide $650–$700M
- Covered ~90% of cost inflation in Q2; expects full coverage by Q4, a quarter early

Sources: [PPG Q2 release (SEC 8-K)](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000079879/000007987926000246/exhibit99-2q2026earningsre.htm), [Seeking Alpha](https://seekingalpha.com/pr/20598385), [Quartr Q2 summary](https://quartr.com/events/ppg-industries-inc-ppg-q2-2026_3Yy16wUh), [Chartmill](https://www.chartmill.com/news/external/97683/ppg-q2-earnings-lag-estimates-despite-strong-sales-growth)

Q3 commentary from Q2: organic sales growth guided to low-to-mid single digits, led by aerospace, Latin American architecture, and packaging coatings. Q3 adj. EBITDA margin guided flat to down up to 100bp YoY.

## What to Watch (ranked)

1. **Adj. EPS vs. ~$2.14 consensus.** Q2 missed on a flat-YoY print. A beat needs cost recovery to show up in margin, not just in price.
2. **Q3 adj. EBITDA margin vs. the flat-to-down-100bp guide.** Margin is the swing factor. Anything at the low end is a negative read.
3. **FY2026 EPS guide ($7.70–$8.10) and whether the midpoint holds.** Consensus sits near $7.89, slightly above the midpoint. A reaffirm at the midpoint or below is a risk.
4. **Organic growth vs. "low-to-mid single digits."** Watch aerospace and LatAm architecture as the drivers. Automotive refinish is the drag.
5. **Price vs. volume split and cost-inflation coverage.** Management said full coverage by Q4. Any slip there is a negative.

**What would need to happen:**
- **Bull:** Price-cost recovery ahead of schedule; aerospace strength continues; packaging holds up.
- **Base:** Guide reaffirmed; price covers inflation on schedule; automotive refinish stabilizes.
- **Bear:** Volume softens in architecture or industrial; inflation coverage lags; the FY range is narrowed to the low end.

**Management commentary to listen for:**
- Bull: "ahead of plan" on cost coverage, raised FY range, aerospace backlog growth.
- Base: "reaffirm," "on track," "in line with expectations."
- Bear: "lower end of range," "softer demand in automotive refinish or architecture," "timing of pricing."
