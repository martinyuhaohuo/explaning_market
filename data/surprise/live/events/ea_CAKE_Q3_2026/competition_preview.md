## 3. Key Metrics to Watch — Ranked

**1. Cheesecake Factory-brand comps, and the traffic/price split (highest importance).**
Q2 was +5.8% on **+2.7% traffic / +3.0% price** — a 350 bps beat vs. the Black Box casual dining index. Against a +0.3% prior-year compare (the easiest of the year), the whole bull case is that this is a durable brand upcycle, not a one-quarter menu-innovation pop ("bites" and "bowls," rewards launch, higher ad awareness). **Price is ~3% in Q3, so anything under ~+3% comps means traffic went negative.** Industry context is brutal: full-service restaurant sales ran **-2.23% YoY in September 2026** and traffic has been negative in 18 of 19 months. A +5%-plus traffic-led print is a genuine share-gain story; a price-led +3% is not.

**2. Q4 guide and the 4% pricing step-up.**
Management flagged Q4 menu pricing of ~4% vs. just under 3% in Q3. That's the single clearest elasticity test in the model. The buy-side wants to hear Q4 comps guided to +4% or better *with traffic still positive* and FY26 reaffirmed at ~$4.0B / ~5.4% net income margin. Any hedge on 4Q traffic at 4% pricing will be read as peak-comp.

**3. North Italia — fifth-quarter-plus problem child.**
-3% comps in Q2, flat-or-negative for five straight quarters, with 6–7 openings planned in FY26 (the second-largest unit bucket). Management is pushing value price points, targeted/in-mall digital advertising, and has pre-committed that "improvement will take time." **An inflection to flat-or-better is an underappreciated upside catalyst; another -3% raises the question of whether you should be opening 6–7 units a year into negative comps.**

**4. Flower Child — the multiple driver.**
+13% comps in Q2 (on +10% in Q1), $5.3M AUV, 20.1% restaurant-level margin, 44 units against a stated 700-unit TAM, 7 openings in FY26. This is what justifies CAKE trading at ~24x FY26E instead of a 12x casual-dining multiple. Watch comps (does double-digit hold?), AUV on the new-class openings, and any sharpening of the unit-growth algorithm.

**5. Margin durability — specifically labor.**
Labor was **34.1% of revenue in Q2 vs. 34.9%** (80 bps of leverage from retention/productivity) and drove op margin from 6.8% to 7.6%; CAKE-brand restaurant-level margin hit 20%, best in a decade. Offsetting: F&B costs crept to 21.8% from 21.6%. Q3 is a seasonally lower-volume quarter (hence the 4.3% guided net margin vs. ~5.4% FY), so expect sequential deleverage — the question is whether the *YoY* labor delta is still favorable.

**Secondary:** unit count (374 company-owned + 36 international licensed at Q2-end; "as many as 26" FY26 openings); capital return pace (only $25M returned in Q2 — $9.3M buyback, $15.7M dividend — light for a stock that has doubled); FY26 capex ~$210M; any first FY27 framing.

| **Bear** | 25% | $975–990M (in/below guide, +7.5–9%) | $0.80–0.87 | ~4.1% | +3% or less; traffic flat/negative | Q2 pop proves promo-driven and laps; full-service industry traffic drag bites; commodity creep + seasonal deleverage compress margin; cautious Q4 commentary on 4% pricing | **-12% to -20%** |

**What tips base → bear:** the phrase to listen for is any version of *"we saw some moderation in trends through the quarter"* or a Q4 comp guide with a 3-handle. With price at 3% in Q3 and 4% in Q4, decelerating comps mathematically mean negative traffic — and this stock is no longer priced for negative traffic.

**What tips base → bull:** traffic growth *sustained above 2.5%* plus an explicit North Italia inflection. That combination converts the story from "cyclical casual-dining recovery" to "multi-brand compounder," which is the only way the $120–122 targets get defended.

**Historical calibration:** Q2 FY2026 (reported 7/28/26) — +22% EPS beat, comps +5.8%, stock **+12%** the next session against a 9.78% implied move (14.38% max move, an "outside" move). Prior-year Q3 (10/28/25) was the opposite shape: EPS beat, revenue light, comps +0.3%. The market has been paying for *comps and traffic*, not EPS.

## 5. Catalyst Checklist

1. **CAKE-brand comps vs. ~+5.5% whisper (not the ~+3% price floor)** — determines whether the brand upcycle narrative survives. Traffic sign is more important than the headline.
2. **Adj. EPS vs. ~$1.00 whisper (vs. $0.92 published, ~$0.87 guided)** — a $0.93 "beat" is a miss against real expectations.
3. **Q4 guide / FY26 reaffirm-or-raise at ~$4.0B and ~5.4% net income margin** — a reaffirm-only is a negative given the stock's move since July.
4. **North Italia comp inflection** — the one line item where expectations are genuinely low and upside is unpriced.
5. **Flower Child comps + any sharpened unit-growth or AUV framing** — the valuation input; a deceleration from +13% hits the multiple, not the EPS.
6. **Labor % of revenue YoY** — the proof that Q2's 80 bps was structural retention/productivity rather than a favorable comparison.
