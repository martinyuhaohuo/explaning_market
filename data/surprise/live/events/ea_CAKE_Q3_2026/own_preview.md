## Recent Performance (Q2 FY2026, reported 7/28/2026)

- Revenue of $1,029.6M, up 7.7% YoY, versus about $994.5M consensus.
- Comparable restaurant sales up 5.8% YoY (prior-year Q2: +1.2%).
- Adjusted EPS of $1.44. The Zacks consensus was $1.17, but the Benzinga-based figures differ, so treat the beat size as uncertain.
- Restaurant-level margin of 20%, a decade high.
- Flower Child comps +13%. North Italia comps −3%.
- Up to 26 new restaurants planned for FY26; capex about $210M.

## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Comparable restaurant sales (CheesecakeFactory brand).** Q2 was +5.8%. The question is whether momentum held into Q3 or faded. A sharp drop from Q2 would be the clearest negative.
2. **Restaurant-level margin.** Q2 at 20% was a decade high. Holding or exceeding that level supports the margin story. Any slip back toward the high teens would be read as a warning on food, labor, or occupancy costs.
3. **Revenue versus the $980M–$990M guide.** A result at or above the top of the range is the baseline bullish case. A miss of the low end would be a problem.
4. **Flower Child vs. North Italia divergence.** Whether North Italia's decline is widening and whether Flower Child keeps growing double digits.
5. **Q4 and FY26 guidance.** FY26 revenue of about $4.0B implies a specific Q4 run-rate. Watch whether management raises, holds, or narrows, and what it says about the 26 openings.
