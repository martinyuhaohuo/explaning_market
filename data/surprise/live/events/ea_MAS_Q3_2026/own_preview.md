## Prior Quarter Context (Q2 2026, reported Jul 29)

- Net sales fell 3% to $1,992M. That missed the ~$2.08–2.09B consensus.
- Adjusted EPS rose 26% to $1.64, beating the ~$1.30–1.32 consensus. Reported EPS was $1.60.
- Adjusted gross margin was 43.8%, up 610 bps from 37.7%. The margin lift was helped by tariff refunds.
- Plumbing sales fell 3%. Decorative Architectural sales fell 4%.
- North America fell 5% in local currency. International rose 4%.
- Management guided to mid-single-digit commodity inflation in both segments in 2H, and the guide includes Section 232 and Section 301 tariffs.

## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Organic sales by segment and North America volume.** Q2 revenue missed, and North America fell 5%. The question is whether repair and remodel demand is stabilizing or still declining.
2. **Adjusted EPS quality.** How much of the beat came from tariff refunds, cost savings, and gross margin versus the underlying business. Watch for any one-time items.
3. **Gross margin durability in 2H.** Mid-single-digit commodity inflation and tariff costs could compress the 43.8% Q2 level.
4. **FY2026 guidance.** A raise on top of the $4.40–4.60 range would be read as confidence. A reaffirmation with weaker 2H commentary would be read as a negative.
5. **Strategic investments.** Management cited "targeted strategic investments" as a drag on Q2 sales. Ask whether spending continues into 2027 and what returns they are expected to produce.
6. **Free cash flow and capital return.** Confirm buyback and dividend commentary.

| **Bull** | Sales decline narrows, with North America flat to down low-single digits | Beat, with margins holding above prior-year levels | Repair/remodel stabilizes; FY guide raised again or the 2H tariff and inflation headwind is absorbed | Positive gap-up; likely a move above the typical muted reaction |

**What would need to happen:**
- **Bull:** Management cites order trends or channel inventory improving, and says the tariff refunds are not the main driver of the margin beat.
- **Base:** Management reaffirms the FY range and gives a balanced read on demand.
- **Bear:** Management talks about "elevated uncertainty," softer 4Q volume, or incremental tariff costs.

**Historical reaction context (from search, approximate):**
- Q1 2026: EPS beat by about 17.6%. The stock fell about 0.6% that session.
- Q4 2025: EPS beat by a small margin. The stock fell about 2.7%.

- Q2 2025: large beat (~20%). The price reaction was not confirmed.
- Pattern: beats have often produced flat-to-negative reactions. Revenue and guidance have driven the stock more than the EPS headline.

## Catalyst Checklist

1. **Revenue vs. consensus, especially North America.** Q2 missed on sales. A second miss would likely dominate the reaction despite EPS strength.
2. **FY2026 adjusted EPS guide ($4.40–4.60).** The buy side will want to see the 2H implied EPS and whether tariff refunds are included.
3. **Gross margin trajectory.** The 43.8% Q2 level is the anchor. Any guide-down on 2H margins would be a key negative.
4. **Tariff and commodity commentary.** Confirm the mid-single-digit 2H commodity inflation view and any Section 301 exposure.
5. **Strategic investments and cost savings.** Whether the investments are one-time or ongoing, and how much of the savings reach the bottom line.
