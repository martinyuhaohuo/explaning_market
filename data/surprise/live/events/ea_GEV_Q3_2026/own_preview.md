## Prior Quarter Context (Q2 2026, reported Jul 22)

- **Orders:** $24.2B, up 88% organically. Backlog reached $176B, up $13.0B sequentially.
- **Gas power:** Gas backlog plus slot reservations rose from 100 GW to 116 GW. Management expects at least 125 GW by year-end 2026.
- **Revenue:** $11.1B, up 22% (12% organic). Beat the ~$10.75B estimate.
- **Adjusted EBITDA margin:** 11.3%. The 12–14% target was kept.
- **FCF:** $5.1B. Cash balance $13.1B.
- **Guidance:** Revenue and FCF guidance were raised. Benzinga puts FY26 revenue at $45.5B–$46.5B.
- **EPS:** Sources conflict. Benzinga shows $2.47 vs. a $3.22 estimate; Teleborsa cites a $3.04 consensus. One aggregator says the stock fell about 8.6% on the print. I could not verify that move.

## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Orders and book-to-bill:** Whether the Q2 surge (+88%) holds or was lumpy. Gas and electrification orders are the swing factor.
2. **Gas slot reservations and GW backlog:** Progress toward the 125 GW year-end target. This is the main long-cycle signal.
3. **Adjusted EBITDA margin vs. the 12–14% target:** Q2 was 11.3%. Watch for expansion in Power and Wind.
4. **Revenue vs. ~$12.0B consensus:** Check segment mix, especially Power services versus equipment.
5. **FCF and guidance raise or hold:** Whether FY26 revenue and FCF guidance move again.
6. **EPS vs. consensus:** Given the conflicting estimates, focus on the quality of the beat or miss (margin, tax, and below-the-line items) rather than the headline number.

## Catalyst Checklist

1. **Orders vs. the Q2 surge:** Whether $24.2B proves repeatable. Buy-side will want sequential durability, not just a year-over-year rebound.
2. **Gas GW trajectory:** Confirmation of the path to 125 GW by year-end. This is what the stock is priced on.
3. **Margin path to 12–14%:** Q2 was 11.3%. A credible quarterly step-up is needed.
4. **FY26 guidance:** Raise, hold, or cut on revenue and FCF. The buy-side expects at least a hold.
5. **Narrative and capital allocation:** Any changes to buybacks, M&A, or Wind strategy.
