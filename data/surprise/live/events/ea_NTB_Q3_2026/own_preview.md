- **Core EPS:** $1.58 vs. $1.51 estimate (beat). Reported EPS was $1.16, with core net income of $63.9M.
- **Revenue:** $158.3M vs. $156.9M estimate (beat).
- **Net interest income:** $95.6M, up $2.3M QoQ and $6.2M YoY.
- **NIM:** 2.74%, down 1 bp QoQ and up from 2.64% a year earlier.
- **Deposit cost:** 1.25%, down 1 bp QoQ.
- **Non-interest income:** $63.4M.
- **Efficiency ratio:** 67.6% reported; 57.0% core (up from 56.4% in Q1).
- **Corporate:** R&H Guernsey acquisition closed. The CIBC Caribbean acquisition is pending, with close expected in 1H 2027.
- **Dividend:** $0.50 per share quarterly.
- **Guidance:** No forward NII or NIM guidance was found in the excerpts I saw. The Q2 call transcript is the place to check.

## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Net interest income and NIM.** This drives the quarter. Watch whether NIM holds near 2.74% and whether NII grows again with the Q2 trajectory (+$2.3M QoQ).
2. **Deposit costs and deposit mix.** Deposit cost is 1.25%. Competitive pressure is noted in management commentary, so any rise would hurt NII.
3. **Core efficiency ratio.** It was 57.0% and rising. Expense growth from the acquisitions (R&H Guernsey integration) is the risk.
4. **Non-interest income.** It was $63.4M in Q2. Check fee trends and whether the R&H Guernsey contribution is visible.
5. **Credit quality and provisions.** Not researched. Check the allowance, nonaccruals, and provision expense in the release.
6. **Capital, buybacks, and CIBC Caribbean deal updates.** Check any change to timing, financing, or capital ratios.
