## Context from Q2 FY2026 (reported July 29, 2026)

- Revenue was $261.2M, up 9% year over year. Adjusted EBITDA was $34.4M, up 13%. Net income was $23.2M. This was the 21st consecutive quarter of record revenue and adjusted EBITDA.
- FY2026 guidance was raised to revenue of $1.018–1.038B and adjusted EBITDA of $130–140M, roughly 10% growth at the midpoint.
- Pre-booked revenue rose 14% and forward bookings rose 20%. Medi-Spa revenue grew 17%. Wellness centers are on 208 ships.
- Capital returns: a $0.05 quarterly dividend, and $37.1M left under the $75M buyback. Cash was $41.6M and total liquidity $91.6M.
