## Company Context

- **Q2 2026 (reported Jul 28):** Revenue $2.13B, up 4% QoQ and down 2% YoY. Net income $112M, or $0.31 per diluted share. Adjusted EBITDA $283M (13.3% margin), including about $40M from IEEPA tariff refunds. Excluding the refunds, adjusted EBITDA was about $243M. **The Q2 beat was flattered by one-time refunds.**
- **Q3 outlook:** Assumes Middle East conditions stay as they were in Q2. Regional activity is expected to rise 10–15% sequentially, with guidance set toward the low end. If that uplift fails to materialize, EBITDA impact is about $20–25M. Guidance excludes any further IEEPA refunds.
- **Q2 drag:** Lower capital equipment sales. Management said first-half orders should lift second-half shipments.
- **Q3 guidance gap:** No explicit Q3 revenue or EBITDA dollar ranges were found in the summaries. Verify against the full release at investors.nov.com.

## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Adjusted EBITDA and margin, ex-tariff refunds.** Q2 underlying was about $243M. The key question is whether core margin holds or improves without one-time help.
2. **Q3 revenue vs. ~$2.19B.** Check whether the Middle East regional uplift is showing up in the numbers.
3. **Capital equipment orders and backlog.** Management's second-half shipment thesis depends on them.
4. **Q4 / full-year commentary.** The buy-side will be looking for confirmation of the second-half ramp and any update to the Middle East assumption.
5. **Drill bits and artificial lift share gains, and digital services growth.** These offset equipment weakness.
6. **Tariff refund accounting.** Any additional refunds are excluded from guidance, so any upside here would be non-operational.
