## Recent Context (Q2 2026, reported Jul 30)

- Organic net new assets were about $23B, or about 4% annualized. Management said the second half should improve on pipeline and more normal advisor movement.
- Recruited assets were $25B for the quarter, up 35% year over year. Trailing-twelve-month recruiting was $89B.
- Asset retention was 97%.
- **Core G&A guidance was lowered** to $2,140–2,165M for 2026, including Commonwealth-related costs.
- The Commonwealth conversion is on schedule for Q4 2026, with expected asset retention of about 90%.
- **Data conflict on EPS:** one source gives adjusted EPS of $5.84 and GAAP diluted EPS of $4.74. Benzinga gives an actual of $5.60. Verify the reported figure in the 8-K.

Sources: [LPL Q2 2026 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001397911/000162828026051046/a2026q2earningsrelease.htm), [Pulse 2.0 recruiting release](https://pulse2.com/lpl-announces-recruited-assets-of-25-billion-for-q2-2026-up-35-year-over-year/), [Nasdaq Q2 call highlights](https://www.nasdaq.com/articles/lpl-financial-q2-earnings-call-highlights).

## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Organic net new assets vs. the second-half ramp.** Management guided to better organic growth in H2. Q3 NNA of roughly $20B+ would support that. Watch the cadence and any Commonwealth-related noise.
2. **Recruiting and advisor movement.** Recruited assets of $25B in Q2 were strong. Pipeline commentary matters for Q4 and into 2027.
3. **Asset retention.** Q2 was 97%. Any drop would be a warning, especially ahead of the Commonwealth conversion.
4. **Core G&A and margins.** The lowered expense outlook of $2.14–2.165B is a key check. Any further raise would hurt the margin story even with revenue beats.
5. **Commonwealth integration.** Update on the Q4 conversion timing, retention expectation (about 90%), and integration costs.
6. **Full-year guidance.** The buy-side will focus on whether the H2 recovery is reflected in the outlook.
