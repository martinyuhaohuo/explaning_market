## Context from Q2 FY26 (reported July 29)

- **Revenue:** $22.8M, down 5% YoY (down 6% in constant currency). U.S. was $14.2M. International fell 6%, but grew 12% excluding China.
- **China:** Sales were held up while the company awaited renewal of its registration certificate. This is the main swing factor for international.
- **EPS:** -$0.24 vs. about -$0.28 expected. Adjusted EBITDA loss was $5.1M, down 39% YoY.
- **Gross margin:** A record 78%.
- **FY26 guidance:** Revenue $90–92M, reaffirmed. Gross margin now about 76%. Opex $109–111M, including about $15M of stock-based comp. Cash decline of about $23M, assuming no credit facility draws.
- **Management:** Confident in the revenue guide and expects a return to global sales growth later in the year.

**Implied H2 math:** H1 revenue is about $43.4M (Q1 $20.6M + Q2 $22.8M). Hitting the $90M low end requires about $46.6M in H2. Q3 at $22.0M plus Q4 at $25.3M gets to roughly $90.7M. The Street is therefore modeling the low half of the range, so a raise is unlikely. A cut is the main risk.

## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Revenue vs. $22.0M, and the YoY trend.** A return to growth would be a major positive. Management has said growth returns "later in the year."
2. **China registration renewal status.** Any update on timing or whether revenue resumes in Q4 matters more than the quarter's result.
3. **FY26 revenue guidance ($90–92M).** Reaffirm, narrow to the low end, or cut. The consensus of about $90.9M sits in the lower half.
4. **Gross margin.** Q2 hit a record 78%, and the full-year guide is about 76%. Holding 76%+ supports the margin story.
5. **Opex and cash burn.** Watch whether the $109–111M opex range and roughly $23M cash decline hold. Watch any credit facility draw.
6. **U.S. growth.** U.S. is the bulk of revenue, so its trajectory is the core of the growth narrative.
