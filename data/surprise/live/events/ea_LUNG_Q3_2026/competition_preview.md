## 1. The Setup in One Paragraph

Pulmonx is a sub-$100M market-cap single-product medtech (Zephyr Endobronchial Valve for severe emphysema) emerging from a brutal transition year: sales force attrition, a management change in Q3 2025, a DOJ False Claims Act settlement in December 2025, and three straight quarters of year-over-year revenue declines. Q2 2026 broke the downtrend in sentiment — a modest revenue beat, a record 78% gross margin, and a 39% reduction in adjusted EBITDA loss drove a +14.4% one-day move and the stock is now up ~40% since June 30 and ~79% off the 52-week low. **Q3 is the proof-of-concept quarter: consensus $21.93M implies the first year-over-year revenue growth (+2.0%) in four quarters, and management must defend a Q4 ramp to ~$25.3M (+12% YoY) to hold the $90–92M full-year guide.** The stock has already paid for the "turnaround is working" story; the burden of proof has shifted.

## 3. The Arithmetic That Matters

This is the single most important slide in the preview. The guide is heavily back-end loaded:

| | Revenue |
|---|---|
| 1H26 actual | $43.35M |
| Q3'26 consensus | $21.93M |
| **Implied Q4'26 to hit guide midpoint ($91M)** | **$25.7M** |
| Q4'25 actual | $22.60M |
| **Implied Q4 YoY growth** | **+13.7%** |
| **Implied Q4 sequential growth** | **+17.2%** |

At the FY consensus of $90.55M, implied Q4 is ~$25.3M (+12% YoY, +15% q/q). Pulmonx has never posted a $25M+ quarter. Q4 does carry favorable seasonality (deductibles met, elective procedure pull-forward) and Q4'25 was itself a −5% YoY comp, so the bar is beatable — but **an in-line Q3 leaves zero slack.** Every dollar Q3 misses by must be added to an already heroic Q4.

**Second-order consequence — the covenant.** The March 2026 Perceptive facility ($40M drawn, $20M more available on revenue milestones, 2031 maturity, ~7% + max(1M SOFR, 3.75%)) carries a minimum trailing-12-month revenue covenant **starting at $85M**. Post-Q3 TTM at consensus = $87.9M, a cushion of only ~$2.9M (3.3%). A Q3 print below ~$19.0M would put TTM revenue essentially at the covenant line. For an $87M-cap company with ~$16M of net cash, that converts a revenue miss into a financing question — a non-linear risk the equity is not obviously pricing. *(Verify the exact step-up schedule in the credit agreement; the 10-Q discloses the opening threshold but not the full ladder.)*

## 4. Key Metrics to Watch — Ranked

**1. Total revenue vs. $21.93M, and specifically the YoY sign.** Positive YoY growth is the narrative hinge. Pulmonx has beaten revenue consensus in **8 of the last 8 quarters**, but the beats are small ($0.1–0.9M) and consensus already embeds the inflection. A $21.5M print — flat YoY — technically "misses by 2%" but breaks the recovery story.

**2. U.S. revenue growth and treating center adds.** U.S. ($14.2M in Q2, +7% sequential) is where the sales force rebuild shows up. Rep average tenure has fallen to ~1 year from a historical 2.5, with a 6–9 month productivity ramp — meaning reps hired in Q1–Q2 should begin contributing *now*. Watch: (a) U.S. YoY turning positive, (b) treating center adds ≥12 (Q2 pace) against the ~40/year target, (c) any commentary on rep turnover re-accelerating.

**3. FY guidance treatment.** Three outcomes, materially different: reaffirm $90–92M (base), narrow to $90–91M (soft cut — read as a walk-back of the double-digit exit), or cut below $90M (thesis break). A *raise* is unlikely and would be a strong bull signal.

**4. Gross margin durability.** 78% in Q2 was a record, well above the ~76% FY guide. The guide implies ~74% in 2H. If Q3 holds 77–78%, the FY GM guide was simply conservative and the EBITDA trajectory improves — a quiet beat. Note China shipments resume at structurally lower gross margin, but not until early 2027, so that is a 2027 headwind, not a Q3 one.

**5. Cash burn and the $23M FY decrease.** Cash was $55.8M at June 30. The FY plan implies roughly $7M of combined 2H burn against a ~$5.1M quarterly adjusted EBITDA loss — tight, and dependent on working capital. A Q3 cash balance above ~$52M is on-plan; below ~$50M raises the odds of a capital raise into a $2 stock (highly dilutive; note the 1M Perceptive warrants struck at $1.92 are now in the money).

**6. Pipeline / optionality (lower near-term stock impact, high terminal-value impact).** CONVERT II enrollment on track for 2027 completion; AeriSeal projected to expand the Zephyr addressable market ~20% and already holds CE Mark plus FDA Breakthrough designation. Watch for European AeriSeal commercialization timing (gated on CONVERT I publication) and China distributor shipment confirmation for early 2027.

## 7. Catalyst Checklist (in order of likely stock impact)

1. **Q4 implied guide / "double-digit exit" language.** The only way to hold $90–92M is a ~$25M+ Q4. If management reaffirms with conviction and the Q3 base supports it, the stock re-rates. Any hedging here outweighs a Q3 revenue beat.
2. **U.S. revenue YoY sign.** Positive = the sales force rebuild is real and the ~40 centers/year engine is turning. Negative for a fourth consecutive quarter = the $2 price is the story, not the turnaround.
3. **Revenue vs. $21.93M, with the covenant as a tail risk.** Beat is the base case; a sub-$20M print introduces a TTM-revenue covenant and financing overhang that would be punished far beyond the revenue delta.
4. **Gross margin and cash.** 77%+ GM plus cash ≥$52M validates the operating leverage narrative (adj. EBITDA loss cut ~39% YoY in Q2) and pushes the dilution question out. Deterioration on either revives it.
5. **Pipeline / strategic updates.** CONVERT II enrollment pace, AeriSeal Europe commercialization timing, China distributor shipment confirmation for early 2027. Lower immediate impact, but these are the terminal-value drivers for a company with $545M of accumulated deficit and ~0.8x EV/sales.

## 8. Trading Setup

**Recent performance:** ~$2.02, up ~40% since June 30 and ~79% off the $1.13 52-week low, but still ~30% below the $2.89 52-week high. The entire move post-dates the July 29 Q2 print — the market has already priced a meaningful probability of the inflection.

**Implied move:** I could not source live option-chain or straddle pricing for LUNG — options on a $2 / $87M-cap name are thin and screens did not return usable implied-vol data, so **treat the figure below as a realized-move estimate, not a market-implied one.** Based on the sourced reactions (+14.4% and −1.7%) and the stock's realized volatility, a reasonable expected absolute move is **~12–15%**, with a fat left tail: the bear case is a −25%+ round trip of the post-Q2 rally, whereas the bull case runs into resistance at the $2.89 high. My scenario distribution is roughly symmetric in probability but **negatively skewed in magnitude** — the asymmetry is unfavorable at $2.02 after a 40% run.

**Positioning view:** this is a setup where the fundamental trajectory is improving but the risk/reward into the print is unattractive. Low-delta long exposure (if executable at reasonable spreads) or waiting for the post-print guidance language is preferable to carrying full equity risk through the event. The highest-expected-value trade is likely *after* the call: if management reaffirms the $90–92M guide with a credible Q4 bridge and U.S. revenue is growing, the stock is a 0.8x EV/sales asset with a validated inflection — buy the confirmation rather than the hope.
