## Recent Results and Context

- **Q2 2026 (reported 2026-07-28):** Distributable earnings of $0.23/share against a $0.28 quarterly dividend. Consensus was about $0.25 per one source. Management said it was at the low end of guidance because loan closings were delayed.
- **Dividend coverage:** The gap is about $0.05/quarter. CEO said the company remains on plan to cover the dividend by year-end.
- **Deployment:** About $75M originated in Q2, plus a $24M loan closed in July. More than $85M of repayments came in, including a full $26.5M office loan payoff.
- **Yield:** Weighted average all-in yield of 7.7%, down from 7.84% in Q1 (decelerating).
- **Credit:** Management cites a risk rating of 2.9 and no realized losses. One third-party summary reports a $4.9M provision and a GAAP net loss of $0.9M in Q2. That is not confirmed.
- **Liquidity:** About $70M cash and $393M of undrawn secured facility capacity.
- **Dividend history:** Cut from $0.35 to $0.28 in 2025. $0.28 has held through 2026. Payout ratio is high (one source cites ~99%).

## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Distributable EPS vs. $0.28 dividend.** Is the coverage gap closing? A print at or above $0.28 is the key positive signal. Anything near $0.23 again keeps the dividend-cut question alive.
2. **Dividend commentary.** Any reaffirmation of the $0.28 rate, or hedging on "coverage by year-end," moves the stock more than the quarter itself.
3. **Originations vs. repayments and pipeline.** Net portfolio growth and July–October closings are the main earnings drivers. Watch for any slip in closing timing.
4. **Net interest margin / all-in yield.** Continued compression from 7.84% would signal pricing pressure.
5. **Credit quality.** Non-accrual balances, risk ratings (2.9 currently), and any provision. Any realized loss would be a negative surprise.
6. **Leverage and liquidity.** Deployment of the December rights-offering capital and remaining facility capacity.

## Catalyst Checklist

1. **Distributable EPS vs. $0.28 dividend:** Coverage is the main question for investors. Consensus EPS is unknown, so judge it against the dividend.
2. **Dividend reaffirmation and the "coverage by year-end" timeline:** Buy-side expects a clear reaffirmation. Any change is a narrative shift.
3. **Closing pipeline and capital deployment:** Whether Q2's delayed closings have converted, and what the Q4 outlook is.
4. **Yield trajectory (7.7% in Q2, decelerating):** Continued decline reduces the earnings path.
5. **Credit and any repayments or payoffs:** Non-accruals, provisions, and whether repayment proceeds get redeployed quickly.
