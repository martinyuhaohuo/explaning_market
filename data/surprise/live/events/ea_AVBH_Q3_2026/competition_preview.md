# Earnings Preview — Avidbank Holdings (NASDAQ: AVBH)

**Q3 FY2026 (quarter ended 9/30/26) · Report: Monday, Oct 26, 2026 after close (4:00 PM ET / 20:00 UTC) · Call: Tuesday, Oct 27, 11:00 AM ET**
*Prepared 10/23/26. Price reference $31.70. No position recommendation implied.*

---

## 1. Setup in One Paragraph

Avidbank is a ~$2.6B-asset Bay Area commercial bank (IPO'd August 2025, $61.3M net proceeds) running a high-margin, C&I-heavy book — 50% C&I including $323M of venture lending — at a 4.26% NIM. The growth story is intact (loans +16% y/y, deposits +16% y/y), but the last three prints have traded on two things only: **margin direction and provision**. Management guided NIM *down* toward 4.20% on the July call; the Fed then **hiked 25bp on Sept 16** to 3.75–4.00%, the first hike since July 2023. That hike post-dates the guide, and for a bank with a majority-floating C&I book it is a modest Q3 tailwind (~2 weeks of benefit) and a full-quarter Q4 tailwind. **The central question is whether the 4.20% guide is now stale to the upside — and whether credit lets the P&L show it.**

*Source: MarketBeat consensus, retrieved 10/23/26. Thin coverage — ~5 analysts. No published whisper number; treat the $0.868 as a loose anchor, not a hard bar.*

**Read-through on the consensus revenue line:** $29.26M is *below* Q2's $29.73M, because Q2 carried ~$1.3M of non-recurring BOLI death-benefit income. Backing that out, consensus implies **core revenue up ~3% sequentially**, i.e. NII of roughly $27.3–27.8M on ~$1.6–1.9M of normalized fee income. That math only works if NIM holds near 4.26%, not at the guided 4.20%. Consensus is quietly assuming the guide is conservative — the same conclusion the September hike supports, but the two are not independent cushions.

**My base case: revenue ~$28.9M, EPS ~$0.83** — a modest miss on the headline EPS, a near-in-line revenue print. The gap to consensus is almost entirely provision.

**Provision is the swing variable.** On ~10.8M diluted shares and a mid-27% tax rate, **every $1.0M of provision is ~$0.067 of EPS.** Consensus EPS of $0.868 embeds a benign ~$1.3–1.5M provision. Q2 was $2.8M; Q1 was $1.4M. A repeat of Q2's provision alone takes EPS to ~$0.78 with everything else in line.

**What management commentary would signal each:**
- **Bull:** CFO Patrick Oakes revising the NIM floor *up* from 4.20%, or language like "deposit costs have peaked." Any buyback authorization. SBA gain-on-sale income arriving earlier than modeled.
- **Base:** "In line with the guidance we gave last quarter" on NIM; reaffirmed low-double-digit growth; expense step-up framed again as "an investment in the future."
- **Bear:** Criticized-loan totals rising a second consecutive quarter; any widening of the SaaS discussion beyond the two known ~$4M credits; the phrase "cash balances crossing over loan balances" applied to more than isolated names — CEO Mark Mordell named that as the early-warning trigger for venture losses.

**Historical calibration — reactions have been remarkably muted:**

|---|---|---|

Three straight prints inside ±1.5%, including two clear EPS misses. AVBH has beaten consensus EPS in only 4 of 8 quarters, and the market has stopped paying much attention to the headline. **The bear case is the only scenario with real tail risk**, and it is a credit event, not an EPS event — the asymmetry sits in the provision line, not the margin line.

## 5. Catalyst Checklist

1. **NIM vs. the 4.20% guide.** The guide was issued July 23, seven weeks before the Fed hiked. Consensus revenue implicitly assumes ~4.26%. If management simply hits its own guide, revenue misses — a case where meeting guidance reads as a miss.
2. **Provision vs. ~$1.5M.** This is where the $0.868 lives or dies. Q2's $2.8M (inflated by a $1.9M construction charge-off) repeating costs ~$0.09.
3. **Criticized-loan trajectory and the $29M CRE relationship.** Management called it well-collateralized and on a 24-month workdown. A second quarter of rising criticized balances reframes the whole credit narrative for a bank whose NPLs went from 0.07% to 0.65% in four quarters.
4. **Venture/SaaS disclosure.** $323M venture lending (29% of C&I) and $165M SaaS. The buy side wants the Q1-style deep-dive updated, not a one-liner. Vertical vs. horizontal SaaS split is the specific tell.
5. **Expense run rate and SBA ramp.** Personnel was guided from $9.5M toward $10M. Whether the SBA division shows any gain-on-sale revenue this quarter, or is pure cost for another two quarters, sets the 2027 efficiency path.

### Data Caveats
- Consensus is from a single retail aggregator (MarketBeat) with ~5 contributing analysts; there is no verifiable buy-side whisper for a name this size. Estimate dispersion is likely wide and the $0.868 point estimate carries less information than it would for a liquid name.
- My base-case EPS of ~$0.83 is built from Q2 actuals plus management's own guide (NIM 4.20–4.26%, NIE ~$14.5M, tax mid-27%, ~10.8M diluted shares). It is a reconciliation of disclosed guidance, not an independently modeled forecast.
- Q1'26 reported NIB deposits at 33.1% of total while a Q2 slide summary indicated ~24%; I could not reconcile these from the primary 8-K, so **no NIB figure above should be relied on** — it is listed as a metric to watch, not a baseline.
- Prior-year "revenue" and EPS comparisons use adjusted figures; Q3'25 GAAP was a **–$37.7M loss** on securities restructuring. Any y/y GAAP comparison this quarter is meaningless.
- Analyst price targets differ by source ($35.33 vs. $38.80 averages); both are reported rather than reconciled, as the contributing-analyst sets are not disclosed.
