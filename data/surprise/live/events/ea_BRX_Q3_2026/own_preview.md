## Company Guidance (raised at Q2, July 27, 2026)

Source: [Q2 2026 8-K exhibit](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001581068/000158106826000027/brx-20260727ex991.htm)

- **FY26 Nareit FFO/share:** $2.35–$2.37 (was $2.34–$2.37). Low end raised.
- **FY26 same-property NOI growth:** 5.00%–5.75% (was 4.75%–5.50%). Raised.
- **Uncollectible revenue:** 60–85 bps of expected revenue.
- **Expected gain on sale:** $62M ($0.20/share).
- **Q2 actuals:** Nareit FFO $0.58/share (vs. $0.56 a year earlier). Total leased occupancy 94.8%; anchor 95.9%; small shop 92.6% (a record).

## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Same-property NOI growth vs. the 5.00–5.75% range.** A raise would be the cleanest positive signal. Management already flagged strong NOI.
2. **FFO/share vs. the $2.35–$2.37 guide.** Watch whether the low end holds or moves up again. Q2 was $0.58.
3. **Total occupancy.** Q2 dipped sequentially on planned redevelopment move-outs. Management expects second-half growth, so a recovery to or above Q1 levels would support the story.
4. **Credit losses and straight-line rent reversals.** Q2 included about a $3M charge from tenant bankruptcies that held FFO growth back. A repeat would be a negative surprise.
5. **Redevelopment pipeline and acquisitions.** $347.8M across 44 projects at a ~10% expected yield. Four acquisitions totaling $164.3M in H1 (including Mayfair). Any change to the pipeline or yield targets matters.

## Catalyst Checklist

1. **SPNOI vs. the 5.00–5.75% range.** Whether it is raised again is the main upside lever.
2. **FFO guidance (FY26 $2.35–$2.37).** The buy side will want the low end to hold and early 2027 color.
3. **Occupancy recovery** from the Q2 redevelopment dip, especially in anchor and small-shop space.
4. **Credit losses and bankruptcy-related rent reversals.** Management has flagged these as a headwind.
5. **Capital allocation.** Acquisitions, dispositions ($123M in H1), and the $347.8M redevelopment pipeline.
