## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Revenue vs. guidance and consensus.** Q2 revenue grew 9% to $10.7M. The Q3 guide implies roughly 7% growth, which would be a further deceleration. A print at or below the low end of the range would likely be the main negative.
2. **ARR and ARR growth.** ARR was $42.3M, up 11% year over year. Whether ARR growth holds near 11% matters more to the bull case than the quarter's revenue.
3. **Adjusted EBITDA and margin trajectory.** Management raised the FY adjusted EBITDA target to at least $12.7M. Confirming the Q3 $3.4M–$3.6M range supports the margin story.
4. **EPS quality.** Q4 2025 reportedly missed on adjusted EPS. Watch whether the $0.26–$0.28 guide is reaffirmed or beaten.
5. **Capital allocation.** Management is reportedly weighing buybacks or a dividend with excess cash. Any concrete announcement would be a catalyst.

- *Base:* Management reaffirms the FY guide, gives no new color on growth drivers, and keeps capital-return plans vague.
- *Bear:* Management lowers the FY revenue or EBITDA range, cites churn or slower bookings, or says growth is a multi-quarter slowdown.

## Catalyst Checklist

1. **Q3 revenue vs. ~$11.15M consensus.** The guide already sits below consensus, so a print near the midpoint may be read as a miss. Pre-release expectations are probably set near the low end.
2. **FY26 guidance.** The buy-side will want the $43.5M–$44.0M revenue range and the ≥$12.7M EBITDA target reaffirmed or raised. Any cut is the biggest downside risk.
3. **ARR growth trajectory.** Growth at 11% in Q2 is the key bull metric. Watch for a deceleration in the ARR number.
4. **Capital deployment.** A buyback or dividend would be a clear narrative shift.
5. **Q3 EPS vs. $0.26–$0.28.** Consensus differences across sources mean the "beat" threshold is unclear. Confirm the number before the release.
