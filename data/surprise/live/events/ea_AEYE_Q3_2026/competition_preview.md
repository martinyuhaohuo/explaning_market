## 2. Key Metrics to Watch — Ranked

**1. ARR and the sequential dollar add (most important).** $42.3M at 6/30/26, +11% YoY, +$1.1M sequentially. With ~90% of revenue recurring, ARR is the only forward-looking line. **Bogey: ≥$43.3M, with a ≥$1.0M add.** An add under $0.8M breaks the trend and makes FY27 double-digit growth arithmetically hard.

**2. Enterprise channel inflection.** 41% of ARR; Q2 revenue was **flat YoY** even as Enterprise ARR grew at a 17% annualized sequential clip — management's explanation is a mix shift out of non-recurring work. Q3 is the quarter that claim gets tested. Enterprise revenue turning positive YoY = thesis validated; another flat quarter = the mix-shift story starts sounding like stagnation.

**3. Partner/Marketplace durability.** 59% of ARR, +16% YoY, driven by SMB marketplace and state/local government. Watch customer count (~129,000, +9,000 YoY) and any commentary on ASP per customer — management has flagged "supplemental AI products" as the ARPU lever.

**4. FY26 guide + first framing of 2027.** A third consecutive EBITDA raise (≥$13.0M) is the bull trigger. Explicit confirmation of the $15M exit run-rate is the base case. Watch for the first qualitative 2027 revenue growth framing — the buy-side needs to know whether 8% is the new normal or a trough.

**5. Capital allocation.** $15M authorization (Oct 20, 2026, expires 10/18/27) = ~16% of market cap, versus $8.7M cash and **$16.8M of term debt**. H1'26 repurchases were only $0.475M/82k shares — minimal. Credibility hinges on actual Q3/Q4 buyback dollars and whether debt paydown competes for the same cash.

**6. Margin quality + the GAAP crossover.** Adj. gross margin 84% (79% GAAP); R&D cut to 12% of revenue from 17% YoY via internal AI adoption. Management guided litigation expense *down* in H2'26. If EBITDA upside comes from opex cuts rather than revenue, the market should discount it.

**7. Housekeeping:** CFO search still open (Georgevich vacated the seat on May 4, 2026). A named CFO is a modest positive; continued vacancy is a governance overhang on a company now promising capital returns.

## 4. Catalyst Checklist

1. **ARR sequential add vs. the ~$1.1M run-rate** — the single cleanest leading indicator; revenue is backward-looking on a ~90% recurring base.
2. **Enterprise revenue YoY (41% of ARR)** — flat became the pattern in Q2; buy-side needs the mix-shift explanation to resolve into reported growth.
3. **FY26 guidance action + $15M exit run-rate confirmation** — a third EBITDA raise is the only demonstrated upside trigger; a revenue trim to the low end is the bear trigger.
4. **Buyback execution dollars (not authorization)** — $15M vs. $8.7M cash and $16.8M term debt; H1 pace was negligible.
5. **European Accessibility Act monetization** — pipeline/bookings evidence, not regulatory color. Plus any CFO appointment.
