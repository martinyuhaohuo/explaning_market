## Prior Quarter Context (Q2 2026, reported July 29)

- **EPS:** $1.67 (flat YoY). Versus consensus, sources conflict: $1.63, $1.70, or a small beat.
- **Revenue:** $922.4M, up ~7.8% from $855.5M.
- **Marine transportation:** Revenue up ~9%. Margins hit by fuel-cost headwinds in inland marine and elevated shipyard activity in coastal. Inland barge utilization in the low 90% range, with pricing improving.
- **Distribution & Services:** Revenue $385.4M (+6%). Power generation revenue +8%, operating income +27%.
- **Guidance:** Full-year EPS growth guidance of 5–15%. Management said results should trend toward the upper end. Call summaries cite inland revenue growth in the high-teens to ~20% and coastal revenue growth in the mid-single digits. These are secondary summaries, not verified against the release.

## Key Metrics to Watch (Ranked)

1. **EPS vs. $1.95–$2.00.** Whether the Q2 fuel headwind eased and shipyard activity normalized.
2. **Marine margins (inland and coastal).** Fuel recovery and pricing are the swing factors. Margin direction matters more than the revenue print.
3. **Inland utilization and pricing.** Low-90s utilization with improving rates supports the high-teens revenue growth outlook.
4. **Full-year guidance.** Confirmation of the upper end of the 5–15% range is the key test. A reaffirmation at the top end is roughly priced in; anything below the midpoint is a clear negative.
5. **Distribution & Services / power generation.** Continued strength in power generation supports the segment's growth.
6. **Free cash flow and leverage.** Not covered by my sources. Check the release.
