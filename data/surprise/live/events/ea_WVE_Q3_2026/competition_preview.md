## 4. Key Metrics to Watch — Ranked

**1. Cash balance and the runway sentence (highest weight).** Q2 ended at $490.6M with runway stated **"into Q3 2028."** Q2 burn was ~$54M. At a ~$190M EV, the runway clause is the valuation. Any softening of the language — "into 2028," or dropping the quarter entirely — is the worst single outcome tomorrow. Watch also for disclosed ATM usage: Wave had ~**$148.6M remaining** under the amended $250M Jefferies program as of Aug 7, 2026, having already sold $101.4M. Selling stock at a $3 handle is deeply dilutive and would confirm the market's financing fear.

**2. WVE-007 Phase 2a data-release commitment.** Monotherapy Phase 2a dosing is underway (BMI 35–50, ± T2D); the tirzepatide combination Phase 2a was initiated Sept 23, 2026. Management has explicitly **not committed to a data-release date** and may unblind at three or six months. A firm date — especially 1H 2027 — is the most powerful thing they can say. Equally, the combination arm is the rehabilitated thesis: preclinically, INHBE siRNA plus GLP-1 doubled weight loss versus GLP-1 alone. Enrollment color here matters more than any financial line.

**3. WVE-006 registrational trial start and partnering.** On Oct 1, 2026 Wave announced FDA alignment on a **single two-year registrational trial with a one-year interim analysis supporting potential accelerated approval on AATD biomarkers** — a genuinely good outcome, and the market gave it nothing. Watch for trial initiation timing and, critically, whether Wave will fund a two-year registrational program alone after GSK returned the asset in February 2026. A partner would be a step-change.

**4. Opex trajectory.** R&D stepped from $45.9M (Q3 FY25) to $51.3M (Q2 FY26); G&A from $18.1M to $24.8M. Two Phase 2a trials plus a registrational program against a $3 stock makes the Q2 ~$76M quarterly opex run-rate the key question. Evidence of prioritization (or a quiet deprioritization of WVE-003/WVE-N531) is bullish for runway even if it narrows the pipeline.

**5. WVE-008 (PNPLA3) CTA filing**, guided to H2 2026 — a cheap credibility check on execution.

**6. One-time items from redomiciliation.** Wave moved from Singapore to Delaware effective **Aug 7, 2026**. Expect possible transaction costs or tax true-ups distorting the Q3 P&L; strip these before judging burn.

**What would signal each:** The bull case is almost entirely a *language* case — a committed data date and an untouched ATM. The bear case is a *financing* case. Note the asymmetry in operational prerequisites: Wave does not need to beat anything financially tomorrow, it needs to show it can reach WVE-007 Phase 2a data without issuing equity at $3.

**Historical reaction calibration:** earnings-day moves have been modest relative to data days. The Q2 FY2026 miss (revenue $2.27M vs $7.45M est.) produced only about a **–5%** reaction. Q1 FY2026 beat hugely on the GSK deferred-revenue pull-forward (revenue $38.2M vs $8.45M; EPS –$0.13 vs –$0.34) and did not re-rate the stock. By contrast, clinical data moved it **–50%** in a session. **The print is not the catalyst; the milestone language is.**

## 6. Catalyst Checklist

1. **Cash + runway sentence** vs. the Q2 "into Q3 2028" baseline — at a ~$190M EV this is the valuation, and the clause is a binary credibility test.
2. **WVE-007 Phase 2a data timing** vs. the buy-side's hope for a committed 1H 2027 date — management has pointedly refused to commit; breaking that silence is the biggest upside lever.
3. **WVE-006 registrational trial start + partner language** vs. consensus that Wave cannot self-fund a two-year trial post-GSK handback.
4. **ATM usage disclosed in the Q3 10-Q** vs. the hope of zero — issuing into a 52-week low would validate the de-rating.
5. **November research day agenda** (bifunctional oligonucleotides, PNPLA3) — the near-dated catalyst the print will route expectations toward.

For context, WVE's IV ranged from a **40.1% low (Aug 26, 2026)** to a **229.2% high (Jun 16, 2026)**. Given a $3 handle and the modest ~5% reaction to the Q2 miss, I'd frame **~8–12%** as a reasonable earnings-day expectation — but verify the front-expiry straddle on your own terminal before putting on anything volatility-dependent, and note that any pipeline data released alongside the print breaks that range entirely.

**Positioning view.** Risk/reward tomorrow is skewed to the downside in the near term and the upside in the medium term, which argues against the print as an entry. The stock already discounts a financing and a Phase 2a failure; what it does not discount is a committed WVE-007 data date or a WVE-006 partner — neither of which Wave has shown urgency to deliver on an earnings call, and both of which are more likely to surface at the **November research day**. Base case is a quiet in-line print and a drift. For those long, the runway sentence is the stop-loss trigger; for those flat, the research day is the better-defined event.
