## Prior Quarter Context (Q2 2026, reported July 29)

- Revenue of $1.31B, up 32% YoY (record). Diluted EPS of $0.62, which beat consensus.
- Net income of $573M, but $129M of gains came from deconsolidating Robinhood Ventures Fund I. Strip these out when judging underlying quality.
- Net deposits of about $21.7B (headline $22B). Trailing-twelve-month net deposits were $75.7B.
- Funded customers of 28.4M (+7% YoY). Total Platform Assets of $369B (+32%). Gold subscribers of 4.8M. ARPU of $187 (+24%).
- 2026 Adjusted Operating Expense + SBC guidance tightened to $2.675–2.775B. Diluted share count expected roughly flat for 2026.
- I did not find explicit revenue or EPS guidance. Check the Q2 call transcript.

## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Revenue vs. consensus ($1.34B–$1.41B).** The spread between sources is wide, so the whisper number matters more than either published figure.
2. **Net deposits and Total Platform Assets.** Q2 was a record, so a sequential decline would likely read as a deceleration.
3. **Transaction-based revenue, especially crypto and options.** Sensitive to market volumes. I did not find Q3 volume data in my searches.
4. **Net interest revenue.** Driven by margin lending and cash balances. No Q3 figures found.
5. **Opex vs. the $2.675–2.775B guidance range.** Management just tightened it, so a change would be a signal.
6. **Gold subscribers and ARPU.** Gold at 4.8M and ARPU of $187 are the main recurring-revenue indicators.
7. **EPS quality.** Q3 comparisons will not include the Q2 Ventures deconsolidation gain, so watch how much of EPS is operating versus one-time.

## Scenario Analysis

Revenue and EPS thresholds are my own estimates, anchored to the consensus range above. Stock reaction is qualitative. Price targets are not included.

| Scenario | Revenue | EPS | Key Driver | Stock Reaction |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Bull | ≥ $1.45B | ≥ $0.58 | Record deposits, strong trading volumes, Gold growth, opex at or below the low end | Large upside, likely above the options-implied move |
| Base | $1.34B–$1.42B | $0.50–$0.57 | In line with consensus; deposits steady; guidance held | Move within the implied range, direction set by the guidance and commentary |
| Bear | < $1.34B | < $0.50 | Trading volume softens, deposits decelerate, opex rises or guidance is cut | Sharp decline, following the pattern of recent post-print selloffs |

**Commentary signals:**
- **Bull:** Management raises or reaffirms opex guidance and points to continued deposit momentum and new product growth.
- **Base:** Standard "record platform" messaging, no guidance change.
- **Bear:** Caution on near-term trading activity, or any mention of higher investment spending that pushes out margin expansion.

**Historical context:** Per the search results (dates and reference points vary by source), HOOD's average next-day move is roughly 8–10%, and it has moved sharply in both directions. Earnings-day closes were higher in 6 of its last 16 reports. The April 2026 report produced a 13% drop, and the January 2026 report about -10%.

## Catalyst Checklist

1. **Revenue vs. $1.34B–$1.41B consensus.** The buy side has been looking for growth to hold above 30%. A slowdown below 20% would likely be punished.
2. **Net deposits vs. Q2's record ~$22B.** A sequential decline would be read as weakening momentum.
3. **Opex and SBC vs. the tightened $2.675–2.775B range.** Confirms whether the cost discipline story is holding.
4. **EPS quality.** Confirm that the EPS beat, if any, is operating, not from investment gains.
5. **Narrative shift.** Any new product, international expansion, crypto/prediction-market commentary, or M&A would reset expectations. Nothing specific was found in my searches.

## Trading Setup

- **Options-implied move:** About 9.7% per Barchart's preview of the July 29 report. This is the most recent figure I found, not a current quote. Get the live straddle price before sizing a position.
- **Recent performance:** Barchart described a "five-quarter selloff streak." I did not find current price or YTD performance, so pull those from a live quote.
- **Setup:** The options market is pricing a large move, which is typical for HOOD. Compare the implied move to the scenario table above. If you expect the base case, the implied move is likely wider than the realized move, so selling premium would be the relevant trade. If you expect a bull or bear case, the premium may be cheap.
