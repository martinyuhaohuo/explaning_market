## 2. Key metrics to watch, ranked

**1. Event contracts (prediction markets) revenue — the single biggest swing factor.**
Q2 did $156M on a record 13.6B contracts, up >10x YoY, from near-nothing. The sell side has not converged on this line, so it is where surprise lives in both directions. Known negatives: prediction-market volumes fell ~23% m/m in August. Known positives: NFL season, and a visible mix shift into bitcoin/gold/oil volatility contracts — Robinhood said in late October those are its fastest-growing products, which matters because financial-market contracts are higher-frequency and less seasonal than sports. Watch **net revenue per 1,000 contracts**: Rothera (the Susquehanna JV DCM, top-3 in the US, ~2M users) lets Robinhood capture exchange and clearing economics it previously paid away to Kalshi/ForecastEx, so revenue can grow even on flat volume. Rough bands: >$150M is good, <$120M is a problem.

**2. Net deposits and the deposit-growth narrative.**
Q2 was a record $21.7–22B (28% annualized). Management then flagged July core brokerage inflows moderating to "around $4B" ex-Trump Accounts. Three months at $4B would be ~$12B and roughly 13% annualized — below the >20% long-term target and, in my view, the most likely source of a "good quarter, bad stock" outcome. The buy side will anchor on whether August/September reaccelerated.

**3. Crypto — where consensus looks too high.**
Disclosed notional: August $17.5B (+61% m/m, −38% YoY), September 1–24 ~$17B. That is not a volume base consistent with the $135.6M consensus, which implies +36% sequentially off Q2's $100M. Unless take rate moved up materially, crypto is the most probable miss line. Management had already characterized the start of Q3 as slower than Q2 on crypto.

**4. Operating expenses against the FY guide.**
FY2026 adj. OpEx + SBC guidance was tightened to **$2.675B–$2.775B**, implying a H2 envelope of ~$1,414M–$1,514M, or roughly **$707M–$757M** in Q3. A print at the low end with the full-year guide trimmed again is the cleanest bull signal available on this call; above $740M with compliance and credit-card provisioning cited is the bear signal.

**5. Net interest revenue and the margin book.**
The Fed *hiked* 25bp on Sept 16 to 3.75–4.00% — the first hike since 2023 — so the rate backdrop is a tailwind, not a headwind, and the December hike the street now expects extends it. Margin receivables were $21.6B exiting Q2; $23.6B–$24.1B is the expected range. Offsets: securities lending is collapsing (consensus −85% YoY) and segregated-cash interest is shrinking. Also watch the **credit-card allowance ratio** (4.99% in Q2) — a spike to >5.5% on fast receivables growth would read as credit deterioration at exactly the wrong moment in the tape.

Secondary: Gold subscribers and attach rate (4.84M / 17%), platform assets at quarter-end (Aug $384B; Q3-end likely ~$390B+, unaffected by the October selloff), ARPU, options contracts (Aug 292.5M, +50% YoY; Sept 1–24 ~234M), equity notional (Aug $335B, +68% YoY).

## 3. Scenario analysis

| Scenario | Revenue | EPS | Key driver | Stock reaction |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Bull** (~25%) | >$1.50B | >$0.65 | Event contracts >$150M with Rothera take-rate expansion; Q3 net deposits >$18B; OpEx <$685M and FY guide cut toward $2.675B; margin book >$24B | **+8% to +14%** → $100–$106, into 50-/200-day resistance at $103–$107 |
| **Base** (~45%) | $1.43–$1.47B | $0.55–$0.60 | Record revenue on options/equities strength; event contracts roughly flat q/q; crypto misses; deposits decelerate to mid-teens annualized; OpEx mid-envelope | **−3% to +4%** → $90–$97. | **Bear** (~30%) | <$1.38B | <$0.48 | Event-contract volumes down >25% q/q off the August air pocket; crypto ~$90M; core deposits ~$12B; OpEx >$740M or FY guide raised; credit-card allowance >5.5% | **−10% to −15%** → $79–$84, through $85.30 support toward the $73 level |

**What management commentary signals each:** Bull — explicit quantification of Rothera routing share and net revenue per contract, plus a monthly deposit walk showing September > August > July. Base — qualitative "strong momentum" language with no deposit cadence disclosed. Bear — hedging on Q4 event-contract seasonality, any softening of the >20% net-deposit growth target, or incremental compliance/legal spend tied to the sports-contract regulatory fight.

**Historical calibration:** HOOD has fallen after **each of its last four reports**, declines of 3.61% to 13.24%, average absolute move 9.14%. The pattern through 2026 has been fundamentals beating and the stock selling off on guidance and expectations. That history is the strongest argument against sizing long into this print.

## 4. Catalyst checklist

1. **Event-contract revenue vs. the unofficial ~$150M bar** — the only line with genuine two-sided surprise potential, and the one the bull case on "prediction markets are underappreciated" (Evercore, Rosenblatt) rests on.
2. **Q3 net deposits and the month-by-month cadence** — does the July $4B core run-rate recover? This is the KPI that underwrites the long-term asset-gathering story and the multiple.
3. **FY2026 OpEx + SBC guide ($2.675B–$2.775B)** — trimmed, reaffirmed, or raised. With revenue growth decelerating from +32% to ~+12%, the margin story has to carry more weight.
4. **Crypto revenue print vs. $135.6M consensus** — my expected miss line; watch whether management frames it as cyclical or structural.
5. **Narrative items:** Rothera economics and multi-venue strategy (Kalshi/ForecastEx retained), the Cboe KPI-contract launch announced at HOOD Summit, Dubai/international licensing, tokenized equities in 120+ countries, banking and credit card (>1M cardholders, $17B annualized purchase volume), and any buyback expansion — with the stock 38% off its high, an upsized authorization is a plausible defensive lever.
