## What to Watch (ranked)

1. **Production vs. the 7.4–7.6 Bcfe/d range.** A Q4 peak is planned, so Q3 should be on track. Any cut to the range would be a negative surprise.
2. **Realized gas price vs. Henry Hub, including hedges.** This is the largest driver of EPS variance against consensus.
3. **Capex and rig count.** Watch for any move toward the top or bottom of 11–12 rigs and $2.75–$2.95B.
4. **Buyback pace and FCF.** Continued repurchases at the Q2 pace would signal confidence. Watch whether the leverage target changes.
5. **Western Haynesville appraisal (~$75M) and 2027 outlook.** Early 2027 framing will likely drive the reaction more than the quarter itself.
