## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Guidance update:** Whether FY2026 guidance is raised, held, or cut, and the Q4 outlook. This drives the reaction more than the print.
2. **Oilfield Services vs. Industrial & Energy Technology (IET) revenue and margins:** IET orders and backlog (LNG, gas tech) are the growth story. Check whether IET still outgrows OFS.
3. **Segment operating margins:** Direction versus Q2, especially OFS margins.
4. **Orders and book-to-bill:** Especially IET equipment and LNG awards.
5. **Free cash flow:** Conversion and the capital return pace (buybacks/dividend).
6. **Revenue and EPS vs. consensus:** Only meaningful once the consensus base is confirmed.
