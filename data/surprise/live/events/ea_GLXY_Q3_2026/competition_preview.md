## 1. The One Thing That Matters

**This print will not trade on EPS.** Two facts frame the setup:

1. **The quarter itself was mechanically good.** Bitcoin rose from **$58,559 (Jun 30) to $83,554 (Sep 30) — +42.7% QoQ**. Galaxy carried **$1.16B of net digital assets and investments** into the quarter. Treasury & Corporate, which produced a **$(42)M** adjusted gross loss in Q2, almost certainly flipped to a large mark-to-market gain. Layer on the **first full quarter of ~$80M Helios Phase I leasing revenue at >90% project-level adjusted EBITDA margin** (company guidance), and Q3 should be Galaxy's first clearly profitable quarter of 2026.
2. **The stock is at 52-week lows anyway**, down ~30% in two weeks and ~64% from the high — because **CoreWeave**, the *sole tenant* for Helios's first 800 MW gross, is trading like a distressed credit: 2031 bond YTM **~15.8%** (record), 5-yr CDS **~1,040–1,140bps**, ~**$52B** of debt and lease liabilities, **~$7.5B due within 12 months of Jun 30**.

So: good numbers, bad narrative. The reaction function is dominated by **what management says about CoreWeave rent collection and credit protection**, not by the EPS line.

## 4. Scenario Analysis

| Scenario | Adj. Gross Profit | EPS | Key Driver | Stock Reaction |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Bull** (25%) | **>$650M** | **>$0.80** | Data Centers prints full $80M+ with explicit confirmation CoreWeave is **current on rent**, plus disclosure of credit enhancement (deposits/LCs/parent support) **or** a non-CoreWeave tenant LOI on Phase III/Merlin | **+18% to +30%** ($17.50–19.50) |
| **Base** (50%) | **~$510M** | **~$0.40** | Guidance delivered, treasury marks up, CoreWeave discussed qualitatively and constructively but with no new hard protections disclosed | **-4% to +8%** ($14.25–16.50) |
| **Bear** (25%) | **<$300M** | **$0.00–0.15** | Any of: rent timing/deferral language, a Phase II/III re-trade, Digital Assets EBITDA still negative, or incremental equity-funding need for the 5.7 GW pipeline | **-15% to -25%** ($11.00–12.75) |

**Bull needs operationally:** Phase I at full 133 MW for all 92 days, triple-net economics intact, Digital Assets AGP >$200M on volume recovery, and management volunteering the CoreWeave credit detail rather than being asked. **Management tell:** specific, quantified language on lease security — security deposits, letters of credit, collateral, cross-default terms. CIO Chris Ferraro has previously said Galaxy is "comfortable taking on pretty significant exposure to CoreWeave relative to the size of our business"; a *change* in that framing in either direction is the single most market-moving sentence on the call.

**Bear needs operationally:** no new counterparty disclosure plus any hint that Phase II capex ($448M/qtr and rising, $180M of equipment deposits already placed) outruns the funded envelope. **Management tell:** hedging on the "100% funded" Phase II claim, or re-opening the Q2'27 Phase II delivery date.

## 5. Catalyst Checklist (ranked)

1. **CoreWeave rent collection + credit protection** — Is Galaxy paid, in full, on time, and what stands behind the remaining 14.75 years? CoreWeave's CDS at ~1,040bps and a 15.8% bond yield mean the market is pricing real default probability on 100% of Helios's contracted cash flow. *This is 60%+ of the reaction.*
2. **Data Centers segment vs. the $80M/>90% guide** — the first clean quarter of the "Galaxy is an AI landlord" story. A miss here breaks the one part of the equity story that isn't crypto beta.
3. **Digital Assets adjusted EBITDA turning positive** — the buy side expects the operating business to stop subsidizing itself in a +43% BTC quarter. $66M → ~$140M AGP with positive segment EBITDA is the bar.
4. **Treasury policy / BTC beta** — management flagged a "modest reduction" in digital asset exposure in Q2. Does Galaxy monetize Q3 gains into cash to self-fund Phase II? That de-levers the equity story and is quietly bullish for multiple.
5. **Tenant and pipeline diversification** — 5.7+ GW pipeline (Helios 1.6 GW approved; Merlin 74–500 MW energizing 2028; Caspian ~700 MW; Selene ~900 MW). Any **non-CoreWeave** lease or LOI is the highest-convexity surprise available.
6. **Q4-to-date commentary** — BTC is ~$80.1–80.4K today vs. $83,554 at Sept 30, so Q4 marks have *already* started negative. Expect management to be asked; expect them to deflect to operating earnings.

### Bottom line

The numbers should be the best Galaxy has printed all year — modeled at roughly **+$0.40 EPS on ~$510M of adjusted gross profit**, far above the stale $(0.06) consensus. **Do not trade the beat.** The stock is a levered bet on one tenant's solvency, and the only disclosure that re-rates it is hard detail on CoreWeave lease security. Absent that, a strong print into a 145%-above-spot target gap most likely produces a muted-to-negative reaction, exactly as it did in August.
