## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Adj. EBITDA ex-metal lag vs. the FY guide.** Q2 was ~$310M ex-lag. The FY range of $980M–$1.02B implies a noticeably weaker H2 run rate. Check whether management holds the range.
2. **FY2026 guidance (EBITDA, FCF).** Whether the raise holds, is narrowed, or is cut matters more than the quarter itself. Watch for a cut to the FCF "above $300M" figure.
3. **Segment mix.** Aerospace and Packaging strength vs. Automotive Structures and Engineering weakness in Europe.
4. **Recycling and metal price lag.** The Q2 $129M lag benefit should not be repeated in Q3. Check how management frames the reversal.
5. **Free cash flow and leverage.** Q2 FCF was $90M. Working capital and capex timing drive the Q3 number.
