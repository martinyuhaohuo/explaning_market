## Recent Context

- **Q2 2026 (Aug 6):** FFO as Adjusted of $0.40 vs. $0.38 consensus (record). Revenue $122.8M. Same-property NOI +3.2% in Q2, +2.8% YTD.
- **Guidance raised:** FY2026 FFO guided to $1.50–$1.54, up $0.02 from $1.48–$1.52. The source labels conflict (FFO vs. FFO as Adjusted), so confirm the wording in the filing.
- **Drivers:** Rent commencements, higher recovery income, lease termination income, and accretive capital recycling. H1 included ~$8.4M (~$0.06/sh) of one-time environmental reimbursements in Q1. **Watch whether Q3 FFO excludes this to avoid a false "decline" read.**
- **Capital recycling:** Bought The Shops at West Falls Church and a Shoppers World leasehold ($51.1M combined). Under contract to sell a Kohl's-anchored center in Morris Plains, NJ for $60.5M.
- **Dividend:** $0.21 quarterly.

## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **FFO as Adjusted vs. consensus and guidance.** A beat is likely given the track record, but the magnitude matters.
2. **FY2026 FFO guidance.** Does management raise again, hold, or narrow? Holding at $1.50–$1.54 after a beat would read as cautious.
3. **Same-property NOI growth.** Sustaining ~3% is the bull point. Below 2% would be a negative signal.
4. **Leasing spreads and occupancy.** Watch for rent commencement pace and any tenant bankruptcies or go-dark risk.
5. **Recovery income and one-time items.** Quality of the beat. Strip out lease termination fees and reimbursements.
6. **Capital recycling progress.** Closing of the Morris Plains sale and the use of proceeds (buybacks, acquisitions, or debt paydown).
7. **Balance sheet and leverage.** Net debt/EBITDA trend.

## Scenario Analysis

These are my illustrative assumptions anchored to the guidance range, not sourced estimates.

| Scenario | FFO as Adj. (Q3) | FY26 Guidance | Key Driver | Stock Reaction (est.) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Bull | Beat by $0.02+ | Raised above $1.54 | SP NOI ≥3%, clean recurring beat, strong leasing | +3% to +5% |
| Base | In line to small beat | Reaffirmed or narrowed | Steady NOI, normal recoveries | −1% to +1% |
| Bear | Miss or beat only from one-time items | Low end or cut | NOI slowdown, tenant credit issues, one-time items flattering the quarter | −4% to −7% |

**Bull:** Management reports higher same-property NOI, raises guidance, and points to the capital recycling pipeline. Language on leasing demand would support this case.
**Base:** A small beat with guidance held. The stock reaction is limited because the Q2 beat was largely expected.
**Bear:** Beat quality is weak (one-time items), or the commentary signals slower rent commencement or tenant stress. Management commentary on bankruptcies or go-dark risk would be the signal.

**Historical context:** UE has beaten EPS in each of the last five reported quarters, but reactions have been small. Q2 2026 moved +0.5%. Q1 2026 moved ~+0.9%. The next-day move after the last report was +0.42%. A large move would require a guidance surprise, not a beat alone.

## Catalyst Checklist

1. **Q3 FFO as Adjusted vs. consensus.** A beat of $0.01–0.02 is expected. Quality of the beat matters more than the headline.
2. **FY2026 FFO guidance.** Investors want a raise or at least a reaffirmation at the top end.
3. **Same-property NOI and leasing.** Sustaining ~3% growth supports the multiple.
4. **Capital recycling and the Morris Plains sale.** Closing and redeployment of proceeds (buyback vs. acquisition).
5. **Dividend and balance sheet.** The $0.21 dividend is likely to be maintained. Watch for any change in leverage targets.
