**Comparability caveat:** The Stellar Bancorp merger closed July 1, 2026, so Q3 is the first full quarter with Stellar. Reported revenue, expenses, share count, and deposits will step up sharply, and some estimates may not fully reflect the deal. Expect headline "beat/miss" noise. Organic trends matter more.

## Recent Context

- **Q2 2026 (reported July 29):** GAAP diluted EPS $1.67; $1.62 excluding non-recurring items, up 14.1% YoY. NIM (tax-equivalent) 3.47%, up from 3.18% a year ago. Net interest income $330.6M, up 23.5% YoY. Deposits $32.6B.
- **Guidance:** Sources disagree on the 2026 NIM range. Quartr cites 3.70% to 3.75% (raised), with 3.80% to 3.85% targeted for 2027. Barchart cites 3.70% to 3.80%. Check the call transcript. Loan growth guided flat for the rest of 2026.
- **Stock:** Recent quotes around $73 to $75. 52-week range roughly $61.07 to $77.20.

## Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Net interest margin vs. the 3.70% to 3.75% guide.** This is the core bank driver. Watch loan repricing and deposit cost trends.
2. **EPS and the organic vs. acquired split.** Strip out Stellar contribution and merger costs. Is the core beat/miss real?
3. **Non-interest expense vs. the $244M to $250M guide.** Any overrun, or a sign that cost synergies are slipping, matters.
4. **Loan growth and the flat-loan guide.** Check whether period-end loans and pipeline support flat-to-up commentary.
5. **Credit quality.** Net charge-offs, non-performing loans, and reserve build, especially on the acquired Stellar book (credit marks and day-one reserves).
6. **Deposit costs and mix.** Deposit beta and non-interest-bearing share.
7. **Fee income and capital.** Fee trends, CET1 post-merger, and buyback intentions.
8. **Q4 and FY2027 commentary.** Early 2027 NIM target (3.80% to 3.85% per Quartr) and expense outlook.

**What would signal each:**
- **Bull:** Management raises the 2026 NIM view, reiterates the 2027 target, and says deposit costs are falling faster than loan yields.
- **Base:** Reaffirmed guidance, with commentary that the merger is "on track."
- **Bear:** Expense guidance raised, NIM outlook cut, or reserves increased on acquired loans.

**Historical context:** Q2 2026 beat ($1.62 vs. ~$1.56 per one source) and the stock edged up modestly. Q1 2026 drew a small negative reaction (about -1%). Sources don't give a reliable multi-quarter average move.

## Catalyst Checklist

1. **NIM vs. the 3.70% to 3.75% guide**: the buy-side will want to see the margin hold or expand despite the Stellar mix shift.
2. **Expense run-rate vs. $244M to $250M**: confirms merger synergy timing.
3. **Organic loan growth commentary**: "flat" is the guide; any upside is a positive surprise.
4. **Credit on acquired Stellar loans**: a surprise here is the most likely source of a sharp move.
5. **2027 NIM target (3.80% to 3.85%) and capital return**: narrative anchor for the stock.
