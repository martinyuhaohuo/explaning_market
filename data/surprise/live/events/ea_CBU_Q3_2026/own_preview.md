## Prior Quarter Baseline (Q2 2026, reported July 28)

From the company's [Q2 release](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000723188/000110465926087355/tm2621422d1_ex99-1.htm):
- **EPS:** $1.16 GAAP and operating. Some aggregators report $1.22, which I could not reconcile with the release, so I used the release figure. Q2 consensus was about $1.18–$1.19, so this was a slight miss.
- **Revenue:** $223.2M GAAP (+12.0% YoY). Consensus was about $222M.
- **NIM:** 3.46% GAAP, 3.49% FTE, up 19 bps YoY and 3–4 bps QoQ.
- **Loans:** $11.28B (+7.3% YoY, +1.4% QoQ).
- **Deposits:** $14.71B (+7.4% YoY, −1.1% QoQ) on seasonal government-deposit outflows.
- **Provision:** $4.6M.
- **Guidance:** No numeric full-year guidance in the release. Management said it expects "acceleration across all of our businesses into the second half." One aggregator lists FY26 targets of 5–6% loan growth, 3–4% deposit growth, 10–11% NII growth, and 6–7% non-interest revenue growth, and a $20–25M provision. I could not verify those against the release.
- **Dividend:** Raised to $0.49 from $0.47.
- **Insurance:** Revenue was soft on lower contingency income and weak premium markets. This is a known weak spot.

## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **NIM and NII growth.** The NII trajectory is the core earnings driver. Watch whether NIM holds above 3.45% and whether management's 10–11% NII growth view is reaffirmed.
2. **Deposit trends.** Q2 deposits fell QoQ. Check whether government-deposit outflows reversed and what the Q4 seasonal outlook is.
3. **Loan growth.** Q2 was +1.4% QoQ. Check whether this is tracking the 5–6% annual pace.
4. **Non-interest revenue, especially insurance.** Check for a recovery in contingency income and organic growth. This was the Q2 weak point.
5. **Provision and credit quality.** Q2 provision was low at $4.6M. Watch for any reserve build or NPL migration.
6. **EPS vs. $1.18–$1.19.** A beat is likely if NII holds up, but the revenue beat in Q2 was largely adjusted-basis.
