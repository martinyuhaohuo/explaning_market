## 2. Key Metrics to Watch — Ranked

**1. Net interest margin (highest signal).** Q2 2026 NIM was **3.46% GAAP / 3.49% FTE**, +4 bps QoQ and +19 bps YoY. Management guided Q3 to **+1 bp to −2 bps** on seasonally elevated overnight borrowing costs, with expansion resuming in Q4 and a **"low-to-mid 3.5%"** exit rate for 2026. *This is the single most important line.* A NIM at or above 3.49% FTE beats a guide management already set low; below 3.46% implies deposit costs are biting harder than the seasonal story allows.

**2. The securities-repricing bridge.** >$1B of securities cash flows over 18 months, yielding ~2%, redeployable into ~6% loans. On the Q2 call the CFO pushed the meaningful start to **Q4 2026 / Q1 2027**. Any pull-forward is the bull case for 2027 NII; any slippage guts the $5.18 FY27 consensus.

**3. Loan and deposit growth.** Loans $11.28B (+7.3% YoY, +1.4% QoQ) against a **5–6% FY loan growth** target management said it was tracking "toward the higher end." Mortgage pipeline was described as the strongest in seven years. Deposits $14.71B fell 1.1% QoQ in Q2 on municipal seasonality — management guided **3–4% FY deposit growth** and said the outflow reverses in H2. **Deposits failing to rebound in Q3 is the cleanest bear tell**, especially given the Q2 "deposit drift" narrative already attached to the stock. LDR of 76% and a stated refusal to pay above wholesale for deposits is the defense.

**4. Insurance segment inflection.** Insurance revenue was **$13.2M, −4.8% YoY** in Q2 and down 6.5% YTD on lower contingent commissions and a soft premium market. Management conceded it will miss prior guidance for the year. Watch for (a) sequential stabilization and (b) any announced agency M&A — management flagged a pipeline of deals that "could be much more needle movers" for 2027.

**5. Fee mix and the other two engines.** Noninterest revenue was **$84.0M, 37.6% of total** in Q2: employee benefit services $34.9M (+7.7%, pre-tax +16.2%), wealth $10.4M (+19.8%, pre-tax +46.5%), banking fees $21.3M (+6.1%). ClearPoint Federal Bank & Trust ($1.5B AUM) closed June 1, 2026 — **Q3 is the first full quarter of contribution**. The fee-mix percentage crossing 38% supports the "diversified, deserves a premium multiple" thesis the bulls need.

**6. Expenses.** Core noninterest expense guided to **$550–555M for FY2026** (+7–8%), including $8–9M from Santander branches and $4–5M from ClearPoint. Q2 ran $137.7M (+6.7%); efficiency ratio 61.7%, improved from 64.8%. Management credited AI productivity for flat-ish employee costs despite headcount growth. **A raised expense guide is the most likely source of a negative surprise that isn't NIM.**

**7. Credit.** Provision $4.6M in Q2 against **$20–25M FY guidance**; NPLs 0.50% of loans, NCOs 0.12% annualized, ACL 0.81% (~8x trailing-12-month charge-offs). Credit is currently a non-event — which means it only matters if it breaks.

**8. Capital.** Tier 1 leverage 9.26%, TCE/TA 6.86%. Buybacks have been token (258,471 shares in H1). Management is explicitly prioritizing insurance M&A over repurchases.

## 4. Catalyst Checklist

1. **FTE NIM vs. ~3.49%** (the Q2 level, which management guided roughly flat). Above = the lowered bar was sandbagging; below = the deposit-cost story is real. Highest beta to the stock.
2. **Deposit balances vs. $14.71B** — the Q2 municipal outflow must reverse. Two consecutive down quarters converts "seasonal" into "deposit drift," which is exactly the narrative that cost the stock 2.8% last print.
3. **Reaffirmation of FY expense ($550–555M) and loan growth (5–6%)** — the buy-side expects both unchanged. Any upward expense revision outweighs an in-line EPS beat.
4. **Insurance revenue sequential trajectory + M&A pipeline commentary** — this is the one fee business going backwards, and management has already pre-announced the miss. The question for 2027 is whether acquisitions replace the organic shortfall.
5. **Securities-redeployment timing language** — the entire $5.18 FY2027 EPS consensus leans on the ~2%→~6% reinvestment. Watch whether "beginning in Q4" slips to "Q1 2027" or later.
