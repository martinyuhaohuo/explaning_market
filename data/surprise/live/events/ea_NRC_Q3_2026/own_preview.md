## Prior Quarter Context (Q2 FY26, reported July 28, 2026)

- Revenue $35.4M, up 4% YoY. Adjusted EPS $0.31. GAAP EPS ($0.15), a GAAP net loss of $3.3M.
- Adjusted EBITDA $9.4M (27% margin).
- Free cash flow only $0.1M, and operating cash flow $1.4M. Both were depressed by about $2.9M of cash payments tied to adjustment items.
- TRCV (total recurring contract value) $151.9M, up 11% YoY. This is the key growth metric.
- The GAAP-to-adjusted gap came mainly from accelerated stock vesting and associated cash bonuses tied to management transition.
- CEO Trent Green cited the strongest bookings for The Governance Institute in seven years and a strong second half.
- No formal full-year guidance was stated. The quarterly dividend is $0.16.

## Key Metrics to Watch (Ranked)

1. **TRCV and growth rate.** Q2 was +11% YoY. Watch whether bookings strength from The Governance Institute shows up as acceleration toward double digits.
2. **Revenue vs. $35.8M.** Growth has been low-single-digit (+4%), so a clean beat matters more than the headline number.
3. **Adjusted EBITDA margin and adjusted EPS vs. $0.21.** Check whether the quarter's margin holds near 27% after the one-time compensation items roll off.
4. **GAAP vs. adjusted reconciliation.** Confirm that the adjustment items are non-recurring. Repeated "one-time" charges will hurt credibility.
5. **Free cash flow.** Q2 FCF was just $0.1M. A rebound would support the dividend narrative.
6. **Management and leadership commentary.** Check the status of the executive transition and any restructuring-related severance.
7. **Full-year outlook.** None was given last quarter. Any formal FY26 guidance is a new data point the market will reward or punish.
