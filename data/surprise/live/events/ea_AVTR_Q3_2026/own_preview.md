## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Organic growth vs. the guide.** Q2 organic was -0.4%. The FY guide implies roughly flat to slightly positive organic growth in H2, so Q3 must hold near flat for the raise to stay credible.
2. **Bioscience Products (BMP).** Q2 organic was -5.6%, but orders grew double digits and book-to-bill was 1.1. Management says BMP returns to growth in H2. Confirming this is the main bull test.
3. **VWR Distribution & Services.** Returned to organic growth (+1.7%) in Q2 on pharma and biotech activity. Watch whether that holds.
4. **Gross margin.** Q2 was 31.7%, down 120 bps on mix, inflation, and volume. Expansion would support the EPS guide.
5. **Adj. EBITDA margin and FCF.** Guides are 14.8%–15.3% and $500M–$550M. Adjusted net leverage was 3.3x at Q2, so deleveraging progress matters.

**Bull:** Management reports BMP orders converting to H2 growth and says VWR momentum is durable. A raise at the top of the FY EPS range would likely drive the move.

**Base:** Results match consensus and the guide is reaffirmed. Commentary on H2 BMP recovery and leverage would decide the direction.

**Bear:** Organic growth turns negative again, or BMP recovery slips to 2027. Management might cut the FY organic range. The company's history suggests this scenario could produce a large move.

1. **Q3 organic growth vs. ~flat expectation.** A positive print supports the FY raise.
2. **BMP orders and book-to-bill vs. the 1.1 Q2 level.** Sustained order growth is the bull case.
