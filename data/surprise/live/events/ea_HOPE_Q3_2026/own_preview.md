## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **NIM and deposit costs.** Does the sequential expansion continue? Watch the cost of interest-bearing deposits versus asset repricing.
2. **Revenue vs. ~$151.7M consensus.** Q2 revenue beat, so the bar is slightly higher.
3. **Loan growth.** Is the ~20% end-of-period target on track, and how much is MANUBANK-driven versus organic?
4. **Credit quality.** Provisions, NPLs, and net charge-offs. Any CRE weakness would matter most to the stock.
5. **Efficiency ratio and PPNR growth.** Tests whether the 25–30% PPNR guide is achievable.
6. **MANUBANK closing timeline.** Any update on approvals.
7. **Q4 EPS outlook** (~$0.35 consensus).
