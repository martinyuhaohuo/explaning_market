## Key Metrics to Watch (Ranked)

1. **Revenue vs. ~$493M and core growth.** Q2 core growth was 10%. Whether that holds tells you if the beat-and-raise pattern continues.
2. **Adjusted EBITDA margin vs. Q2's 27.7%.** Sustained margin expansion matters more to the buy-side than a small EPS beat.
3. **Full-year guidance.** Watch for a second raise versus a hold. The bar is higher after the Q2 raise.
4. **Segment mix.** On the Q2 call, institutional markets were strong while residential and parts of commercial were soft. Any change in that split is a key read.
5. **Free cash flow and tariff effects.** Check whether FCF stays at or above $350M and how much of the Q2 benefit was one-time refund.
6. **Capital allocation.** Buyback pace after the Q2 $50M repurchase, and leverage.

**Note on EPS:** Webull reported a $0.67 statutory EPS for Q2, versus $0.50 adjusted. The gap is likely the tariff refund and other items. Confirm which figure the consensus uses before comparing.

## Catalyst Checklist

1. **Revenue and core growth vs. ~$493M consensus.** A beat that repeats the Q2 pattern is likely priced in. A miss on core growth would be the main negative surprise.
2. **FY2026 guidance direction.** The buy-side will expect at least a maintained range after a Q2 raise. A cut or a narrowed range to the low end is the main risk.
3. **Institutional vs. residential split.** Residential softness was already flagged. Any sign that it is spreading to commercial would change the narrative.
4. **Margin trajectory.** Sustained EBITDA margins near or above 28% support the premium multiple.
5. **Capital returns and FCF.** The $350M FCF floor and buyback pace are the cushion if top-line growth slows.
