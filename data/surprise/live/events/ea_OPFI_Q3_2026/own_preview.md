## Guidance Math

- H1 2026 adjusted EPS: Q1 $0.35 + Q2 $0.33 = **$0.68**.
- Full-year guide of $1.34–$1.51 implies **H2 EPS of $0.66–$0.83**, or about **$0.33–$0.42 per quarter**.
- The ~$0.41 Q3 consensus sits at the top of that implied quarterly range. Meeting consensus would require the company to hit the high end of its own guidance.
- Management says growth returns in H2 2026. The delayed credit line launch and LOLA system timing are the stated reasons for the cut.
- Originations fell about 9% in Q2 to $212M as underwriting tightened. Net charge-offs rose to 52% of receivables from 43%. These are the metrics most likely to drive a guidance revision.

## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Net charge-offs (% of receivables)**: Q2 was 52%, up from 43%. Any further rise threatens the H2 recovery story.
2. **Originations and loan growth**: Whether the Q2 pullback (-9% YoY) reversed as underwriting loosened.
3. **Adjusted EPS vs. ~$0.41**, and whether it stays in the implied $0.33–$0.42 quarterly range.
4. **FY2026 guidance**: Whether management holds, narrows, or cuts again. A second cut would be the largest negative.
5. **New credit line and LOLA timing**: Concrete launch dates are the evidence the H2 ramp is real.
6. **BNCCORP / BNC National Bank acquisition**: Expected to close in Q4 2026. Watch for any timing or financing change.
7. **Long-term targets**: The 2027–2028 targets are unchanged, including $3 EPS by end-2028. Management will be pressed on whether that holds after two sets of misses.
