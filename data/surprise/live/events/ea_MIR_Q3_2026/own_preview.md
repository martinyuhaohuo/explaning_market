## Recent Results and Guidance

- **Q2 2026 (reported Jul 28):**
  - Revenue $266.8M, up 19.7% reported. Organic growth was only ~1.2%.
  - Revenue was below the ~$269.3M estimate. Adj. EPS of $0.12 beat the $0.10 estimate. GAAP EPS was $0.03.
  - Adj. EBITDA $65.3M (+27.5%), a ~24.5% margin.
  - Orders $291M (+40%, including the Paragon and Certrec acquisitions). Excluding them, orders were $229M (+10%).
- **FY2026 guidance (reaffirmed in July):**
  - Total revenue growth 22–24%, organic growth 5–7%.
  - Adj. EBITDA $285–300M, margin 25–26%.
  - Adj. FCF $155–175M.
  - Adj. EPS $0.48–0.55.
- **Commentary:** Management said it was adjusting the end-market mix in Medical while keeping guidance. Quartr reports that H2 needs $605–623M of revenue with 7.5–11.2% organic growth.

## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Organic growth vs. the H2 ramp.** H1 organic growth was low, and the full-year 5–7% target requires a sharp H2 acceleration. A Q3 print that doesn't show it is the biggest risk to guidance.
2. **Revenue vs. ~$275M.** Q2 missed on revenue while beating on EPS, so the market will look at top-line quality first.
3. **Adj. EBITDA margin vs. the 25–26% full-year range.** Watch whether the margin holds with the acquisitions included.
4. **Orders and book-to-bill, organic.** Organic orders growth of +10% in Q2 is the best evidence for the H2 ramp. Watch whether it stays at that level.
5. **FY guidance.** Reaffirm, raise, or cut. A cut to organic growth or EPS is the main downside trigger.
6. **Adj. FCF and cash conversion.** Needed to support the $155–175M target.
7. **Medical segment mix changes.** Management flagged these in July; get the details on the call.

## Catalyst Checklist

1. **Organic revenue growth vs. the implied H2 ramp (~7.5–11%).** This is what the buy side will focus on; the headline revenue number matters less.
2. **FY2026 guidance (revenue, EBITDA, EPS, FCF).** The buy side expects a reaffirmation, with EPS near the top of range given the ~$0.53 consensus.
3. **Organic orders and book-to-bill.** Organic orders at +10% were the bright spot in Q2; the question is whether they hold.
4. **Medical end-market adjustment.** Expect detail on the mix change and any effect on margins.
5. **Acquisition contribution (Paragon, Certrec).** Separating acquired growth from organic is essential to read the quarter.
