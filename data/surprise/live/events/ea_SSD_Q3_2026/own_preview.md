## 3. Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Revenue vs. ~$625–640M and North America growth.** Q2 growth was price-led with flat-to-down units. Check whether volumes turned positive or pricing carried growth again.
2. **Gross margin trajectory.** Guidance calls for a slight full-year decline. Check whether Q3 is in line with that, and whether raw-material costs (steel, and freight in particular) are still pressuring the margin.
3. **Operating margin and the land-sale gain.** Check whether the $10–12M gain is in Q3 or Q4, and whether the reported operating margin is adjusted for it. This can make EPS look better than the underlying business.
4. **Europe performance.** Q2 growth was +7.6%. Check whether it held up.
5. **Full-year guidance (net sales, operating margin, EPS).** This matters more than the quarter. Look for a raise or a hold, and whether the 19.7%–20.5% operating margin range is narrowed.
6. **Cash flow, capex, and buybacks.** Capital return is a secondary driver. I did not find data on Q3 free cash flow.

## 4. Scenario Analysis

These are my illustrative estimates, not sourced forecasts.

| Scenario | Revenue | EPS | Key Driver | Stock Reaction (illustrative) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Bull** | $650M+ | $2.85+ | Volumes turn positive in NA, Europe strong, FY guide raised | +6% to +10% |
| **Base** | $635M–$645M | $2.65–$2.75 | Modest beat, FY guide reaffirmed, margin in line | −2% to +4% |
| **Bear** | <$625M | <$2.50 | Volumes soft, gross margin pressure, FY margin guide trimmed | −6% to −10% |

**Bull:** Unit volumes are positive, or the company signals H2 demand recovery. Management raises the operating margin range or net sales guidance. Commentary points to backlog or order strength and continued pricing.

**Base:** The quarter beats modestly, the company holds full-year guidance, and management says the margin is on track. Stock moves are muted unless the tone changes.

**Bear:** Revenue misses, NA units decline again, gross margin falls below expectations, and the company narrows or cuts the margin range. Commentary about housing or construction softness would reinforce this.

**Historical reactions (limited data):**
- Q2 FY25 (Jul 2025): gapped up about 8% ($166 → $180) on a $0.08 EPS beat
- Q3 FY25 (Oct 2025): reported about +6% in pre-market trading
- Q1 FY26 (May 2026): roughly flat after hours (~+0.2%) on a ~13% EPS beat

Post-earnings moves have been mixed, and beats don't reliably produce large rallies.

## 5. Catalyst Checklist

1. **Revenue vs. ~$630M consensus midpoint.** A miss would hurt more than usual given the Q2 beat. Watch whether the Q2 pricing tailwind is fading.
2. **Full-year operating margin guide (19.7%–20.5%).** Buy-side wants to see the range held or raised, and the land-sale gain excluded from the underlying number.
3. **NA unit volumes.** Any sign of a return to unit growth would drive a re-rating; continued declines would cap the stock.
4. **Gross margin commentary.** Watch for an update on raw-material and freight costs.
5. **Capital allocation.** Check buyback and dividend commentary, and any M&A.
