## 2. Key Metrics to Watch — Ranked

**1. North America volume, now that price has lapped (highest importance).**
H1 revenue growth was **5–6 points of price**; management guided that to **~1 point in H2**. So NA growth from here ≈ volume. NA was $483.6M in Q3 2025 and grew 6.0% in Q2 2026 on price with *declining* units. With consensus at +0.3% total, the Street is embedding roughly **flat-to-slightly-negative volume**. Any print showing NA volumes down mid-single-digits breaks the "growth above market" thesis.

**2. Gross margin vs. steel and tariffs.**
Q3 2025 GM was 46.4%; Q2 2026 was 47.4%. Management explicitly flagged **rising steel prices, tariffs, and higher depreciation** making "a more challenging backdrop, particularly as we move through the back half of 2026," with FY gross margin "slightly lower." Watch consolidated GM ~46% and NA GM vs. 49.0% LY.

**3. The FY operating-margin bridge — the single biggest guidance risk.**
H1 2026 ran **22.5%** operating margin ($283.7M on $1,259.1M). FY guidance of 19.7–20.5% implies **H2 of only ~16.7%–18.3%**. But consensus EPS of $2.45–$2.56 requires a Q3 operating margin of roughly **21.4%–22.4%** — about **100bps above where even the top of FY guidance points**, which in turn leaves an implausibly thin Q4 (~13–14% *including* the Stockton gain, vs. 13.9% in Q4 2025 with no gain). Either guidance proves conservative again (it has in 7 of 8 quarters) **or the Street's Q3 is too high and the H2 margin reset lands tonight.** This is the crux of the setup.

**4. Europe trajectory.**
Record **13.7%** operating margin in Q2 (vs. 12.0% in Q3 2025), with a medium-term **15% target** reaffirmed. Europe has been the volume bright spot (+7.6% in Q2 on volume *and* price). Note FX: Europe got a **+$3.7M** tailwind in Q2 and **+$8.1M** in Q3 2025 — a reversal would flatter/pressure the optics.

**5. 2027 setup and the pricing decision.**
Early framing on 2027 housing starts, whether they push another price increase into an affordability-constrained market (management called this "challenging"), carryover of the **$30M** cost-savings program, and commercialization of the **Cornerstone** cloud truss platform launched at BCMC this fall (management framed it as a long-tail opportunity, not a near-term revenue driver).

**6. Capital returns / FCF.** H1 operating cash flow $248.5M, inventory down $80.7M from year-end, $98.7M repurchased in H1 against a raised **$200M** authorization and a commitment to return **≥35% of FCF**. Cash $450.5M vs. $333.4M debt.

**Macro backdrop:** September US housing starts fell **5.2%** to a 1.22M SAAR; single-family dropped **4.6%** to 859k — the weakest in a year, though still +2.6% YoY — with the 30-year fixed near 7.5%. Management's FY assumption is US starts down low-single-digits.

**What each requires operationally:**
- **Bull:** evidence the company is out-growing a declining start environment on units — component manufacturing and OEM channels grew mid- and high-single-digits in Q2 and would need to accelerate — plus steel cost pass-through holding. Tell: management raising the floor of FY margin guidance rather than merely reaffirming.
- **Base:** price contributes ~1pt as guided, volumes roughly flat, margin steps down sequentially from 25.2% but stays above 20%. Tell: "reaffirm" language with Q4 weighted to the land-sale gain.
- **Bear:** the H2 reset management telegraphed arrives all at once in Q3. Tell: any language on "lower volumes," softening backlog at component manufacturers, or steel cost recovery lagging — especially if paired with the low end of the FY range.

**Historical calibration — reactions have been muted even on big beats:**

## 4. Catalyst Checklist

1. **Q3 operating margin vs. ~21.5% (consensus-implied)** — the real bar, well above the ~18–20.8% that FY guidance arithmetic supports. A 20% print is a "miss" on the number that matters even if EPS clears $2.45.
2. **North America organic volume** — with price down to ~1pt, this is the first clean read on units in two years. The buy-side wants flat-to-positive against single-family starts at a one-year low.
3. **FY2026 operating-margin guidance action** — reaffirm 19.7–20.5% (base), raise the floor (bull), or trim (bear). Remember Q4 now carries the full $10–12M Stockton gain, so a reaffirm is a *higher-quality* statement than it looks: it requires real Q4 operating performance, not just the gain.
4. **Gross margin and steel/tariff commentary** — whether cost inflation is being recovered or compressing the 46–47% range.
5. **Any first look at 2027** — pricing intentions, starts assumption, and cost-savings carryover. Management has historically used the Q3 call to frame the next year; a cautious frame would override an in-line quarter.
