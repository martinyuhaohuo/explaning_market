The Q3 EPS consensus is nearly flat year over year. This corrects my earlier search summary, which called it a "sharp rise." Given the Q2 remittance softness, a flat-to-slightly-up quarter is roughly what the Street has priced in.

## Recent Context

- **Q2 2026:** Adj. EPS was $2.82, up about 10% year over year. Revenue was $1.11B, up about 3%. Sources disagree on the consensus: $2.97 (MarketBeat) versus $3.03 (Chartmill) for EPS, and $1.14B versus $1.17B for revenue. The quarter was reported as a miss under both sets of numbers.
- **Guidance:** FY2026 was reaffirmed, with adj. EPS growth of 10–15% and revenue growth of about 6%. Aggregators disagree on whether the July update was a raise or a reaffirmation, so check the release wording.
- **Remittance:** Cross-border revenue fell about 5%, driven by lower US-to-Mexico volumes. Remittance operating income was $137M, down 14%. Digital remittance (Ria Digital transactions) grew about 33%, which is the bright spot.
- **Capital return:** $50M of buybacks in Q2 (705K shares).

## What to Watch (ranked)

1. **Cross-border/remittance revenue and margin.** Is the US-Mexico volume decline stabilizing? Is digital growth offsetting it?
2. **EPS vs. ~$3.65.** A beat matters more if it comes from operations, not from a tax or buyback benefit.
3. **FY2026 EPS guidance vs. the $10.57–$11.05 range.** Holding the range is likely the base case. The market will react to whether the low end is cut or the high end is raised.
4. **EFT/epay segment and ATM volumes.** Confirms whether the core business is still growing.
5. **Free cash flow and buyback pace.** Supports the capital return story if guidance is held.

| **Bull** | ≥$1.21B | ≥$3.85 | Digital remittance and EFT strength; remittance volume stabilizes | Upside; guidance raised toward top of range |

| **Bear** | <$1.17B | <$3.50 | Remittance declines deepen; low end of guidance cut | Downside; second consecutive soft quarter |

**Bull signals in commentary:** Cross-border volume trends improving, digital adoption accelerating, buyback pace maintained or increased.

**Bear signals in commentary:** Remittance pressure described as "persistent," guidance language shifting to "lower end," margin pressure from pricing or compliance costs.

**Historical context:** I could not confirm the Q2 2026 closing move. One report indicated pre-market shares were down about 5% after a roughly 14% run-up over the prior month. I did not find a reliable multi-quarter reaction history, so I can't give a calibrated average move.

## Catalyst Checklist

1. **Remittance revenue vs. the decline trend.** A second quarter of double-digit decline would likely drive a negative reaction.
2. **Adj. EPS vs. ~$3.65.** Beat quality matters: check whether the beat is operational.
3. **FY guidance language.** Reaffirmed versus narrowed toward the low end.
4. **Digital remittance growth (Ria Digital).** This is the narrative offset to the core decline.
5. **Capital allocation.** Buyback pace and any change to the share repurchase program.
