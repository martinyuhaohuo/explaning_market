## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Distilling Solutions sales and brown goods.** Brown goods fell 59% to $14.3M in Q2. The company blamed weak demand for aged and new distillate whiskey. Any stabilization or further decline here is the biggest swing factor.
2. **Gross margin by segment, especially Ingredient Solutions.** Its margin fell from 21.7% to 10.1%. Confirm whether this is mix or cost pressure.
3. **FY26 guidance, reaffirmed or cut.** The guide implies a weaker second half. Watch whether the $480M–$500M revenue and $90M–$98M EBITDA ranges hold. Q2 reaffirmation was a neutral signal.
4. **Leverage and cash flow.** Year-to-date operating cash flow was -$40.7M, which includes a $48.7M contingent consideration payment. Implied free cash flow was about -$51M. Watch leverage commentary and any debt paydown plan.
5. **Tax and share count.** The effective tax rate guide rose to about 23% because of a Kansas tax law change. Share count is about 21.4M.

## Catalyst Checklist

1. **Q3 revenue vs. ~$118–121M consensus.** Revenue is the weak line. A miss would likely dominate the reaction.
2. **FY26 guidance vs. the $480M–$500M / $90M–$98M ranges.** The buy-side will want clarity on whether the back half can deliver.
3. **Distilling Solutions outlook.** Look for any specific comment on bulk whiskey demand, which the company has not given.
4. **Balance sheet and cash.** Leverage at 3.5x and negative cash flow are the main concerns for the stock.
5. **Strategic changes.** Leadership changes were announced with Q2. Watch for any restructuring, asset sales, or capital allocation shift.
