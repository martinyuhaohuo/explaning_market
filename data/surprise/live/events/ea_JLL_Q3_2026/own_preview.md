## Context from Q2 2026 (reported late July)

- Revenue was $6.93B, up 11% year over year, versus about $6.82B expected.
- Adjusted EPS was $5.26, up 59%, versus about $4.52–4.56 expected.
- Leasing Advisory grew 24% in local currency. Capital Markets Services grew 19%.
- Free cash flow was $438M, up 52%. First-half buybacks were $410M.
- Guidance was raised. The midpoint implies 34% adjusted EPS growth. I could not confirm the exact range.
- Management guided Real Estate Management Services to mid-to-high single-digit revenue growth, with a Q4-weighted second half.
- Management guided Investment Management advisory fees to low-single-digit growth. Incentive and transaction fees should land near the low end of the historical range, weighted to Q4.
