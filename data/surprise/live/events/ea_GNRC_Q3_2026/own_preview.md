## Prior Quarter Baseline (Q2 2026, reported July 29)

- **Net sales:** $1.17B, up 11% YoY. Slightly below the ~$1.18B estimate.
- **C&I:** $556M, up ~29%.
- **Residential:** $617M, down ~2%.
- **Adj. EBITDA:** $291M (24.8% margin), including ~$71M of tariff refunds.
- **Adj. EPS:** $2.91, including a ~$0.90 tariff-refund benefit. Excluding it, EPS is ~$2.01, which matches the $2.01 estimate MarketBeat cited. **The headline beat was largely the refund, not the operating quarter.**
- **Data center backlog:** ~$1.6B, with ~$1B of orders in the prior 90 days. About $1.35B is scheduled for 2027 delivery. FY26 data center revenue is guided to ~$450M.
- **Guidance changes:** C&I growth raised to low-30s% (from mid-to-high 20s). Residential trimmed to high-single-digit growth (from ~10%) on soft outage activity. Adj. EBITDA margin raised to 20–21% (from 18.5–19.5%), with ~1.5 points from tariff refunds.
- **Capacity:** Sussex, WI plant on track to start by end of Q3. Management sees a path to triple large-generator capacity in 12 months. 2026 capex ~4.5% of sales.

## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Underlying adj. EBITDA margin ex-tariff refunds.** This is the cleanest read on whether the 20–21% guide holds without one-time help. Watch whether management separates refund effects in the Q3 print.
2. **Data center revenue and backlog conversion.** Check whether the ~$450M FY26 target is still intact and whether new hyperscale orders (a second customer was added in Q2) show up in backlog.
3. **C&I growth vs. the low-30s% guide.** A reading below ~25% would suggest the raise was too aggressive.
4. **Residential sales and outage activity.** Residential was already cut. Any further weakness, or commentary on storm season and channel inventory, is the main downside risk.
5. **FY26 guidance reaffirmation or raise.** Net sales growth in the mid-to-high teens and the 20–21% margin range are the bar. Any cut to either is likely to dominate the reaction.
