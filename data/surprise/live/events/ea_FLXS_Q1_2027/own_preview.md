## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Sales vs. the $111M–$115M range.** The bottom half would signal soft demand. Management says consumer demand is the biggest source of variability.
2. **Operating margin vs. 6.5%–7.0%.** Energy-driven cost pressure (raw materials, freight, transport) is the main margin risk. Q4 margin was ~7.5%, so a drop would read as contraction.
3. **Soft seating growth vs. homestyles exit.** Management expects soft seating growth to offset the homestyles category being exited. Watch the segment split and whether the offset holds.
4. **Gross margin and cost pass-through.** Evidence of pricing offsetting energy-driven inbound and outbound costs.
5. **FY27 commentary.** Any full-year update, and the effect of the Section 232 upholstered-seating tariff step-up from 25% to 30% on January 1, 2027. The tariff item comes from a third-party analysis, not company guidance.
6. **Free cash flow and balance sheet.** Q4 FCF was strong, so the question is whether it held up in Q1.

**Check on the EPS consensus:** My rough math doesn't support Sidoti's $1.25. At a 6.5%–7.0% margin on $111M–$115M, operating income is about $7.2M–$8.1M. After tax and assuming ~5.3M shares, that is roughly $1.00–$1.15 EPS, depending on interest income, tax rate, and share count. I did not verify the share count or tax rate, so treat this as a sanity check. If the margin guide holds, the $1.25 figure looks high and the EPS "beat" bar may be lower than the headline suggests.
