## 3. Key Metrics to Watch — Ranked

**1. FY2027 guidance (dominant, ~70% of the stock reaction)**
Management declined specifics in July, citing "planning stages." They now have to put a number down. Two sub-questions: (a) does the September high-single-digit price increase hold, and (b) what elasticity assumption is baked in? Scalzo flagged elasticity of **"one or higher"** — meaning pricing may be revenue-neutral at best. Reverse-engineering DA Davidson's $12 target (7× CY27 EBITDA) implies they model **~$190M CY27 EBITDA**, down another ~14% from FY26. I'd put the Street's working range at $200–215M. A guide at or above $220M is a genuine upside surprise; below $195M breaks the stock.

**2. Atkins decline rate — has the bleeding stopped?**
Atkins fell **−24.6%** in Q3 ($84.6M) with retail takeaway −23.9%. Management said distribution losses should stabilize "through Q4" as comps ease. Watch for the decline to narrow to the high-teens/low-20s. If Atkins is still −25% or worse, the "resetting the retail baseline" narrative loses credibility and FY27 has no floor.

**3. Quest bars inflection**
Quest grew just **+1.1%** ($230.3M) in Q3 with takeaway +1.4%. The internals are bifurcated: chips +17%, milkshakes +~50%, but **bars consumption −5%** — and bars are the bulk of the brand. Scalzo called bars the "highest priority." Quest household penetration rose 120bps to 20.5%, which is the genuinely encouraging datapoint. Quest needs to be ≥+3% for the "Quest carries the company" thesis to work.

**4. OWYN — distribution resets and litigation overhang**
OWYN grew +3.6% ($34.8M) in Q3, but management pre-warned of **"distribution losses over the next 6 to 12 months"** following pea-protein quality problems. Cumulative OWYN impairments hit $200M — ~70% of the purchase price in under two years. A securities class action (class period Oct 24, 2024 – Apr 8, 2026; lead plaintiff deadline was Oct 13, 2026) alleges the company concealed the supplier switch and management departures. Any quantification of FY27 OWYN distribution losses is a direct negative.

**5. Gross margin bridge and the September price increase**
Cocoa deflation is being "significantly offset" by whey spikes; protein, packaging, and freight inflation persist. Q4 is the clean read on whether productivity can hold the line before pricing kicks in (September = FY27 Q1).

**6. Another impairment?**
Q4 is the annual goodwill/intangible testing quarter. FY26 YTD non-cash impairments already total **$331M**. With the stock at $9.95 and market cap ($880M) far below book-implied values, a further Atkins-related write-down is a live possibility. Non-cash and largely irrelevant to the thesis — but headline risk on a 7:00 AM tape.

**7. Capital allocation**
$213M repurchased YTD, ~$158M authorization remaining, net debt/EBITDA 1.2x, cash $123.9M. On my estimates FY26 FCF lands around $110–125M — a **13–14% FCF yield**. Continued aggressive buyback at these prices is the strongest signal management can send; a pause would read as a liquidity or covenant worry.

| **Bear** (25%) | <$320M (below guide) | ≤$0.35 | **<$195M** or **no guidance given** | GM misses the "best quarter" promise; OWYN losses quantified; fresh impairment; elasticity >1 on September pricing | **−15% to −25%** |

### What each requires operationally

- **Bull:** Needs evidence the Atkins distribution reset is *finished*, not ongoing, plus a Quest bars innovation pipeline with named launches and timing. Verbal tell: Scalzo giving a specific household-penetration target and a marketing-spend path toward his stated "10% of sales" ideal *without* guiding EBITDA down to fund it.
- **Base:** Management guides FY27 conservatively, frames it as an "investment year," and leans on the balance sheet. Tell: ranges wider than normal, explicit refusal to call the inflection point.
- **Bear:** The giveaway phrase is any version of "we are not providing full-year guidance at this time" or deferring to an investor day. Given a new CEO and an active securities class action, this is a real possibility — and at 5.2× EV/FY26E EBITDA the stock is cheap but the market has shown it will not pay for an un-guidable turnaround.

## 5. Catalyst Checklist

1. **FY2027 adjusted EBITDA guidance vs. the ~$200–215M Street bar** — the single number that resets the valuation. Below $195M validates DA Davidson's $12 target as generous.
2. **Atkins Q4 net sales decline vs. −24.6% in Q3** — needs to narrow materially or the stabilization thesis dies.
3. **Q4 gross margin vs. ~34% implied / 32.5% in Q3** — management set this bar themselves; missing it is a credibility event on input-cost management.
4. **September price-increase elasticity commentary** — first real-world read. Any mention of volume declines worse than 1:1 pressures all FY27 revenue models.
5. **Buyback activity and FY27 capital allocation** — with ~$158M remaining and a ~13% FCF yield, aggressive repurchase at $10 is the cheapest credibility management can buy.
6. **Secondary:** fresh impairment charge, OWYN distribution-loss quantification, any litigation disclosure in the 10-K, Quest innovation/GLP-1 positioning for the Atkins relaunch (marketplace testing was flagged for FY27).

## 7. How I'd Frame It

Risk/reward is skewed positive *on valuation* and negative *on catalyst*. A 5.2× EV/EBITDA multiple with a 13% FCF yield, 1.2× net leverage, and a founder-CEO buying back stock is not where durable businesses trade — but the market is explicitly refusing to underwrite FY27 until it sees a number. Long exposure into the print is a bet that Scalzo guides FY27 EBITDA to $210M+ and quantifies the Atkins floor. Given that management declined to guide in July and the bear tail is a 20%+ drawdown, **the better expression is defined-risk (call spreads or post-print entry) rather than outright long into a 10–13% implied move.** The asymmetry improves materially the morning after.
