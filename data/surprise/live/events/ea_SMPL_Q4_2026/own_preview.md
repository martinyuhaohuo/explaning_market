## Recent Context (Q3 FY2026, reported 2026-07-09)

- Net sales $357.0M, down from $381.0M (-6.3%), beat consensus of ~$343M.
- Adj. EPS $0.42 vs. $0.51 a year ago, beat Zacks consensus of $0.35.
- GAAP net loss of $52.0M, driven by an ~$82M goodwill/brand impairment.
- Adj. EBITDA $57.2M vs. $73.9M.
- Atkins down 24.6%; Quest and OWYN growing.
- The stock surged pre-market on the beat.

**Updated FY2026 guidance:**
- Net sales: $1.345B–$1.355B (-6% to -7% y/y), narrowed from $1.31B–$1.35B.
- Gross margin: down ~375 bps (prior: down 300–350 bps).
- Adj. EBITDA: $220M–$225M (-19% to -21% y/y).
- No full-year adj. EPS guidance was found.

## Key Metrics to Watch (Ranked)

1. **Net sales vs. $322M–$332M guide and ~$328M consensus.** Given the Q3 beat, a result at or above the top of the range would be a positive signal.
2. **Adj. EBITDA vs. $52M–$57M guide.** This is the best read on margin health after the gross margin compression.
3. **Gross margin trajectory.** Is the ~375 bps FY decline holding, and what is the Q4 exit rate?
4. **Atkins trend.** A continued ~25% decline is the main drag; watch for stabilization.
5. **FY2027 outlook.** Early guidance on sales, EBITDA, and EPS will likely drive the reaction more than Q4 itself.
6. **Impairment and cash flow.** Whether further impairments are flagged, and free cash flow and debt paydown.

## Scenarios

| Scenario | Revenue | EPS | Key Driver | Stock Reaction |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Bull | ≥$332M (top of guide) | ≥$0.45 | Quest/OWYN growth offsets Atkins; margins at or above guide; FY27 framed as stabilizing | Positive; the Q3 pattern suggests a beat-and-raise can move the stock sharply |
| Base | $322M–$332M | ~$0.40–$0.44 | In-range print; FY27 guidance roughly in line | Muted to modest move, direction set by FY27 commentary |

**Operational signals for each case:**
- **Bull:** management raises or narrows to the upper end of FY guidance, cites Quest/OWYN momentum, and shows gross margin recovery plans.
- **Base:** reaffirms the range, gives a cautious FY27 framework, and reiterates the protein and pricing strategy.
- **Bear:** pushes out margin recovery, signals more promotional spending, or flags further impairments.
