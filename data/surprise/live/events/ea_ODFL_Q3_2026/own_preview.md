## Key metrics to watch (ranked)

1. **Operating ratio.** Q2 was 70.1%, and management's target is sub-70%. A print at or below 70% supports the margin story; a miss would hurt.
2. **Tonnage per day.** Q2 was −4.1% y/y. Any sign of volume recovery matters because Q2 growth came mostly from pricing.
3. **Yield (revenue per hundredweight).** Q2 was +15.2%. Check whether pricing growth is holding or decelerating, since it is now the main driver.
4. **EPS vs. ~$1.60.** A beat is likely given Q2's pattern, but the stock reacted weakly to Q2's beat, so the beat alone may not move it.
5. **Capex and free cash flow.** One source says 2026 capex was raised to about $380M. Confirm this figure and its effect on FCF.
6. **Q4 / full-year commentary.** No formal quarterly guidance was found. Watch for any commentary on the Q4 outlook and the industrial cycle.
