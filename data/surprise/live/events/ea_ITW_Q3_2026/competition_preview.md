## 2. The Bar: What Guidance Actually Implies

This is the most important piece of arithmetic in the setup.

- FY26 guide (raised at Q2): **GAAP EPS $11.35–$11.55**, organic +3–4%, total revenue +4–5%, op margin 26.5–27.5%, FCF >100% of net income, ~$1.5B buyback, tax 23–24%.
- H1'26 actual EPS: $2.66 (Q1) + $2.84 (Q2) = **$5.50**. → H2 implied **$5.85–$6.05** (mid $5.95).
- Applying the FY25 H2 split (Q3 $2.81 / Q4 ~$2.70, i.e. 51/49) → **guidance-implied Q3 EPS ≈ $3.00–$3.03**.

**Consensus at $2.97–$2.99 sits at or marginally *below* the guidance-implied run-rate.** Same on the top line: management framed H2 organic at **~4.5%** (Q2's pace), which on the $4.06B comp plus ~1% FX gets you to **~$4.28B** — roughly $20M above consensus. Consensus embeds ~3.9% organic, leaving ~60 bps of cushion.

**Conclusion: the bar is modest and the skew is mildly to the upside on revenue and EPS.** ITW has beaten EPS consensus in **8 of the last 8** quarters. The risk in this print is not the quarter — it's the *margin comp* and the *2027 framing*.

## 3. Key Metrics to Watch — Ranked

**1. Operating margin vs. the 27.4% comp (highest importance).** Q3'25 was a record Q3 margin, so this is the hardest comp of the year. Two offsetting forces: enterprise initiatives contributed **120 bps** in both Q1 and Q2 (FY guide: >100 bps), against a **~40 bps price/cost timing drag** in Q2 from crude derivatives and resin. CFO Michael Larson explicitly said that drag should **moderate in Q3 and improve further in Q4**, and defended a **40% full-year incremental margin**. If Q3 incrementals come in below ~35%, the "self-help offsets the cycle" thesis — the entire reason the stock carries a 23.5x multiple — takes damage.

**2. Organic growth: ≥4.5% or not.** Management committed to holding Q2's pace through H2. Anything with a 3-handle reads as deceleration regardless of the EPS beat.

**3. Order rates in Welding and T&M/E.** On the Q2 call management said "order growth continues to outpace revenue growth" in both. This is the single best forward indicator in the release/call. If that language softens or disappears, it validates the JPMorgan short-cycle-plateau thesis.

**4. FY26 guidance action.** Narrowing to the **top half ($11.45–$11.55)** is the base case. A midpoint hold at $11.45 is a mild negative given FX has moved favorably; any trim would be a significant negative.

**5. Automotive OEM and Food Equipment inflections.** Auto was (0.4)% organic in Q2 (NA +1%, China +1%, Europe −5%) against a guide to outgrow builds (down ~2%) by 200–300 bps — consensus only asks for +0.5%. Food Equipment improved from −6% (Q1) to −2% (Q2) and was guided to turn **positive** in H2. Both are low-expectation call options.

**6. First 2027 framing.** No formal guide, but management will be pushed. Reference points they've already set: 40%+ incremental margins "in a normal environment" and a **30%+ operating margin by 2030**. Sell-side 2027 EPS sits around $12.37 (Zacks).

**What each requires operationally**

- **Bull:** needs margin, not revenue. Enterprise initiatives at 120+ bps *and* the 40 bps price/cost headwind flipping neutral or positive — that combination alone is worth ~$0.06–0.08 vs. consensus. Plus at least one of Food Equipment or Auto OEM inflecting positive.
- **Base:** essentially "Q2 again, minus the surprise." ITW delivers the beat it always delivers; the stock does nothing because the guidance raise is already in the $11.44 consensus.
- **Bear:** does **not** require an EPS miss. Q3'25 is the template — EPS beat ($2.81 vs. consensus), revenue missed, FY outlook tightened, and the stock fell **5.1%**.

## 5. Catalyst Checklist

1. ☐ **Organic growth ≥4.5%** (mgmt H2 commitment) vs. ~3.9% consensus-implied — the deceleration referendum.
2. ☐ **Op margin >27.4%** with price/cost drag <20 bps and incrementals ≥40% — the self-help referendum.
3. ☐ **FY26 EPS narrowed to $11.45–$11.55+**, not held at $11.45 — read the implied Q4 ($2.92–$3.00) carefully.
4. ☐ **"Orders outpacing revenue"** language retained for Welding and T&M/E; any quantification of AI-infrastructure / data-center / defense exposure.
5. ☐ **Auto OEM back to positive organic** and Food Equipment organic ≥0%, delivering on the H2 inflection promise.
6. ☐ **2027 framing** — willingness to defend 40% incremental margins and the 2030 30% margin path into a flatter PMI backdrop. Also watch for M&A ("active but disciplined"; a semiconductor-manufacturing bolt-on closed in Q2) and buyback pace against the new $6B authorization.
