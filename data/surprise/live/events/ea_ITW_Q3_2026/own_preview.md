## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Organic revenue growth vs. the 3–4% full-year guide.** Q2 printed +4.5%, so a sustained rate above the guide supports a raise. Watch price vs. volume split.
2. **Operating margin vs. the 26.5–27.5% FY range.** Enterprise Initiatives (ITW's operating system) should add more than 100 bps. Margin expansion is the main quality signal.
3. **EPS vs. ~$2.97.** A beat of a few cents is typical for ITW. Confirm whether the consensus includes the same tax and other items as the company's GAAP guide.
4. **FY2026 EPS guidance.** A raise above $11.55 (top of range) would be a clear positive. Holding it would read as neutral to negative after a Q2 raise.
5. **Segment performance.** Check the largest segments (Test & Measurement, Food Equipment, Welding, Construction Products) for organic growth and margin. I did not verify segment-level consensus.
6. **FCF and buybacks.** Q2 FCF conversion was strong. The company has targeted above 100% of net income and about $1.5B in buybacks for the year (per a third-party summary; verify against the release).

## Catalyst Checklist

1. **Organic growth vs. the 3–4% guide.** The market will focus on whether Q2's +4.5% was sustained. Stronger organic growth supports a multiple.
2. **Operating margin vs. the 26.5–27.5% range.** Enterprise Initiatives progress is the key quality metric.
3. **FY2026 EPS guidance vs. $11.35–11.55.** Buy-side expects a raise or a reaffirmation at the top of the range. A hold after a Q2 raise can disappoint.
4. **Capital allocation.** Buyback pace and FCF conversion. These are the usual levers if growth is flat.
5. **Narrative and macro.** Watch commentary on demand in short-cycle segments, tariffs, and pricing. Any strategic or portfolio change would be a new catalyst. I found none in the sources reviewed.
