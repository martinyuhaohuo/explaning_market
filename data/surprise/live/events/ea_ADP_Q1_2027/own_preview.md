## Prior Quarters and Guidance Context

- **Q4 FY2026:** Adj. EPS $2.64 vs. $2.62 consensus (small beat). Revenue slightly below consensus. Stock reaction was positive, but the sources describe it as in-line.
- **Q3 FY2026:** EPS $3.37 vs. $3.30 consensus. Revenue $5.94B, up 7.0% year over year. Shares rose about 8% on the day and drifted higher afterward. Management raised FY2026 guidance for the second straight quarter to 6–7% revenue growth and 10–11% EPS growth.
- **FY2027 guidance (issued with Q4):** 5–6% revenue growth and 9–11% adjusted EPS growth. The Street is reportedly already modeling Q1 above the top of the range, so the bar is higher than the guide implies.
- **Q1 FY2026 (year-ago base):** EPS $2.49, up 6.9%.

## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Adj. EPS vs. $2.77–2.78.** ADP has beaten EPS in recent quarters, but by small margins (about 1–2%). A beat of that size is largely priced in. Watch whether the beat is operating or driven by interest income and buybacks.
2. **Revenue growth vs. ~6–7%.** Check whether growth is in the upper half of the FY27 range. Also check the PEO/pays-per-control trends and client funds interest income, which can swing reported revenue.
3. **Pays per control (PPC) and employment trends.** These are ADP's leading indicators for US employment and drive the core Employer Services business. A slowdown would be a bigger issue than a small EPS miss.
4. **Client funds interest income and float.** Rate sensitivity affects revenue and margin. Management's commentary on the rate path matters.
5. **Full-year guidance.** The key question is whether management raises FY27 EPS growth above 9–11%. A raise is the main upside catalyst; a reaffirmation at the current range is likely to be read as neutral to slightly negative given the elevated expectations.

- **Bear:** Citing softer small-business hiring, a PEO slowdown, or pressure on float income, or narrowing the guide to the low end.

## Catalyst Checklist

1. **EPS vs. $2.77–2.78 whisper.** Whisper is likely above consensus given the Street's view that Q1 will exceed the top of the FY27 guide. A small beat may not be enough.
2. **FY27 guidance revision.** The buy-side expects at least a reaffirmation, with a raise as the upside case. Any cut would be the largest negative.
3. **Pays per control and employment commentary.** This is the read-through for the broader labor market and the core business.
4. **Client funds interest income and float trends.** Sensitive to rates, and often a source of revenue surprises.
5. **Capital return.** Buyback pace and dividend commentary, which support the stock when growth is steady.
