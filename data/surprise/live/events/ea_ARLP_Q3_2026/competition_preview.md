## 3. Key metrics to watch — ranked

1. **Oil & Gas Royalties segment revenue and realized $/BOE.** This is the entire variance story. Q2 set a record at $46.3M; a full AllDale quarter into a $100+ Brent tape should produce something in the $78–92M range. Anything under ~$70M segment EBITDA means either volumes disappointed or the Haynesville gas weighting diluted realizations more than expected.
2. **Coal segment EBITDA expense per ton.** Q2's $38.68 (−6.3% YoY) was the bull-case proof point. Hamilton ramping from a mid-May restart is the execution risk — new-mine startups usually run hot on cost before they run cheap. Watch whether Appalachia holds near $46/ton after the Metiki longwall cessation (which drove a $37.8M Q1 impairment).
3. **Tons sold vs. the ~9M guide, and FY26 guidance narrowing.** With 34.3M tons already committed and priced, a volume miss is a logistics/production problem, not a demand problem — it defers rather than destroys revenue.
4. **2027 commitments.** The 29.4M-ton 2027 book (26.7M domestic / 2.7M export) needs to grow toward the 30.5–31M implied by Craft's "1.0–1.3M ton increase in 2027." New domestic tonnage *priced above* the current ~$55 realization is the single most important forward datapoint for the multiple.
5. **Distribution and coverage.** Q2 coverage was 1.39x on $108.2M DCF. The base case implies ~$145–155M DCF and ~1.9–2.0x coverage. Six quarters at $0.60 plus that coverage makes a raise, a buyback authorization, or term-loan paydown a live decision.

## 5. Catalyst checklist

1. **O&G royalty revenue vs. the ~$46M run-rate** — the whisper is well above published consensus; a sub-$70M segment EBITDA print is the most likely source of a negative surprise even on a headline beat.
2. **Coal expense per ton inside the $37–39 guide** — proves the Hamilton restart is accretive rather than dilutive in its first full quarter.
3. **Distribution decision** — $0.60 hold is base case; a raise re-rates the yield story, but Craft has repeatedly prioritized balance-sheet flexibility, debt paydown ($590.2M total debt, 0.67x net leverage) and royalty M&A over distribution growth.
4. **2027 contracted tonnage and pricing** — the bridge from "9% yield" to "growth story" runs entirely through data-center-driven PJM demand converting into priced tons.
5. **Capital allocation narrative** — cumulative O&G investment has passed $1.0B; another royalty acquisition announced alongside the print would confirm the pivot and pressure the coal-multiple framing.

**Noise, not signal:** the ~+$16M bitcoin mark (646 BTC, $37.9M at Jun 30; BTC +43.5% in Q3 to ~$84,000) and any further Metiki-related charges. Also note the July 1 Saminco Solutions master supply agreement via Matrix Design Africa is a **related-party transaction** with CEO Joseph Craft, approved by the independent conflicts committee — governance-sensitive holders may probe it on the call.

## 7. Risks to this preview

- **Estimate quality is poor.** Coverage is thin (2–6 contributors) and the published mean predates both the oil move and a full AllDale quarter. Treat the $0.82–0.85 consensus as a weak anchor; the Noble ($0.98) and Sidoti (FY $3.00) revisions are better-informed.
- **The $/BOE assumption is the softest input.** AllDale III/IV adds Haynesville gas alongside Permian/Anadarko/Bakken oil, so blended realizations may lag WTI's +32% move more than modeled. No disclosed commodity mix for the acquired acreage was found in the reviewed sources.
- **Coal price is still rolling down.** Realized price fell 5.3% YoY in Q2 as legacy contracts expire. The volume and cost story is doing the work; a stable-price quarter is the guide, not an upside lever.
- **Q3 FY2025 per-ton realization is derived, not disclosed** in the reviewed sources — the YoY price trend is described qualitatively rather than quoted as a precise comparable.
- **Oil at ~$100–103 (Oct 23) is geopolitically driven** (Middle East supply disruption). The royalty tailwind is real for Q3 and Q4 but should not be capitalized into the multiple.
