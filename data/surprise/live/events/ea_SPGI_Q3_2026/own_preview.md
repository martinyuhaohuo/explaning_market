## Structural caveat: Mobility spin-off

Mobility Global was spun off on July 1, 2026 and is treated as discontinued operations. Q2 "pro forma" figures exclude it. Management says the new adjusted guidance is **not directly comparable** to prior guidance, which assumed a full-year Mobility contribution. Confirm that the Q3 consensus is on the same ex-Mobility basis before comparing the numbers. Mixed bases are the most likely source of a false "beat" or "miss."

## Recent results and guidance

- **Q2 2026 (reported 7/28):** Pro forma revenue $3.678B (+11%); adjusted EPS $4.83 (+23%), vs. $4.81 consensus. GAAP EPS $4.12.
- **Q2 segments (reported, y/y):** Ratings +17% ($1,339M); Indices +20% ($534M); Market Intelligence +6% ($1,290M); Energy +2% ($568M).
- **FY2026 guidance (new):**
  - Adjusted revenue growth 5.9%–7.9%; organic constant-currency 6.0%–8.0%
  - Adjusted EPS $17.50–$17.75
  - GAAP diluted EPS $16.35–$16.60
  - Adjusted operating margin expansion 35–60 bps
  - Tax rate 22%–23%; capex $190M–$210M
- **Discrepancy:** One source said adjusted EPS guidance was raised. The release gives no prior numeric range to compare, so I could not confirm a raise. Other reporting says Ratings and Indices revenue guidance was raised. Treat "EPS guidance raised" as unverified.
- **Analyst reaction after Q2:** Barclays cut its target to $553 and kept Overweight. It noted guidance sits below some analysts' full-year EPS estimates (about $18.37). Benzinga reported the stock fell despite the beat.

## Key metrics to watch (ranked)

1. **Ratings revenue and issuance mix.** Q2 was +17%, the main growth engine. Watch whether the rate of growth holds against the raised 5%–8% full-year guide. Watch for a slowdown in investment-grade and high-yield issuance.
2. **Adjusted EPS vs. ~$4.43 on a like-for-like basis.** Confirm the basis first. A clean beat would be above ~$4.45. A reported figure above $4.83 would signal the Q2 run-rate persisted.
3. **Margin trajectory.** FY guide implies +35–60 bps adjusted margin expansion. Watch for a quarterly margin print that tracks that path, given cost and Mobility stranded-cost questions.
4. **Market Intelligence and Energy.** Both were slow in Q2 (+6% and +2–3%). Any improvement here would help the narrative. Further weakness would reinforce the "diversified growth is lagging" argument.
5. **FY guidance change.** Reaffirm, raise, or cut the $17.50–$17.75 adjusted EPS range. Because the range is non-comparable, the market will read it against consensus (~$18.37–$18.98 cited in sources, but those may be on the old basis). Any gap to consensus is likely to drive the reaction.
