## 2. Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Gross margin — the whole thesis.** Q2 printed a record 42.6% (from 41.0%), but the U.S. core still carried ~100bp of tariff drag (improved from ~150bp in Q1). Management said it would "recover that 1%" as high-tariff inventory sells through. **Watch for GM ≥41% in Q3** (vs. 39.1% LY). Anything under 40% says the tariff recovery has stalled and freight/FX are winning.
2. **SG&A as % of sales.** Jumped to ~32% in Q2 from 29% on My Medic's advertising load. Q3 2025 ran 33.0%. If SG&A dollars grow faster than the ~17% sales growth, the margin story is being spent away — this is exactly what broke Q1 (EPS −41% on +14% sales).
3. **Westcott cutting tools / back-to-school.** Q3 is the sell-in quarter. Q3 2025 was damaged by **customer order cancellations** on tariff uncertainty (U.S. +1% total); Q2 2026 saw Westcott +8% on volume with retailers restoring promotions. **This is the easiest comp in the P&L** and the single biggest organic swing factor.
4. **My Medic: revenue and whether it crosses into profit.** ~$4.3M at break-even in Q2; ~$19M annual run-rate with **~35% of sales in Q4**. Management guided operating leverage to appear "in Q4 and into Q1," so Q3 should still be thin — a Q3 operating loss is not a thesis-breaker, but a *lowered* Q4 framing is.
5. **Organic (ex-My Medic) growth.** +6% in Q1, +8% in Q2. Sub-5% would mean the acquisition is masking a decelerating core.
6. **Balance sheet / dry powder.** Bank debt less cash was $27.3M at 6/30 (from $22.8M LY) after ~$14.5M for My Medic and ~$1.6M for a German product line. New **$65M facility (HSBC/City National, matures Jul 2029)** leaves room for another deal. TTM FCF $15.5M.

## 4. Catalyst Checklist

1. **Gross margin ≥41%** vs. 39.1% LY — proof the tariff drag is genuinely transitory rather than structural. *This is the print's whisper number.*
2. **Revenue ≥$57M** (≈+16%) — needed to keep FY26 tracking to the mid/upper half of $220–230M. 9M would be ~$172M, leaving Q4 at $48–58M (Q4 2025 was ~$47.6M, and My Medic adds ~$7M in Q4 that wasn't in the base). Below $55M and the $230M end comes off the table.
3. **My Medic Q4 framing** — management has pre-sold Q4 as the leverage quarter (35% of My Medic's annual sales, Black Friday + hunting season, 500k social following). Any deferral of that is the most likely source of a de-rating.
4. **Med-Nap hospital certification** — guided "complete by year-end 2026." Completion unlocks the U.S. hospital channel and is the cleanest new-TAM headline available on this call.
5. **Narrative/strategic:** progress toward the **$300M/3-year** target, capacity under the new $65M facility for the next acquisition, Spill Magic's Tennessee expansion (+30% YTD), and the next-gen smart compliance software rollout.
