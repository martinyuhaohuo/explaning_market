**Flag:** The Q2 EPS beat (~3x consensus) is large relative to the 8% organic sales growth. Check whether it includes one-time items or acquisition accounting. That determines whether Q3's $0.67 bar is a realistic base.

## Management Commentary (Q2 Call, Qualitative Only)

- Gross margin expected to expand through the rest of 2026 as higher-cost tariff inventory rolls off. Tariff pressure should ease over coming quarters.

- No numeric full-year guidance was found.

## Key Metrics to Watch (Ranked)

1. **Gross margin vs. Q2's 42.6%.** Tests the tariff-recovery thesis. Compression would be the main negative signal.
2. **Revenue vs. ~$56.5M, with organic growth.** Separate My Medic contribution from core growth. Core growth was 8% in Q2.
3. **EPS vs. ~$0.67, and the quality of the beat or miss.** Look for one-time items and tax effects.
4. **Q4 and FY2026 commentary.** Any update to My Medic leverage timing, tariff outlook, and the margin-recovery cadence.
5. **Net debt and acquisition pipeline.** Net debt rising with M&A could draw questions about capital allocation.
