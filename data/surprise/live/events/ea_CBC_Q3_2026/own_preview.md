## Recent results and guidance

- **Q2 2026 (reported Aug 4):** Net income $113.8M. Diluted EPS $0.47, up from $0.41 a year ago. Net interest income $212.8M (+9.1% YoY). NIM 4.40% (4.43% FTE), up from 4.26% a year ago. Provision $3.5M. Net charge-offs 0.10% of average loans. Efficiency ratio 46.5%. Average deposits $15.4B, down 1.1% QoQ. Cost of deposits 1.10%. Consolidated CET1 28.6%.
- **Q1 2026:** EPS $0.46 beat about $0.44.
- **Guidance:** No numerical guidance. Management expects "steady growth heading into the second half." The Q2 call summary (single third-party source, unverified) said NIM could improve gradually from back-book repricing, with intense loan-pricing competition.
- **Capital:** $100M buyback authorized Aug 3, 2026. About $7.6M repurchased in Q2. Quarterly dividend $0.12. Roughly $1.9B of excess capital above the 13.5% target, so buybacks are a live lever.

## Key metrics to watch (ranked)

1. **NIM vs. 4.40% in Q2.** The main driver of the NII beat or miss. Watch deposit costs (1.10%) and securities repositioning yield (~4.5%).
2. **Revenue vs. ~$288–292M.** Check whether NII growth is offset by fee income.
3. **EPS vs. $0.49 and the $0.51 top estimate.** A beat above $0.51 would be a clear positive surprise.
4. **Deposit trend.** Deposits fell 1.1% QoQ. Any further decline would be a negative signal.
5. **Credit.** Provision (Q2: $3.5M) and net charge-offs (0.10%). Any step-up matters.
6. **Buyback pace and any change to the $100M authorization.** Capital return is a potential upside surprise.
7. **Efficiency ratio (46.5%).** Branch openings (three opened in Q2) add cost.

## Catalyst checklist

1. **NIM vs. 4.40% (Q2).** The market will judge the NII line against the margin trajectory, so a flat or lower NIM is the main risk.
2. **EPS vs. $0.49 consensus, $0.51 top estimate.** Q2 was a slight miss ($0.47 vs. $0.48), so the bar is not high. A beat would be the cleanest positive.
3. **Second-half outlook.** Buy-side will press for specifics behind "steady growth." Expect a demand for quantified NIM and loan-growth commentary.
4. **Capital return.** Pace of buybacks against the $100M authorization and about $1.9B of excess capital. A larger program would be a clear positive surprise.
5. **Deposits and funding.** A second quarter of deposit decline would test the growth narrative.
