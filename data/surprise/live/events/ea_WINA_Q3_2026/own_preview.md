## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Royalty revenue growth.** Core earnings driver. Q2 was about +8%. Any slowdown below high single digits would be a negative signal.
2. **EPS vs. $3.23.** Three misses in a row. A fourth would challenge the consensus model, which is likely built on thin coverage.
3. **SG&A and operating margin.** Q2 SG&A grew faster than revenue. Watch whether the advertising fund and technology spend is still front-loaded or is now recurring.
4. **Franchise count and openings.** The pipeline (87 awarded) is the leading indicator for future royalties.
5. **Cash deployment and capital return.** Cash rose sharply from $10.3M at the end of FY25 to $26.0M in Q2. Buyback or dividend commentary matters for the stock.

1. **EPS vs. $3.23 (and whisper).** Three straight misses set a low bar, but a fourth miss would test credibility.
2. **Royalty growth vs. Q2's ~8%.** This is the cleanest read on the franchise model. Whether it holds is the key question.
3. **SG&A trajectory.** Whether the Q2 increase was one-time or continues shapes the margin story.
4. **2027 advertising fund campaigns (Once Upon A Child, Play It Again Sports).** Management already flagged these. Watch for budget size and whether they add cost.
5. **Capital allocation.** Cash of $26.0M and a stated "funded through 2027" position open the door to buybacks or a dividend change. Any announcement would be a catalyst.
