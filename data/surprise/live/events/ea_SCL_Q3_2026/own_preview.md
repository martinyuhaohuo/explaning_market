## Prior Quarter Context (Q2 2026, reported July 29)

- Adjusted net income $27.1M; reported net income $22.9M.
- Adjusted EBITDA $74.4M, up 45% year over year. EBITDA $69.1M, up 37%.
- Organic sales volume up 6%. Global volume up 3%.
- Surfactants adjusted EBITDA up 59%; Polymers up 22%.
- Free cash flow was negative $15.0M on higher working capital.
- A $5.1M restructuring charge related to a plant closure.
- Management credited Project Catalyst savings and margin recovery, but also said customer **pre-buys** tied to the geopolitical situation flattered the quarter.
- EPS was reported as $1.18 against a $0.61 consensus ([Chartmill](https://www.chartmill.com/news/SCL/Chartmill-52015-Stepan-NYSESCL-Jumps-After-Crushing-Q2-Estimates-with-Surfactant-and-Polymer-Demand)). The adjusted figure implies roughly $1.2 per share, which is consistent with that number.

## Key Metrics to Watch (Ranked)

1. **Surfactants volume and margin.** Is the Q2 volume and margin recovery sustained after pre-buys? Check for any Q3 air-pocket commentary.
2. **Gross-to-EBITDA margin trajectory.** Q2 showed strong expansion. Expect the question to be whether Project Catalyst savings are holding.
3. **Working capital and free cash flow.** Q2 FCF was negative. A second negative quarter would weigh on the stock.
4. **Polymers volume (+5% in Q2)** and pricing versus raw material pass-through.
5. **Q4 and full-year commentary.** Any reiteration of the outlook, plus pre-buy reversal risk for Q4.
6. **Restructuring and plant closure costs.** Watch whether further charges are flagged.
