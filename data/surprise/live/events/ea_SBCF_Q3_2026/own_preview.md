## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Net interest margin.** Q2 reported 3.83%, with the core margin ex-accretion at 3.65%. Watch whether core margin keeps expanding.
2. **Acquired-loan accretion.** Fell to $8.9M in Q2 from $12.1M in Q1. A further decline would pressure reported NII and makes the core-margin trend more important.
3. **Organic loan growth.** Q2 was 16% annualized. Management's FY guide calls for high single-digit organic loan growth, so a strong Q3 would imply upside to that guide.
4. **Deposit costs and mix.** Cost of deposits was 1.53% in Q2 and falling. Watch whether deposit growth (low-to-mid single digits guided) holds.
5. **Credit quality.** Not researched. Check NCOs, non-performing loans, and provisions in the release.
6. **Fee income and expense run-rate.** Not researched.
