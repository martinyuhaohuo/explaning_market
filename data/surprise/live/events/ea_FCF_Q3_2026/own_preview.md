## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **NIM vs. the low-4% guide.** A print at or above 4.0% supports the 2H outlook. Watch whether management still expects the margin to hold, given deposit pricing.
2. **EPS vs. $0.46 consensus.** Consensus has been beaten in recent quarters, so a modest beat is the baseline expectation.
3. **Net interest income and revenue vs. ~$142M.** Revenue drives the top line; loan growth is the main driver.
4. **Noninterest expense vs. $74–76M guide.** Staying in range protects the efficiency story.
5. **Fee income vs. $24–25M guide.**
6. **Credit quality:** Provision, net charge-offs, and nonperforming loans. Not covered in the sources I found, so check the release directly.
7. **Deposit costs and beta:** The main risk to the margin outlook.
8. **Loan growth:** Whether payoffs moderate as expected and growth approaches the mid-single-digit target.

1. **NIM (4.01% last quarter) vs. the low-4% 2H guide.** The buy-side expects confirmation that the margin is holding despite deposit competition.

4. **Loan growth trajectory** toward the mid-single-digit target, and whether payoffs are moderating.
