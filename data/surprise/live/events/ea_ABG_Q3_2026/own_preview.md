## Prior Quarter (Q2 FY2026, reported 2026-07-28)

Source: [BusinessWire release](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260728173168/en/Asbury-Automotive-Group-Reports-Second-Quarter-Results) and [247 Wall St summary](https://247wallst.com/cards/asbury-automotive-group-q2-2026-earnings-abg-01kym6hv4d54wvpf5gymy7m1xj)

- **GAAP:** Net income $115M, $6.25 per diluted share, down 25% year over year. Revenue $4.4B.
- **Adjusted:** $6.82 per share, down 15% year over year, versus about $6.31 expected. Revenue was $4.39B against $4.49B expected, a miss.
- **Gross profit:** $753M. Used retail gross profit per unit was $2,002, up 16%.
- **Non-cash headwind:** A Total Care Auto deferral cost $0.66 per share. Excluding it, adjusted EPS would have been $7.48.
- **Buybacks:** About 668K shares for $131M.
- **Tekion DMS:** 70% of stores converted as of July 28, with full rollout planned for this fall. This is a key operational risk during the print.
- **Guidance (per a Quartr summary, not verified in the release):** SG&A as a percentage of gross profit to fall toward the low 60s by end of 2027, 2026 CapEx of about $250M, and an effective tax rate near 25% for the rest of 2026. I found no explicit full-year EPS or revenue guidance.

## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Adjusted EPS vs. ~$6.70–7.00.** Check whether the TCA deferral is recurring and how much of it reverses.
2. **Revenue vs. ~$4.55–4.60B.** Q2 missed, and estimates have been cut. A second miss would be harder to dismiss.
3. **Used retail GPU and new vehicle GPU.** The Q2 used GPU gain is the main margin support.
4. **SG&A as a percentage of gross profit.** This is the stated path to the low 60s and the core of the cost story.
5. **Tekion conversion status and any disruption.** Watch for the fall timeline and any cost or sales disruption.
6. **Buyback pace and FY2026 commentary.** Q2 repurchases were about $131M per quarter.

**What would signal each case in commentary:**
- *Bull:* Management reaffirms or raises the SG&A path and says Tekion is on schedule.
- *Base:* Guidance is maintained with no new specifics.
- *Bear:* Lower outlook on GPUs, a delay in Tekion, or a weaker SG&A plan.

## Catalyst Checklist

1. **EPS vs. ~$6.70–7.00:** The TCA deferral makes the reported number noisy. Track the ex-deferral figure, which was $7.48 in Q2.
2. **Revenue vs. ~$4.55–4.60B:** Estimates were cut, so a Q2-style miss would stand out.
3. **FY2026 commentary and SG&A path:** Buy-side will want confirmation of the low-60s target and any change to it.
4. **Tekion rollout:** Completion this fall is a key execution item.
5. **Capital allocation:** Buyback pace and capex (~$250M for 2026).
