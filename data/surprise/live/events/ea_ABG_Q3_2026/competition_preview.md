## 1. The Setup in One Paragraph

ABG goes into this print as one of the most beaten-up names in dealer retail: **‑27.9% YTD** vs. the S&P +15%, a fresh **52-week low of $164.29 on Oct 12**, and trading ~35% below its $258.75 high. A wave of early-October target cuts (Morgan Stanley $230→$184 EW, UBS $250→$193 Neutral, JPMorgan $190→$185 UW) reset the bar — UBS explicitly flagged its **Q3 EPS ~7% below consensus** on slower parts & service, higher SG&A, higher gross costs, and rising floorplan interest. Consensus has come down **~6% in 30 days**, and Zacks carries the stock at Rank #5 with a 0% Earnings ESP. The question is no longer whether numbers are falling; it's whether the **Tekion DMS conversion completes in October as promised** and whether the **used-vehicle volume inflection** management teed up for Q4 is visible in Q3 data.

## 3. The Comp Is Distorted — Read Through It

Three mechanical distortions in the YoY optics. Getting these wrong is the easiest way to misjudge the print:

1. **Herb Chambers lapping is a *tailwind*, not a headwind.** The $1.76B / 33-dealership acquisition **closed July 21, 2025** — Q3 2025 captured only ~72 of 92 days. Q3 2026 has the full quarter. That's roughly **+$150–175M of inorganic revenue** YoY. So consensus's ‑7% headline embeds an **organic decline steeper than it appears**.
2. **2026 divestitures cut the other way.** ABG has disposed of **10 dealerships and 1 collision center across three markets in 2026**, most recently Kia of Owings Mills (closed Oct 19, 2026). Portfolio pruning is a real revenue drag and should be explicitly bridged.
3. **Q3 2025 was an EV-credit pull-forward quarter.** The $7,500 federal credit expired Sept 30, 2025. **BEV sales are down 29.2% YoY through September 2026** (6.2% share). Q3 2025 revenue grew 13% to an all-time record against this; Q3 2026 laps it. Industry Q3 2026 new units were roughly flat (‑0.5%, ~16.1M SAAR), so **the entire ABG revenue decline is company/mix-specific, not market-driven.**

**Net read:** a ‑7% headline revenue print is *not* informative on its own. The number that matters is **same-store revenue** — ‑9% in Q1, ‑7% in Q2. Sequential improvement to **‑4% or better** is the bullish tell; ‑8% or worse confirms the bear case.

## 6. Catalyst Checklist — The 5 Things That Decide the Reaction

1. **Adj. SG&A as % of gross profit vs. ~66%.** The sole bridge to the "low 60s by YE2027" promise that underpins every bull case. Q3 is the stress test because it's the peak Tekion conversion quarter. *This is the number to read first.*
2. **Same-store revenue vs. ‑7% last quarter.** LAD beat EPS by $0.66 on Oct 21, gapped up to $317.95, and **still finished lower** — because same-store revenue fell 1.4% and F&I fell 8%. The market is trading dealers on organic quality, not headline EPS. ABG's same-store line is far worse than LAD's; it needs to be *improving*.
3. **Tekion: "complete" or "substantially complete."** Management committed to **October completion** at 70% done. This date has already been pushed from earlier framing ("fall 2026"). A miss here breaks management credibility on the one self-help lever they control, and it's the lever the $214–230 sell-side targets are built on.
4. **Used-unit inflection vs. the GPU trade-off.** Mgmt flagged margin give-back as volumes scale. Watch the *combination*: units up with GPU above ~$1,900 is clean; units up with GPU below $1,850 means they're buying share. Units still falling means the strategy hasn't started.
5. **Quality of earnings — the TCA deferral.** Q2's adjusted EPS was flattered by **$0.66/share** of non-cash deferral that management said would reverse. If Q3 EPS "beats" *because* of another positive deferral while same-store and SG&A deteriorate, expect the beat to be faded hard. Conversely, a reported miss driven *entirely* by a negative deferral swing is a buyable dislocation.

**Secondary:** capital allocation tone at a 6x multiple (buyback acceleration vs. deleveraging to 3.0x), further portfolio pruning after the 10-store 2026 disposition program, and floorplan interest expense.
