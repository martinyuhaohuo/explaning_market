## Recent Context

- **Q2 2026 missed.** Adjusted EPS was $4.68 against consensus of about $5.61 (Benzinga had $5.66). Adjusted figures exclude a $154.1M gain on vessel sales.
- **Q2 realized TCE:** LR2 $77,749/day, MR $52,027/day, Handymax $49,210/day.
- **Q3 booked TCE as of Jul 28:** LR2 $58,959 (34% booked), MR $47,327 (46%), Handymax $20,800 (38%). These are stale. Most of Q3 has already happened, so the release will be read mainly on realized Q3 rates and Q4 booking.
- **Balance sheet:** net cash $1,307M, gross debt $1,839M. Cash breakeven fell below $11,000/day, a company record.
- **Guidance:** the Q2 release gave no quantitative Q3 or full-year outlook. Management pointed to restocking, refinery dislocation, and new trade routes as rate drivers. Fleet growth is projected at 3–4% a year.
- **Dividend:** $0.45 per share, declared Jul 29.

## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Realized Q3 TCE by class (LR2, MR, Handymax) vs. the booked rates from July.** This shows whether the tanker market held up after late July.
2. **Q4 booked TCE and days booked.** This is the forward signal the buy-side will weigh most.
3. **Adjusted EPS vs. ~$2.06.** Watch for a second straight miss, and whether the headline includes another vessel-sale gain.
4. **Management commentary on rate outlook, restocking, and ton-miles.** Look for specific language, since the company gave no numeric outlook last quarter.
5. **Dividend policy and capital allocation,** including fleet renewal and buybacks. Net cash is large, so capital return is a live question.
