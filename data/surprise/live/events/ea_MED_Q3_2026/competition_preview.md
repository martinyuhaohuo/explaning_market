## 2. What to Watch — Ranked

1. **Catalyst savings quantification.** Management explicitly deferred the dollar target to Q3 results. Prior identified savings: ~$30M. A credible incremental number ($15–25M+, with timing) is the single largest swing factor, because it is what underwrites Q4 profitability on a declining revenue base. Silence or vagueness here is the bear case regardless of the EPS print.
2. **Active earning coach count — sequential rate of change.** 19,500 → 14,000 (Q1) → 11,700 (Q2); Q1→Q2 was −16.4% q/q. Deceleration to **<8% q/q (~10,800+)** means the base is stabilizing; another −15% (~9,900) means the productivity story is just a shrinking-denominator artifact.
3. **Revenue per coach.** $6,529 in Q2 (+41% y/y, +20% q/q, highest since Q2'22), three straight up quarters. Management calls it a leading indicator. A fourth increase to ~$6,800–7,000 with coach decline slowing is the bull confirmation; a *flat-to-down* print breaks the thesis.
4. **Gross margin and SG&A ex-items.** Consensus −$0.35 backs into GP ~$50M (69.5%) and SG&A ~$55M. Trilivy launch spend cost 60bp in Q2. SG&A under $54M would show Catalyst landing early; over $57M means launch costs are swamping the cuts.
5. **Cash and Catalyst cash cost.** $169.8M, zero debt at Q2 — essentially flat across the year ($173.5M at Q3'25, $168.9M at Q1'26). That's **~$16.4/share against an $11.72 stock (0.71x net cash)**. The whole value case rests on that stability; facility rationalization has cash costs. Cash holding ≥$160M is the floor-defining datapoint.
6. **Trilivy traction, and any GLP-1 adjacency.** First full quarter post-rebrand. Watch for new-client acquisition data (not just coach productivity) and whether Trilivy is monetizing GLP-1 users rather than losing to them.

| **Bear** (25%) | $64–68M | $(0.60) – $(0.80) | <10,000 / flat-to-down | Coach decline re-accelerates; Trilivy transition disrupts the field; large unquantified Catalyst charges; Q4 profitability language softened to "progress toward" | **−15% to −25%** |

**Operationally, what each requires.** Bull needs the *denominator* to stop shrinking — productivity gains alone are no longer a surprise. Base is simply the guide midpoint plus the savings number, where the stock trades on the Q4 bridge, not the quarter. Bear needs either a coach-count re-acceleration or a Catalyst charge large enough to reset the cash-value floor.

**Management tells.** Bull: a hard dollar-and-date Catalyst figure plus Q4 described as "profitable." Bear: "progress toward profitability," Catalyst savings described as "annualized run-rate exiting 2027," or a refusal to discuss coach count in favor of productivity.

## 5. Catalyst Checklist

1. **Catalyst program savings target ($ and timing) vs. the ~$30M already identified** — the quarter's single biggest swing factor; it is the bridge to Q4 profitability on a falling revenue base.
2. **Sequential coach-count delta vs. −16.4% in Q2** — the market needs the denominator to stabilize, not just productivity to rise.

4. **Q4 profitability language** — "profitable in Q4" vs. "progress toward profitability," plus a narrowed FY revenue/EPS range (the current −$0.25 upper bound is not achievable).
5. **Cash balance and Catalyst cash costs vs. $169.8M** — defines whether the 0.71x net-cash floor holds; also watch for any capital-return or strategic-review commentary given the sub-cash valuation.
