## 1. The setup in one paragraph

This is not really a Q4 print — it's an **FY2027 guidance event**. The September quarter is heavily de-risked: management guided Q4 to ~$1.55 adjusted EPS and 9–10% organic growth, consensus sits $0.03 above at $1.58, and JCI has beaten consensus in **8 of its last 8 quarters** with a *widening* beat versus its own guide (Q1: guided $0.83, printed $0.89; Q3: guided $1.28, printed $1.42). A Q4 beat is close to the base case. What is genuinely unknown is the FY27 framework management promised to deliver on this call — consensus is **$6.13 (+20.7%)** and parts of the buy side are underwriting double-digit organic growth off a record $21.0B backlog. The stock has already paid for a good outcome: +31.8% over 52 weeks versus +14.4% for the S&P 500 and +6.8% for XLI.

## 4. Key metrics to watch, ranked

1. **FY2027 adjusted EPS and organic growth guidance.** Consensus $6.13. Management explicitly deferred FY27 to this call. The bull case needs double-digit organic; a high-single-digit organic guide with ~$6.00–6.10 EPS is a de-rating risk given the multiple.
2. **Backlog level and sequential build.** $21.0B at Q3. Note the Q4 comp is unusually easy ($14.9B, depressed by the R&LC HVAC divestiture to Bosch), so the *headline y/y %* will look spectacular and is close to meaningless. Watch the **sequential dollar change** and the **book-to-bill**, which is the clean read.
3. **Americas segment EBITA margin.** 21.1% in Q3 (+260 bps). Americas is where data center work lands; the live debate is whether large hyperscaler projects carry above- or below-fleet margins. Americas margin decelerating while Americas revenue accelerates would validate the bear thesis that JCI is buying growth with mix.
4. **Data center disclosure.** High-teens % of FY26 revenue, targeted at ~one-third within 3–5 years. Specifics to listen for: CDU shipments (began in Q4, >$1B pipeline, NVIDIA-certified), the Oct 21 **Armada** collaboration to deploy 500 MW of prefabricated AI capacity on NVIDIA's DSX POD design by 2027, and how much hyperscaler commitment sits *outside* reported backlog.
5. **Service growth and the fire & security drag.** Service grew 7% in Q3 against a "mid-to-high single digit" target; fire & security was flat. Service is the multiple-supporting recurring line, so re-acceleration matters more than the headline beat. Also: FY26 adjusted FCF conversion landing at ~100%, and buyback pace with net debt at 1.9x.

## 5. Scenario analysis

| Scenario | Revenue | Adj. EPS | FY27 guide | Key driver | Stock reaction |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Bull** (~25%) | $7.20B+ (organic +11%+) | **$1.70+** | **≥$6.30**, double-digit organic | Backlog $23B+, book-to-bill >1.2x, Americas margin >22%, CDU ramp quantified, data-center mix guided above high-teens | **+7% to +12%** |
| **Base** (~50%) | $7.05–7.15B (organic +9–10%) | **$1.62–1.67** | **$6.10–6.20**, organic +9–10% | In-line-to-better Q4, backlog $21.5–22.0B, segment EBITA ~21%, service to ~8% | **−2% to +3%** |
| **Bear** (~25%) | $6.85–6.95B (organic +7–8%) | **$1.52–1.57** | **$5.90–6.00** or a wide/vague range | Order growth drops below ~15%, backlog flat sequentially, Americas margin flat q/q, supply-chain or project-timing slippage cited, FCF conversion misses 100% | **−7% to −12%** |

**What each would require operationally.** *Bull* needs Q4 orders to hold ≥20% organic — meaning the data center pipeline is converting faster than the backlog is burning — plus explicit evidence that incremental data-center revenue converts at or above the 45–50% leverage framework. *Base* is simply the guide plus the usual sandbagging, with FY27 set conservatively enough to beat-and-raise through the year (management's established pattern). *Bear* does not require a Q4 miss at all — it only requires a cautious FY27 number. Watch for hedging language like "lumpy," "project timing," "second-half weighted," or any qualifier on the 30%+ long-term operating leverage algorithm; that is how this print turns negative on a beat.

**Historical calibration.** Reactions have been inconsistent and not well correlated with the beat magnitude: Q1 FY26 **+6.0%** on a beat-and-raise; Q2 FY26 **−3.4%** pre-market *despite* beating ($1.19 vs $1.11); Q3 FY26 only **+1.4%** pre-market on a $0.12 beat and a raise. The Q2 and Q3 reactions are the warning — with the stock up 32% in a year and trading at ~24x FY27 consensus, incremental beats are increasingly priced in.

## 6. Catalyst checklist

- [ ] **FY27 adjusted EPS guide vs. $6.13 consensus** — the whole print. Below $6.05 is a problem; $6.25+ with double-digit organic is the bull trigger.
- [ ] **Q4 adjusted EPS vs. ~$1.63–1.67 effective bar** (not the $1.58 printed consensus) — an in-line $1.58 reads as a miss to positioning.
- [ ] **Sequential backlog change and book-to-bill** — ignore the flattered y/y comp; $22B+ sequentially is the bullish mark, flat at ~$21B is the bearish one.
- [ ] **Americas segment EBITA margin vs. 21.1%** — tests whether data-center mix is accretive or dilutive to the margin story.
- [ ] **Narrative/strategic** — CDU revenue quantification, Armada/NVIDIA DSX POD economics, any portfolio action, buyback sizing, and whether management sets an investor-day date to frame the longer-term data-center target.
