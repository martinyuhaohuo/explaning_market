## Recent Track Record

- **Q2 2026 (reported Jul 29):** EPS -$0.17 vs. -$0.25 est.; revenue $93.5M vs. $92.9M est. Both beat. Revenue grew 5.5% YoY, and Adjusted EBITDA rose 12.9% to $32.0M. Glo Fiber Expansion Markets revenue grew 32.8% to $26.3M. The company added its 100,000th Glo Fiber customer and a record 6,200 net additions. Incumbent-market revenue fell 6.0%, driven by a 14.1% drop in video RGUs. ([Shentel Q2 release](https://investor.shentel.com/node/25471), [Finviz](https://finviz.com/news/373954/shenandoah-telecommunications-company-reports-second-quarter-2026-results))
- **Q1 2026:** EPS missed (-$0.31 vs. -$0.25); revenue beat.
- Revenue has beaten consensus in each of the last four quarters.

## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Glo Fiber net adds and Expansion Markets revenue growth.** Q2 set a record at 6,200. Any slowdown would be the main negative. Fiber is now 51% of revenue.
2. **Adjusted EBITDA vs. the FY guide run-rate.** The guide implies a step-up in the back half. Watch whether Q3 tracks it.
3. **Incumbent-market decline.** Video RGU losses of -14.1% are the drag. Watch whether the rate is stabilizing.
4. **Net loss and EPS vs. -$0.20 to -$0.22.** Negative EPS consensus is driven by fiber build depreciation and interest costs, so the EBITDA trend matters more.
5. **Capex pacing.** Net capex of $220–250M implies a meaningful slowdown from 2025. Confirming it supports the FCF narrative.
6. **Wireless segment.** I did not find Q2 or Q3 wireless figures, so this is a gap.

**Base:** Results in line, guide reaffirmed, with management commentary on fiber penetration and cost discipline.
**Bear:** Net adds slow, incumbent losses accelerate, or management signals a guide cut. Watch for language about tariffs or chip supply, which the guide already excludes.

## Catalyst Checklist

1. **Glo Fiber net adds vs. Q2's 6,200 record.** The buy-side will expect continued growth at this pace.
2. **Adjusted EBITDA vs. the $131–136M FY guide.** Whether the company reaffirms, narrows, or raises matters more than the EPS print.
3. **FY capex guide ($220–250M).** Any change signals whether the build is on schedule.
4. **Incumbent video decline.** Commentary on churn and the wireless business.
5. **Narrative:** any change to fiber M&A, debt refinancing, or the wireless strategy.
