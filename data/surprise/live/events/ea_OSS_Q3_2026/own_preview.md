## Prior Quarter Context (Q2 FY2026, reported Aug 5, 2026)

- Revenue $9.3M, up 62% YoY, against roughly $8.5M consensus.
- Non-GAAP EPS of -$0.01 against -$0.03 estimated.
- Bookings $15.1M, a record. Book-to-bill 1.6x for the quarter, 1.7x year to date.
- Gross margin 39.1%, down from 41.3%, attributed to a higher mix of customer-funded development work.
- GAAP net loss from continuing operations $7.3M, including a $6.25M legal settlement.
- Cash and short-term investments $31.4M at June 30.
- FY2026 revenue growth guidance raised to 25–30% (from 20–25%). Benzinga shows an FY revenue range of $40.3–41.9M. Gross margin about 40%. Positive EBITDA and adjusted EBITDA for the year.
- Risks flagged: memory-component supply constraints and the federal budget outlook.

## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Revenue vs. ~$11M, and the Q4 implied figure.** Whether Q3 plus the Q4 implied run-rate supports the raised 25–30% FY growth.
