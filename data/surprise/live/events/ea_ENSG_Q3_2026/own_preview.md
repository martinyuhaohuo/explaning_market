## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **FY26 guidance raise or reaffirm.** The stock reacts more to guidance than to the EPS beat. The Q4 2025 rally of ~14% came on guidance, and Q1 2026 fell ~3% on an in-line print.
2. **Same-facility occupancy.** Q2 was 84.1%, up 2.7 pts year over year. Further gains support the margin story.
3. **Operating margin and insurance costs.** Management cited normalized insurance costs and a 25% tax rate as tailwinds. Any reversal would hurt.
4. **Acquisition pace and contribution.** Q2 added 20 operations (398 facilities across 17 states). Watch the Q3 count and the revenue they add.
5. **Revenue growth.** Q2 was up ~17% year over year, partly acquisition-driven. Organic growth is the question.

**Bull signals:** Management raises FY26 EPS above $7.85, cites accelerating occupancy, or discloses further acquisitions.
**Base signals:** Reaffirmed guidance, "continued momentum" language, and no change to margin outlook.
**Bear signals:** Guidance cut or narrowed to the low end, commentary on wage or insurance pressure, or softer occupancy trends.
