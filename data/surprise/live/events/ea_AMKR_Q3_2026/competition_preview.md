**Sensitivity (≈249M diluted shares):** every 50bps of gross margin ≈ $10M ≈ **$0.04/share**; every $50M of revenue at ~40–45% incremental margin ≈ **$0.06–0.07/share**.

## 3. Key Metrics to Watch — Ranked

1. **Q4'26 revenue + GM guidance vs. $2.04B / ~19–20%.** Highest-weight variable by a wide margin. Last quarter's −24% was a guidance reaction, not an earnings reaction.
2. **Gross margin delivered within 18.5–19.5%** and, critically, whether the mix-driven expansion is framed as durable or as a one-quarter artifact of the computing/communications crossover.
3. **Computing revenue ≈ $540M (+~30% q/q).** This is the HDFO data-center CPU ramp — the entire AI-packaging thesis. A miss here breaks both the revenue and the margin guide simultaneously.
4. **Communications decline magnitude + SiP-to-Vietnam qualification timeline.** Management split the weakness ~50/50 between market factors (memory supply constraints, Android build patterns) and the internal Korea→Vietnam migration, and said the headwind runs **into Q4 and potentially 1H27**. Any extension of that timeline is a 2027 estimate cut.
5. **Capex and free cash flow.** FY26 capex held at $2.5–3.0B with only $688M spent in 1H; 1H OCF of $382M already puts FCF at **−$306M YTD**. That implies **$1.8–2.3B of H2 capex**. Watch the cadence, the cash balance ($2.5B cash vs. $2.5B debt), and how/when the NVIDIA prepayments land in cash flow.
6. **2027 framing.** Management has already flagged Arizona as a **margin headwind in 2027 and 2028 from underutilization**. Any attempt to quantify it is the single biggest risk to the FY27 $2.81 consensus.

## 5. Catalyst Checklist

1. **Q4 revenue guide vs. $2.04B** — the whole trade. A guide with a midpoint below ~$1.95B likely prints a double-digit drawdown; a midpoint above ~$2.08B with GM ≥20% is the squeeze case given depressed positioning.
2. **Q3 EPS vs. the $0.82 whisper, not the $0.78 consensus** — with a 4+ quarter beat streak and ~44% average surprise, a $0.79–0.80 "beat" is effectively an in-line print.
3. **Computing segment dollars (~$540M) and HDFO/2.5D program count** — four 2.5D and four HDFO products were slated to launch in 2026; confirmation of launches is the proof-of-execution datapoint.
4. **Communications trough timing** — does management still say 1H27, and does the 50/50 market-vs-migration split hold?
5. **Capital allocation / 2027 margin bridge** — H2 capex cadence, NVIDIA prepayment cash timing (now **$3B total**: $1.5B Arizona prepayment announced Jul 23, plus **$1.3B Korea announced Oct 21**, with interposer wafer capacity targeted **+300% by end-2028**), and any quantification of Arizona underutilization drag.

### Caveats & flags

- **Consensus dispersion is wide for a single quarter:** $0.774–$0.80 EPS and $2.02–2.06B revenue across Zacks, LSEG/Yahoo, and Investing.com. This note uses the $0.78–0.79 / $2.02–2.04B band; a "beat/miss" call may differ by a penny depending on the vendor.
- **Segment dollar figures are estimates**, derived from reported percentage mix applied to $1,898M of Q2 sales — Amkor discloses end-market mix in percentages, not dollars.
- **Scenario probabilities and stock-reaction ranges are analyst judgment**, calibrated to the 12% implied move and the historical reaction distribution — not market-derived.
- One screener-sourced datapoint of ~$992M of trailing-12-month insider selling circulated pre-print; treat cautiously without verifying Form 4 detail, since Amkor's register includes large long-standing concentrated holders whose sales aren't a clean signal.
- At least one site published a "Amkor Reports Q3: Full Details" item dated Oct 25, before the release. Per the IR newsflow, the most recent actual company release as of this writing is the Oct 21 NVIDIA/Korea announcement — **no Q3 results were out at the time of writing**; disregard any pre-dated "results" content.
