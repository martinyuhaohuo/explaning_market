## 2. The Setup: Guidance Is Back-End Loaded

This is the crux of the quarter. Reconciling disclosed actuals against the raised full-year guide:

| | Adj. EBITDA |
|---|---|
| Q1 2026 actual | $225M |
| Q2 2026 actual | $269M |
| **H1 2026 actual** | **$494M** |
| Q3 2026 guide | $275–$285M |
| FY26 guide (raised Jul 22) | $1,065–$1,085M |
| **Implied Q4 2026** | **~$291–$311M** |

Against a Q4 2025 base of roughly $270M (*estimated* — derived from FY25 guidance of $965–985M less disclosed quarterly actuals; treat as approximate), the full-year guide embeds **roughly 8–15% Q4 EBITDA growth** — an acceleration from the 8% growth posted in Q2 and well above the Q3 guided rate. There is very little slack. A Q3 at the low end ($275M) forces Q4 to ~$296–316M to hold the full-year range, which is why the guidance language will matter more than the quarter itself.

### Operational math that has to work

**Tours is the swing factor.** Reconciling Q3 2025 (200K tours × $3,304 VPG = $661M vs. $682M reported gross VOI sales) implies a ~1.03x factor for non-tour channels. Applying that to the Q3 2026 guide midpoint ($720M gross VOI at $3,325 VPG) requires **~210K tours, or ~+5% YoY**. Context on the trajectory: tours were +5% in Q1 2026 but decelerated sharply to **+1% in Q2**. Re-accelerating to +5% is the quarter's biggest operational ask, and VPG guidance of $3,300–$3,350 is essentially flat-to-+1.4% YoY, so there is no price offset available if tour flow disappoints.

**Loan loss provision is the bear thesis.** Trend as a percentage of gross VOI sales: Q3 2025 **21.4%** ($146M) → Q1 2026 **18.2%** ($100M) → Q2 2026 **~20.3%** ($141M). 1H 2026 ran favorably versus the prior-year comp, and if Q3 lands near 20% against 21.4% last year that is roughly a $10M EBITDA tailwind — a material chunk of the guided increase. The risk is that this reverses. Industry timeshare financing rates currently run **15–18%**, far above conventional mortgage rates, which pressures both affordability at the point of sale and the credit performance of the existing book.

**Travel & Membership has to stop bleeding.** The segment went from +1% revenue in Q3 2025 to −5% in Q2 2026, with EBITDA −11% in Q2. If the ~5% revenue decline persists, Q3 2026 T&M lands near $160M revenue and ~$52M EBITDA versus $58M last year — a ~$6M headwind. Backing out corporate costs of roughly $23M (Q3 2025: $231M VO + $58M T&M = $289M segment vs. $266M consolidated), hitting $280M consolidated requires **Vacation Ownership EBITDA of roughly $251M, or +8.7% YoY**. Achievable — VO EBITDA grew 13% in Q2 — but it means the entire print rests on one segment.

## 3. Key Metrics to Watch, Ranked

1. **FY26 adjusted EBITDA guidance ($1,065–$1,085M)** — reaffirm, raise, or trim. Given the implied ~$291–311M Q4, even a reaffirm is a demanding statement. Any narrowing toward the low end reads as a cut.
2. **Provision as % of gross VOI sales** (vs. 21.4% LY, ~20.3% in Q2) plus any commentary on delinquencies and the mix of financed sales. This is where consumer stress shows up first.
3. **Tours growth** (needs ~+5% vs. +1% in Q2) **and VPG** vs. the $3,300–$3,350 guide. Watch new-owner mix — new owners carry lower VPG and higher provisions, so a favorable VPG driven by owner-mix shift is lower quality.
4. **Travel & Membership revenue and EBITDA trajectory** — is the decline bottoming or deepening.
5. **Capital return and early 2027 framing** — buyback pace against the $88M/quarter Q2 run-rate, net leverage (below 3.2x at Q2), and any preliminary 2027 commentary on the October call.

## 4. Scenario Analysis

| Scenario | Revenue | Adj. EPS | Adj. EBITDA | Key Driver | Est. Stock Reaction |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Bull** (25%) | $1.10B+ | $2.15+ | $288M+ (above high end) | Tours +6%+, VPG ≥$3,350, provision ≤19.5%, FY26 guide raised above $1,085M | **+8% to +13%** |
| **Base** (50%) | $1.07–1.09B | $2.03–2.08 | $278–283M | Tours +4–5%, VPG ~$3,320, provision ~20.5%, FY26 reaffirmed | **−3% to +5%** |
| **Bear** (25%) | <$1.06B | ≤$1.95 | $270–275M (below low end) | Tours flat/down, VPG <$3,300, provision ≥22%, FY26 trimmed to ~$1,040–1,060M | **−10% to −16%** |

**Bull — what must happen:** Tour flow re-accelerates on marketing investment and new-owner channels, provision rate holds near 1H levels, T&M stabilizes sequentially, and management raises the full year. *Listen for:* explicit confidence in Q4 tour pipeline, "no change in consumer credit behavior," and a raise rather than a reaffirm.

**Base — what must happen:** A guide-consistent quarter where VO strength offsets T&M softness and the full year is held together by the buyback. *Listen for:* reaffirmation with hedged Q4 language. **Important asymmetry: a clean in-line quarter with a mere reaffirm may not be rewarded.** In Q1 2026, TNL beat EPS by 10.5% and still fell 12.9% in the session because the full-year EBITDA guide was only in line. This is the most relevant precedent on the tape.

**Bear — what must happen:** Tour growth stalls as 15–18% financing costs bite, the provision rate reverts to or above last year's 21.4%, T&M declines steepen, and the back-end-loaded Q4 becomes untenable. *Listen for:* "more selective underwriting," "tour mix shift," or any reframing of the full-year range.

**Historical reaction context:** TNL has beaten EPS consensus in 5 of the last 8 quarters (2 of the last 4). Q3 2025 (Oct 2025) beat and shares surged. Q2 2026 (Jul 22, 2026) printed $1.88 — matching consensus per MarketBeat, a 2.6% miss per Zacks' $1.93 — but the raised full-year guide drove shares to an all-time high within a week. Q1 2026 was the outlier and the cautionary tale: a large beat, an in-line guide, and a 12.9% decline. **Pattern: this stock trades on the guide, not the quarter.**

## 5. Catalyst Checklist

1. **FY26 EBITDA guide vs. $1,065–$1,085M** — the dominant variable. Arithmetic requires a ~$291–311M Q4; the market will test whether management defends it.
2. **Provision rate vs. ~20% (Q2) / 21.4% (Q3 2025)** — the direct read on whether 15–18% financing rates are impairing credit. Bears own this line item.
3. **Tours vs. ~+5% required** — Q2's deceleration to +1% is the open question; no VPG offset is available given flat guidance.
4. **T&M inflection** — three quarters of deterioration; stabilization would remove a persistent drag on the sum-of-parts.
5. **Capital allocation / 2027 framing** — at ~8.5–9x our estimated FY26 adjusted EPS of ~$7.40, buyback pace is a meaningful part of the EPS story, and any 2027 color will reset the multiple debate.

But consensus EPS is pinned to the top of management's own EBITDA range, Q4 is heavily back-end loaded, and the Q1 2026 precedent proves that an in-line guide gets punished here regardless of the quarter. **Risk is skewed to the downside on a reaffirm-only outcome, with the asymmetry turning favorable only on an explicit raise.** Speculative call positioning into that setup is the uncomfortable detail — it means a merely adequate print has little cushion.
