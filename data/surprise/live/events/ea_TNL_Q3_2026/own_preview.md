## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Gross VOI sales vs. $700M–$740M guide.** This is the core demand signal. Q2 beat its range, so landing at or above the top end would support the raise narrative.
2. **VPG vs. $3,300–$3,350.** This shows pricing and mix health. Q2 came in above its guide.
3. **Adj. EBITDA vs. $275M–$285M, and FY guide.** Whether management raises FY again or only holds it.
4. **Adj. EPS vs. $2.07.** Watch how much of the beat comes from buybacks versus operations.
5. **Acquisition integration and financing.** The two deals add owners, so check synergy and accretion commentary.
6. **Inventory write-downs and impairments.** Any repeat would be a quality-of-earnings question.
