## 2. The Setup: What Management Already Told Us

Q2 FY2026 (reported Jul 28) was a blowout — revenue $13.38B (+7%), organic +6%, **unit case volume +5%**, price/mix +2%, concentrate sales +4%, comparable EPS $0.97 (+11%), comparable operating margin 35.6% vs 34.7%. Stock rose ~6–7%. Trademark Coca-Cola posted its strongest volume growth in 17 years ex-COVID rebound.

Guidance was **raised**: FY26 organic revenue ~**5%** (high end of prior 4–5%), comparable EPS growth to **9–10%** (from 8–9%) off a $3.00 2025 base, FCF ~$12.4B ($14.6B OCF less $2.2B capex), underlying tax rate 19.9%. Currency flips from a Q3'25 **6-point EPS headwind** to a FY26 tailwind (~3 points on EPS, ~1 point on revenue), partly offset by a 2–3% revenue headwind from acquisitions/divestitures.

**The key arithmetic nobody is pricing:** Q1 ($0.86) + Q2 ($0.97) = $1.83 9M-to-date through H1. Add Q3 consensus $0.88 → $2.71. The FY26 guide of $3.27–$3.30 leaves **only ~$0.56–$0.59 for Q4 vs. $0.58 last year — essentially flat.** Either (a) the guide is conservative and gets raised again, (b) consensus Q3 is phased too high, or (c) management is reserving room for affordability investment and A&D dilution. Resolving this is the single most important thing on the call.

## 3. Key Metrics to Watch — Ranked

1. **Unit case volume, and specifically North America volume.** Q2's +5% was flattered by favorable weather, FIFA World Cup activation, and an easy comp. All three reverse in Q3. Does volume hold ~3%, or does it decay to the 0–1% of Q3'25? Zacks explicitly flags "volume headwinds across key markets, particularly North America and Europe."
2. **Asia Pacific price/mix and India share.** APAC price/mix was **−9%** in Q2 — the cost of CEO Henrique Braun's affordability-first strategy. APAC added the most volume (+8%) yet **lost value share in NARTD, driven by competitive losses in India**, and APAC operating income reportedly fell ~8% in H1 despite revenue growth. Consensus has APAC revenue **−3.1% Y/Y**. This is where the bull thesis is most fragile: volume bought at negative mix is not quality growth.
3. **FY26 comparable EPS guidance — raise, reaffirm, or trim to the low end.** Given the flat-Q4 math above, a reaffirmation at 9–10% is effectively a modest Q4 raise; a trim to "~9%" would be read as a miss even on a Q3 beat.
4. **Comparable operating margin expansion.** Q2 delivered +90bps. Watch gross margin against the **US cane sugar conversion** (rolled out across US markets in October following political pressure) and aluminum/tariff input costs — both are new, under-modeled COGS variables.
5. **Price/mix vs. concentrate sales split, group-wide.** Q1 was +8% concentrate / +2% price/mix; Q2 +4% / +2%. A shift toward concentrate-led growth with thinning price/mix tells you the affordability strategy is spreading beyond APAC.

**Secondary:** CCBA close timing (75% controlling interest to Coca-Cola HBC, $3.4B equity value, targeted to close by end-2026; post-close Bottling Investments drops to ~5% of consolidated revenue from 13% in 2024) and the quantified 2027 A&D headwind; any early 2027 framing; and the **IRS transfer-pricing appeal** before the 11th Circuit (a $6B tax court ruling against KO; full exposure could exceed $20B with interest through 2025).

**What signals each on the call.** *Bull:* management quantifies NA volume as positive and frames affordability as "mix-accretive by mid-2027"; raises FCF. *Base:* "consumer backdrop stable in aggregate, but many remain under pressure" repeated verbatim — the Q2 language — with no guidance change. *Bear:* hedged language on Q4 phasing, "stepped-up investment in affordability," or any deferral of the APAC margin recovery timeline.

**Historical calibration.** The reaction distribution is wider than the averages suggest: **Jul 28, 2026 +7.3%** (vs. ±3.2% implied), Oct 2025 ~+4.3% peak (vs. ~±2.7% implied), Feb 2026 ~−2.5% peak (vs. ~±3% implied). ## 5. Catalyst Checklist

- [ ] **Unit case volume vs. ~+3% whisper** — the entire re-rating since Q2 rests on volume-led growth being structural, not weather- and World-Cup-driven.
- [ ] **North America volume sign (+/−)** — PepsiCo's Oct 8 read-through was mixed: PBNA revenue +5% but **organic revenue down slightly**, with CSD performance *trailing* the category even as organic volume improved sequentially. If the category is soft and Pepsi is losing CSD share, KO should be the taker — failure to show that is a thesis problem.
- [ ] **APAC price/mix vs. Q2's −9%** — improvement = affordability working; deterioration = share bought, not earned.
- [ ] **FY26 comparable EPS guide** — reaffirm at 9–10% is the bar; the flat-Q4 implied math means a true raise is available if Q3 beats.
- [ ] **Comparable operating margin** vs. ~32.5% implied / 31.9% LY, with explicit commentary on cane sugar and aluminum.

**View.** The implied move looks modestly *cheap* relative to the last two prints, but the *reaction skew is negative.* With ~3% upside to consensus PT, 27x forward, +33% YTD, a −1.31% Earnings ESP, and the toughest volume comp of the year, an in-line quarter is not enough — the stock needs volume durability plus an APAC mix inflection to extend. Conversely, the flat-Q4 guidance math is an underappreciated source of upside if Q3 beats cleanly. Long gamma over long delta into the print.
