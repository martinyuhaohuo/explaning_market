## Prior Quarter (Q2 FY2026, reported Jul 28) and Guidance

- Net revenue rose ~7% to ~$13.4B. Organic growth was ~6%.
- Comparable EPS was $0.97 (+11%), against ~$0.93 consensus. Reported EPS was $1.03.
- Global unit case volume grew 5%, led by India, China, the US, and Brazil.
- **FY26 guidance raised:**
  - Organic revenue growth ~5% (from 4–5%)
  - Comparable currency-neutral EPS growth 7–8%
  - Comparable EPS growth 9–10% (implies ~$3.27–3.30 vs. $3.00 in 2025)
  - Free cash flow ~$12.4B (from ~$12.2B)
- Management expects to deliver at the high end of revenue guidance. It cited H1 strength and FIFA World Cup activation.
- H2 faces tougher comparisons and six fewer days in Q4.
- CCBA refranchising should help H2 margins. The divestiture is a 2–3 pt revenue headwind if it closes in late Q3 or Q4.
- Effective tax rate guidance is 19.9%.

## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Organic revenue growth vs. ~5% FY guide.** Q3 needs to hold the high-end pace. Watch the price/mix vs. volume split. Management has said the split will vary by quarter.
2. **Comparable EPS vs. $0.88.** A beat of a few cents is the usual bar. Check the basis (comparable vs. GAAP) before reacting.
3. **Unit case volume.** Q2 was +5%. A slowdown would challenge the premium narrative.
4. **Operating margin and CCBA benefit.** Check whether the H2 margin uplift is showing up early.
5. **FY guidance changes.** Any move above 9–10% comparable EPS growth, or a reaffirmation with a "high end" tone, is the likely swing factor.
6. **Free cash flow** vs. the ~$12.4B target, and the fairlife/Webster ramp commentary.
