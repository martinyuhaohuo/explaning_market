## Recent Context

- **Q2 2026:** FFO was $0.51 per diluted share, vs. $0.52 a year earlier. Six-month FFO was $1.02, vs. $1.04. Net income was $0.09 per diluted share.
- **Q2 tone:** Management said Q2 FFO came in ahead of internal expectations, helped by newly commenced office leases. The CEO said the outlook supports the guidance midpoint, with upside to the upper half if leasing, multifamily and hotel trends improve.
- **Same-store cash NOI:** +0.3% in Q2, or +1.3% excluding a one-time reserve on an office tenant receivable.

## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **FFO per share vs. ~$0.50–0.51 run-rate.** Q2 was $0.51. A Q3 print in the low-$0.50s would be in line with the trend. Confirm the consensus basis (FFO vs. EPS) before comparing.
2. **FY2026 FFO guidance.** The key question is whether management holds $1.96–2.10 or narrows toward the upper half. Narrowing up is the bullish signal.
3. **Same-store cash NOI and leasing.** Watch office occupancy, leasing spreads, and whether the Q2 office lease commencements carry into Q3.
4. **Multifamily and hotel trends.** The CEO flagged these as the swing factors for the upper end of guidance.
5. **Office tenant credit.** The Q2 reserve on an office tenant receivable is a risk to watch for further reserves or tenant issues.
6. **Balance sheet and dividend.** Leverage, refinancing, and dividend coverage matter for a REIT.

## Scenarios

| Scenario | Revenue | FFO/EPS | Key Driver | Stock Reaction |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Bull | Beat ~$111M+ | FFO above ~$0.52 | Leasing and hotel/multifamily strength; guidance raised toward upper half | Positive, roughly +3% to +5% |
| Base | In line, ~$110–112M | FFO ~$0.50–0.52 | Guidance reaffirmed at midpoint; steady same-store NOI | Muted, roughly ±2% |
| Bear | Miss, <$109M | FFO below ~$0.49 | Office reserves or tenant issues; guidance cut or pressure on the low end | Negative, roughly −4% to −7% |

**Bull:** Management reaffirms or raises toward the upper half, and points to strong office leasing and occupancy. Guidance and commentary signal that Q2's momentum held.

**Base:** Results are in line, guidance is reaffirmed at the midpoint, and commentary is steady without a new catalyst.

**Bear:** Office tenant stress or softer hotel and multifamily trends push FFO toward the low end. Guidance moves down, or management warns about 2027.

**Historical context:** Aggregator data is thin and conflicting. One source puts the average post-earnings move at about −1.3% over 19 reports. Recent quarters ranged from roughly +1.4% to +3.9% in the session after the print. EPS surprise has weak predictive power for the move (R² about 0.05). Treat these as rough.

## Catalyst Checklist

1. **Q3 FFO vs. ~$0.51 run-rate:** The beat or miss against the Q2 level and the ~$0.50 Benzinga estimate matters most.
2. **FY2026 FFO guidance:** Holding $1.96–2.10 is neutral. Narrowing toward $2.05+ is positive. Any cut is a clear negative.
3. **Office leasing and same-store NOI:** Shows whether the Q2 lease commencements are real momentum.
4. **Hotel and multifamily commentary:** Management tied the upper end of guidance to these.
5. **Office credit and reserves:** Any new tenant reserves or impairments would weigh on sentiment.
