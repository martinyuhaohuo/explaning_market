## Recent results (Q2 2026, reported 2026-07-28)

- **Revenue:** $26.4M, down from $27.3M a year earlier.
- **FFO:** $1.6M, or $0.02/share, flat year over year.
- **AFFO:** $1.4M, or $0.01/share, down from $0.02.
- **GAAP net loss:** widened to $16.6M, driven by a $7.7M impairment on an asset held for sale.
- **Occupancy:** 67.4% at June 30, 2026, slightly down from year-end 2025.
- **Subsequent event:** sold Greenwood Plaza for about $19.4M and used $8.5M to repay debt.
- **Balance sheet:** management said a recent debt refinancing added flexibility.
- **Strategic review:** formally launched, with BofA Securities and JLL as co-financial advisors.

## Key metrics to watch (ranked)

1. **Strategic review update.** Any timeline, bidder interest, or asset-sale news will likely dominate the reaction more than the quarterly numbers.
2. **Occupancy and leasing.** Watch for movement from the 67.4% level and any new or renewed leases.
3. **FFO and AFFO per share vs. the ~$0.02 / ~$0.01 run rate.** Q2 came in at $0.02 FFO and $0.01 AFFO.
4. **Revenue trend.** Q2 was down about 3% year over year, and Q1 was down 3.3%.
5. **Impairments and GAAP loss.** Further write-downs on held-for-sale or other assets would weigh on sentiment.
6. **Dividend and capital allocation.** Any change to the payout or to debt paydown plans.
