## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Core FFO vs. ~$0.53 consensus and the 2026 guide.** The raised range implies a strong H2. Q3 needs to be at least ~$0.53 for the midpoint to hold.
2. **FY2026 guidance changes.** Watch whether the $2.09–$2.13 Core FFO range is raised again, reaffirmed, or narrowed. Investment volume is the biggest swing factor; the range was just lifted to $300–$400M.
3. **Investment volume and yields.** Year to date is $234M at 9.5%. Whether the company reaches $300M+ with mostly structured/preferred equity (12% cash yields) versus acquisitions (~10%) affects both growth and risk.
4. **Same-property NOI and occupancy.** Shopping-center NOI growth has been 10%+, helped by non-recurring recoveries. Check how much of that persists. Occupancy (95.4% total, down from year-end) is the weak spot.
5. **Dispositions and capital recycling.** Sales at ~6.7% caps funding higher-yield deals. Check the Carolina Pavilion closing and any new sales.
6. **Leverage and dilution.** Net debt/EBITDA of 5.8x is improving. Watch how much ATM equity was issued in Q3, since that dilutes FFO per share.

## Catalyst Checklist

1. **Core FFO ≥ $0.53 vs. consensus**: a beat alongside a raise is the bullish setup.
2. **Guidance: Core FFO range and investment volume.** The buy side wants to hear the $2.10+ level hold, or move up, with the $300–$400M investment range reaffirmed or raised.
3. **Same-property NOI and occupancy trajectory.** Whether the 5–6% full-year growth guide holds without one-time recoveries.
4. **Structured-investment pipeline and credit quality.** The preferred-equity book is growing and carries higher risk. Any watch-list or reserve commentary matters.
5. **Capital allocation and ATM usage.** Share issuance around ~$20 is a source of dilution concern if the stock falls.
