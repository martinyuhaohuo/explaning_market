## 2. Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Branded checkout TPV growth, CN (guide ~+2%).** This is the whole thesis. It carries most of PayPal's transaction gross profit. +3% or better = stabilization becomes re-acceleration; +1% or a sequential decel = the bear case is confirmed and the multiple doesn't matter.
2. **Transaction margin dollars, ex-interest on customer balances.** Q2 was +3% ex-interest on +1% headline. The ex-interest line is the clean read on underlying unit economics; consensus TM% of 44.6% implies ~140bps of YoY compression.
3. **FY26 non-GAAP EPS vs. the ~$5.38 bar.** With Q2 at $1.38 and Q3 consensus $1.31, the nine-month run-rate sets the implied Q4. Any trim to $5.30–5.35, or a TM$ cut below $15.6B, is the single most likely source of a violent downside reaction.
4. **Braintree/PSP volume and its margin.** Q2 accelerated to **+13%** (from +11% in Q1), ninth straight profitable quarter. BofA's standing worry is pricing pressure pushing large merchants to Adyen — watch whether PSP growth is being bought with take rate.
5. **Venmo monetization.** Q2: TPV **+14%** (7th straight double-digit quarter), Pay with Venmo **+44%**, debit card MAAs **+50%**, revenue +~$60M. The new card offer (up to 4% cashback on dining/entertainment) is a margin-cost question as much as a growth one.
6. **Agentic commerce / OpenAI.** PayPal adopted the Agentic Commerce Protocol in Oct 2025 and was to connect its merchant network to ChatGPT Instant Checkout during 2026. Any *quantified* volume datapoint would be the first real one — currently a free option in the stock.
7. **BNPL (+26% in Q2), FCF, buyback pace** ($1.5B / ~33M shares in Q2), and the **$120–140M H2 restructuring charge** against the $1.5B gross savings plan (~$400M landing by end-2026, ~40% AI-driven).

**What each requires operationally:**

- **Bull:** US branded volume inflects on the high-value-consumer push (rewards, Venmo card, checkout modernization) *without* a commensurate incentive cost; the ex-interest TM$ bridge improves even as rate income decays. Management tell: a hard number on branded checkout exit-rate or a raised FY branded checkout range — not adjectives.
- **Base:** The "stability, not recovery" pattern of Q1/Q2 repeats. Beat on EPS via cost control and buyback, in line on volume. Management tell: FY guide language unchanged, continued framing around 2027 as the inflection year.
- **Bear:** EU regulatory changes bite branded checkout (Morgan Stanley's flag) and/or unbranded take rate compresses. Management tell: the FY EPS guide held only by cost actions while TM$ goes to the low end, or "second-half weighted" becoming "2027 weighted."

## 4. Catalyst Checklist

1. **Branded checkout CN growth vs. ~+2% guide / ~+2–3% buy-side whisper** — the one number that decides the direction. A 100bp miss here overwhelms an EPS beat.
2. **FY26 non-GAAP EPS vs. $5.38 and TM$ vs. $15.6B** — the Street will do the implied-Q4 arithmetic live during the 8:00 AM call. Reaffirm = base case; trim = bear case regardless of the Q3 beat.
3. **Any commentary on strategic alternatives / M&A.** The board rejected **$60.50/share (>$53B) from Stripe + Advent** on July 15, reportedly holding out near ~$70; talks collapsed in August (stock −15%); speculation restarted in September and again on **Oct 20** around an unconfirmed all-stock approach from a "major West Coast technology group." A single sentence from Lores on this likely moves the stock more than the P&L.
4. **TM$ ex-interest, and the rate assumption.** The July guide baked in no further cuts. If management re-cuts the interest-income assumption, the clean-growth story gets harder to tell even with good volume.
5. **Narrative proof points:** quantified agentic-commerce/ACP volume, PYUSD, the $1.5B savings plan's tracking ($400M by year-end), and whether the five-pillar plan under CEO **Enrique Lores** (since March 1, 2026; CFO/COO **Jamie Miller**) gets any new financial framing ahead of the 2027 outlook due early next year.

### Caveats on the data

Conflicts that could not be reconciled and were left un-smoothed:
- **Prior-year Q3 revenue base** is reported as both $8.42B (MarketBeat) and $8.47B (Zacks/TipRanks); YoY growth figures shift ~20bps accordingly.
- **Q2'26 earnings-day reaction** is reported as +2.6% (Bloomberg options data) and "almost +4%" / +4.9% elsewhere.
- One secondary source described Q3 guidance as "low *double*-digit" CN revenue growth and a low-double-digit EPS decline; this contradicts PayPal's own 8-K (low-single-digit EPS decline) and was treated as an error.
- **FY26 consensus EPS** (against which the $5.38 guide would be judged) and segment-level Venmo/branded-checkout consensus were not available in reachable sources — the branded checkout "whisper" of ~+2–3% is inferred from sell-side preview commentary, not a surveyed number.
- The Oct 20 takeover report remains **unconfirmed**; treat it as a positioning factor, not a fact.
