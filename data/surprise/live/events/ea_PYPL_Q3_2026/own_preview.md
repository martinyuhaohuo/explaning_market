## Guidance Context (from Q2 2026, reported July 28)

- **Q3 and FY26 EPS:** Management guided GAAP and non-GAAP EPS to low- to mid-single-digit declines year over year for both Q3 and FY26. This is the central tension for the print (see below).

## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Adj. EPS vs. $1.31–1.34, and Q3 guide vs. the implied range.** Low-to-mid single-digit decline from prior-year Q3 EPS would point to roughly $1.25–1.31 if the prior-year base is about $1.34. That is at or below consensus, so the guide is the key test. This is my inference, not a sourced figure.
2. **FY26 EPS and transaction margin dollars guide.** A raise on TM$ with a flat or lower EPS guide would be read as a mix or cost story.
3. **Branded checkout TPV growth.** Q2 was about 2%. Anything under low single digits reinforces the bear case on core checkout share.
4. **Venmo monetization and TPV growth (+14% in Q2).** Revenue per user and any Venmo-specific guidance matter.
5. **Transformation charge scope and cost savings.** Confirm the $120–140M range and any headcount or restructuring detail.
6. **Revenue vs. ~$8.71B.** Q2 beat by about $0.2B, so a repeat beat is plausible, but a revenue beat alone has not driven the stock.
7. **Buybacks and FCF.** $6B buyback and $6B+ FCF support the per-share story.
