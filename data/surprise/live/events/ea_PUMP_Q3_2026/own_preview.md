## Prior Quarter Baseline (Q2 2026, reported 2026-07-29)

- **Revenue:** $306M, up 13% sequentially from $271M in Q1. Roughly in line with the $310.5M consensus, slightly below it.
- **Net loss:** $8M, or -$0.07 per diluted share, versus about -$0.01 expected. Management attributed the miss to fleet activation costs, out-of-basin downtime, and June Permian weather.
- **Adjusted EBITDA:** $45M, 15% margin.
- **Liquidity:** $784M cash, $905M total liquidity.
- **Capex:** FY2026 guidance cut to $525–595M from $540–610M. Completions capex is $125–145M, and one FORCE electric fleet buyout moved into early 2027. PROPWR capex held at $400–450M.
- **Commentary:** A 13th frac fleet was expected to activate later in Q3. Management expects PROPWR (power) to turn positive in H2 2026 and into 2027. Contracted PROPWR capacity is about 350 MW. Management gave no explicit revenue guidance.

## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Revenue vs. ~$327M.** Does the 13th fleet activation and the sequential ramp show up?
2. **Adjusted EBITDA margin vs. Q2's 15%.** Better margin would show that fleet activation costs are fading.
3. **EPS vs. the loss trend.** A loss narrower than Q2's -$0.07 would be read as progress.
4. **PROPWR timing and contracted MW.** Confirmation of the H2 positive turn and any new contracts above 350 MW.
5. **Free cash flow and capex.** Whether the trimmed $525–595M range holds and cash stays above $780M.
