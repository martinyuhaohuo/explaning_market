## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)
1. **Adj. EBITDA vs. the run-rate needed for guidance.** The $8.2B–$8.5B range implies the back half must hold up. Q2 was $2.12B. Watch whether Q3 tracks toward the midpoint.
2. **FY2026 guidance.** A raise or a reaffirmation at the midpoint is the base case. A cut would be the largest downside risk.
3. **Volumes.** Refined products and NGL throughput growth (Q2: +8% and +7%). Watch for any deceleration.
4. **EPS vs. ~$1.40–$1.45.** Useful, but secondary to EBITDA and guidance for this company.
5. **Capex and free cash flow.** Watch for any change to the $2.7B–$3.2B range and how it's funded. Leverage and dividend coverage matter to income holders.
