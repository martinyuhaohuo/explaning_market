## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Service organic growth and modernization orders.** This is the core of the margin and multiple story. The Q2 modernization orders growth of +9% is the number to beat.
2. **Adj. operating margin vs. the flat-to-down guide.** Q2 was down 180 bps. Any further contraction would hurt the thesis that the margin has bottomed.
3. **FY EPS guide ($4.01–4.05).** The company has already cut twice. Holding or raising the low end is the bar. A third cut would likely be punished.
4. **New equipment orders and backlog.** New equipment is the weak spot and the main driver of the revenue miss risk.
5. **FCF and buyback pace.** Q2 included $800M of repurchases. Watch whether capital returns continue at that pace.
