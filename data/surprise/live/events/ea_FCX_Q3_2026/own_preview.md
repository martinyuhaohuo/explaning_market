## Key Metrics to Watch (Ranked)

1. **Copper volume vs. 750M lb guide.** This is the main driver. Any miss on PTFI ramp timing matters more than the EPS print.
2. **Unit net cash cost vs. $1.90/lb FY guide.** Shows whether the Grasberg ramp is absorbing fixed costs as expected.
3. **Realized copper and gold prices.** Price leverage is the main source of EPS upside if volumes are soft.
4. **Q4 and FY26 volume guidance.** The H2 step-up (>20% copper, >65% gold) has to be reaffirmed, or the market will discount it.
5. **Capex and Grasberg update.** Any change to the $4.3B capex plan, the 2027 full-capacity date, or the Bagdad decision timing.
6. **Free cash flow and net debt.** Watch for dividend or buyback commentary.
