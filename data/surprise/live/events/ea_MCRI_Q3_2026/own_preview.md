3. **Adjusted EBITDA margin.** Q2 was 37.2%. Watch whether labor-cost pressure persists.
4. **EPS vs. ~$1.74.** Q2 beat, and EPS has been running ahead of revenue growth, partly from lower share count and below-the-line items.
