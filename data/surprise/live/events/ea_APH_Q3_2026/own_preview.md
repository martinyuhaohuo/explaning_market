## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Organic growth and book-to-bill.** A book-to-bill above 1.0 supports the Q4 outlook. Organic growth near 30% is the bar to clear.
2. **Q4 revenue and EPS guide vs. consensus.** Past reactions have followed forward guidance more than the beat itself.
3. **Operating margin ex-tariff recoveries.** Any further recoveries are excluded from guidance, so a recovery in Q3 would flatter the headline.
4. **Tariff and FX commentary.** Watch for the size of any remaining IEEPA recovery and the effect of the FX assumption.
5. **Acquisition contribution.** Check how much of the growth comes from El.Com and Wilder versus organic.
