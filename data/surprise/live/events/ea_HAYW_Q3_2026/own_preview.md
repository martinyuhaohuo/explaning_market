## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Revenue vs. ~$252M, and North America growth.** NA carried Q2. Whether it holds high single digits is the main test of the thesis.
2. **Europe / Rest of World trajectory.** A fourth quarter of decline would weigh on the guide.
3. **Gross margin vs. 48.7% in Q2.** Tariffs and inflation are the key pressure. Watch whether net price fully offsets them.
4. **Adj. EPS vs. $0.15 to $0.16.** Q2 beat by about $0.01 to $0.03. Repeat beats are priced in to some degree.
5. **FY26 guidance reaffirmation or raise, and the implied Q4.** The FY range of $0.84 to $0.87 minus YTD EPS implies a sizable Q4. Any cut to the low end would be a negative surprise.
6. **Channel inventory commentary.** Management has flagged balance. Any re-stocking or destocking would move the quarter.
7. **FCF and capital allocation.** Confirm the ~$200M FCF target and the use of deleveraging capacity.

## Catalyst Checklist

1. **Revenue vs. ~$252M consensus, with NA growth:** This is the first test of whether Q2 strength continues into the back half.
2. **FY26 guidance ($0.84 to $0.87 EPS, ~5% sales growth):** The buy side will want a reaffirmation at minimum, and ideally upside. Any cut is the main downside risk.
3. **Gross margin vs. 48.7%:** Tariff and inflation headwinds against net price. Compression below Q2 would dampen the beat.
4. **Europe / RoW recovery or further decline:** The biggest narrative swing factor, given the −8% in Q2.
5. **Capital allocation and leverage at 1.5x:** Buybacks or M&A commentary could shift the narrative.
