## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Revenue trend.** Q2 2026 fell 23% YoY and Q3 2025 was $9.5M. A decline of 20% or more would put revenue near $7.5M. Watch whether the decline is narrowing.
2. **Asia/Hong Kong member activity.** Management cited cautious Chinese consumer spending, regulatory uncertainty, and member policy violations that disrupted business. Active member count (28,030 at Q3 2025) and new orders are the best read-throughs.
3. **Gross margin.** It was 75.2% in Q2 2026, up from 73.9%, helped by moving manufacturing to East Asia. Sustaining this matters for the loss narrowing.
4. **Operating expenses and cost savings.** SG&A was $3.3M in Q2. Management expects more savings in H2 2026, and the restructuring targets $1.5M in annualized savings by mid-2026.
5. **Cash and capital return.** Cash and securities were $18.6M at June 30, 2026, down from $28.9M at year-end 2025. Operating cash use was $2.2M in H1. Watch the dividend (cut to $0.10 per quarter guided for 2026) and the buyback ($15.9M remaining).
