Source: [WorldOil](https://worldoil.com/news/2026/6/1/weatherford-to-acquire-ncs-multistage-expanding-completions-portfolio/) and [Barchart](https://www.barchart.com/story/news/2231006/weatherford-announces-definitive-agreement-to-acquire-ncs-multistage-expanding-completions-portfolio-and-unconventional-resource-exposure)

- Both boards approved. Advent International, the controlling shareholder, signed off.
- Expected close in 2H 2026, subject to regulatory approval.
- At least $15M annual cost synergies within 18 months of closing.
