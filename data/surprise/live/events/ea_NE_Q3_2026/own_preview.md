## Prior Quarter Context (Q2 FY2026, reported Jul 27)

- Revenue $720M; contract drilling $679M, down from $743M in Q1 on Brazil suspensions (Noble Faye Kozack and Noble Courage).
- Adjusted EBITDA $212M. Adjusted EPS $0.01, which missed consensus. GAAP net loss $37M, or $(0.23) per share.
- Utilization of the 29 marketed rigs fell to 64% from 68%.
- Backlog $6.8B, down from ~$7.5B in Q1 per a Quartr summary, though one source says the drop is significant. About $200M in new contracts since April, including a 6-well deal for the Noble Viking and a 3-well deal for the Noble Claus Bachmann.
- FY2026 revenue and Adjusted EBITDA guidance lowered; capex guidance held.
- Q3 dividend of $0.50 per share declared. This is large relative to recent EPS and is a likely topic on the call.

## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Utilization and Q4 contract-drilling outlook.** Utilization fell in Q2. The market will want to see whether Brazil resumes and whether idle rigs get work.
2. **Adjusted EBITDA vs. guidance.** The Q2 guidance cut means the bar is now lower. Margin direction matters more than the headline EPS.
3. **Backlog and new contracts.** Backlog dropped from ~$7.5B to $6.8B. Any new awards or reaffirmed contract values will be scrutinized.
4. **Revised FY2026 revenue and EBITDA guidance.** This is the main driver of the reaction and the number the buy side will focus on.
5. **Dividend sustainability and free cash flow.** Capex guidance was held. Check whether cash generation covers the $0.50 payout.
