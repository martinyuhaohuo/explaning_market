### The guidance arithmetic says consensus is stretched

FY26 guidance (cut on July 27) is revenue **$2,800–2,900M** and adj. EBITDA **$850–925M**. Against H1 actuals:

- H1'26 revenue = $786M (Q1) + $720M (Q2) = **$1,506M** → implied H2 = $1,294–1,394M, or **~$647–697M per quarter**.
- Consensus Q3 of $714M sits **at or above the top of that implied quarterly average**. For consensus to hit without a guidance raise, Q4 has to come in at $580–680M.
- But Q4 should be the *stronger* half-quarter: the Noble Endeavor's 11-well South America contract ($300k/day) starts late 2026, and both Brazil rigs are back working a full quarter.
- Same exercise on EBITDA: H1 = $489M → implied H2 = $361–436M (~$180–218M/qtr). A $0.23 adj. EPS print on ~160M shares (~$37M adj. net income) likely requires EBITDA near **$215–230M**, i.e. the *top* of the implied band.

**Conclusion: consensus Q3 embeds the high end of a guidance range that was already cut once this year, and implicitly front-loads H2 into the weaker quarter.** This is the central asymmetry of the setup.

## 2. Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **FY26 adj. EBITDA guidance ($850–925M).** Already cut from $940–1,020M in July. A third revision lower is the single most damaging outcome available — it would break the "trough is in" narrative the stock has rallied on.
2. **Fleet status report / backlog vs. $6.8B.** Backlog has slid from $7.5B (Q1) → $6.8B (Q2) as burn outpaced awards. The FSR accompanying the print is where the market will look for BlackRhino, BlackHawk, Stanley Lafosse and Noble Viking fixtures — management named these as the units standing between today and full marketed-fleet utilization.
3. **Brazil / ANP resolution.** The May–June ANP audit suspended the Noble Faye Kozack and Noble Courage, costing ~$43M in Q2, with a further ~$15M revenue reduction spread across H2 through the contract's early-January 2027 end. Both rigs are operating again, but management said in July it "cannot say much" about the administrative discussions. Any quantified settlement — or an incremental cost — moves the stock.
4. **Floater contracting %, utilization and dayrates.** Floaters contracted fell to 61% from 68%; floater utilization 59% from 65%; average floater dayrate $412,650; jackups $197,380 at 83% utilization. Watch whether the Q3 contracted percentage inflects or keeps sliding — this is the cleanest read on white space.
5. **Dividend coverage.** See below; this is the quiet risk.
6. **2027 framing.** Management has guided to ~$2.3B of 2027 backlog conversion, a "meaningful earnings inflection by 2H27," and a "realistic path to full utilization" of the marketed fleet by late 2027. Whether those three phrases survive intact is the whole bull case.

### The dividend math deserves its own line

The $0.50/quarter dividend costs ~$80M/quarter (~$320M annualized). FY26 guidance implies EBITDA midpoint ~$888M less capex midpoint $640M less cash interest on $1.9B of debt — leaving roughly $130–150M of free cash before taxes and working capital. **H1 actuals confirm it empirically: FCF of $169M (Q1) + $(59)M (Q2) = $110M against ~$160M of dividends paid.** The dividend is being funded partly from the $456M cash balance, not from current-year cash generation. GuruFocus flags a 126% payout ratio on net income and an Altman Z of 2.25; the FCF framing is the more relevant one. No buybacks were executed in H1 2026.

Management's own framing — the dividend affords "shareholders the luxury of being paid to wait" — is honest about what it is: a bridge to the 2H27 inflection. It is defensible if the inflection arrives. If 2027 slips, the dividend becomes the debate.

**What each requires operationally.** *Bull* needs the Brazil rigs to have run clean for a full quarter plus at least one meaningful idle-unit contract at a rate with a "4" in front of it. *Base* is simply the Q2 run-rate plus the Endeavor ramp, with costs behaving. *Bear* is a repeat of the Q2 failure mode — revenue roughly fine, costs and non-operating items destroying the EPS line — compounded by a guidance revision.

**Historical calibration.** Q2'26 (Jul 27): adj. EPS $0.01 vs. $0.19 expected, FY guidance cut → **-5.5%**. Q1'26 (Apr 26): beat → **+14.6%** against a 5.4% implied move. Q3'25: EPS miss → stock dipped. The pattern is clear — this stock does not trade in a 3% band on earnings day.

## 4. Catalyst Checklist

1. **FY26 adj. EBITDA vs. $850–925M** — reaffirm, raise, trim, or low-end steer. The arithmetic above says in-line consensus and unchanged guidance are hard to hold simultaneously.
2. **Fleet status report: backlog vs. $6.8B and the four uncontracted units** — the buy-side wants dollar-denominated awards, not "constructive discussions."
3. **Brazil/ANP administrative outcome and the Faye Kozack gap** — the Tullow Ghana award (up to 10 wells, announced Sept 29) starts *mid-2027*, leaving a white-space window from the January 2027 Petrobras contract end. How that gap gets filled or financed matters.
4. **Dividend reaffirmation plus an explicit 2027 FCF coverage path** — a fifth straight $0.50 declaration with a credible bridge is table stakes; hedged language would be read as a cut telegraphed.
5. **Leading-edge dayrate commentary** — management cited mid-$400,000s/day in July and warned pricing improves "organically" rather than spiking. Third-party trackers quoting $500k+ for top-tier 7G units should be treated cautiously; anchor on Noble's own disclosed fixtures (bp UKCS semi at $320k/day; Endeavor at $300k/day).

The highest-probability path is a soft Q3 against a reaffirmed FY range and constructive 2027 commentary — a print the stock sells modestly after a 12% run. Expressing this via options rather than delta looks preferable given the 3.5% implied move versus an 8-for-8 history of larger moves.
