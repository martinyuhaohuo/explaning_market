## Context from Last Quarter (Q2 FY2026, reported Jul. 29)

- Sales of $921M, up 6% reported and 5% organic.
- Adj. EPS of $2.32 vs. ~$2.10–$2.11 consensus. This included about $0.08 from one-time IEEPA tariff refunds, so the underlying beat was smaller.
- Orders above $1B, a record, with 28% organic growth. Health & Science Technologies (HST) grew orders 47% organically.
- Raised FY26 guidance. Q3 guide of $2.20–$2.25.

## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Orders and book-to-bill.** Q2 orders grew 28% organically. Check whether HST strength (life sciences, semis, data center) continues or was lumpy. This drives the H2 revenue outlook.
2. **Organic revenue growth vs. the 5–6% FY guide.** Confirms whether the raise is holding.
3. **Underlying EPS excluding the tariff refund.** Q3 has no comparable one-time benefit. A Q3 print near the $2.20–$2.25 guide with no add-backs would look like a clean beat.
4. **FY26 EPS guide ($8.70–$8.85).** Watch for a raise, a reaffirm, or a low-end narrowing. A reaffirm after a strong Q3 can read as a soft H2.
5. **Gross margin and segment operating margin.** Check for price/mix and tariff cost pass-through, especially in Fluid & Metering Technologies (FMT).
6. **Free cash flow and working capital.** Strong orders can strain working capital.
7. **FY27 commentary.** Early framing, including any tariff or pricing headwinds, will drive the reaction more than Q3 itself.

## Catalyst Checklist

1. **Q3 adj. EPS vs. $2.20–$2.25 guide and ~$2.20 consensus:** a beat ex-tariff items matters more than the headline.
2. **FY26 EPS guide vs. $8.70–$8.85:** a raise is the expected base case; a reaffirm reads as conservative or a soft H2.
3. **Orders and book-to-bill:** whether the >$1B Q2 level repeats. This is the main bull/bear differentiator.
4. **FMT segment trends and tariff cost recovery:** the weaker segment and the main margin risk.
5. **FY27 framing:** any early commentary on pricing, tariffs, or end-market growth.
