## 3. The Setup — Two Things Changed Since Guidance

**(a) The rate regime flipped.** The FOMC **raised** the funds target 25bp to **3.75%–4.00% in September 2026** — the first hike since 2023 — and December is live for another. The October 28 meeting (the morning after HAFC prints) is expected to be a skip. Management's "stable NIM" guide was explicitly conditioned on *no* Fed move, so that condition has broken. The question on the call is whether a hiking cycle is a net positive (asset repricing, 30.7% noninterest-bearing funding) or a net negative (deposit competition in a fiercely contested Korean-American deposit market, CRE borrower stress at 61% of the book). **This is the single most important piece of new information tonight.**

**(b) A quantifiable, under-modeled NII tailwind.** On July 30 HAFC issued **$55M of 10-yr sub notes at 6.50% fixed** and noticed for redemption **all $110M of its 3.75% fixed-to-floating 2031 notes**, which were set to float to **3M SOFR + 310bp (≈7.0% at current rates) from September 1, 2026**. Redemption was expected on or about Sept 1. Math: ~$7.7M/yr of floating cost replaced by ~$3.6M/yr → **~$4.1M annualized, ~$1.0M/quarter pretax, roughly 5bp of NIM** on ~$7.6B average earning assets. Q3 captures only ~one month of that (~2bp, partly offset by double-carrying both instruments in August); **Q4 gets the full benefit.** If management doesn't volunteer this, ask for it.

## 4. Key Metrics to Watch — Ranked

1. **NIM and the forward NIM framing (3.36% base).** Tailwinds: sub-debt refi (~2bp in Q3, ~5bp in Q4), CD repricing relief, Sept hike flowing into variable-rate loans. Headwinds: deposit competition, August double-carry. Watch the **$612K FHLBank San Francisco dividend-practice change** that cost Q2 ~3bp — management called it a practice change, not a timing item, so do *not* assume a clean reversal; ask whether the lower run-rate is permanent. **Bar: ≥3.40% is good; <3.33% breaks the "stable NIM" promise.**
2. **Credit — CRE classified/criticized migration.** The most important risk line. Q2 NPLs were pristine at 0.15% and one downgraded retail CRE loan was framed as "well secured" and "isolated." Peer OP Bancorp just reported **criticized loans surging to $77.6M from $33.4M**, driven by CRE additions, with NCOs up to 0.14% annualized. If HAFC shows similar migration at 61% CRE concentration, the multiple compresses regardless of EPS. **Watch classified/criticized totals and special mention, not just NPLs.**
3. **Noninterest income / SBA premium income ($8.3M base).** Guided to ~$45M of SBA production in H2 with premium income back to historical trends. This is the main swing factor in the fee line and the easiest path to an upside surprise. Also watch any residential mortgage sale gains.
4. **Loan growth and the C&I engine.** C&I +1.6% q/q and **+27.6% y/y**, now 18% of loans; loans were flat-to-down in Q2 ($6.55B → $6.54B) on $156.4M of payoffs. FY guide is low-to-mid single digits, which requires H2 acceleration. **Watch period-end loans, production (vs. $371.9M), and payoff levels.**
5. **Corporate Korea utilization + capital return.** $1.2B of deposits (17% of total) but clients won't draw credit lines on "economic uncertainty, rising energy costs." Any sign of line utilization inflecting is a 2027 earnings unlock. On capital: 1.99M shares remain authorized; $13.6M returned in Q2 ($5.2M buyback at $30.24 avg + $0.28 dividend). At 1.20x TBV, buyback pace is now a judgment call — slowing it is defensible, not bearish.

Consensus target of $34.25 implies only ~5% upside — **the sell side is effectively flat-footed and the stock is priced for a good quarter.**
- **Options-implied move: not available.** HAFC's options chain is illiquid (20-day average volume ~188k shares, $955M cap) and no credible implied-move quote exists for this print — MarketChameleon gates it behind a paywall and the earnings-watcher universe doesn't cover the name. **Use the historical distribution instead: ±3–4% is the realistic one-day expectation, with a fat left tail on a credit surprise.** Do not size as if a quoted implied move exists.
- **⚠️ The day-after tape is contaminated — this is the key execution risk.** Three overlapping events hit the Oct 28 session: (1) the **FOMC decision at 2:00pm ET** (expected hold at 3.75–4.00%, but the statement/presser sets the 2027 rate path that drives every bank multiple); (2) **Hope Bancorp (HOPE) reports pre-market Oct 28**, the closest large Korean-American comp, and will set the sector tone before HAFC trades on the print; (3) HAFC's own numbers. **Attributing Oct 28 price action to HAFC's results alone will be wrong.** If you want clean exposure to the print itself, the after-hours session on Oct 27 is the only uncontaminated window.
