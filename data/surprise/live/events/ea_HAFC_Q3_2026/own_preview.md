**Prior quarter (Q2 2026, reported 2026-07-21):**
- EPS: $0.79 diluted, against a $0.77 consensus (beat). Sources vary from $0.78 to $0.80.
- Revenue: $72.2M, below the ~$75.5M consensus (miss).

## Key Metrics to Watch (Financials, ranked)

1. **Net interest margin.** Management guided to stability, with a likely range of ±1–3 bps absent Fed action. A print below ~3.34% would signal deposit-cost pressure.
2. **Revenue vs. ~$75–76M.** Q2 missed on revenue while beating on EPS, so the question is whether the mix shifted from interest income to fees or whether NII growth is lagging.
3. **Deposit costs.** Management flagged savings and money-market repricing as a upward pressure, with only modest CD relief.
4. **Credit quality.** Provision levels and non-performing trends (not sourced in this search; check the release).
5. **Loan growth.** Guidance is low-to-mid single digit for 2026, led by C&I and CRE. Check whether Q3 is tracking within that range.
6. **Expenses.** Management guided to near the current run rate. A miss here would matter more if revenue is light.

- *Bear:* Guidance cut on loan growth, a deposit-pricing warning, or a provision build.

## Catalyst Checklist

1. **NIM vs. 3.36%.** The margin is the main driver of earnings. Any move outside a few basis points will be read as a trend.
2. **Revenue vs. ~$75–76M.** Following Q2's miss, a second shortfall would be a credibility issue.
3. **2026 loan growth guidance.** The buy side will want a reaffirmation or an explanation of any cut.
4. **Deposit cost commentary.** Competitive pressure is the main margin risk.
5. **Credit and provisioning.** Not covered by my searches, so check the release directly.
