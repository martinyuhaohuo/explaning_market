## The one thing that matters

PACCAR beat in Q2 (EPS $1.43 vs. $1.33) and the stock rose ~2% to $137 — then derated 23% in under three months with no guidance cut. Four brokers slashed targets in the last three weeks (JPM $164→$142, Wells Fargo $157→$122, Citi $140→$115, Bernstein $148→$121). **The market has already repriced PCAR for a weak 2027, not a weak Q3.** This print will trade on the Q4 margin guide and management's first real framing of 2027 — not on the $1.61 EPS line.

**⚠️ Two data hazards to know before the tape moves:**

1. **Revenue basis mismatch.** The widely-quoted "$7.54–7.56B consensus" is the *truck/parts/other* line, excluding Financial Services (it reconciles exactly to a $6.10B year-ago base). PACCAR's reported *consolidated* revenue will likely print ~$8.0–8.1B. Expect at least one headline screaming a "$500M revenue beat" that is purely a basis artifact. Check which line any beat/miss claim is using.
2. **Sell-side units sit below the company's own guide.** Consensus deliveries of 40,107 are ~4.5% *under* the ~42,000 PACCAR guided to on the Q2 call. Either the Street is quietly fading the guide on soft orders, or models are stale. Either way, the unit bar is lower than the EPS bar.

## Key metrics to watch — ranked

1. **Q4 delivery and gross margin guide.** The single highest-information item. Management said on the Q2 call that Q4 margins expand beyond 14.5%. Anything that walks that back — or a Q4 unit guide below ~42,000 — breaks the margin-expansion thesis that the remaining bulls own.
2. **2027 Class 8 market framing and the pre-buy question.** On the Q2 call PACCAR argued for "a stronger 2027 and not much drop-off, rather than a huge prebuy at the end of the year." The EPA still has not finalized 2027 NOx rules (proposal July 9, comments closed Aug 29, no final rule as of early October), and PACCAR flagged $6,000–7,000/truck nonconformance penalties under the proposal. If management now hedges to "awaiting clarity," that validates the derating.
3. **Order book / build slots.** Q2 exit: slots ~90% full through year-end, expected sold out within 1–2 months. ACT put September Class 8 net orders at 18,700, −9.5% y/y, on regulatory uncertainty (FTR saw 21,300, +3% — the two disagree). Need PACCAR's own orderboard commentary to adjudicate.
4. **Truck segment gross margin and price/cost.** 13.1% (Q1) → 14.4% (Q2) → 14.5% guided. Tariff pass-through and local-for-local production are the swing factors.
5. **Parts: growth acceleration + ~29.8% margin.** This is the multiple-support asset. Record $1.75B revenue / $417M pretax in Q2. FY guide of 3–5% needs H2 acceleration to hold; parts holding ~30% margin at $1.79B is what keeps the 16x forward multiple defensible.
6. **PACCAR Financial credit quality.** Past dues, used-truck residuals, loss provisions. Quietly the biggest tail risk in a prolonged freight downturn and under-discussed into this print.

**What management commentary signals each:** Bull = a specific 2027 market number with a floor, plus a Q4 gross margin figure starting with 15. Base = "we'll give 2027 guidance in January." Bear = repeated use of "regulatory uncertainty" as the reason for not framing 2027, or any language about *moderating* build rates.

**Historical calibration:** Options have over-priced PCAR earnings in 8 of the last 8 reports — average implied ±2.8% vs. average actual 1.1%, and the stock stayed inside the band 100% of the time. Recent actuals: Q2'26 −3.1%, Q1'26 −1.2%, Q4'25 −1.1%, Q3'25 +0.6%. **I would not extrapolate that calm.** That sample covers a period when PCAR carried a premium multiple and a stable narrative; today the stock is 24% off its high with a $115–$150 target spread. The distribution into this print is materially fatter than the 1.1% history.

## Catalyst checklist

1. **EPS vs. $1.61** — my work says the guide mechanically produces ~$1.54, so this is live. But note units are modeled *below* guide, so a unit beat with a small EPS miss is a very plausible, confusing outcome.
2. **Q4 gross margin guide vs. the implied "above 14.5%"** — the actual swing factor for FY2027 estimates. Buy-side wants 15.0%+.
3. **2027 commentary / EPA pre-buy** — the narrative item. Three brokers cut targets ~20% on this; management either defends the "stronger 2027" call or concedes.
4. **Parts H2 acceleration** — needs to show up this quarter to validate the FY 3–5% guide. Watch margin alongside revenue.
5. **Order intake and 2027 slot opening** — the bridge between September's soft ACT print and PACCAR's claimed demand strength.
