## Q3 Guidance and Full-Year Outlook

From the [Q2 call transcript](https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/07/28/paccar-pcar-q2-2026-earnings-call-transcript/?.tsrc=rss):
- Q3 deliveries about 42,000 (+8.5% QoQ). The European summer shutdown partly offsets build-rate increases.
- Q3 gross margin about 14.5%. Management expects further margin improvement in Q4.
- Parts gross margin not guided. Financial Services Q3 not guided.
- Full year: Parts sales +3% to +5%, weighted to H2. Capex $700M to $750M. R&D $450M to $480M. Tax rate not discussed.
- US/Canada Class 8 retail 230k to 270k (midpoint ~250k). Europe above-16t ~310k. South America above-16t 100k to 110k.
- Freight: spot rates up ~20%, contract up ~6.5%. Proposed EPA nonconformance penalty estimate $6,000 to $7,000 per truck.

**Internal consistency check:** Consensus revenue of ~$7.54B is flat QoQ, but the guided delivery increase is ~8.5%. Either consensus assumes lower truck pricing or mix, or parts/other is expected to dip. The delivery-to-revenue math is worth checking on the call.

## What to Watch (Ranked)

1. **Q4 and full-year delivery and margin commentary.** Management has already guided to Q4 margin expansion. The stock will likely trade on whether Q4 and 2027 are framed as improving.
2. **Q3 truck gross margin vs. 14.5% guide.** A miss would challenge the margin-recovery narrative, especially since truck margin was only 9.4% in Q2.
3. **Truck deliveries vs. ~42k.** Confirm the build-rate ramp and whether the European shutdown was worse than expected.
4. **Parts growth and margin.** Parts at 29.8% gross margin is a key earnings support. Watch for a sustained record.
5. **Regulatory and cost items.** EPA nonconformance penalty treatment, tariffs, and any emissions-related pricing. These can shift the 2027 narrative more than the quarter.

## Catalyst Checklist

1. **Q3 EPS ~$1.61 vs. consensus** and margin. The EPS consensus implies ~10% growth from Q2, which the 8.5% delivery increase and flat margin roughly support.
2. **Q4 margin and delivery guide.** The buy-side will want Q4 margin above Q3 and a clear H2 build ramp.
3. **Truck margin vs. the 14.5% guide** and the Q2 9.4% truck-segment base. This is the cleanest test of the recovery.
4. **Parts growth tracking vs. 3% to 5% full-year.** A reaffirmation supports the earnings floor.
5. **Freight and regulatory narrative.** Spot freight +20% and the EPA penalty estimate may come up as 2027 demand and cost drivers.
