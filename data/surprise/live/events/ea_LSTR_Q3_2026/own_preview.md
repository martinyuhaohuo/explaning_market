## Prior Quarter Context (Q2 FY2026, reported 7/28/2026)

- **Revenue:** $1.432B, up 18% YoY, above expectations.
- **EPS:** $1.44, up 20% YoY. This beat the Zacks consensus of $1.42 and was roughly in line with other estimates (MarketBeat had $1.49, Benzinga $1.47).
- **Gross profit:** $132M, up from $109M. Variable contribution: $199M, up from $170M.
- **Earnings quality issue:** Insurance and claims expense rose, mainly from unfavorable development on prior years' claims.
- **Guidance:** No formal Q3 guidance. Management cited market volatility. Reported July 2026 truck loads were up 5% YoY and revenue per load was up 26%. Management expects continued momentum from BCO and agent growth and ongoing technology and capacity investment.

## Key Metrics to Watch (Ranked)

1. **Insurance and claims expense / prior-year development.** This was the Q2 weak spot. Any further adverse development would hurt margins even if revenue is strong.
2. **Revenue per load and truck/load volume.** Q2 showed strong revenue per load (+26% in July). Watch whether volume growth (+5% in July) holds in August and September.
3. **Variable contribution margin and gross profit.** Whether the Q2 margin expansion continues.
4. **BCO (Business Capacity Owner) and agent count growth.** Management flagged this as a driver.
5. **Capacity and purchased transportation costs.** Rising carrier rates squeeze spread.
6. **Q4 outlook commentary.** Buy-side will look for a directional read on Q4 and any commentary on the freight cycle.
