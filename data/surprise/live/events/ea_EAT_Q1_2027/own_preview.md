## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Chili's same-store sales and traffic.** Q4 FY26 comps rose 5.6%. Management assumes mid-single-digit comps and positive traffic for the rest of FY27. Any slowdown in Q1 would challenge that assumption.
2. **Restaurant-level margin and commodity inflation.** Management expects commodity inflation of about 4% in Q1, easing to about 1% by Q4. A Q1 print above 4% would signal the cost path is off.
3. **FY27 guidance reaffirmation or raise.** The guidance range is wide ($0.80). The buy-side will want to see whether the midpoint holds, especially given the 53rd-week benefit (management estimates about +2% revenue and about +$0.70 EPS).
4. **Maggiano's trajectory.** Q4 comps fell 2.5%, and guidance assumes roughly flat revenue and profit for the brand. Anything worse than flat undercuts the turnaround story.
5. **Traffic vs. pricing mix.** Price-led comps with weak traffic would be read negatively, even with a headline beat.
