## Prior Quarter (Q2 2026, reported Aug 6)

- **Sales:** $2.0B, up 4% year over year. Revenue beat was reported at $2.01B against roughly $1.89–1.94B.
- **Adj. EBITDA:** $207M, a 10.3% margin, up 270 bps year over year.
- **Adj. EPS:** $0.19, against consensus that various sources put at $0.64–$0.70. **This is a large miss and conflicts with the EBITDA strength. Verify against the release before relying on it.**
- **Guidance:** Sales raised about $225M to $7.65–7.85B. Adj. EBITDA raised about $25M to $800–850M. Adj. FCF $275–375M. Adj. EPS $1.75–2.25. One source says EPS guidance was cut versus a ~$2.44 consensus; the sources don't agree on whether EPS guidance moved up or down.
- **Other:** $54M of YTD cost savings. About $44M of buybacks in Q2, with roughly $200M planned for 2026. Eaton Mobility deal on track for close in Q1 2027.

## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Adj. EBITDA margin vs. ~10.3% in Q2.** The guidance implies about 10.6% for the full year. Holding or beating this is the main test of the cost program.
2. **Adj. EPS vs. the consensus range.** Given the Q2 miss and the spread in estimates, the reported number could diverge sharply from whatever line the market uses.
3. **FY26 guidance, particularly EPS and FCF.** Any cut to the $1.75–2.25 EPS range would be read negatively. A raise of the EBITDA or sales ranges would help.
4. **Commercial-vehicle demand commentary.** The Q2 raise cited favorable CV demand. Watch for any change in the outlook.
5. **Cost savings progress and FCF conversion.** Watch the savings run-rate beyond the $54M YTD and whether FCF stays inside $275–375M.
6. **Eaton Mobility timing and capital return.** Confirm the Q1 2027 close and buyback pace.

**Bull signals:** Management raises the EBITDA range again, cites CV order strength, and reaffirms buybacks.
**Base signals:** Guidance held, cost-savings targets reiterated, no new charges.
**Bear signals:** EPS guidance cut, FCF range lowered, restructuring or impairment charges, or weaker CV demand language.

1. **Adj. EPS vs. consensus (~$0.65–0.79).** The estimate spread means "beat" or "miss" depends on which number the market uses. Check the whisper.
2. **FY26 EPS guidance ($1.75–2.25).** The buy-side will want confirmation of the midpoint or a narrowing toward the upper half.
