## Company Guidance

From the Q2 2026 release (Sept 9, 2026), [SEC 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0002127043/000212704326000008/q22026earningsrelease.htm):

- **Q2 2026:** Revenue $208M (+10% YoY). Net income $37M, down from $59M a year earlier. This is the item to probe: revenue grew while net income fell by about a third.
- **Q3 2026 guidance:** Same-store sales +3.0% to +4.0%; Adjusted EBITDA growth of at least 13%.
- **FY2026 guidance:** Same-store sales +2.5% to 3.0%; net unit growth at least 8%; Adjusted EBITDA growth at least 20%.
- **Q2 commentary:** The CEO said the same-store sales acceleration "has continued into the third quarter." Q2 same-store sales were +2.3%, driven mainly by transactions.

## Key Metrics to Watch (Ranked)

1. **Same-store sales vs. the 3.0% to 4.0% guide.** Landing above the range supports a raise. Q2 was +2.3%, so Q3 needs a clear step up.
2. **Adjusted EBITDA growth vs. the 13% floor, and margin.** Watch whether it beats the floor and whether the margin is expanding.
3. **Net income and EPS vs. consensus.** Net income fell sharply in Q2 while revenue rose. Find out whether the drop came from one-time items, IPO-related costs, interest, or core margin pressure.
4. **FY2026 guidance changes.** Same-store sales (2.5% to 3.0%), net unit growth (8%+), and Adjusted EBITDA (20%+). A raise on SSS or EBITDA is the main upside lever.
5. **Traffic vs. check.** Q2 growth was transaction-led, which is healthier than price-led growth. Check whether that held in Q3.
6. **Net unit growth and franchisee health.** Openings pace and franchisee economics drive the long-term story.
