## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Q3 EPS vs. $6.39.** The main test. Q2 beat by roughly $0.46–0.56, so a repeat beat would fit the trend, but the bar has moved up.
2. **FY26 EPS guidance vs. $25.00–$25.75.** Whether the range is raised again, held, or narrowed. Given the consensus gap noted above, the buy side will focus on whether guidance holds up.
3. **VITAS revenue and hospice census/average daily census trends.** Drives the core of the business. Not verified for this quarter.
4. **Roto-Rooter revenue and margin.** Sensitive to residential call volumes and pricing. Not verified for this quarter.
5. **Revenue vs. $685M.** Q2 beat by about $8M, so revenue is a secondary test.
6. **Margins and GAAP-to-adjusted EPS gap.** Q2 showed a wide gap ($6.06 vs. $5.13 GAAP). Investors will check whether it is narrowing.
