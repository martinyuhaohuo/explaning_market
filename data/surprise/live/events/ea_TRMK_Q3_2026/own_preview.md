## Context from Q2 2026

- **Reported EPS:** $0.97 vs. $0.96 consensus. Excluding non-routine items (a $73.8M mortgage loan sale and a Visa share exchange, together adding $6.9M to net income), operating EPS was $0.97.
- **Revenue:** $213.4M, a miss.
- **Net interest income (FTE):** $168.6M, up 3.1% QoQ and 4.3% YoY.
- **Net interest margin:** 3.84%, up 3 bps.
- **FY2026 guidance (affirmed):** NIM 3.80–3.85%; mid-single-digit growth in loans and deposits (ex-brokered); mid-single-digit growth in net interest income, noninterest income, and noninterest expense.
- **Credit:** Provisions expected to normalize, closer to Q1 levels than Q2.
- **Beat history:** TRMK beat non-GAAP EPS estimates in each of the last four reported quarters, by roughly 0–8%. Revenue has been mixed.

## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Net interest margin vs. the 3.80–3.85% guide.** This is the main earnings driver. Watch deposit costs and any sign of margin pressure from rate or deposit mix.
2. **Net interest income growth.** Check whether it stays on the mid-single-digit path.
3. **Revenue vs. ~$215M.** Q2 missed on revenue, so this is a key test.
4. **Provision for credit losses.** Confirm the "normalizing" commentary. A higher provision than expected would hurt.
5. **Noninterest income and expense.** Check whether fee income tracks guidance and whether expenses stay in the mid-single-digit range. Q2 included one-time gains, so look for underlying (core) fee trends.
6. **Loan and deposit growth.** Confirm mid-single-digit growth ex-brokered deposits.
7. **FY2026 guidance reaffirmation or change.** The buy-side will focus on whether the NIM range and growth targets hold.
