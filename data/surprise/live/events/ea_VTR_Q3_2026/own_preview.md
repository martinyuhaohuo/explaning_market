## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **SHOP same-store NOI and occupancy.** This is the core growth driver. Watch whether the 16% NOI growth and +300 bps occupancy gains are holding, and whether management extends the occupancy trajectory into Q4.
2. **Normalized FFO/sh vs. consensus.** Track whether the beat pattern continues. Q2 came in at $0.97 against a ~$0.96 estimate.
3. **Full-year guidance direction.** A further raise on the $3.85–$3.90 range would likely be the main positive catalyst. Maintaining the range would read as neutral.
4. **Investment pipeline and funding.** Confirm the $4.5B investment volume, the cost of capital, and whether acquisitions remain accretive given interest expense (~$646M for the year).
5. **Margin and expense trends.** Watch SHOP operating margin and labor/operating cost inflation, which can offset occupancy gains.

## Scenario Analysis

These are my illustrative estimates, not sourced figures.

| Scenario | Revenue | Normalized FFO/sh | Key Driver | Stock Reaction |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Bull** | >$1.80B | ≥$1.02 | SHOP occupancy and rate gains accelerate; FY guide raised above $3.90 | Up 4–6%+ |
| **Base** | ~$1.77B | ~$0.99–$1.00 | In-line print; FY guide reaffirmed or nudged higher | Roughly ±2% |
| **Bear** | <$1.73B | <$0.97 | SHOP occupancy plateaus; costs rise; acquisition pace slows or guide is trimmed | Down 4–6%+ |

**Bull signals:** management raises FY guidance above $3.90, reports occupancy gains continuing into Q4, and gives an accretive 2027 investment outlook.
**Base signals:** reaffirmed guidance, steady SHOP NOI, and no major changes to the investment or funding plan.
**Bear signals:** guidance cut or narrowed to the low end, weaker SHOP NOI growth, or higher financing costs without offsetting investment yields.

**Historical context:** Market Chameleon's undated snapshots show the average post-earnings move over the past four quarters was about ±3.6% to ±4.4%. A 2018 Schaeffer's piece reports the stock rose the day after five of its last eight reports. Those data points are old or undated, so weight them lightly. Per the same source, EPS beats have had little correlation with the next-day move (R² ≈ 0.01), so guidance is likely the stronger driver.

## Catalyst Checklist

1. **SHOP NOI/occupancy vs. expectations:** The strength of this segment has been the main growth story, so any deceleration would matter most.
2. **FY2026 normalized FFO guidance:** Buy-side will look for a raise above the $3.85–$3.90 range.
3. **Q3 normalized FFO vs. ~$0.99:** Confirm the consensus basis first. A beat with guidance held may not move the stock much.
4. **Investment volume and capital plan:** Whether the $4.5B pipeline is still on track and how it is funded.
5. **Narrative shift:** Any changes to the 2027 outlook, dividend, or capital allocation, which are not covered in the sources I found.
