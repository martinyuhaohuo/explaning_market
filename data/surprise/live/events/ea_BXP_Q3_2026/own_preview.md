## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **FFO vs. $1.80–$1.82 guidance and consensus.** A print at or above the top of the range is the baseline for a clean beat.
2. **Full-year FFO guidance ($6.99–$7.05).** Watch for a further raise versus a hold. Since Q2 already beat, the buy-side will look for upside to the top end.
3. **Revenue vs. $866M–$892M.** Q2 was $895.7M, so the lower consensus implies a sequential decline. Confirm the correct figure before reacting to a "miss."
4. **Same-property NOI and occupancy/leasing.** Office leasing volume, renewal spreads, and tenant-retention commentary drive the office REIT narrative.
5. **Balance sheet and capital recycling.** The $700M senior notes offering (Aug 2026) and dispositions or JV activity bear on leverage and the dividend outlook.
