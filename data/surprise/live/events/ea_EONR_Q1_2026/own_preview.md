## Key metrics to watch (ranked)

1. **Liquidity and financing:** cash, going-concern language, new debt or equity. Cash was only $0.13M at March 31.
2. **Production trajectory:** oil volumes against the guided return to Q4 2025 levels in Q2 2026, and the first output from the horizontal San Andres wells.
3. **Horizontal well results:** management said two wells would come online in October with results in November. Compare initial rates with the 400–600 bbl/d target.
4. **Opex and G&A:** management said G&A would fall (insurance, legal, accounting). Q1 G&A rose 33%.
5. **Derivative exposure:** with oil at about $81, hedge marks can swing reported revenue as they did in Q1.
6. **Restatement follow-through:** FY2025 was restated (Oct 9) for a Q4 2025 depletion error, which improved the net loss by $2.6M. Check the controls commentary.
