## Prior Quarter Context (Q3 FY26, quarter ended May 31, 2026)

- Comparable net merchandise sales +10.7% (+6.9% constant currency). FX added about 3.8 points.
- Net merchandise sales $1.45B (+12.5%; +8.5% constant currency). Total revenue $1.48B.
- Average ticket +5.0%; transactions +7.1%.
- Net income $39.7M (+12.9%); diluted EPS $1.28. Operating income $65.6M vs. $56.2M.
- Management said it was "encouraged" by trends heading into the final quarter. No explicit Q4 or full-year guidance was found.

## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Comparable sales growth, constant currency.** Q3 was +6.9%. Whether momentum held is the core question. Watch for a slowdown or acceleration in ticket vs. transactions.
2. **Revenue vs. ~$1.46–1.51B.** The range is wide, so the sources need reconciling before the print.
3. **EPS vs. $1.15–1.27.** The spread is wide enough that a "beat" depends on which consensus the market uses.
4. **Operating margin.** Q3 operating income rose faster than sales. Check whether that held in Q4.
5. **FX impact.** FX was a 3.8-point tailwind in Q3. A smaller tailwind would make the constant-currency number more important.
6. **FY27 outlook and club pipeline.** Watch new openings (Costa Rica club #10 announced with this release; Chile and Costa Rica #11 previously flagged).

**What would need to happen:**
- **Bull:** Transactions keep growing at Q3's pace, ticket stays positive, and operating leverage continues.
- **Base:** Sales growth moderates from Q3 levels but stays solid, with flat-to-modest margin movement.
- **Bear:** Transactions decelerate sharply, or the FX tailwind reverses and cost pressures weigh on margins.

**Commentary signals:**
- Bull: "Momentum continued into the new fiscal year," or upbeat club-opening and margin language.
- Base: Reiterated "encouraged" tone without numerical guidance.
- Bear: Caution on consumer spending, FX, or cost inflation.

## Catalyst Checklist

1. **Comparable sales (constant currency) vs. the Q3 +6.9%.** A sustained level above that supports the bull case.
2. **Revenue and EPS vs. the consensus range.** Reconcile the sources first. The reaction will likely hinge on the higher estimates if the company lands near $1.27.
3. **FY27 outlook.** The buy-side will want at least directional commentary on sales growth, margins, and capex for new clubs.
4. **Ticket vs. transaction mix.** Q3 was driven by transactions (+7.1%). A reversal would be a warning sign.
5. **Capital allocation and club pipeline.** Expansion news (Costa Rica, Chile) and any change to dividend or buyback plans.
