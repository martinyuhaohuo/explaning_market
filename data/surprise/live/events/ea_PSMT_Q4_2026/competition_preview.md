### The "+24.5% growth" bar is easier than it looks

This is the single most mis-read fact in the setup. Q4 FY25 was an artificially depressed base:

| Q4 FY25 drag | Amount | EPS impact |
|---|---|---|
| CFO transition costs | $1.6M | ~$0.04 |
| San Diego HQ relocation | $1.1M | ~$0.02 |
| Tax rate 32.0% vs. 28.4% FY avg. | ~$1.7M | ~$0.05 |
| **Normalized Q4 FY25 EPS** | | **~$1.13–1.14** |

Against a normalized base, consensus $1.27 implies **~+12% EPS growth** — right in line with the 9M FY26 trend of +10%. **Consensus is not heroic; it's a trend-line number.** The risk is not the operating bar, it's what sits below the operating line (see §3).

## 2. Key Metrics to Watch — Ranked

**1. Other expense / FX losses (the whole ballgame).** Q3 FY26's EPS miss was *entirely* below the line: **$10.5M net FX losses** vs. $7.2M a year prior, against +16.7% operating income growth. Q4 FY25 carried $6.4M. At a ~29% tax rate, **every ~$1.4M of incremental FX loss ≈ $0.03/share.** The gap between a $5M and an $11M other-expense line is roughly $1.38 vs. $1.20 — i.e., the difference between a 9% beat and a 5% miss, holding operations constant. Watch specifically **Trinidad**: management flagged TTD conversion as the recurring culprit and disclosed $59.7M of non-readily-convertible TTD cash at FY25 year-end.

**2. Comparable club sales, total *and* constant currency.** Q3 printed +10.7% total / **+6.9% cc** — a ~380bps FX translation tailwind (COP averaged ~3,192/USD in calendar Q3 2026, dramatically stronger YoY). The reported number flatters; the cc number is the real demand signal. Comp base is 54 clubs. Q4 FY25 comp was +7.5%.

**3. The forward comp datapoint.** PSMT gives no formal guidance, so the intra-quarter comp disclosure *is* the guide. On the Q3 call they gave four weeks through June 28: **+11.2% total / +6.5% cc** — a strong Q4 start. Expect a September/October figure this time. **A cc comp starting with a 6 or better is bullish; a 4-handle is the bear trigger.**

**4. Membership economics.** Q3: accounts 2.1M (+8.6%), **12-month renewal rate 90.5% — an all-time high for the second straight quarter**, Platinum penetration 21.3% vs. 16.1%, membership income $25.7M (+17.6%). Membership income is ~100% incremental margin and the highest-quality line in the P&L. Q4 FY25 membership income was $22.6M; +15% implies ~$26M. Any renewal-rate rollover or Platinum stall would hit the multiple harder than an EPS penny.

**5. Gross margin.** Q3: 16.0% of net merchandise sales, +20bps, driven by non-foods mix. Q4 FY25 was 15.7%, flat YoY. Mid-16s would confirm the mix story is durable.

**6. SG&A and pre-opening drag.** Q3 SG&A 13.3% of revenue (vs. 13.2%), with ~10bps from Chile pre-opening. Q4 FY25 was 13.5% but included the $2.7M of one-offs. Chile pre-opening *builds* from here into the spring 2027 launch.

**7. Digital.** Q3: record $99.6M, +26.2%, **6.9% of net merchandise sales**. Crossing $100M and 7% penetration is a clean, quotable milestone.

**8. Club count / capex.** Ended FY26 at **58 clubs** (10th Costa Rica club, Ciudad Quesada, ~42,000 sq ft sales floor, opened August 2026). Pipeline to **63**: Jamaica, Guatemala, 11th Costa Rica, and first Chile (Las Condes, Santiago — mall format, spring 2027). ~$100M capex across several fiscal years for three clubs plus central operations.

## 3. Scenario Analysis

| Scenario | Revenue | EPS | Key Driver | Stock Reaction |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Bull** (25%) | $1.49B (+12%) | **$1.38–1.42** | Comps +10% / cc +7%; GM 16.1% (+40bps); **other expense ≤$5M**; tax ~28%; Sept/Oct cc comp ≥7% | **+6% to +9%** |
| **Base** (50%) | $1.46–1.47B (+10%) | **$1.26–1.30** | Comps +9% / cc +6.5%; GM +20–30bps; other expense $7–8M; tax ~29% | **-1% to +3%** |
| **Bear** (25%) | $1.44B (+8%) | **$1.12–1.18** | cc comps decelerate to ~5%; GM flat; **FX losses ≥$11M** and/or tax >31%; heavier Chile/CR pre-opening | **-7% to -12%** |

**Bull — what must happen:** The FX line normalizes. COP/CRC strength finally shows up as a *translation tailwind without a transaction offset*, and Trinidad conversion costs don't repeat at Q3 scale. Operating income ~$66–68M (+25%+). *Management tell:* unprompted framing of the FX line as "transitory" or a stated Trinidad remediation; a Sept/Oct cc comp with a 7-handle.

**Base — what must happen:** The pattern of the last eight quarters simply repeats — **revenue beats, EPS lands at or just under consensus.** Note the record: PSMT has beaten consensus EPS in only **2 of the last 8 quarters** while beating revenue in essentially all of them. Modest revenue upside is the single highest-confidence call in this preview (consensus $1.46B looks ~1–2% light if the FX tailwind held and net merch grew ~11%). *Management tell:* FX described as "ongoing volatility," no quantified fix.

**Bear — what must happen:** Another double-digit FX loss, or the tax rate falls into minimum-tax positions again as it did in Q4 FY25 (32%). Critically, **the bear case does not require an operational miss** — that's what makes it live.

## 4. Catalyst Checklist

1. **Other expense / net FX loss line vs. ~$7M** — the binary determinant of beat vs. miss. Three straight quarters of below-the-line leakage have conditioned the buy side to discount the operating beat. Sensitivity: ~$0.03 EPS per $1.4M.
2. **Constant-currency comp vs. ~+6.5%** — strips out the COP/CRC translation flattery. This is the number that defends a 30x multiple; the reported total comp is not.
3. **Sept/Oct intra-quarter comp disclosure** — PSMT's only guidance substitute. Last time: +11.2%/+6.5% cc. A cc print with a 4-handle reframes the whole FY27 model.
4. **Renewal rate and Platinum mix** — 90.5% and 21.3% are both records. Membership income is the multiple's justification; a crack here is a de-rating event, not an earnings event.
5. **Chile/FY27 capex and pre-opening cadence** — $100M program, spring 2027 Santiago opening. The buy side wants quantified FY27 pre-opening drag. Vagueness here, combined with 30x, invites multiple compression.
6. **Tax rate vs. ~29%** — the minimum-tax dynamic that cost $0.05 in Q4 FY25 is a repeatable Q4-specific risk.

**Read:** The stock has already de-rated ~14% off its high into the print, which cushions the bear case somewhat — this is not a setup where positioning is euphoric. But at ~32x FY26E there is **no valuation floor** under a Trinidad-driven EPS miss, and the left-tail history confirms it. The combination of 5.8% short interest against ~235K ADV means a clean beat on the FX line has genuine squeeze fuel; the same thin liquidity amplifies a disappointment.

**Positioning view.** The asymmetry is unattractive for a long into the print: the highest-confidence element (revenue beat) is the element the market has repeatedly refused to pay for, while the low-confidence element (the FX line) is the one that sets the move. **Preferred expression: own the Tuesday-noon call, not the Monday-close release.** The 20-hour information gap means Tuesday morning reprices on an EPS headline with no context — if that headline misses on an FX line while cc comps and renewal rates hold, the Tuesday-morning gap down is a buying opportunity into the call, not a thesis break. Conversely, with the estimated earnings-day implied move (~5%) sitting below my bull and bear scenario magnitudes (+6–9% / -7–12%) and well above the base case (-1% to +3%), **options look modestly cheap relative to the tail scenarios and expensive relative to the modal outcome** — favoring long-gamma structures over outright directional premium selling.

**Thesis-break signals (not earnings noise):** renewal rate below 90%, Platinum penetration flat sequentially, or constant-currency comps below 5%. Everything else on this list is a quarter, not a story.
