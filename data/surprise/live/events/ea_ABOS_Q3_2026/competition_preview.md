## 1. The setup in one paragraph

This is not an earnings story. ABOS is a pre-revenue, clinical-stage Alzheimer's company whose entire equity value rests on **ALTITUDE-AD** — the 542-patient Phase 2 of sabirnetug (ACU193, anti-amyloid-beta-oligomer mAb) with topline guided to "late 2026," i.e. plausibly within weeks of this print. The P&L is a burn-rate disclosure. What matters on Monday is (a) **any change in the readout timing language**, (b) the **cash balance and runway/going-concern wording**, and (c) whether management pre-commits to how it funds itself through and after the data. The stock enters the print at $1.99 — below its 50-DMA ($2.55) and 200-DMA ($2.48), ~40% below the March 2026 private placement price of $3.30, and near the low end of its 52-week range.

## 3. What to watch — ranked by what actually moves the stock

**1. ALTITUDE-AD topline timing language (90% of the information content).**
Guidance has drifted before: "2H 2026" (Mar 2025) → "2026" (Nov 2025) → "late 2026," reconfirmed at the September 16, 2026 investor day. Listen for whether "late 2026" becomes a *month* ("December"), stays verbatim, or softens to "around year-end" / "by early 2027." A narrowing is a modest positive; any softening is the bear case and is worth far more than the EPS line. Note that the three **CTAD posters (Nov 16–19, Boston) are baseline characteristics and patient/caregiver experience only — not efficacy**, so CTAD is not the readout venue and should not be confused for one.

**2. Cash, runway, and the going-concern paragraph.**
The Q2 10-Q stated plainly: *"we have concluded that substantial doubt exists about our ability to continue as a going concern,"* with cash funding operations only "into early 2027." Does the Q3 filing repeat, escalate, or retire that language? With ~$80M of cash against ~$21M/quarter of clean burn, the arithmetic supports more than "early 2027," so a runway *extension* to "into 2H 2027" is a realistic positive surprise and would be read as management buying itself the option to raise *after* data rather than before.

**3. Financing posture into the readout.**
The untouched ATM is the tell. Raising equity at $1.99 — versus the $3.30 March placement — is deeply unattractive, which is precisely why the market fears it. Also in the capital structure: a **$31.6M K2 HealthVentures term loan** (greater of 9.65% or Prime+1.15%, matures Nov 1, 2027, extendable to Nov 2028 on financing milestones, $1.6M final payment fee, $20M undrawn, up to $2.5M convertible at $2.53, plus a 730,769-share warrant at $1.95). Any commentary on drawing the remaining $20M tranche or amending the facility is materially better for equity holders than an ATM at $2.

**4. Readout content and analysis plan.**
Topline is expected to carry **iADRS (primary)**, CDR-SB, safety including **ARIA rates**, and key biomarkers across 35 mg/kg and 50 mg/kg IV Q4W vs. placebo over 18 months. Watch for any pre-specification detail (subgroup/ApoE4 handling, powering assumptions, whether both doses are pooled) and for confirmation that no blinded safety signal has surfaced. Management cannot say much here, but *how* they frame expectations calibrates the post-data reaction function.

**5. EBD program and partnership optionality.**
ACU301/ACU401 (JCR J-Brain Cargo, low-volume subcutaneous, up to 40-fold greater frontal cortex exposure in NHPs) with **IND targeted mid-2027**, and up to $555M of JCR milestones owed. A partnership or non-dilutive funding announcement on this portfolio would be the highest-value non-data catalyst available to them.

**Operationally, what each requires.** *Bull* needs the database lock to be done or imminent — that is what lets a CFO put a month on the calendar — plus a credible statement that they can reach topline and digest it without an immediate raise. *Base* is the default: a company this close to a binary readout says as little as possible. *Bear* does not require bad trial news; it only requires ambiguity about *when*, because at $2.00 with a going-concern flag the market reads delay as forced dilution at a worse price.

**Historical context.** ABOS prints are typically non-events: median earnings-day move **−1.51%**, middle 50% of reactions between **−6.67% and +2.00%**, only 44% closing higher. Q2 2026 (Aug 12): +0.45% in the first five minutes, **+4.15%** by close *despite* the $0.09 EPS miss — direct evidence that the market correctly ignored the JCR charge and traded the pipeline commentary. Q1 2026 (May 12): −2.33% early, **−3.88%** by close on an EPS beat. **The EPS line has no demonstrated signal value in this name.** Prior four quarters: Q3'25 $(0.44) vs $(0.66)E; Q4'25 $(0.41) vs $(0.53)E; Q1'26 $(0.33) vs $(0.49)E; Q2'26 $(0.45) vs $(0.36)E.

## 5. Catalyst checklist

- [ ] **Topline timing language** vs. "late 2026" — the only line in the release that can re-rate the stock by double digits on its own.
- [ ] **Cash balance vs. my ~$80M** — a print below ~$75M implies either higher-than-modelled trial close-out costs or working-capital drag, and tightens the financing clock.
- [ ] **Runway sentence + going-concern paragraph** — "into early 2027" unchanged = base case; any extension = bull; any shortening = bear.
- [ ] **ATM / term-loan activity in Q3** — check the 10-Q cover share count (72,317,285 as of Aug 7) and the financing section. A higher count means they sold stock near the lows.
- [ ] **Readout content & venue framing** — confirmation that CTAD is baseline data only and topline is a standalone release; any detail on the pre-specified analysis.
- [ ] **EBD/JCR partnership commentary** — non-dilutive capital is the one thing that defuses the whole bear case.

**Bottom line:** fade the EPS beat. Position for the language on timing and runway, and recognize that Monday is a risk-management checkpoint ahead of the real binary event rather than the event itself.
