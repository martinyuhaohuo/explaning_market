## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Organic constant-currency revenue growth:** Is it holding near 10% (Q2) or decelerating toward the 8–9% FY guide? The FY guide implies slower growth in the back half.
2. **FY EPS guidance:** Does management raise again above $4.83, hold, or narrow? A second raise would be a clear positive signal.
3. **Adjusted EBITDA margin:** Is cost discipline keeping margins expanding alongside revenue?
4. **U.S. markets vs. international:** Segment mix and the U.S. financial services and consumer-credit demand trend.
5. **Free cash flow and leverage:** Cash conversion and capital deployment (buybacks or M&A).
