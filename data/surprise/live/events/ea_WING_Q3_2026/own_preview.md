## Key Metrics to Watch (Ranked)

1. **Domestic same-store sales.** This is the central issue. Q2 was −7.5%, and FY guidance is −4% to −6%. Holding that range requires Q3 and Q4 to improve on Q2. A Q3 print near −7% or worse makes the full-year guide look hard to reach.
2. **Full-year SSS guidance.** A cut below −4% to −6%, or a reversal toward the low end, is the most likely source of a negative reaction.
3. **Unit growth.** Guidance is 15–16% global unit growth, with Q4 the largest openings quarter. Q2 had 102 net openings. Watch whether Q3 openings keep pace.
4. **Adjusted EBITDA margin.** Q2 EBITDA grew 12.5% on 6.4% revenue growth, so margins expanded. Watch whether that holds with traffic down.
5. **SG&A and below-the-line items.** Guidance is SG&A of $140–143M (including $3M restructuring), SBC of about $24M, D&A of about $33M, and net interest of about $43M. Changes here matter less than the top items.
