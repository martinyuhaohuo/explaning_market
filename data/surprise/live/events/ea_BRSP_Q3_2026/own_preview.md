## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)
1. **Distributable EPS vs. ~$0.11**. This is the headline number. Q2 came in at $0.12 DE and $0.13 adjusted, so a print at or above $0.11 would be in line with recent trend.
2. **Dividend coverage.** Compare DE per share with the current dividend. A payout not covered by DE tends to drive the reaction more than the EPS headline.
3. **Book value trend.** Q2 NBV was $6.81. A further decline would weigh on the stock, especially with buybacks ongoing.
4. **Loan originations and portfolio growth.** Look for net positive funding and the pace of redeployment from the lease sale.
5. **Credit quality.** Watch-list and REO balances, plus any new impairments or CECL reserve changes. GAAP losses this quarter would be read against credit trends.
6. **Buyback pace.** Management is already buying back heavily. Any update on the program's size or remaining authorization matters.
