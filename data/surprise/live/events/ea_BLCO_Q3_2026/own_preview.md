## Prior Quarter and Guidance (Q2 FY2026, reported Jul 29, 2026)

Sources: [company release via BioSpace](https://www.biospace.com/press-releases/bausch-lomb-delivers-broad-based-second-quarter-growth-raises-full-year-2026-guidance) and [Stockwatch](https://wwww.stockwatch.com/News/Item/Z-C!BLCO-3845482/C/BLCO).

- Revenue was $1.394B, up 9% reported and about 8% constant currency. Q2 beat the ~$1.37B consensus.
- Adj. EBITDA was $241M ($246M ex-acquired IPR&D). GAAP net loss was $14M.
- Pharmaceuticals grew 15% to $354M, driven by MIEBO and XIIDRA. Premium IOLs were 13% of Surgical revenue, up from 6%.
- Operating cash flow was $153M, versus $35M a year earlier.
- **FY2026 guidance raised:**
  - Revenue: $5.44–5.54B (was $5.42–5.52B).
  - Adj. EBITDA ex-IPR&D: $1.025–1.075B (was $1.010–1.060B).
  - FX tailwind trimmed to $45M from $50M.

## Recent Developments

- **CEO exit, Oct 12, 2026:** Brent Saunders left and Chairman Thomas Appio became interim CEO. The company gave no reason. Appio is also CEO of parent Bausch Health, which owns about 87% of BLCO. Reported share drops range from about 9% to about 10%, with some sources citing 5–7% intraday. RTTNews put the close at $15.72, down 9.13% ([RTTNews](https://www.rttnews.com/story.aspx?Id=3698243); [Benzinga](https://www.benzinga.com/node/62308532)).
- **Saunders' separation:** A reported $75.5M lump sum, with equity awards forfeited ([Panabee](https://www.panabee.com/news/bausch-lomb-ceo-brent-saunders-departs-company-raises-2026-guidance)).
- **Guidance signal:** Coverage says the companies expect to raise 2026 adj. EBITDA guidance with Q3 results. This is unconfirmed beyond press coverage.
- **Parent overhang:** Evercore ISI flagged what Bausch Health does with its controlling stake as a watch item.

**Bull signals:** Management raises the EBITDA guide, names a permanent CEO timeline, and gives Q4 color that goes beyond the FY range.
**Base signals:** Guidance is reaffirmed or nudged up, with no new strategy changes.
**Bear signals:** Guidance is cut or held despite the expected raise, or management gives vague commentary on the CEO search or the parent's stake.
