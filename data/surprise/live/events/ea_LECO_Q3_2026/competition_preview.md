## 3. Key Metrics to Watch — Ranked

**1. Volume vs. price decomposition (most important).** Q2 FY2026 delivered +10.1% organic with Americas volume +$49.7M — the **first volume expansion in over two years** after roughly nine quarters of compression. Guidance frames FY organic as ~⅓ volume, ⅔ price. Price comps harden materially in 2H because tariff-driven pricing was already in the Q3'25 base. If volume isn't visibly accelerating, the growth rate mechanically decays and the multiple is not defensible.

**2. Americas Welding adj. EBIT margin vs. the 19–20% 2H guide.** Q2 printed 19.7%; Q3'25 was 18.2%. Below 19% reads as mix/price-cost slippage, not seasonality.

**3. Harris Products margin normalization.** Q2's 20.4% adj. EBIT included ~100bp from **tariff refunds that management explicitly told investors not to extrapolate**. The 2H guide is 18–19%. Harris is where a clean-looking revenue beat can come with an ugly margin print — watch the quality of the beat here specifically.

**4. FY2026 guidance action.** Currently low-double-digit net sales growth (raised from high-single-digit in July). Reaffirm vs. raise vs. trim sets the Q4 implied number. A reaffirm at ~$11.18 FY EPS after a Q3 beat implicitly *cuts* Q4.

**5. Price-cost and the LIFO drag.** Guided to neutral in 2H after -10bp in Q2; FY LIFO headwind ~$10M. Q2's gross margin actually *fell* 50bp YoY to 36.8% despite the revenue beat — this was a primary reason the stock sold off on a 4.6% EPS beat.

**6. Automation orders, backlog and XpertLINC.** Management cited **record automation backlog** across all segments, ~80% Americas-weighted, and a high-single to low-double-digit trajectory. **XpertLINC**, the physical-AI robotic welding platform launched Oct 20, 2026, is the first genuine narrative-expansion lever in years — any order or pipeline quantification is upside optionality not in numbers.

**7. International Welding toward 10–11%.** Q2 at 10.6% adj.; the chronic underperformer. Europe remains the structural drag, plus a flagged **$6–7M quarterly Middle East conflict headwind**.

**8. Cash conversion.** 149% in Q3'25, 138% in Q2'26, FY target 100%. Capex $110–130M FY.

| **Bear** (25%) | $1.14–1.16B (+7–9%) | $2.70–2.78 | Harris price roll-off bites; price-cost turns negative; International misses 10%; automation orders digest; FY trimmed toward high-single-digit | **-6% to -10%** |

### What each scenario requires operationally

**Bull** needs the Americas volume line to be *larger* than Q2's +$49.7M, confirming the recovery is broadening rather than a restock. Management tell: quantified automation order growth, a raised FY range, "seventh/eighth consecutive month of favorable macro data," and transportation turning positive after Q2's mid-single-digit decline.

**Base** needs the Q2 pattern to simply repeat — price carrying revenue, volume positive but not accelerating, margins inside the guided bands. Management tell: guidance "reaffirmed," incremental margin language unchanged at mid-20%.

**Bear** needs the price contribution to fade faster than volume fills in. Management tell: hedged language on price-cost, a second consecutive YoY gross margin decline, any walk-back of the 2H Americas 19–20% band, or new caveats on tariff refunds.

## 5. Catalyst Checklist

1. **Americas volume dollars vs. Q2's +$49.7M** — the single number that determines whether the volume-inflection thesis survives contact with a hard price comp.
2. **Americas Welding adj. EBIT margin vs. 19–20%** — the guide management put its credibility behind in July; a miss here is a guidance-integrity event, not a rounding issue.
3. **Harris adj. EBIT vs. 18–19%** — tests whether Q2's tariff-refund benefit was correctly ring-fenced. The most likely source of a low-quality beat.
4. **FY2026 guidance language and the implied Q4** — a raise validates durability; a reaffirm after a beat is a stealth Q4 cut and likely sells off.
5. **Automation order/backlog quantification + XpertLINC pipeline** — the only live multiple-expansion narrative; unquantified in consensus.
6. **Gross margin YoY direction** — two consecutive declines would confirm the bear read that price-led growth is margin-dilutive.
