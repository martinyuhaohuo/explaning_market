## Recent Context

- **Q2 2026 (reported Jul 27):** Core FFO of $1.84 beat the $1.76 estimate and exceeded the top of management's guidance by $0.05. Consolidated net loss was $8.08 per diluted share, driven by a non-cash $1.1B valuation allowance on the UK business held for sale. Q2 revenue was reported at $484.6M, well below the figure one Benzinga table shows as the estimate, so the revenue comparison is suspect.
- **UK exit:** The Park Holidays UK sale is expected to close in 2H26, pending regulatory approval. Guidance does not include its effect. Watch for closing timing and any proceeds or deleveraging commentary.

## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Core FFO vs. guidance and consensus.** A beat is likely given the Q2 pattern, but the Q3 range is narrow, so the bar is tight.
2. **Same-property NOI and occupancy/rate growth (MH and RV).** Is the raised 4.5%–5.3% range holding up? Watch for RV seasonality and renewal rate increases.
3. **FY2026 Core FFO guidance.** Another raise is plausible if Q3 beats. A raise driven only by the UK sale would be lower quality.
4. **Balance sheet and capital allocation.** Net debt, leverage, UK sale proceeds, and buyback or acquisition pace.
5. **UK sale timing and use of proceeds.** Any update on the regulatory timeline is a catalyst.

**What management would need to say for each case:**
- **Bull:** "Ahead of plan on rate," raised NOI outlook, UK sale closing soon, and capital redeployment.
- **Base:** Reiterated guidance and steady occupancy, with commentary that the UK exit is near.
- **Bear:** Softer RV bookings, slower MH renewal increases, a UK delay, or a leverage concern.

## Catalyst Checklist

1. **Core FFO vs. ~$2.28 midpoint and the $2.33 top of the range.** Beats above the top of the range are what the stock has been rewarded for.
2. **Same-property NOI and rate growth vs. the raised 4.5%–5.3% range.** Buy-side will focus on whether the guide was raised for the right reasons.
3. **FY2026 Core FFO guidance vs. the $7.10 top end and ~$6.94 consensus.** A further raise is the main upside lever.
4. **UK sale status and proceeds.** Closing timing and the use of proceeds (debt paydown vs. buybacks).
5. **Narrative: capital allocation.** Any new acquisition or buyback program, or changes to the dividend.
