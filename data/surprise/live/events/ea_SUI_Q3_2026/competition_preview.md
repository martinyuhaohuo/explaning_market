## 1. The Setup in One Paragraph

This is the first quarter SUI reports as a **pure-play North American MH/RV REIT** — the £768M/~$1.03B Park Holidays UK sale to Aermont Capital closed Sept 22, 2026. The P&L optics will be ugly and largely meaningless: headline revenue is down ~19% YoY because UK sits in discontinued operations, and GAAP EPS is distorted by the $1.1B non-cash valuation allowance taken in Q2. **Core FFO is the only number that matters**, and management guided Q3 to $2.23–$2.33. The stock sits ~13% below its 52-week high with sell-side targets being trimmed (RBC $154→$147, Barclays $147→$143, Wells Fargo $141→$136) — expectations are de-risked going in. The real event is not Q3; it's what management says about the **2027 "clean" earnings base** and **how $1.03B of UK cash gets deployed**.

## 3. Key Metrics to Watch (Ranked)

1. **RV same-property NOI (highest variance).** FY guide is **0.0%–1.8%**; Q2 was **−0.7%** and Q3'25 was **−1.1%**. Q3 is peak transient season, so this quarter determines whether the FY RV number lands at the top or bottom of the range. Management said on the Q2 call that pacing was "solidly within expectations" with record inquiry capture — that claim gets marked to market here.
2. **MH same-property NOI and rent growth.** The engine: +8.8% in Q2, +10.1% in Q3'25, FY guide 5.7%–6.7%. Revenue growth is site-rent driven (+6.2% in Q2). Watch for **2027 rent increase notices**, typically sent in the fall — the best forward indicator for 2027 MH revenue.
3. **Core FFO vs. the $2.23–$2.33 guide**, and whether FY is raised versus merely narrowed.
4. **UK proceeds deployment + leverage.** Net debt/TTM Recurring EBITDA was **3.9x** at 6/30 on $4.1B debt (3.3% weighted-avg rate, 6.9-yr maturity). ~$1.03B of cash could take that to ~3.0x. YTD through Sept 21: **3.5M shares repurchased for ~$425M** against a $1B authorization running to May 2027. Quoted acquisition yields of "low-to-mid 4%" sit below SUI's own implied cap rate, which argues for more buyback.
5. **Occupancy.** Same-property adjusted blended occupancy 98.8% in Q2 (−10 bps YoY); MH/annual-RV blended 97.9% (−20 bps); annual RV 100.0%. Also watch **annual-vs-transient conversions** (~100 net in Q2) — conversions flatter annual revenue, but management has signaled it over-converted previously and is now optimizing mix rather than maximizing.
6. **CFO search.** Still open as of the Q2 call (Fernando Castro-Caratini in seat; search "progressing well"). A named permanent CFO alongside a credible 2027 bridge would be a governance positive under new CEO Charles Young.

## 4. Scenario Analysis

| Scenario | Core FFO/sh | Revenue | Key Driver | Stock Reaction |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Bull** (~25%) | **≥$2.33** (beats high end) | $575M+ | RV same-prop NOI turns **positive (+1.5% or better)**; MH NOI ≥6.5%; FY raised to **$7.08–$7.12**; management commits majority of UK proceeds to buyback at ~17x FFO and frames 2027 Core FFO growth of 4–6% off a clean base | **+4% to +7%** |
| **Base** (~50%) | **$2.28–$2.32** | $560–572M | MH strong, RV roughly flat (0% to +1%); FY narrowed to the **upper half ($7.02–$7.10)**; occupancy stable; proceeds commentary stays non-committal ("thoughtful allocation") | **−1% to +2.5%** |
| **Bear** (~25%) | **≤$2.25** | <$558M | RV same-prop NOI negative again, pushing FY RV to the 0% floor; adjusted blended occupancy breaks **below 98.5%**; FY trimmed toward **$6.94–$7.02**; $1B sits in cash while 2027 UK dilution math lands worse than modeled | **−5% to −8%** |

**Operationally, what has to be true:** The bull case requires transient RV revenue to have inflected in the peak summer quarter — not just "stable pacing" but actual rate and occupancy gains — plus continued expense discipline (payroll, utilities, taxes all helped Q2). The bear case is RV posting a third consecutive negative same-property NOI quarter, which would reframe a large minority of the NOI base as structurally no-growth and make the 17x multiple hard to defend.

**Management tells:** Watch for "narrowing" vs. "raising" on FY guidance; any quantified 2027 dilution figure from UK + RV dispositions; and whether they offer a **pro-forma run-rate Core FFO** for the pure-play entity. Refusing to quantify 2027 dilution is a bear tell.

## 5. Catalyst Checklist

1. **Core FFO vs. $2.28 midpoint / $2.30+ whisper** — after two straight high-end beats, in-line is a mild miss.
2. **RV same-property NOI print** — the swing factor; peak-season proof-of-concept for the mix-optimization strategy.
3. **FY26 guidance action** — a raise to ≥$7.08 confirms the Q2 inflection; a trim is the bear trigger.
4. **UK proceeds allocation** — buyback (accretive; signals management thinks the stock is cheap) vs. 4%-cap-rate M&A (dilutive against a ~5.9% implied FFO yield) vs. debt paydown. Buy-side wants buyback.
5. **Preliminary 2027 framing** — 2027 MH rent notices, the clean post-UK earnings base, and a permanent CFO.
