## 2. Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Revenue vs. ~$39M guidance-implied pacing.** A "beat" against $29.9M that still prints below $36M is a guidance miss in disguise. This is the single highest-information number in the release.
2. **FY2026 guidance treatment.** The $135M–$165M range is absurdly wide ($30M, or ~20% of the midpoint) this late in the year. Narrowing it is the real news; *where* they narrow it is the trade. Affirming the full wide range for a third straight quarter is itself a soft negative.
3. **Gross margin and GenAI mix.** GM has re-rated hard on Ask Sage: 21.3% → 34.0% (Q1) and 25.0% → 32.8% (Q2). Is 33%+ structural? Watch for any **Ask Sage ARR disclosure** — guided to ~$25M ARR for 2025 at acquisition (~6x YoY), and management has still not given a post-close update. Disclosure would be a bull signal; continued silence is a tell.
4. **Backlog and the $225M award caveat.** Backlog: ~$247M (YE25) → $281.9M (Q1, boosted by a $53M sole-source classified prime) → **$269.6M (Q2, −4% QoQ)**. Note it was $376M at Q3 FY2025 — the YoY trend is down sharply. On Oct 13 the company announced **>$225M in awards since July** (>25 federal GenAI awards: FBI, NASA, USCG, DOE, VA; CENTCOM/SOUTHCOM; classified intel; CBP recompetes with SAIC and Unissant) — but explicitly flagged as **total potential value including unexercised options and ceiling**, not funded. How much lands in reported backlog is the credibility test.
5. **Adjusted EBITDA trajectory and cash burn.** Adj. EBITDA is going the *wrong* way despite margin expansion: $(7.0)M → $(9.9)M (Q1), $(8.5)M → $(11.6)M (Q2); H1 $(21.5)M. Q2 SG&A was $31.8M vs. $21.5M. Cash/investments: $456.6M (9/30/25) → $431.5M → **$409.8M (6/30/26)**, ~$22M/qtr. Debt is negligible (D/E 0.01), so there's no solvency issue — but any commentary on deploying the ~$400M into "accretive M&A" (a stated H2 priority) cuts both ways.

### Bottom line

The vendor consensus spread means **this print will likely be reported as a "big beat" even in my base case** — anyone anchored to $29.9M gets a +25% revenue surprise on a result that merely tracks the guidance midpoint. The durable question is whether a company guiding to 17% midpoint growth can show *operating leverage*: gross margin is up ~800–1,280 bps YoY, yet adjusted EBITDA losses widened in both H1 quarters. Guidance treatment and the funded portion of the $225M in October awards matter more than the headline. With 34% of the float short, almost no borrow, bullishly skewed options, and a stock that already bounced 11% off a 52-week low *before* the print, the downside tail has gotten fatter today, not thinner.
