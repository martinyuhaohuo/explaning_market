1. **Q3 revenue vs. ~$1.63–$1.65B consensus.** Revenue has missed in recent quarters, so this matters more than the EPS print.
2. **FY26 guidance (revenue $6.2–$6.5B, EBITDA $500–$560M).** The buy-side will want to hear that the range holds despite the Q2 cut.
