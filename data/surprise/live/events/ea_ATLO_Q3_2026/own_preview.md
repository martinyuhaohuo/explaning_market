## Key Metrics to Watch (Ranked)

1. **Net interest margin:** Does the Q2 expansion (2.65% → 3.18%) hold? A reversal would be the biggest negative surprise, since the gain came from funding costs and yields.
2. **EPS vs. ~$0.68:** Q2 beat by ~$0.02. A repeat beat would be consistent with the trend; a miss would be read against the NIM story.
3. **Loan growth:** Loans have been shrinking YoY. Any return to growth would be a positive narrative shift.
4. **Credit quality (ag exposure):** Third-party commentary flagged rising problem loans and substandard loans tied to specific agricultural relationships. The sources conflict on magnitude (one says substandard-impaired loans rose 31% to $19.1M, another says substandard loans reached $51M), so check the company's own 10-Q figures for nonperforming loans and provision expense.
5. **Noninterest income and efficiency ratio:** Not researched; check against the prior quarter.
6. **Dividend and capital:** Not researched.
