## 3. The Actual Question This Print Answers

Travelzoo is mid-transition from a legacy travel-advertising/commerce model to a paid subscription club ($50/yr, $1 30-day trial). The company front-loads acquisition cost and recognizes the fee ratably over 12 months — so reported EPS is depressed by design while deferred revenue builds. Management quantified it: ~$(0.60) EPS drag in 2026 for ~$1.20 of claimed 2027 accretion as cohorts hit zero-cost renewal.

**Where the bodies are buried:** membership growth has been masking an eroding core. Backing membership fees out of reported revenue:

| | Q3'25 | Q1'26 | Q2'26 | Q3'26E (base) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Advertising & commerce | $18.6M | $19.7M | $18.2M | **~$17.1M** |
| — YoY | ~flat | **-5%** | **-13%** | **-8%** |
| Membership fees | $3.6M | $4.6M | $5.0M | **~$6.0M** |
| Total revenue | $22.2M | $24.3M | $23.2M | **~$23.1M** |
| GAAP op income | $0.5M | $3.4M | **$(2.8)M** | **~$(1.2)M** |
| EPS | $0.01 | $0.23 | **$(0.21)** | **~$(0.10)** |

Membership revenue series (company slides): 1.2 / 1.1 / 1.2 / 1.4 / 1.6 / 2.4 / 3.0 / 3.6 / 4.1 / 4.6 / 5.0 from Q4'23 → Q2'26. Sequential adds have been +$0.4–0.5M; Q2'26 delivered record trial starts on doubled marketing, so **Q3 should show an accelerated step-up (+$0.6M or better) or the acquisition spend didn't work.**

To hit $22.97M consensus, membership must add ~$2.4M YoY while advertising/commerce declines no worse than ~8%. That is the whole print in one sentence.

## 4. Key Metrics to Watch — Ranked

1. **Advertising & commerce revenue (derived, or from the 10-Q mix table).** Q2 was -13% YoY. A third consecutive deceleration means the subscription build is a treadmill, not a transition. Need ≥ -8%.
2. **Membership fee revenue, sequential.** ≥$5.6M (+$0.6M QoQ) validates Q2's $4.6M marketing burst; ≤$5.3M says acquisition efficiency broke.
3. **Deferred revenue.** $13.4M at 6/30 (from $8.7M at 12/31/25). This is the forward-revenue bank *and* the cash source funding marketing. Needs to clear ~$14M. A flat or down balance is the single most bearish line in the release.
4. **Marketing spend and the stance on it.** Q2 S&M was $13.7M (59% of revenue), with member-acquisition advertising ~$4.6–5.5M. Consensus at -$0.09 implicitly assumes ~$1M+ of marketing moderation. Management said on the Q2 call: *"As long as our investments provide positive ROI and a relatively quick payback, we will continue to invest at these levels."* **If they hold the line, EPS likely prints worse than consensus even on in-line revenue.** This is the main source of asymmetry.
5. **Cash and the buyback.** $6.8M cash (plus $0.75M restricted) at 6/30, down from $10.0M at year-end after $5.2M of H1 buybacks; working capital deficit $14.3M; stockholders' deficit $(6.6)M; Q2 operating cash flow $(1.7)M; no disclosed credit facility. 300K shares remain on the March 2026 authorization. Cash in the $5M handle plus continued repurchases would be an aggressive posture worth questioning on the call.
6. **Travelzoo META launch** — promised for Q3'26 as an exclusive club benefit. Delivered or slipped.
7. **Paying-member and renewal disclosure.** Club Members were cited at ~4.7M vs. ~2.3M a year earlier on the Q2 materials, but *paying*-member counts and renewal rates are not in the 10-Q — and the quarterly member-count series on the slides is not clearly labeled (I could not reconcile it against the "~17.2 / ~30.0+" projection figures, which appear to be member counts rather than revenue). The first real renewal cohort should be maturing now; actual renewal-rate disclosure would be the most thesis-relevant new datapoint management could offer. Treat the $1.20 2027 accretion claim as unverified until they show renewal math.

## 7. Call Script — Five Questions

1. What was advertising-and-commerce revenue growth ex-membership, and when does it stop declining?
2. What is the actual renewal rate on the first cohorts to hit the 12-month mark, and how does it compare to the assumption inside the $1.20 2027 EPS figure?
3. Will marketing stay at Q2 levels in Q4 and into 2027, and what is the trigger to cut it?
4. With $6.8M of cash at 6/30, a $14.3M working capital deficit and no disclosed facility, what is the minimum cash level at which buybacks stop?
5. Did Travelzoo META launch in Q3 as stated, and what is its measured effect on trial conversion?

---

**Bottom line:** the skew is to the downside on the reported EPS line — consensus at $(0.09) requires marketing moderation that management explicitly declined to promise — but the skew on the *stock* is more balanced because $5.08 near 52-week lows already discounts a bad print. The bull trigger is narrow and specific: advertising/commerce stabilizing at better than -8% YoY with deferred revenue above $14M. Absent that, membership growth is just re-pricing the same shrinking user base, and the 2027 accretion story loses its base.
