## 2. What to Watch — Ranked

**1. Uninsured admissions growth (the whole thesis).** Q1 uninsured admissions +15.6%; Q2 exchange-sourced adjusted admissions −15% with uninsured +26,500 equivalent admissions 1H. Management's original assumption was that 80–85% of exchange losers would land on other coverage; actuals ran ~100% to uninsured ("these patients migrated almost one for one to uninsured" — Hazen). FY exchange headwind was raised from $(600–900)M to **$(1.0–1.2)B**. The single most important disclosed number tomorrow is whether uninsured growth decelerates below ~10%. Watch the three concentrated divisions — Gulf Coast, North Florida, South Atlantic — which ran −25% to −28% on exchange volume and account for roughly half the total impact.

**2. Elective surgery trajectory.** The cleanest read on affordability-driven demand destruction. 1H elective inpatient surgery **−6%** (vs. −2% in 2025); Q2 outpatient surgery −3.4%, inpatient surgery −2.3%. Emergent inpatient surgery (two-thirds of cases) held +2%. If electives stay down 5–6%, the mix problem is structural, not transitory. Consensus models outpatient cases −1.3%.

**3. Medicaid supplemental payments / Florida DPP cadence.** Guidance assumes **$300–500M net benefit** for FY2026 (revised up from a $(50–250)M drag), but management explicitly flagged a **$100–300M headwind in 2H** as prior-year retro recognition laps. Any 1H-style "surprise" accrual will be treated as low-quality by the buy side. Also watch the revenue-quality optics: salaries & benefits was 41.0% of revenue in Q2 vs. 43.6% in Q3'25 — much of that "improvement" is DPP revenue inflating the denominator.

**4. FY2026 guidance action.** The $28.70–30.50 EPS range is unusually wide with one quarter left ($14.77 booked in 1H). Narrowing with the low end lifted toward ~$29.25+ reads as a modest positive; holding the full range reads as a dodge; any cut is a disaster given the 3-month move.

**5. Cost curve / resiliency program.** Same-facility cost per equivalent admission was essentially flat YoY ex-supplemental payments, −1.4% sequentially. Marks guided that **Q4 adj. EBITDA growth should exceed Q3's** and that the resiliency programs may "bend the cost curve" into 2027. Professional fees still growing ~8.5% (anesthesia, radiology) despite the Valesco in-sourcing.

## 4. Catalyst Checklist

1. **Uninsured / exchange-sourced admissions growth vs. the ~15% run-rate** — determines whether the $1.0–1.2B FY headwind is the ceiling or a floor. This is the number that moves the stock.
2. **FY2026 EPS guidance vs. $29.45 consensus** — low-end raise required; the Street is positioned for a narrowing after the July cut.
3. **First explicit 2027 framing** — HCA does *not* guide 2027 until January, so the call Q&A is the entire event. Quantification of the 2027 exchange headwind (and any assumption about CARE Act passage) is the swing factor.
4. **Medicaid supplemental / Florida DPP sustainability into 2027** — the 2H $100–300M lap headwind plus renewal risk. Investors will haircut any further retro benefit.
5. **Policy read-through** — a bipartisan Senate group is near-final on the **CARE Act** (two-year enhanced-PTC restoration, income cap, $25 minimum premium); the House passed a three-year extension 230–196 in January that stalled in the Senate. Management will be asked how a 2027 restoration would flow through. Note HCA reports *first* in the group — UHS (Oct 28) and Tenet (Oct 29) follow — so this print sets group sentiment.
