## Key Metrics to Watch (Ranked)

1. **Exchange/uninsured volume and payer mix.** This drove the guidance cut. Watch self-pay share, uninsured admissions, and bad debt. Any further deterioration in the $1.0–1.2B estimate is the main downside risk.
2. **Same-facility admissions and adjusted admissions.** Q2 was +2.5%. Sustained growth above that supports volume-led margin recovery.
3. **Revenue per equivalent admission (pricing and acuity).** Supplemental Medicaid payments are a tailwind, so check how much is in the number and whether it recurs.
4. **Adj. EBITDA margin.** Versus the Q2 run-rate and the guidance range. Management's H2 implied EBITDA is not derivable here because I lack Q1 figures.
5. **FY2026 guidance.** Whether management holds, narrows, or cuts again. A cut would be the second in a row. Q4 implications matter more than Q3 beats.

## Scenarios

These are my analytical framings. They are not sourced forecasts. Revenue and EPS are relative to the ~$19.4B / ~$6.70 consensus.

| Scenario | Revenue | EPS | Key Driver | Stock Reaction |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Bull** | Beat (>$19.8B) | Beat (>$7.00) | Exchange volume stabilizes, supplemental payments land, FY guide held or raised | Sharp rally, likely outsized given the sell-off |
| **Base** | In line (~$19.3–19.6B) | In line to small beat | FY guide maintained, exchange impact in line with $1.0–1.2B | Muted to modest move; direction set by H2 commentary |
| **Bear** | Miss (<$19.0B) | Miss (<$6.50) | Uninsured mix worsens, FY guide cut again | Sharp decline, possibly multi-day |

**What would signal each:**
- **Bull:** Management says the exchange headwind is at or below the low end of its range. Self-pay trends flatten. Supplemental payment visibility improves.
- **Base:** Reaffirmed FY range, "tracking to guidance" language, no new policy or payer headwinds quantified.
- **Bear:** Wider exchange-impact estimate, bad debt rising, a second cut, or cautious 2027 language.

**Historical context:** Per one third-party analysis, the stock fell about 8.8% after the April 24, 2026 report (its first miss in over a year). It rose about 7.1% in January 2026 after a beat. The average absolute Day 0 move is about 5%, with smaller Day +1 follow-through, and partial reversals are common. Treat these as secondhand numbers and verify them.

## Catalyst Checklist

1. **Exchange/uninsured impact vs. the $1.0–1.2B estimate.** This determines whether the July cut was conservative. Any change is a major mover.
2. **FY2026 EPS and EBITDA guidance vs. the $28.70–30.50 / $15.4–16.1B ranges.** The buy-side will expect a reaffirmation at minimum. A cut would likely be the main negative.
3. **Admissions growth and payer mix.** Same-facility admissions above the Q2 +2.5% would support the recovery narrative.
4. **Supplemental Medicaid program benefit.** Confirm size, timing, and whether it recurs into 2027.
5. **Early 2027 commentary.** Management's tone on exchange policy and volumes will drive multiples more than the Q3 print.
