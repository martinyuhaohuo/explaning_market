## Prior Quarter Context (Q2 2026)

- Core EPS of $3.42 beat consensus. Estimates ranged from $3.12 to $3.27 across sources.
- Board authorized a buyback of up to $4.2B through 2028 and affirmed the dividend.
- No formal quantitative guidance. Management flagged continued cat frequency and severity headwinds that could pressure the combined ratio.
- Q1 2026 missed, mainly on higher-than-expected cat losses and non-cat weather claims (one source).

## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Combined ratio and catastrophe losses.** This is the main swing factor. Compare the cat load with the company's commentary and the Q3 storm season.
2. **Core EPS vs. ~$3.15 consensus.** A beat is the base expectation given Q2, but consensus has moved up.
3. **Personal lines vs. commercial lines underwriting margins.** One Barchart piece argues personal lines are retreating, which analysts may be underweighting.
4. **Property premium growth and pricing.** One preview cites ~10% property premium growth and ~6% ex-WC pricing. This comes from a preview, not from the company.
5. **Buyback pace and book value per share.** The $4.2B authorization is a capital-return signal.
