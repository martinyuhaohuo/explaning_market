## Recent results (Q2 2026, reported July 28)

- **Core FFO:** $0.38 per diluted share, $0.01 above consensus and $0.02 above Q2 2025.
- **GAAP EPS:** -$0.09 vs. about -$0.06 consensus.
- **Revenue:** $144.1M vs. $133.7M estimate.
- **Leased occupancy:** approaching 90% at June 30. Economic occupancy in the in-service portfolio: over 80%.
- **Signed, not yet commenced:** about $39M of annualized cash rent. This is the main near-term earnings driver.
- **Leasing:** about 460K sq ft across 42 transactions. Cash rent up 14%, GAAP rent up over 32% on space vacated within a year. Expansions exceeded contractions for a ninth straight quarter.
- **Guidance raised:** Core FFO $1.50–$1.55 (midpoint up $0.025). Same-store NOI growth 5%–8% (up 200 bp).

## What to watch (ranked)

1. **Core FFO vs. about $0.38.** The Q3 bar is roughly flat with Q2. A print above $0.38 would support the raised guidance.
2. **Commencement of the $39M signed-not-commenced rent.** Progress here is the clearest read on whether H2 Core FFO can support the $1.50–$1.55 range.
3. **Leased vs. economic occupancy.** The roughly 570 bp gap between them is the key lease-up question. Any further closing of that gap is positive.
4. **Same-store NOI growth vs. the raised 5%–8% range.** Management's commentary on H1 (about 10% cash NOI growth per the CEO, 9% for Q2 per the COO) appears to be on different bases, so check the release for the exact figure.
5. **Guidance change and capital allocation.** Any change to the FY range, dispositions, or debt refinancing. No M&A or restructuring news was found.

**Operational conditions for each case:**
- **Bull:** a meaningful portion of the $39M commences in Q3, and leased occupancy moves toward or above 90%.
- **Base:** occupancy holds, and commencements arrive on the schedule management already gave.
- **Bear:** commencement slips into 2027, or same-store NOI decelerates.

**Management commentary to listen for:** comments on the timing of commencements, on the shape of H2 Core FFO, and on whether same-store NOI guidance is being reaffirmed.
