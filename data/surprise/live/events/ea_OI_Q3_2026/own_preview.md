## Where the Company Stands

**Q2 2026 (reported Jul 28):** Net sales $1,668M (-2% YoY). Adjusted EPS $0.09 vs. $0.53 a year ago and ~$0.24 consensus. Reported EPS was -$6.33 after an $873M goodwill impairment.

- Europe: segment profit $6M (0.9% margin, vs. 12.1%). Prices -4%, energy costs high, plant restructuring added cost, and two furnace events.
- Volumes: -4.5% overall, Americas -7%, Europe -2%.

## What to Watch (ranked)

1. **Europe segment margin and energy cost trajectory.** Q2 margin was 0.9%. Any recovery toward high single digits would be a clear positive. Further deterioration would confirm the thesis is broken.
2. **Full-year adj. EBITDA guide.** The $1.0–1.1B range needs a sharp H2 ramp. Watch whether management holds, narrows to the low end, or cuts again.
3. **Leverage and FCF.** At ~4x net leverage, any further EBITDA cut pressures the balance sheet. Watch the cash-flow guide and any asset-sale or dividend commentary.
4. **Americas volume recovery.** Management says the July furnace event is resolved. Confirm with Q3 volume trends (a -7% decline was Q2's figure).
5. **Fit to Win delivery.** $200M in 2026 savings is the main offset to pricing pressure. Net Q2 benefit was $50M, so a miss on this line would hurt H2.
