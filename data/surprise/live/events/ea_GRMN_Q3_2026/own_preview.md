## Prior Quarter (Q2 2026, reported Jul 29)

- Revenue +11% to ~$2.02B. Pro forma EPS $2.81 (+29%) vs. ~$2.30 consensus. GAAP EPS $2.80. Margins were helped by a $21M tariff refund.
- **Fitness:** $757M (+25%). Management expects Fitness to be the largest contributor to FY growth.
- **Outdoor:** $483M (−2%), with softness in consumer auto and adventure watches. Management expects a stronger second half on launch timing.
- **Marine:** $341M (+14%). **Aviation:** $269M (+8%).
- **Headwinds:** higher memory costs in H2. A near-term auto OEM program gap before a Mercedes-Benz launch in 2027.

Sources: [MarketBeat call highlights](https://www.marketbeat.com/instant-alerts/garmin-q2-earnings-call-highlights-2026-07-29/), [the5krunner](https://the5krunner.com/2026/07/30/garmin-q2-2026-earnings/), [Barchart press release](https://www.barchart.com/story/news/3521538/garmin-announces-second-quarter-2026-results). Some sources disagree on segment margins, so I did not rely on them.

## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Fitness revenue growth and margin.** This is the main growth driver. Watch whether the ~25% pace holds and whether memory costs compress gross margin.
2. **Gross margin vs. memory-cost headwind.** The H2 cost pressure is the main risk to the EPS beat cadence. Watch for any commentary on pricing offsets.
3. **Outdoor recovery.** Management called for H2 improvement. Q3 needs to show a clear step up from −2%.
4. **Marine and Aviation.** Both grew in Q2. Continued growth supports the guide without a Fitness-only story.
5. **FY guidance change.** A third straight raise is the bar for a strong reaction. A reaffirmation with a higher Q4 implied number would be read as neutral to mixed.
6. **Auto OEM timing.** Any update on the 2027 Mercedes-Benz ramp and the near-term gap.

**What management would need to say:**
- *Bull:* "Raising full-year" with specifics on Fitness and memory-cost mitigation.
- *Base:* "Reaffirming" with H2 commentary that stays on plan.
- *Bear:* Lower H2 Fitness expectations, or a margin outlook that cites memory costs as a bigger drag than planned.

**Historical context:** Garmin's largest earnings moves have come on beats plus raised guidance: Q2 2016 about +11%, Q2 2019 about +7%, Q3 2023 about +10%, Q4 2024 about +13%, and Q4 2025 about +17% pre-market. ## Catalyst Checklist

1. **Revenue and EPS vs. ~$2.0B / ~$2.38.** Consensus looks beatable on the Q2 run-rate, so the bar is a beat with margin quality, not just a beat.
2. **Fitness growth vs. the ~25% pace.** A deceleration would be the most damaging datapoint.
3. **Gross margin and memory-cost commentary.** The H2 headwind is the main question on the call.
4. **FY2026 guidance (EPS $10.00, revenue ~$8.05B).** Buy-side will want a raise or a clear reason it cannot be raised.
5. **Outdoor H2 setup and auto OEM timing.** These are the narrative items most likely to shift the multiple.
