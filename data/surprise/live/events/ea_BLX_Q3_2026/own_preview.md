## 2Q26 Baseline (Last Reported Quarter)

- Net profit $66.5M, EPS $1.77, up 4% YoY
- Net interest income $73.3M, up 8% YoY
- NIM 2.24%, down about 10 bps QoQ and 12 bps YoY. Management blamed abundant liquidity and competition for quality assets.
- Credit portfolio a record $14.47B, up 19% YoY. Commercial portfolio $13.03B, up 20% YoY.
- 98.4% of the portfolio in Stage 1. Provisions rose to $8.6M, driven by growth and one exposure moving to Stage 3.
- FY2026 guidance as reported by a secondary summary, not verified against the press release: commercial portfolio and deposit growth 13–15%, NIM ~2.30%, efficiency ratio ~28%, adjusted ROE 14–15%, Tier 1 ratio 15–16%.

## Key Metrics to Watch (Ranked)

1. **NIM vs. the ~2.30% full-year guide.** At 2Q26's 2.24% and falling, the back half needs to recover. This is the main tension in the print.
2. **Credit portfolio growth vs. 13–15% guide.** Growth has been running near 19–20% YoY, so the guide looks conservative unless management changes it.
3. **Provisions and Stage 3 migration.** Watch whether the single Stage 3 migration becomes a trend.
4. **Efficiency ratio (~28% guide) and ROE (14–15%).**
5. **Deposit growth and funding cost** as the driver of margin.

For each scenario:
- **Bull:** NIM stabilizes or rises, deposit growth funds loans cheaply, and management reaffirms or raises the NIM guide.
- **Base:** Management says competition for assets persists and keeps guidance intact.
- **Bear:** Management acknowledges NIM will land below 2.30%, or credit costs rise again.

## Catalyst Checklist

1. **NIM vs. the ~2.30% guide.** Investors will look for a sign that the Q2 compression has bottomed.
2. **Loan growth vs. the 13–15% guide.** Actual growth is running near 20%. Whether management raises the guide decides how the print reads.
3. **Provisions and credit quality.** Any further Stage 3 migration would challenge the clean-credit story.
4. **Dividend.** A quarterly dividend of $0.6875 per share was approved for August. Watch whether it is maintained or raised. [Source](https://mercado.com.ar/finanzas/bladex-aprobo-un-dividendo-trimestral-de-us-06875-por-accion-para-agosto)
