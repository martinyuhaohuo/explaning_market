## Q2 2026 Baseline (reported Jul 28)

- **Net income:** $63.2M. **Diluted EPS:** $0.69 GAAP and adjusted.
- **Revenue:** $196.7M (NII $152.4M, nonint. income $44.3M), roughly flat vs. Q1.
- **NIM:** 3.72%, down from 3.77%. Adjusted 3.62% vs. 3.64%.
- **Efficiency ratio:** 54.0%, improved from 54.8%.
- **Loans:** $13.195B, down about 2% QoQ. **Deposits:** $15.129B, up 2.7%. LTD ratio fell to 87.2%.
- **Credit:** NPLs rose to $67.4M (0.51% of loans) from $46.6M, driven mainly by one commercial credit with a partial charge-off. NCOs fell to 0.19%. Provision $2.2M. ACL 1.24% of loans.
- **Nonint. income:** Wealth fees $20.0M, AUC/AUA $16.51B. Treasury management $4.8M.
- **Expenses:** $112.6M, down 13% from Q1, which included CrossFirst integration restructuring costs.
- **Capital:** CET1 12.53%. TBVPS $20.40. $63.1M of buybacks in Q2 at ~$26.98 average.
- **Guidance:** No numeric guidance. +100bp shock adds ~1.8% to 12-month NII.

## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Net interest margin.** Has drifted down two quarters running. Whether it stabilizes near 3.70% is the main margin question, and the buy-side will want the spot margin and Q4 outlook.
2. **Credit quality.** NPAs more than doubled-ish in Q2 (from $49.9M to $70.3M). Watch whether NPLs stay elevated, whether there are more large credits, and provision versus NCOs.
3. **Expense run-rate.** Q2's $112.6M is the new base after restructuring. Any reversion toward Q1 levels would be a negative surprise.
4. **Revenue vs. $196–197M.** Revenue has been flat for two quarters, and the loan book is shrinking. Watch whether deposit growth supports NII.
5. **Fee income and capital return.** Wealth fee growth and the pace of buybacks at prices near $27 versus TBVPS of $20.40.
