## Q2 2026 Baseline (Jul 29)

- Revenue: $2.01B vs. ~$1.91B consensus (beat)
- Tons shipped: 804K (+22.6% QoQ, mostly from the merger). Same-store tons +4.0% QoQ.
- Average selling price: $2,495/ton (+4.5%). Same-store ASP +7.2%.
- GAAP EPS: $0.30. Adjusted EPS: $0.52 vs. ~$0.40 consensus (beat). A $15.7M purchase accounting adjustment weighed on GAAP.
- Synergies: ~$5M realized in Q2; $13–14M expected in Q3
- Net debt: $913M

[Source](https://www.streetinsider.com/Press+Releases/Ryerson+Reports+Second+Quarter+2026+Results/26835922.html)

**Q3 guide implies:** shipments down 3–5% QoQ, ASP flat to +2%. Pricing is the key swing factor.

## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Adj. EBITDA ex-LIFO vs. $88–92M guide.** This is the cleanest read on core profitability and the synergy ramp.
2. **Average selling price per ton and the commentary on Q4.** Flat-to-up ASP guidance is the bar. Metals price trends will drive the outlook.
3. **Tons shipped vs. the –3% to –5% QoQ guide.** Volume weakness with firm pricing would be a positive signal.
4. **Synergy progress and the full-year run-rate target.** Q3 should show $13–14M.
5. **LIFO impact and inventory gains or losses.** Metals volatility can distort reported margins.
6. **Net debt and deleveraging.** Net debt of $913M is high, and the market will watch the path down.
7. **Q4 guidance vs. consensus.** This will likely drive the stock more than the Q3 print.

## Catalyst Checklist

1. **Adj. EBITDA ex-LIFO vs. $88–92M.** Shows whether the merger is delivering. A miss here is more damaging than a GAAP EPS miss, which is distorted by purchase accounting.
2. **Q4 guidance (revenue, ASP, shipments) vs. the buy-side's expectation.** Management's tone on metals pricing will likely set the reaction.
3. **Synergy trajectory and full-year target.** Investors want evidence the Olympic deal is accretive.
4. **Net debt and leverage path.** $913M net debt is a focus. Any update on debt paydown or capital allocation matters.
5. **Narrative and consistency.** Check whether management keeps the GAAP vs. adjusted EPS split clear. Confusing presentation has hurt the stock.
