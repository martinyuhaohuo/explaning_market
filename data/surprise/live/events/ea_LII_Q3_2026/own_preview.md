## 2. Prior Quarter Context (Q2 FY2026, reported Jul 29)

- **Revenue:** $1.545B, up 3%, missed the ~$1.58B estimate.
- **EPS:** GAAP diluted $7.72 (flat YoY), against a ~$7.65 estimate. Chartmill headlined it as "earnings in line, revenue miss," and the stock fell.
- **Home Comfort Solutions (residential):** $936M, down 7%. Segment margin 23.7%, down 130 bps.
- **Building Climate Solutions (commercial):** $610M, up 24% (15% organic, 9% acquisitions). Segment margin 25.5%, up 100 bps.
- **Gross margin:** ~34.9%, up ~40 bps. **Operating margin:** 23.0%, down 30 bps.
- **Tariffs:** About $30M of tariff refunds boosted Q2 and arrived earlier than expected. Management says pricing has largely offset tariff and inflation pressure. Q2 EPS was flattered by this one-time benefit.
- **Commentary:** Residential demand improved sequentially from Q1, but new construction remains a headwind. Management expects a meaningful residential recovery mainly in 2027.

**Implied H2 math (my arithmetic, not company guidance):** Q1 adjusted EPS was $3.35 and Q2 GAAP was $7.72, so H1 is about $11.07. Full-year guidance of $23–24 therefore needs roughly $11.9–12.9 in H2. A Q3 consensus near $7.5 implies Q4 of about $4.4–5.4. Mixing adjusted and GAAP figures makes this rough. The point is that the back half has to carry a lot, and Q3 guidance is the key swing factor.

## 3. Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Residential revenue and volume trend.** Is the -7% in Q2 improving sequentially? Watch volume versus price/mix, and replacement versus new construction.
2. **Commercial growth and organic rate.** Was +15% organic in Q2 sustainable? Watch margin expansion (25.5% in Q2).
3. **FY2026 EPS guidance.** Does management hold $23–24, narrow it, or cut again? A second cut would be the most damaging outcome.
4. **Segment margins, especially Home Comfort.** It fell 130 bps in Q2. Check for further pressure from volume deleverage and tariffs.
5. **Tariff and material cost commentary.** The Q2 refund was one-time, so check the run-rate headwind and whether pricing covers it in H2.
6. **FCF reaffirmation** ($750M to $850M). A cut would be a negative signal on working capital or demand.

## 4. Catalyst Checklist

1. **Revenue vs. ~$1.58B consensus.** A second straight miss would reinforce the residential weakness narrative.
2. **Q3 EPS vs. ~$7.5.** Q3 EPS is mostly a question of margin, since the revenue number alone won't say much.
3. **FY2026 EPS guidance ($23–24).** The buy-side will want either reaffirmation with a credible H2 bridge or a narrowing toward the low end. Holding the range is the base case.
4. **Residential recovery timing.** Management has pointed to 2027. Any pull-forward of that recovery would be a positive narrative shift.
5. **Commercial and M&A.** Acquisitions contributed about 5 points to FY revenue growth. Any further deals or acquisition-contribution changes matter for the growth story.
