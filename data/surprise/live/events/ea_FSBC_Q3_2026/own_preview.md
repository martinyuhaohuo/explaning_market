## Prior Quarter Baseline (Q2 2026, reported Jul 22)

- **EPS:** $0.91 vs. $0.86 consensus, a $0.05 beat. Net income $19.4M, up from $18.6M in Q1 and $14.5M a year earlier.
- **Revenue:** Reported as $42M, below the ~$49M Q3 estimate, so the revenue bar steps up.
- **NIM:** 3.63%, down 7 bps QoQ.
- **Loans:** Held for investment up $306M QoQ (about 7%, roughly 29% annualized). Up 20.3% YoY to $4.52B.
- **Deposits:** Up $330M QoQ (about 7%).
- **Returns:** ROE 16.67%, ROA 1.49%.
- **Other:** The call had no Q&A, a concurrent underwritten equity offering was announced, and a $0.25 quarterly dividend was declared. The offering adds share count, which dilutes EPS going forward.

I found no explicit forward guidance for NIM, loan growth, or full-year EPS.

## Key Metrics to Watch (Ranked)

1. **EPS vs. $0.86.** Watch the beat or miss and the GAAP vs. adjusted basis. Share count will be higher after the offering, so check per-share dilution.
2. **NIM vs. 3.63%.** Direction matters more than the level. Another contraction would be a negative signal for a bank that is growing loans fast.
3. **Loan growth.** Q2 annualized growth was about 29%. Sustained double-digit growth supports the thesis; a sharp slowdown would hurt.
4. **Credit quality.** Watch NPLs, charge-offs, and the reserve ratio. These are not in the sources I found, so pull them from the release.
5. **Deposit cost and funding mix.** Deposit growth of about 7% QoQ means funding costs are a key margin driver.
6. **Capital and dilution.** Post-offering capital levels, tangible book value per share, and the dividend's sustainability.

## Catalyst Checklist

1. **EPS vs. $0.86.** A beat that is mostly from a lower tax rate or one-time items would get less credit than a beat driven by core NII.
2. **NIM direction.** Anything other than flat-to-up will draw questions given the rapid loan growth.
3. **Loan growth pace.** Buy-side will want evidence that the roughly 29% annualized Q2 pace is durable, not front-loaded.
4. **Equity offering and dilution.** Investors will want to know how the offering's proceeds are being deployed and what it means for TBV per share.
5. **Dividend.** The $0.25 quarterly rate needs to look sustainable against the dilution and any credit trends.
