## Prior Quarter (Q2 FY2026, reported Aug 3)

- Revenue of $644.9M, up from $533.7M in Q1. Products were $572.7M and Services $72.2M.
- Non-GAAP EPS of $0.70. GAAP EPS of $0.19.
- Non-GAAP gross margin of 16.7% and operating margin of 7.0%.
- Revenue came in above the top end of guidance. Management tied demand to AI-driven semiconductor capex.
- Management expects gross margin to trend toward 17% through year-end, with a long-term target of 20% at a $4B run rate.
- CFO transition: Sheri Savage is retiring, and Mike Keogh is taking over.
- Capacity expansion is underway in Malaysia, Singapore, and the Czech Republic to support a $4B annualized run rate by mid-2027.

## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Revenue vs. $725M midpoint.** Q2 beat the top of guidance, so the bar is whether Q3 holds above the midpoint.
2. **Non-GAAP EPS vs. $0.93 midpoint.** Watch the gap between non-GAAP and GAAP EPS.
3. **Gross margin vs. the ~17% trajectory.** Tariff-related cost pressure is a flagged risk.
4. **Q4 revenue and EPS guidance.** This matters more than the Q3 print, since the buy-side will judge the AI-capex demand narrative from it.
5. **Customer concentration and Products vs. Services mix.** Analysts flag high concentration and industry cyclicality.
