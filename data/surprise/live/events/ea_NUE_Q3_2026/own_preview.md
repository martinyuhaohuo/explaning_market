## Prior Quarter Context (Q2 2026)

- Adjusted EPS $4.84 vs. $4.53 consensus; revenue $10.40B vs. $10.07B (Benzinga). One TIKR headline says Nucor "missed consensus," which conflicts with this. Verify before citing.
- **Key caveat:** Q2 included pig iron refund benefits. The CFO said further cash refunds are not expected to materially help Q3 or later. The Q3 guide therefore has less one-time support than Q2 did.

## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Q3 EPS vs. $5.55–$5.65 guide and ~$5.80 consensus.** Consensus sits above the top of guidance, so a beat requires running above the range.
2. **Q4 guidance vs. ~$5.14 EPS consensus.** Likely the biggest stock driver. TIKR calls the Q3 guide "best since Q2 2023," so the Q4 outlook is the test of whether that momentum holds.
3. **Steel pricing commentary (HRC/flat-rolled).** Barchart's headline suggests the steel pricing cycle may have already turned. Watch for language on Q4 price resets.
4. **Segment margins and mill utilization.** Steel Mills and Steel Products (metal building, fabrication) margins; any change in raw material spreads.
5. **Cash flow, capital return, and capex/M&A.** Watch buyback pace and any change to the capital allocation framework.

**What would signal each:**
- **Bull:** Management says pricing held through Q4, shipments up, and demand from construction/infrastructure is firm.
- **Base:** Commentary that Q4 is "seasonally softer" with stable pricing and no new cash refunds.
- **Bear:** Q4 price resets lower, imports or weak nonresidential demand cited, and no offset from refunds.

## Catalyst Checklist

1. **Q3 EPS vs. ~$5.80 consensus and $5.55–$5.65 guide.** Beating consensus, not just the guide, is what the buy-side will be checking.
2. **Q4 EPS guide vs. ~$5.14 consensus.** This matters more than the quarter's result.
3. **Steel pricing outlook.** Whether management confirms the cycle has turned or signals a Q4 price decline.
4. **Post-refund earnings quality.** How much of Q3 is underlying versus one-time, given the CFO's refund comments.
5. **Capital allocation.** Buyback or M&A changes would be a narrative shift.
