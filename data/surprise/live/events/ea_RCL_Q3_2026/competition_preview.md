## 1. The Setup in One Paragraph

RCL enters this print with a **structurally unfavorable bar**: sell-side consensus of **$6.38** sits *above* the high end of management's own Q3 guide (**$6.26–$6.36**), the stock has **rallied ~20% in the past month** into the event, and an **October crude spike** (Brent ~$114–117, WTI ~$101–107 on the Iran conflict and Strait of Hormuz disruption) has re-rated the entire cruise complex lower in the final week. Critically, **the Q3 quarter itself is largely insulated** — the oil move is an October phenomenon, after the September 30 quarter close. The stock will therefore trade on **Q4 guidance, 2027 fuel hedge disclosure, and the credibility of the Perfecta 2027 target**, not on the Q3 beat. Note also that RCL's two most recent October prints were both *negative* reactions despite EPS beats — Q3 is the quarter where the raise is smallest and next-year guidance is not yet given.

## 3. Key Metrics to Watch — Ranked

**1. Q4 guidance / FY26 narrowing (highest importance).**
FY guide is $17.73–$17.87; consensus $17.83 already sits in the upper half. A narrow to **$17.85–$17.95 or above** is the bull trigger. Holding the range unchanged is read as a de facto cut, because it implies Q4 absorbs fuel.

**2. 2027 fuel hedge coverage — the swing disclosure.**
RCL has stated **58% of remaining 2026 consumption is hedged below market**. The number nobody has is **2027 coverage**. With Brent at ~$115, every unhedged point of 2027 consumption is a direct hit to the Perfecta math. Expect this to be the most-asked question on the call. A disclosed 2027 hedge ratio above ~50% at favorable strikes is worth several points of multiple.

**3. Net Yields, constant currency.** Guide is ~flat against **~8.5% capacity growth** in the quarter. Flat yields on that much new capacity is still strong absorption. A print of **+1.0% or better** signals pricing power is holding through the geopolitical noise; **negative** yields signal Mediterranean discounting has begun.

**4. NCC ex-fuel per APCD.** Guided **-1.6% to -1.1% cc** — a sharp sequential improvement from Q2's **+3.9%**. This is the leverage engine for the quarter. A failure to deliver the cost decline undermines the whole FY bridge.

**5. 2027 booked position and pricing.** Q2 commentary was "2027 pacing ahead of historical levels" at record rates. Any softening of that language — especially on Europe/Med — is the bear's confirmation.

**6. Perfecta 2027 reaffirmation.** The program targets a **20% adjusted EPS CAGR (2024–2027)** and **high-teens ROIC by 2027**. Management has called 2026 a "23% CAGR over the first two years." Reaffirmation is table stakes; silence is a red flag.

**7. Sandals deal mechanics.** 50% of Sandals for ~**$3B** (~$6B EV, ~10x forward EBITDA), closing early 2027, committed debt financing from Morgan Stanley, guided accretive in 2027. Watch for the first quantified accretion figure and the funding structure.

**8. Balance sheet.** **$2.7B of 2027 maturities, $3.4B in 2028**, ~$4.7B annual capex, $6.9B liquidity — plus the $3B Sandals cash outlay. Leverage trajectory and any refinancing commentary matter more than usual at $115 Brent.

## 4. Scenario Analysis

| Scenario | Revenue | Adj. EPS | Key Driver | Stock Reaction |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Bull** (~25%) | $5.70B+ | **$6.55+** | Yields +1.5–2% cc; costs at low end; FY26 raised **above $18.00**; 2027 hedges disclosed >50% covered; Perfecta reaffirmed with 2027 bridge | **+7% to +13%** |
| **Base** (~45%) | $5.60–5.65B | **$6.40–6.50** | Modest beat; yields +0.5–1% cc; costs in line; FY26 narrowed to **$17.85–17.95**; Q4 guide in line; hedges addressed qualitatively | **-2% to +3%** |
| **Bear** (~30%) | $5.50–5.58B | **$6.25–6.35** | Yields flat/negative on Med softness; FY26 range **held, not raised**; Q4 guided below; management flags material unhedged 2027 fuel | **-8% to -14%** |

### What each scenario requires operationally

**Bull.** Onboard spend must carry the quarter — the Zacks model already assumes **+14.9% onboard growth vs. +5.8% ticket growth**, so the beat has to come from high-margin onboard, casino, and pre-cruise purchases rather than fare. Management needs to pair this with a credible fuel answer. The tell on the call: unprompted disclosure of 2027 hedge percentages and strike levels, plus explicit language that late-booking fares are being *raised* not discounted (they used exactly this framing in Q2).

**Base.** The quarter lands roughly where the guide said, with the usual $0.05–0.12 of conservatism. Management narrows FY26 to the top half but declines to raise materially, citing fuel visibility. Commentary stays at "we are monitoring" on Brent. This is the most likely outcome and it is close to a non-event on the EPS line — which is precisely why the Q4 guide carries the move.

**Bear.** Two paths. The *demand* path: Mediterranean and Middle East itinerary weakness, already flagged in Q2 as a "modest, near-term impact on bookings for select itineraries, primarily due to prolonged geopolitical activity," proves not modest, and yields go negative. The *cost* path: management pre-announces that 2027 fuel is materially unhedged at current strip, forcing the sell-side to cut 2027 below $19.00 and putting the Perfecta 20% CAGR visibly at risk. The second path is the more dangerous one because it hits the multiple, not just the number.

## 5. Catalyst Checklist

1. **Adj. EPS vs. $6.38 consensus (not vs. the $6.36 guide high end).** The published number exceeds management's own ceiling. The functional buy-side bar after a 20% one-month run is likely **~$6.45–6.50**. Below $6.38 and the beat narrative breaks entirely.
2. **FY2026 guide: raised vs. merely narrowed.** The binary that matters most. Raised above $18.00 = bull case confirmed. Range held at $17.73–$17.87 = treated as a cut.
3. **2027 fuel hedge percentage.** The highest-information unknown in the entire print. At ~$115 Brent, this single disclosure can move 2027 EPS by a dollar.
4. **Net yield print vs. ~flat guide, against +8.5% capacity.** Tests whether pricing power survives contact with the Med/geopolitical backdrop.
5. **Perfecta 2027 reaffirmation + first Sandals accretion number.** Reaffirmation is expected; failure to reaffirm is the tail risk. A quantified Sandals contribution would be a new, un-modeled positive.

## 6. Trading Setup

- **Implied move: ±7.3%** (~$20 on a $276 stock). RCL has **exceeded the implied move in 4 of its last 8** reports — options are not obviously mispriced, but the distribution is fat-tailed in both directions.
- **Recent performance:** ~**+20% over the past month**, yet still ~**11% below year-ago levels**. The stock bottomed hard and rebounded sharply — a fragile base to defend into a fuel-spike tape.
- **Last week's tape:** Oct 23 saw cruise names sell off on oil at a five-month high (**NCLH -6%, RCL -4%, CCL -4%**). RCL's smaller decline vs. NCLH reflects a perceived lower fuel/leverage sensitivity. Crude eased ~2% on Oct 26 into the print, a modest tailwind.
- **Valuation:** ~15.5x FY26 consensus and ~14x FY27 — reasonable for mid-teens growth, but not the deep-value setup it was a year ago. The premium leaves little cushion for a 2027 estimate cut.
- **Sell-side drift is negative:** Susquehanna cut $372→$355, Wells Fargo cut $388→$358 (Overweight maintained); TD Cowen nudged $337→$340 (Buy). Targets coming *down* while the stock goes *up* compresses the gap to the $350 average.

**Net view:** Skew is **modestly negative into the print**. The EPS beat is highly probable and almost entirely priced; the risk sits in Q4 guidance and the 2027 fuel bridge, neither of which management can fully resolve on this call. The asymmetry favors fading strength rather than chasing it — and note that a *good* quarter with a *vague* fuel answer is the single most likely path to a negative reaction, exactly as happened in October 2025.

## 7. Caveats & Data Quality

- Consensus figures are aggregated from **MarketBeat and Zacks as of late October 2026** and will drift into the print; MarketBeat shows **$5.61B** revenue vs. the Zacks model at **$5.59B**.
- **No published whisper number was located** for this quarter. The ~$6.45–6.50 functional bar cited above is **inferred** from the 4.9% average surprise, the 7-of-8 beat rate, and the post-rally setup — treat it as an estimate, not a surveyed figure.
- **FY2027 consensus (~$19.75) is derived**, not directly sourced, from the reported "~14x 2027 estimates" at a $276.51 share price.
- **Fuel as a percentage of RCL operating expenses, and 2027 hedge coverage, could not be verified** from available sources. The 58% figure applies only to *remaining 2026* consumption. This is a genuine gap and is why item #3 on the catalyst checklist is ranked as high as it is.
- The earnings-move history table is reproduced from a single options-data source; two of the eight quarters were not itemized and one entry (April 2025) appeared duplicated at the source, so the full eight-quarter series could not be independently reconstructed.
- Reported fleet redeployments (Freedom of the Seas summer 2027 cancellations, Mariner of the Seas shifted to the Mediterranean) date to **March 2026** and appear to be **routine capacity deployment, not geopolitically driven** — I would not treat them as a demand signal.
