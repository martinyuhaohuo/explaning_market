## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Q4 and FY2027 commentary on net yields.** Q3 guide is flat, so the market will look for a credible path back to positive pricing and the 2027 booking curve.
2. **Q3 adjusted EPS vs. the $6.26–$6.36 guide and ~$6.32 consensus.** A beat above ~$6.40 would be the first sign the cost program is outrunning the flat yield guide.
3. **FY2026 EPS guide change.** Another raise is the base case given the pattern. A hold would read as a soft signal.
4. **Geopolitical booking impact.** Check whether the impact is quantified and whether it spreads beyond the affected itineraries.
5. **Fuel and interest.** Fuel is the main swing factor for FY costs. Interest is guided at $980M–$990M.
