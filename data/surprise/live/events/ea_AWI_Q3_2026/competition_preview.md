## 2. The Core Tension: H2 Requires a Sharp Acceleration

This is the single most important thing in the setup, and it is arithmetic rather than opinion.

| | H1 FY26 actual | H2 FY26 implied by guide |
|---|---|---|
| Adj. EBITDA | $296M (Q1 $130M + Q2 $166M) vs. $283M LY → **+4.6%** | $309–324M vs. ~$275M LY → **+13% to +18%** |
| Adj. EPS | $4.05 ($1.69 + $2.36) vs. $3.75 → **+8%** | $4.25–4.45 vs. ~$3.77 → **+13% to +18%** |
| Net sales | $881.9M | $888–918M (Q4 implied $415–445M, +3% to +10%) |

So the FY guide needs H2 profit growth roughly **3x the H1 rate**. Management explicitly said the Q2 raise reflected "stronger-than-expected second-quarter performance, **not** a better market outlook," and flagged that mid-teens freight inflation will "weigh more heavily in H2" after the August pricing actions.

**What makes it achievable:** Q3 FY25 was the easy comp — adj. EBITDA margin fell 120bps to 34.7% on timing-related costs in *both* segments. August price increases, AUV running ~6%, the 2026 acquisitions annualizing, and a shrinking share count (43.2M diluted in Q1 → ~42.3M implied now, against a $2.5B authorization) all help.

**What makes it risky:** freight inflation at mid-teens, Architectural Specialties margins still diluted by Eventscape/Parallel/Geometrik integration, SG&A growth investments, and no material IEEPA tariff refunds assumed in H2.

## 3. Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Mineral Fiber AUV** — FY target ~+6%. This is the entire margin story; MF carries a ~44% EBITDA margin vs. ~19% in AS. If AUV decelerates below ~5% while freight runs mid-teens, the H2 margin ramp breaks.
2. **Mineral Fiber volume** — FY guide assumes only ~**+1%**, so there is little cushion. Negative volume with ABI at 44.7 would be read as the growth initiatives failing to offset the market.
3. **Architectural Specialties order intake** — Q2 was the **fourth consecutive** quarter of double-digit intake, which is the primary source of 2027 visibility. A fifth straight quarter matters more to the stock than the Q3 EPS line.
4. **AS organic EBITDA margin** — reported target ~19% FY (~20% organic). Needs to show the acquisition drag narrowing.
5. **Data center vertical** — YTD wins were up **>50%** as of Q2 across grid, structural solutions, containment, gasketed tiles and DATAZONE. This is the only genuine re-rating lever against a contracting non-res backdrop; investors want it quantified (revenue or pipeline dollars, not just "wins up X%").
6. **9M adj. FCF** — H1 was $147M; the $380–395M guide needs $233–248M in H2. Roughly $90M+ in Q3 keeps it credible on AWI's Q4-weighted cadence.
7. **TEMPLOK and SWAT** — SWAT has driven high-end demand for 13 straight quarters; TEMPLOK pipeline more than doubled from Q1. Both are mix/AUV support.

**Macro backdrop (bearish, and the reason the stock is 20% off its high):** September ABI fell to **44.7** from 47.2, with design contracts at 43.7 — contraction in 47 of the last 48 months, and the Northeast at 39.1. ABI leads CRE investment by 9–12 months, pointing to a weaker 2027 setup. AWI's repair-and-remodel-weighted mix insulates it from new construction more than peers, but it does not immunize it.

## 4. Scenario Analysis

| Scenario | Net sales | Adj. EPS | Key driver | Stock reaction |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Bull** (~25%) | $483–490M (+14–15%) | $2.55–2.62 | MF AUV ≥6% with positive volume; AS organic +15%+ and margin ≥20%; 5th straight quarter of double-digit AS intake; data center revenue quantified; FY EPS raised to **$8.50–8.60** | **+7% to +12%** |
| **Base** (~50%) | $468–477M (+10–12%) | $2.38–2.46 | In-line beat on easy Q3 margin comp; FY EPS **narrowed to $8.40–8.50**; markets still "muted"; freight offsets price | **−2% to +4%** |
| **Bear** (~25%) | $452–463M (+6–9%) | $2.18–2.30 | MF volume negative and AUV decelerates; freight/tariff costs land harder than guided; AS margin dilution persists; FY EPS cut to low end or **$8.20–8.35** | **−9% to −15%** |

**Operationally, what each requires**

- **Bull:** consolidated adj. EBITDA margin ≥36% (vs. 34.7% LY), MF margin ≥44.5%, and management framing 2027 with a concrete AS backlog number. Language to listen for: "pockets of strength broadening," "price/cost turns favorable in Q4."
- **Base:** margin ~35%, management repeating the "muted market, focused execution" formulation and *narrowing* rather than raising. Guidance narrowing to the top half is now largely priced after the de-rating.
- **Bear:** any admission that August pricing is not sticking, that data center wins are lumpy/non-recurring, or a hedge on 2027 — the latter is the real tail risk given ABI. A guidance *cut* here would be the first of the year and would break the three-quarter raise streak.

**Historical reaction calibration**

- **Q2 FY26 (Jul 28):** EPS $2.36 vs. ~$2.25–2.33 consensus, revenue $472M vs. ~$458–462M — shares rallied hard, roughly +3% premarket on the day and ~**+8.5%** over the following week (one source cites a >10% premarket print to $182.72; day-one magnitude differs across sources).
- **Q1 FY26 (Apr 28):** EPS $1.69 vs. $1.82 consensus (−7.1% miss) on severance, acquisition and tariff costs — shares fell ~**5.4%** post-print despite record sales.
- **Q3 FY25 (Oct 2025):** beat ($2.05) with raised guidance — positive reaction.

The pattern is asymmetric and margin-driven: **revenue beats without margin follow-through get sold; EPS misses on "non-recurring" costs are punished anyway.** AWI has also missed revenue consensus several times over the past two years, so the recent three-quarter streak is not the long-run base rate.

## 5. Catalyst Checklist

1. **Adj. EPS vs. $2.39** — but judge it against the $4.25–4.45 H2 bar, not the quarter in isolation. A $2.39 print with a weak Q4 implication is a sell.
2. **FY26 guidance action** — raise vs. narrow. Needs a floor of **$8.40** to preserve the compounder narrative; narrowing to $8.30–8.45 reads as a soft walk-down.
3. **Mineral Fiber price/cost** — AUV ~6% vs. mid-teens freight. The one-line answer on whether August pricing stuck.
4. **AS order intake and 2027 framing** — fifth consecutive double-digit quarter, plus any early 2027 revenue or margin guardrails. With ABI at 44.7, 2027 commentary is probably worth more to the stock than Q3 itself.
5. **Capital allocation follow-through** — the **+10% dividend to $0.373/qtr** (announced Oct 21; payable Nov 19, record Nov 5, eighth straight annual increase) and the **$800M authorization expansion to $2.5B** through 2029 are pre-print confidence signals. Watch Q3 buyback dollars and whether M&A (design-led, à la Zahner/Eventscape) is still being prioritized over repurchase.
