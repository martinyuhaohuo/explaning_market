## 2. Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **R&DS net new bookings & book-to-bill.** Q2 was $3.15B, +19% YoY, 1.22x — the best print in years and the proximate cause of the +13.9% move. Q1 was only 1.04x, so the series is noisy. LTM bookings $11.3B (+13%) have risen four straight quarters. **Management explicitly framed the strong bookings as "a 2027 and beyond indicator"** — this is the forward-earnings lever.
2. **NTM revenue from backlog.** $9.2B at June 30, +7.5% YoY. This is the cleanest bridge to FY27. It needs to hold ≥7.5% and ideally accelerate toward 8%+ to underwrite the street's +6.1% FY27 revenue.
3. **FY26 guidance revision.** Narrowing to the upper half ($17.40–17.48B / $12.95–13.05) is the base case. Narrowing to the *low* end is a de facto cut given the Q4 step-up required.
4. **Commercial Solutions growth trajectory.** +11.6% in Q1 → +8.6% in Q2 (8.4% cc). Deceleration here is the under-discussed risk; it carries the higher-margin analytics/consulting mix.
5. **AI monetization.** IQVIA launched **Life Science Models** (NVIDIA Nemotron, on AWS) at TechIQ on Oct 7, 2026. Management's line has been that AI creates *incremental* demand rather than cannibalizing service revenue. Watch whether any of that converts into disclosed bookings or whether it reads as opex.
6. **Margins, FCF, capital return.** Q2 adj. EBITDA margin 22.76%; FCF $360M (+23%); net leverage **3.59x**; $950M repurchased in H1 with **$2.82B remaining**. Buyback pace at $276 vs. $161 in May is a capital-discipline tell.
7. **Backlog quality.** Last quarter IQVIA volunteered that ~5% of backlog could be inactive trials if adjusted — "far below" a competitor's figure — and said it wouldn't affect guidance. If a sell-side analyst reopens this, it's a narrative risk disproportionate to its P&L impact.

## 3. Scenario Analysis

| Scenario | Revenue | Adj. EPS | Book-to-bill | Key driver | Stock reaction |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Bull** (~25%) | ≥$4.45B | ≥$3.40 | ≥1.25x | Second consecutive >1.2x book-to-bill; NTM backlog growth accelerates >8%; FY EPS raised above $13.05; constructive early FY27 framing | **+8% to +14%** |
| **Base** (~50%) | $4.39–4.42B | $3.30–3.35 | 1.12–1.20x | Modest beat consistent with the 2.2% four-quarter average surprise; guide narrowed to upper half; Commercial Solutions +8–9% | **-2% to +4%** |
| **Bear** (~25%) | ≤$4.33B | ≤$3.20 | <1.05x | Bookings revert toward Q1's 1.04x; NTM backlog growth decelerates below 7%; Commercial Solutions slows to mid-single digits; FY guide trimmed to low end or FX-driven reduction | **-10% to -18%** |

**What makes the base case asymmetric to the downside:** the stock is 2.6% off its 52-week high and ~78% above the May low. Two consecutive double-digit positive reactions have pulled consensus to the *top* of company guidance. An in-line quarter with a mechanical guidance narrow is largely priced; the burden of proof has shifted to bookings.

**Commentary that signals each path.** Bull: management volunteering that bookings momentum is "broad-based" again, RFP volumes up, decision cycles shortening, and any willingness to frame 2027 growth above 6%. Bear: hedging on Q4 linearity, attributing the raise to FX or M&A rather than organic, or declining to characterize bookings durability. In Q2 management called the quarter "exceptionally clean" with no unusual items — the absence of that language would itself be a tell.

## 4. Catalyst Checklist

1. **Net new bookings vs. ~$3.0–3.1B / ~1.15x implied.** Beat → 2027 revenue visibility improves, supports the re-rating. Miss below 1.05x → the Q2 print gets re-labeled a one-off and the whole bull case unwinds.
2. **NTM backlog revenue growth vs. +7.5%.** The buy-side wants 8%+ to validate street FY27.
3. **FY26 guidance.** Upper-half narrow is the hurdle, not a raise. Q4 needs $4.49B / $3.60 at the midpoint.
4. **Commercial Solutions organic growth vs. 8.4% cc.** Third straight deceleration would pressure the mix/margin story.
5. **Preliminary FY27 tone.** IQVIA typically guides in February, so expect qualitative framing only — but tone against $18.47B / $14.39 will move the stock.
