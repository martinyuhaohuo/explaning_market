## Prior Quarter Context (Q2 2026, reported Jul 21)

- **Production:** 634 Bcfe, above the top of guidance.
- **EPS:** $0.39 vs. $0.41 consensus (one source shows $0.34 diluted). Missed.
- **Revenue:** $1.68B vs. $1.76B expected. Missed.
- **Net income:** $211M, down from $784M a year earlier, on lower derivative gains and realized prices.
- **Costs and capex:** Per-unit opex $1.03/Mcfe, at the low end. Capex $666M, 9% below the low end of guidance.
- **FCF:** $330M attributable to EQT.
- **Balance sheet:** $5.7B total debt, $5.5B net debt.
- **Guidance:** FY2026 production raised ~90 Bcfe to 2,375 to 2,450 Bcfe. FY capex cut $25M.
- **Commercial:** 10-year gas supply deal with CPV, a 5-year LNG offtake agreement, and the $77M Blackline Midstream acquisition.

## What to Watch (ranked)

1. **Realized gas price and hedge gains/losses.** This drove the Q2 earnings decline and is the largest swing factor for EPS versus consensus.
2. **Production vs. the 570 to 620 Bcfe Q3 guide and the raised FY range.** Q2 beat volume, so a repeat would support the FY raise.
3. **Unit costs and capex.** Opex and capex came in at the low end last quarter. Watch for the same discipline.
4. **FY2026 guidance and 2027 commentary.** Compression-driven volume growth and the CPV/LNG contracts are the narrative drivers.
5. **Free cash flow and deleveraging.** Net debt is $5.5B. FCF allocation (debt paydown vs. buybacks) matters to the buy side.

## Scenarios

| Scenario | Revenue | EPS | Key driver | Stock reaction (indicative) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Bull | Above ~$1.97B | Above ~$0.55 | Volume above guide, realized prices up, FY raise again | +5% to +8% |

- **Bull:** Management reports volume at or above the top of the range, costs at the low end again, and raises FY guidance or signals 2027 growth.
- **Base:** Volume lands inside guidance, the FY outlook holds, and management talks about LNG and CPV contract ramp.
- **Bear:** Realized price or hedge drag pulls EPS below the low end of the range, and commentary becomes cautious on 2027.

**Historical reactions (weak evidence):** Sources conflict. The post-Q2 next-day move was reported at +8.5% (Unusual Whales). Other figures from TipRanks (Euro-listed line) are +7.3% (Jul 2026), +3.3% (Apr 2026), +1.8% (Feb 2026), and +0.6% (Oct 2025). Q1 2026 beat estimates and the stock still fell. Direction doesn't reliably follow the EPS beat.

## Catalyst Checklist

1. **Realized price and EPS vs. the ~$0.50 middle of the range.** The Q2 miss shows price and hedge effects can override volume.
2. **Q3 volume vs. the 570 to 620 Bcfe guide.** A beat that repeats Q2 would support the FY raise.
3. **FY2026 guidance (production, capex, opex).** Buy-side wants a second raise or at least a hold.
4. **Narrative: CPV gas supply, LNG offtake, Blackline midstream.** Look for 2027 volume and margin commentary.
5. **Net debt and FCF allocation.** Debt reduction versus shareholder returns.
