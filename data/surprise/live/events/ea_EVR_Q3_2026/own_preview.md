## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Advisory fees vs. expectations.** This is the core driver. Watch the number of completed deals and the mix of large transactions. Q2 advisory growth was only 11% despite a strong first half.
2. **Underwriting fees.** Q2 more than tripled, so Q3 comparisons are tough. A sequential decline would not necessarily be negative, but it would weigh on sentiment.
3. **Adjusted operating margin.** Q2 was 19.0%. Compensation ratio commentary matters more than the headline margin.
4. **Forward pipeline commentary.** Management typically speaks to the backlog and the Q4 outlook. This is the main source of any guidance-like signal.
5. **Capital return.** $822.9M returned in the first half. Watch for buyback pace and any change to capital deployment.

**Bull signals:** Advisory growth accelerating, a large-cap M&A mandate win, upbeat Q4 commentary, and a margin above 20%.

**Base signals:** Results in line with consensus, management avoids a forward view, and buybacks continue at a steady pace.

**Bear signals:** Management flags deal delays or a slower Q4 pipeline, the comp ratio moves up, or the call adds macro caution. A macro overhang has been cited in recent coverage, including geopolitical risk tied to the Iran conflict.

## Catalyst Checklist

1. **Advisory fees vs. consensus.** Advisory is most of revenue, so this drives the revenue miss or beat.
2. **Underwriting normalization.** Whether fees hold near Q2 levels, or fall back, shapes how the quarter is read.
3. **Q4 pipeline commentary.** The buy-side will parse this more than the quarter itself, since the company gives no formal guidance.
4. **Adjusted margin and comp ratio.** Margin expansion above 19% would support a bull case.
5. **Capital return and M&A.** Any buyback acceleration or strategic announcement would change the narrative.
