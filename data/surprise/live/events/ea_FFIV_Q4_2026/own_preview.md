## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Product revenue growth and systems strength.** Q3 was +19% product and +32% systems. Does systems (hardware/AI-related demand) hold up, and what does Q4 product revenue imply?
2. **Software growth and ARR trajectory.** Q3 software growth was called softer. Watch the subscription mix and any ARR or renewal commentary.
3. **Gross margin and FY27 margin outlook.** Q4 is guided at 83%–84%. FY27 is guided at 80%–82%, with management flagging component-price volatility. Any cut to FY27 is the most likely negative.
4. **FY27 revenue framework.** The buy-side will want an early read on next-year growth, not just the Q4 beat.
5. **Geographic mix.** APAC declined in Q3. Check whether that persisted.
6. **Perpetual license trend.** Declined slightly in Q3. Watch for further decline.
