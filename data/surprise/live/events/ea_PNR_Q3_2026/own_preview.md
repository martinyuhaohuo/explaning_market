## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Pool segment volume and channel inventory.** Q2 Pool sales fell about 42%, and management is destocking ahead of the 2027 season. The key question is whether destocking is nearing its end.
2. **Q4 and FY2026 guide.** FY adj. EPS of $4.60–4.80 was reaffirmed, and FY sales are guided down 4–7%. Any cut to the adjusted range would be a major negative. Watch whether the guide excludes the pending Taco Group acquisition.
3. **Adjusted vs. GAAP EPS gap.** The GAAP range was cut to $3.86–4.06. Look for the drivers of the gap, such as restructuring or deal costs.
4. **Margins.** Watch adjusted operating margin and price/cost, given tariff and destocking pressure on volume.
5. **Free cash flow and working capital.** Channel destocking should release working capital, so a cash beat would support the narrative.
6. **Residential vs. commercial mix.** Commentary on residential demand is useful for the 2027 pool-season outlook.
