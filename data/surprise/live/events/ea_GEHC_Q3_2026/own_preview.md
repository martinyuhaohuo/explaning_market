## Last Quarter (Q2 FY2026, reported Jul 29, 2026)

- Revenue +5.7% reported, +3.5% organic
- Adj. EPS $1.13, +6.6% y/y, helped by tariff refunds and a lower tax rate. Consensus was about $1.04, so this was a beat.
- Orders +11.1% to a record. Backlog $23.9B.
- Patient Care Solutions was softer.
- Guidance reaffirmed, not raised. The tariff tailwind is fading, and only part of the 2026 refunds is in guidance (~$0.04 EPS, ~$23M EBIT).
- Stock rose ~2.5% on the day.

## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Organic revenue growth vs. the 3–4% full-year range.** Q2 was 3.5%. A print below ~3% would raise doubts about the year.
2. **Adj. EPS and whether tariff refunds are flattering the number.** Check how much of Q3 EPS comes from one-time refunds versus underlying operations.
3. **Adj. EBIT margin trajectory.** The FY guide of 15.4–15.7% needs expansion. Watch for tariff cost pressure and price/mix.
4. **Orders and book-to-bill.** Record Q2 orders with an $23.9B backlog is the core bull argument. Any slowdown in China or the US capital equipment cycle matters here.
5. **Patient Care Solutions and Pharmaceutical Diagnostics.** These are the weaker lines. Watch for further deterioration.
6. **FY guidance: reaffirm, raise, or cut.** Given the backloaded H2 math, a reaffirm with a soft Q3 would likely be read as a mild negative.
7. **FCF tracking vs. ~$1.6B.**

## Catalyst Checklist

1. **Organic growth vs. ~3–4% full-year pace.** It is the cleanest read on demand.
2. **Adj. EPS quality.** Whether the beat, if any, is from refunds or tax rather than operations. The market rewarded Q2's beat only modestly (+2.5%).
3. **FY2026 EPS guide ($4.80–$5.00).** The buy side will look for either a raise or confidence in the H2 ramp. A reaffirm with a soft Q3 is a risk.
4. **Orders and backlog ($23.9B record).** The key bull thesis; any slowdown here would matter more than the quarter itself.
5. **Margin narrative.** Tariff mitigation and the 15.4–15.7% EBIT margin path.
