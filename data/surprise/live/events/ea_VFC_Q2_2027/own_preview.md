## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Revenue vs. consensus and vs. the Q2 guide.** The FY raise to ≥2% depends on the second half, so Q2 must track.
2. **Vans trajectory.** Management guided Vans weak in H1 and improving to roughly −2% or better in H2. Any further deterioration there is the biggest downside risk.
3. **Gross margin and the ~8% operating margin target.** Tariff and promotional pressure are the swing factors. The Q1 EPS miss suggests margin pressure.
4. **North Face, Timberland, and Altra growth.** These carried Q1. Check whether they are still growing.
5. **FCF and leverage.** Confirm the flat-to-up FCF guide and the 2.6x–2.9x leverage range.
6. **Management commentary on the CFO transition.** Any change to guidance cadence or new cost actions would matter.

## Scenario Analysis

| Scenario | Revenue | EPS | Key Driver | Stock Reaction |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Bull** | Beats, ~+2–3% YoY | Beats $0.54 | North Face/Timberland strength, Vans stabilizing early, FY guide held or nudged up | Sharp rally, plausibly +8–15% |
| **Base** | In line, roughly flat to slightly up | In line to slight beat | FY ≥2% reiterated, Vans still weak in H1 | Modest move, ±3–6% |
| **Bear** | Miss, down YoY | Miss | Vans deteriorates, margin shortfall, FY revenue guide trimmed back toward 1% | Sharp drop, plausibly −8–15% |

**Bull:** Requires Q2 revenue growth, margin tracking to 8%, and a reiterated or higher FY guide. Commentary such as "Vans inflecting earlier" or "DTC accelerating" would signal this.

**Base:** Management reiterates the FY outlook and says the H2 ramp is on track. Expect cautious language on Vans.

**Bear:** Management trims the FY revenue outlook, cites tariffs or promotions, or signals more Vans weakness. The CFO transition could amplify the reaction if it comes with a guidance reset.

**Historical context:** VFC has moved sharply on prints. Examples: Jan 2026 fell ~10% on weak Q1 guidance despite a Q4 beat. Oct 2024 rose up to ~19% on a big EPS beat. Aug 2024 rose ~7%. The pattern is that guidance and revenue often matter more than the EPS beat. One source counted 35 moves of more than 5% in the past year, which suggests a high-volatility name.

## Catalyst Checklist

1. **Q2 revenue vs. consensus and the company's own guide.** Revenue has been the cleaner signal, since Q1 showed EPS can miss on margin even when sales beat.
2. **FY27 revenue guide (≥2%).** The buy-side will want it reiterated or raised. A cut would likely dominate the reaction.
3. **Vans H1-to-H2 bridge.** Buy-side needs to see the path to −2% or better in H2.
4. **Margin path to ~8% operating margin.** Q1 EPS miss means this is the credibility test.
5. **CFO transition and combined CFO/COO role.** Watch for any signal of cost actions, guidance changes, or a shift in capital allocation.
