## 2. Last Quarter (Q2 2026, reported Jul 30)

- **Revenue:** $152.2M, down from $171.8M a year earlier (about -11%). This missed expectations.
- **EPS:** $1.06 vs. ~$1.03 expected (beat). GAAP net income was $49M, down from $67M.
- **Gross margin:** Guidance is 74–76% for the full year, and management said it is tracking just north of 75% year to date.
- **Cash:** $855M after the dividend. About $48M of buybacks in the quarter.
- **Management tone:** Blamed soft smartphone demand, higher component costs, and cautious customer purchasing. Said the guidance cut reflects lower volume, not pricing, since long-term agreements keep ASPs stable. Expects H2 revenue above H1, citing Q3/early-Q4 product launches. Pointed to Gen 6 and Gen 8.6 capacity and growth in IT, automotive, and emerging form factors.
- **Track record:** Q4 2025 beat (+9%). Q1 2026 missed badly (-33%). Q2 2026 beat on EPS but missed on revenue.

## 3. Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Revenue vs. consensus and H2 ramp.** The company says H2 > H1. Check whether Q3 revenue is strong enough to support that and the low end of the $630M guide. Q2 was $152M, so Q3 needs a visible step up.
2. **Gross margin vs. the 74–76% range.** A print near 75% confirms the guide. Anything below 74% is a negative surprise.
3. **Material sales vs. licensing and royalty mix.** Material sales drive volume and are most exposed to smartphone demand. Licensing and royalties are steadier. The mix tells you how much of the demand weakness is cyclical.
4. **Q4 and FY2027 commentary.** Whether management reaffirms the full-year range, and early color on 2027 volume.
5. **Capital return.** Buyback pace and the dividend, given $855M cash.

Blue phosphorescent OLED commercialization and Gen 8.6 timing are the main long-term narrative items. I could not verify call detail on either.

## 4. Scenarios

These are my analytical framing, anchored to Q2 revenue of $152M and the Zacks Q3 EPS estimate of $1.13. They are not sourced forecasts.

| Scenario | Revenue | EPS | Key driver | Stock reaction |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Bull** | Clear step-up above Q2 run-rate; supports H2 > H1 | Beats $1.13 | Launch-driven material volume; margin at or above 75% | Positive. Guidance reaffirmed at the top half of the range, or 2027 color that the cycle is turning |
| **Base** | Roughly flat to modest sequential growth; FY at lower end of $630–670M | In line to small beat | Gross margin ~75%; guidance held, not raised | Muted to modestly positive. Reaction hinges on H2 credibility |
| **Bear** | Revenue flat or down; H2 ramp slips | Miss vs. $1.13 | Smartphone weakness; margin below 74%; guidance cut below the range | Negative. Stock sells off on any FY cut or margin miss |

For each scenario, look for these signals in management commentary:
- **Bull:** Specific launch timing and customer volumes; "reaffirm" language on FY guidance; upbeat IT, automotive, and emerging form factor comments.
- **Base:** "Lower end" language repeated; generic H2 language without numbers.
- **Bear:** Words like "further pressure," "push-outs," or "customer inventory"; any change to the gross margin range.
