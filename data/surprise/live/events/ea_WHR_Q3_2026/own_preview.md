## Prior quarter (Q2 FY26, reported Aug 3)
- Net sales about $3.52B, below the ~$3.60B consensus. Ongoing EBIT fell to $62M from $200M a year earlier.
- Management cited sequential margin improvement from price increases and cost take-out.
- FY EPS guide was cut from $3.00–$3.50 to $2.50–$3.00. Revenue (~$15.0B) and margin outlook were held.
- Sources disagree on the Q2 stock reaction, so I'm not using one. The Simply Wall St "+17.5%" headline and a Yahoo snapshot showing −4.85% can't be reconciled from what I found ([source](https://whirlpool.mediaroom.com/2026-08-03-Whirlpool-Corporation-Announces-Second-Quarter-Results)).

## Key metrics to watch (ranked)
1. **Gross and operating margin trajectory.** Does Q3 confirm the sequential recovery management described in Q2? This matters most given the cut.
2. **Revenue vs. ~$3.96B.** Watch North America demand and pricing contribution against volume.
3. **EPS vs. ~$0.93,** and whether it is on an ongoing basis.
4. **FY26 guide ($2.50–$3.00).** Does management hold, narrow to the low end, or cut again? A second cut would be the key negative.
5. **Cost take-out progress and free cash flow,** including tariff and input-cost commentary.
