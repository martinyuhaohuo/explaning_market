## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Sales vs. guidance midpoint ($238M):** This is the first test of whether the Q2 supply problems are behind the company. Revenue at or above $240M supports the bull case.
2. **Gross margin vs. the 33.2%–34.2% range:** Q2 margin pressure came from supply chain and freight. Recovery toward the top of the range would show the cost issues are fading.
3. **Adj. EPS vs. $1.20:** Q2's miss came from operating costs, so watch opex discipline as well as the headline number.
4. **Segment growth:** Management guided growth in every end market. Watch A&D and Electronics/Comms. Watch Defense for the H2 recovery it promised.
5. **Q4 guidance vs. consensus:** Buy-side reaction will depend more on the Q4 outlook than on Q3 results.
6. **Analyst/investor day (Sep 30) follow-through:** Check whether management reaffirmed the longer-term targets it set at that event.
