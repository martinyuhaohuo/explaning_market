## Guidance Baseline (from Q2 call, July 29, 2026)

- Full-year adjusted sales growth of 13–15%, reaffirmed.
- Full-year adjusted EBITDA of $230–240M, reaffirmed.
- Del Monte Foods segment: 2026 net sales raised to $625M (from $600M) and adjusted EBITDA raised to $35M (from $23M).
- Q1 segment gross-margin guidance: Fresh & Value-Added 11–12%, Bananas 3–4%, Prepared Foods 13–14%, Other 12–13%. SG&A $270–280M, capex $85–95M, operating cash flow $40–50M.
- No specific Q3 guidance was found. [MarketBeat](https://www.marketbeat.com/instant-alerts/fresh-del-monte-produce-q2-earnings-call-highlights-2026-07-29/)

## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Bananas and Fresh gross margin.** Chartmill flags persistent margin pressure here. Bananas guided at only 3–4%, so any miss in this segment will dominate the reaction.
2. **Del Monte Foods segment sales and EBITDA** against the raised $625M / $35M full-year marks. The segment has been volatile, and a Q3 trajectory check is the main test of the raise.
3. **Full-year EBITDA ($230–240M) and sales growth (13–15%) reaffirmation.** A cut is the biggest downside risk; a raise is the main upside.
4. **EPS vs. the $0.46–0.51 range.** The Q2 beat suggests upside potential, but the consensus gap makes the "beat" definition ambiguous.
5. **Prepared Foods gross margin (guided 13–14%)** and SG&A against the $270–280M range.
6. **Operating cash flow and capex** against guidance.
