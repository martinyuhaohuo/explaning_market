Q2 comparison: revenue $2,736M (+3% y/y), non-GAAP EPS $3.60 vs. ~$2.07–2.94 consensus. Biogen has beaten EPS in 8 straight quarters.

## 2. Guidance Context

- **FY2026 non-GAAP EPS (updated in Q2):** $12.00–13.00 reported. This includes ~$3.00/share of acquired IPR&D and milestone charges and ~$0.85 dilution from the Apellis acquisition. The underlying range is $15.85–16.85, down from $14.25–15.25 in April on a reported basis. Several sources cite different ranges, so confirm against the release.
- **FY2026 revenue:** now expected to grow mid-single digits vs. 2025, a reversal from the earlier projected decline.
- **H2 2026 combined non-GAAP R&D + SG&A:** $2.65–2.70B.
- **Apellis:** accretive to non-GAAP EPS in 2027; at least $250M run-rate synergies exiting 2027.

## 3. Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Reported vs. underlying EPS:** the IPR&D charge timing and size will drive the headline number. Investors will look past it, so check whether the ~$3.00 charge is booked in Q3 or Q4.
2. **Revenue vs. ~$2.6B:** whether the growth portfolio (which surpassed legacy MS sales in Q2) keeps offsetting legacy MS erosion.
3. **FY revenue guide:** confirm "mid-single digit growth" holds, ideally with a dollar range.
4. **Apellis integration:** deal close timing, synergy cadence, and any update to the 2027 accretion view.
5. **Pipeline:** litifilimab SLE readout (guided by end-2026) and four other registrational readouts over the next four quarters.
