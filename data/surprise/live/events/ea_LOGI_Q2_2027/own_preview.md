## Prior Quarter (Q1 FY27, reported Jul 28, 2026)

- Revenue $1.23B, up 7% YoY, beat consensus by about 2.1%.
- Non-GAAP EPS $1.85, up 47% YoY.
- Non-GAAP operating income $290M, which includes **$61M of one-time tariff refunds**. Ex-refunds, operating income grew about 14% YoY (CFO comment).
- Ex-refunds, Q1 operating income was roughly $229M, so the Q2 guidance midpoint (~$198M) implies a sequential step-down of about 14%.
- Sources: [Q1 release coverage](https://www.barchart.com/story/news/3509327/logitech-announces-q1-fiscal-year-2027-results), [Quartr](https://quartr.com/events/logitech-international-s-a-logn-q1-2027_3ssDzW1I)

**Supplier headwind:** A late-June incident at a semiconductor supplier forced a temporary plant closure. Estimates cited in the coverage: about **$20M** Q2 revenue hit and **up to $200M** Q3 revenue hit, with minimal Q4 impact. The Q3 figure is an analyst estimate from Quartr, not company guidance. Logitech is not issuing a formal full-year outlook, only saying Q1 demand momentum should carry through the year.

## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Q3 outlook and supplier impact.** The biggest swing factor. Does management confirm the scale of the Q3 hit, and does it hold the full-year "momentum" framing?
2. **Revenue vs. guidance range.** Landing at the top ($1.22B) versus below $1.19B is the cleanest signal on demand and on the supplier impact.
3. **Gross margin vs. ~44%.** Tariff-related cost trends and mix matter here.
4. **Operating income vs. $185–210M.** Watch how much of the drop is the planned R&D and marketing step-up versus a supply-driven miss.
5. **Quality of earnings.** Q1 included $61M of tariff refunds. Check whether management separates that from the run-rate, and whether any further refunds are expected.
6. **Free cash flow and inventory.** Inventory build or release tied to the supplier disruption.
7. **Category mix.** Premium peripherals and gaming versus lower-margin lines.

## Scenarios

Revenue ranges are my estimates based on the guidance range and the supplier headwind. EPS is not guided, so I express it qualitatively against the stale $1.32 reference.

| Scenario | Revenue | EPS | Key driver | Stock reaction |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Bull | ≥ $1.22B (top of range) | Above $1.32 | Supplier impact contained; Q3 hit smaller than feared; margins hold | +5% to +8% |
| Base | ~$1.20B (midpoint) | Near $1.30–1.35 | In-line Q2; Q3 hit acknowledged but guided with confidence | –2% to +3% |
| Bear | < $1.19B (below range) | Below $1.25 | Supplier shortfall larger; Q3 cut; margin pressure | –8% to –12% |

**Bull:** Management confirms the $20M Q2 hit, quantifies the Q3 impact below the $200M estimate, and reiterates demand momentum. Commentary like "demand remains strong" and "supply recovering ahead of plan" would signal this.

**Base:** Q2 lands mid-range, with a Q3 headwind framed as timing. Commentary like "temporary," "mitigated," and "no change to our view" would signal this.

**Bear:** Q2 misses the low end, or Q3 guidance implies a sharp decline. Commentary like "supplier recovery pushed out" or "reducing our outlook" would signal this.

**Historical context (from limited search results):**
- Oct 2024: beat and raised, but shares fell more than 10% pre-market. Revenue vs. consensus and guidance drove the move more than the EPS beat.
- Jan 2025: rose on a revenue beat and upbeat outlook (about +5% pre-market).
- Apr 2025: EPS beat but revenue miss; shares fell the following session.
- Late Oct 2025: rose about 3.7% after hours on an EPS beat.
- Pattern: EPS beats alone have not reliably produced gains. Guidance and revenue have mattered more.

## Catalyst Checklist

1. **Q2 revenue vs. $1.19–1.22B range.** The buy-side will want the top half of the range to offset the supplier concern.
2. **Q3 guide and supplier-impact sizing.** The main risk. Watch whether the ~$200M Q3 estimate is confirmed, reduced, or raised.
3. **Gross margin holding near 44%.** Any sequential compression under the tariff backdrop would be read negatively.
4. **Quality of Q1 beat.** Clarity on the $61M tariff refund and run-rate operating income.
5. **Narrative: demand durability.** Management's "momentum carries through the year" line is the key message to reaffirm or retract.
