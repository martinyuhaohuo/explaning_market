## Recent Context

- **Q2 2026 (reported Jul 28):** Adj. EPS $0.40 vs. $0.37 consensus; revenue $2.15B vs. $2.10–2.12B. Growth and rate recovery added $0.10/share.
- **Capital plan:** 10-year plan raised $1.2B to $66.7B, driven by large-load demand in Houston. Management said this came without raising the current equity guide.
- **ERCOT Batch Zero:** More than 17 GW of large-load projects submitted, about 14 GW expected to be eligible.
- **Houston Electric rate case:** $73M revenue requirement increase expected in November 2026.
- **Leverage:** Long-term debt rose to $21.16B at June 30, from $19.90B at year-end 2025.
- **Surprise history:** Mixed. Barchart reports misses in three of the last four quarters, though Q2 2026 was a beat. Q1 2026 adj. EPS was $0.56 vs. $0.58 expected.

## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Adj. EPS vs. $0.50–0.51:** A beat on the Q2 pattern would support the guide. A miss would be read against the 8% growth implied by guidance.
2. **Reaffirmation or raise of FY2026 guidance ($1.89–1.91):** A raise would be a clear positive. Watch whether management says the midpoint is still achievable.
3. **Capital plan and equity needs:** Any signal that the $66.7B plan requires more equity than the current guide is the main risk to the stock.
4. **ERCOT large-load progress:** Eligibility confirmations and demand-charge benefits. Management said these are not yet in the outlook, so they are upside.
5. **Balance sheet:** Debt growth and the interest expense drag (cost $0.01 in Q2).

- **Bear:** Talk of delays in Batch Zero, a financing plan that needs equity, or a softer guide for 2027.

## Catalyst Checklist

1. **Adj. EPS vs. ~$0.50–0.51:** Beats have been the recent trend, but the surprise history is mixed.
2. **FY2026 guidance ($1.89–1.91):** Buy-side expects a reaffirmation at minimum, with the upper end as upside.
3. **Capital plan ($66.7B) and equity financing:** The market will test whether the plan can be funded without dilution.
4. **ERCOT Batch Zero and large-load pipeline:** The main long-term growth narrative; watch for numbers and timing.
