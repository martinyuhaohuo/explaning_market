## 3. Key Metrics to Watch — Ranked

**1. Organic revenue ex-contingents (consensus +1.9%; whisper ~+2.0–2.5%).** This is the entire print. Three straight soft quarters have compressed the multiple from the mid-20s to ~15x forward. A fourth confirms the bear case that BRO's growth engine is now M&A, not sales. Watch the *segment* split against management's own 1.5–2.5% / 2–4% ranges — a Retail hit with Specialty still negative is a bad quarter dressed up as an in-line one.

**2. Adjusted EBITDAC margin and the investment-income bridge.** Q3 2025's 36.6% margin flattered by ~$29M of interest income on Accession financing proceeds held pre-close (deal closed Aug 1, 2025). Backing out investment/other income, Q3 2025 core margin was ~34.2% on commissions & fees. Holding that flat implies **~34.9% reported in Q3 2026, ~170bp lower Y/Y** — which would be *consistent* with guidance, not a miss. If the sell-side scores the headline 36.6% comp, expect a knee-jerk "margin collapse" reaction that is wrong. Conversely, anything below ~34% breaks the "flat ex-investment income" FY promise.

**3. Howden drag: peaking or compounding?** The estimate doubled in one quarter ($31M → $50–60M). A third upward revision, or any sign producer attrition has spread beyond the original cohort, is the most dangerous single disclosure in the release. A reaffirmed or trimmed figure is the cleanest bull trigger.

**4. Specialty Distribution and CAT property.** Marsh's index shows global commercial rates **-5% in Q3 2026** (ninth consecutive quarterly decline), **property -10%**, casualty +1% with US excess casualty up ~13%. Specialty is the most rate-levered unit (43.1% EBITDAC margin in 2025) and the most exposed to CAT property deflation. Management guided to a 2–4% recovery here; it printed -3.5% last quarter.

**5. The Accession lapping point.** Accession closed Aug 1, 2025, so Q3 2026 picks up only ~one incremental month of inorganic revenue — hence the deceleration from +30% to ~+10% total growth. **Q4 2026 is the first fully-lapped quarter.** The acquisition screen is about to come off the organic number, which is precisely why the market will weight any 2027 framing heavily.

**6. Capital allocation.** $400M December maturity (refi vs repay), leverage against the stated 0–2.5x net debt/EBITDAC target (~3x gross on my math), buyback posture, and M&A pricing discipline ("strategic, not for scale"). The ~9% dividend increase to $0.18/quarter — the 33rd consecutive annual raise — is already banked and was part of the October rally.

**Why EPS won't drive the tape:** on a ~$1.55B organic base, **±100bp of organic growth is only ~±$0.02 of EPS** — but historically ~±5% on the multiple. Do not size this on the penny.

**Reaction history (the pattern is unambiguous — organic rules, EPS doesn't):**

| Quarter | Adj. EPS vs cons. | Organic | Reaction |
|---|---|---|---|
| Q4 FY2025 | **Beat** $0.93 vs $0.91 (revenue miss) | -2.8% | **-3.7%** after hours |

Note the Q2 conflict: the initial gap down fully reversed intraday. Treat the "+6.4%" as single-source. The directional lesson stands: BRO has beaten on EPS and fallen twice, and missed on EPS and rallied once.

## 6. Trading Setup

- **Options-implied move: ±7.8%**, vs a **±5.0%** historical average post-earnings move (single vendor, earnings-watcher; BRO did not appear in TipRanks' implied-move screen for the week — treat as indicative, verify on your own chain before sizing).
- **Momentum into the print is the main risk.** $60.82 (Sept 25) → $72.97 (Oct 23) is a ~20% move, driven by a 9% dividend hike, an Oppenheimer *Outperform* initiation, a **KBW double-upgrade from Underperform to Outperform ($77 PT)**, and peer-group beta — **not** by improving organic data. The stock is giving ~3% back today into the event.
- **Valuation / positioning:** ~15.3x forward (deeply de-rated vs. BRO's historical premium), consensus **Hold** from 21 analysts, average target **$74.67** (~5% upside). Recent target cuts: Citizens JMP $78→$75 (Oct 12), Mizuho $83→$78 (Oct 7). Dividend yield ~1.0%.
- **Structural quirk worth exploiting:** the release hits Monday after the close but **the call is not until Tuesday 8:00am ET**. The overnight gap trades on a headline EPS number that doesn't matter, while the information that does — segment organic color, the Howden drag update, and any 2027 framing — lands 16 hours later. The after-hours move and the Tuesday close have diverged in each of the last three quarters.
- **Asymmetry:** with the stock +20% in a month, a Hold-rated consensus, targets ~5% above spot, and only a +1.9% organic bar to clear, upside on an in-line print looks capped near the top of the base case while a fourth soft organic quarter re-opens the $60 handle. Implied vol at ±7.8% vs ±5% realized means premium selling is tempting — but the lapping of Accession plus a possible first look at 2027 makes this a genuinely higher-variance print than the historical average suggests.
