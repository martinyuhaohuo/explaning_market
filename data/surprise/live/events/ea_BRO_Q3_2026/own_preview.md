# Brown & Brown (BRO): Q3 2026 Earnings Preview

**Quarter:** Q3 2026 (July–September 2026)
**Release:** Monday, Oct 26, 2026, after market close (21:00 UTC). Conference call Tuesday, Oct 27, 8:00 a.m. EDT. Since the release lands after the knowledge cutoff (20:00 UTC), this preview contains no reported results.

## Consensus Estimates

| Metric | Consensus | Source / date |
|---|---|---|
| EPS | ~$1.08–$1.09 (adjusted) | Earnings Whispers, Benzinga; snapshots from Aug–Oct 2026 |
| Revenue | ~$1.76–$1.79B | Yahoo Finance (mid-Sep, 12 analysts), Benzinga, Earnings Whispers |
| Prior-year EPS | $1.05 | Therighttrader |

Sources disagree by a cent or two, and the snapshots are up to two months old. Confirm against a live feed before trading. The prior quarter (Q2) reported adjusted EPS of $1.07 (GAAP $0.84) and revenue of ~$1.7B, which was an EPS in-line and revenue-light print. Therighttrader says the June quarter missed EPS by 0.9%.

## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Organic revenue growth.** This is the central issue. Q2 organic revenue fell 0.7%, and organic revenue with contingents rose 0.7%. Year to date, organic revenue is down 0.3%. Any return to positive organic growth would be the main positive surprise. A further decline would confirm the thesis that growth is acquisition-driven.
2. **Specialty Distribution (organic).** Q2 fell 3.5%, pressured by lower catastrophe property rates and ~$10M of new-business timing slippage. Watch whether the timing issue reverses.
3. **Retail (organic).** Q2 grew 1.5%. Keeping this steady or accelerating is needed to offset specialty weakness.
4. **Contingent commissions.** Their timing affects the "with contingents" line and can swing quarterly results.
5. **Margins and EPS quality.** Watch the gap between GAAP and adjusted EPS, plus the EBITDAC margin trend.
6. **Commentary on pricing.** Management said E&S casualty rates will keep rising. Pricing in property is softening. Check how they describe the mix.

## Guidance

I could not find a formal full-year organic revenue guide. The only forward item I found was that programs are expected to grow 2–4% organically in 2H 2026. The buy side will want to hear whether that target is reaffirmed, and whether the specialty weakness is viewed as cyclical or structural.

## Scenarios

Revenue and EPS figures below are illustrative, anchored to consensus. They are not company guidance.

| Scenario | Revenue | EPS | Key driver | Stock reaction |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Bull** | ≥ $1.80B | ≥ $1.12 | Organic growth turns positive; specialty timing reverses; contingents strong | Upside of +3% to +6%, but note the history of selling into beats |
| **Base** | ~$1.76–1.79B | ~$1.07–1.10 | Organic roughly flat; retail solid, specialty soft; in-line EPS | Modest move, roughly ±2–3%, direction set by guidance tone |
| **Bear** | < $1.74B | < $1.05 | Organic declines deepen; specialty pressure persists; margin squeeze | Drop of −5% to −8%, consistent with prior post-print declines |

- **Bull needs:** organic growth above 0%, specialty organic improving from −3.5%, and reaffirmed 2H programs growth.
- **Base commentary:** "stabilizing" organic trends, pricing still positive in E&S, and no change to the 2H target.
- **Bear signals:** organic declines widening, a cut to the 2H programs outlook, or new-business weakness spreading to retail.

**Historical context:** Barchart found the stock fell the day after six of the eight quarters it reviewed. The largest drop was −10.4% after the July 2025 report. The stock also fell after a large Q3 2025 beat. After Q4 2025 it fell about 7%. After Q2 2026 it rose about 5% intraday on an in-line EPS print. Beats have not reliably produced gains.

## Catalyst Checklist

1. **Organic revenue vs. ~flat expectations:** Any sign of growth turning positive is the main upside catalyst. A further decline is the main risk.
2. **Specialty Distribution organic vs. −3.5% in Q2:** Whether the timing issue reverses and cat-property rate declines moderate.
3. **2H 2026 programs growth (2–4% target):** Reaffirmed or cut. The buy side will focus on this.
4. **Contingent commission timing:** It can flatter or distort the "with contingents" figure.
5. **Strategic / M&A commentary:** Acquisition pace, since growth has been acquisition-led. Any change to the M&A cadence would be a narrative shift.
