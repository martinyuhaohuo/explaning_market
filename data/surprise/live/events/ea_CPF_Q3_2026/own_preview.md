## Prior Quarter Baseline (Q2 2026)

- **EPS:** $0.80 vs. $0.78 consensus (beat). Up from $0.67 a year ago.
- **Revenue:** $77.45M, up 6.4% year over year.
- **NIM:** 3.57%, up 4 bp sequentially and 13 bp year over year. Confirmed in the 8-K and 10-Q.
- **Net interest income:** $62.8M.

**Guidance from the Q2 call (per third-party call summaries):**
- Full-year NII growth of 4% to 6%, unchanged.
- NIM expected to hold steady or edge up in 2H26, assuming the Fed is on hold.
- Deposit costs expected to stay fairly steady.
- Other operating expense growth of 2.5% to 3.5%, toward the low end.
- Low single-digit loan and deposit growth for the full year.

## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **NIM and NII.** Does NIM hold at or above 3.57%? The guide implies steady to slightly higher NIM in 2H, so a sequential decline would be a negative surprise.
2. **EPS vs. $0.85 to $0.87.** CPF beat in Q4 2025 and Q2 2026. A third beat would support the trend.
3. **Revenue vs. ~$77M.** Watch fee income and non-interest income mix.
4. **Deposit cost and beta.** Any pickup in deposit costs would pressure the NIM outlook.
5. **Credit quality.** Provisions, charge-offs, and nonperforming loans. Not covered in my sources, so check the release directly.
6. **Expense guidance.** Confirm opex growth stays at the low end of 2.5% to 3.5%.
7. **Loan and deposit growth.** Low single digits is the guide. Anything above or below moves the NII outlook.
8. **Capital return.** Buybacks and dividend commentary.
