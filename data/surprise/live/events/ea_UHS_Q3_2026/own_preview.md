## What to Watch (Ranked)

1. **Revenue vs. $4.72B.** Q2 was $4.64B, so consensus implies only about 2% sequential growth. A beat is plausible if the Q2 trend held.
2. **EPS vs. $5.07–5.26.** The range across sources matters. Under $5.07 would likely be read as a miss.
3. **FY26 guidance.** Management lowered the EPS range at Q2. The buy-side will focus on whether they hold, raise, or cut the range. Holding or nudging up is probably the minimum for a positive reaction.
4. **Margins and labor costs.** Watch salary, wage, and benefit trends and the cost of contract labor. Margin compression is the main risk to EPS.
5. **Volume and payer mix.** Behavioral and acute admissions, adjusted patient days, and exchange/Medicaid exposure drive the revenue narrative. I could not verify segment data from the sources I reached, so check the press release and 10-Q.
