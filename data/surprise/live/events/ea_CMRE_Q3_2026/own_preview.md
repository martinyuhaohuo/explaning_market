## Key Metrics to Watch (Ranked)

1. **Charter rates and fixture momentum.** Management said on the Q2 call that rates are firming across most vessel sizes. Q3 revenue depends on how much of that showed up in realized rates versus fixtures still ahead.
2. **Off-hire and dry-dock days.** Q2 revenue fell partly because of more idle and off-hire time, mostly scheduled dry-dockings. Q3 depends on how many of those days fell in the quarter and whether the schedule clears in Q4.
3. **Adjusted EPS vs. $0.66.** Check whether the beat or miss is driven by operations or by one-time items.
4. **Dividend.** The quarterly dividend is $0.125 ($0.50 annualized). Any change to that level, or the policy behind it, will move the stock more than a small EPS variance.
5. **Liquidity and financing.** Liquidity was $423M at Q2. The company closed $920M in new bilateral financing and expects up to $331M more in Q3. It says there are no debt maturities until 2030. Watch for any updated leverage or capital-allocation commentary.
