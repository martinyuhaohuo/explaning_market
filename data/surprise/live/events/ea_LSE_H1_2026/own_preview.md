## 3. Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Revenue trajectory vs. the FY2025 decline.** Is the downturn bottoming? Watch the trend in quarterly revenue, not just the year-over-year number.
2. **Gross margin.** FY2025 gross profit fell sharply. Stabilization or expansion would be the first sign of operating recovery.
3. **Opex discipline.** Opex exceeded gross profit in FY2025, so cost control matters as much as top line.
4. **Overseas expansion and natural gas trading.** Management named these as growth drivers. Look for quantified contribution.
5. **Energy storage capacity build-out.** Long-term targets exist, but watch for timing and capex in the near term.
6. **Cash flow and balance sheet.** Management emphasized low leverage post-IPO; confirm cash levels and operating cash flow.
