## Prior Quarter Context (Q2 2026, reported Aug 5, 2026)

- **EPS:** Reported $9.55, adjusted $9.41. Zacks cited a consensus of $7.68, so Q2 beat by a wide margin.
- **Drivers:** Stronger realized refining margins and higher renewable-fuel regulatory credit values. Midstream also improved on margins and volumes, with record NGL fractionation and LPG exports.
- **Operations:** Refining utilization 96%, clean product yield 86%.
- **Balance sheet:** Total debt down $6.6B to $20.6B; net debt $16.5B. Management targets net debt below $16B by year-end 2026 and more second-half buybacks.
- **Strategic:** Midstream EBITDA run-rate target of $4.5B by end-2027. Golden Triangle Polymers and Ras Laffan Polymers JVs are on schedule for full operations in 2027.

**Data conflict:** AAII labels $9.41 EPS and $51.0B revenue as Q3. That is the Q2 adjusted figure, so the AAII label is wrong. Q3 has not been reported yet.

## What to Watch (ranked)

1. **Refining margin and utilization vs. Q2.** This is the main earnings driver. Q2 had strong realized margins, and Q3 consensus at ~$9.10 implies a step down from Q2's $9.41 adjusted. Watch whether management says margins held into Q3.
2. **Renewable fuels regulatory credit values.** Q2 benefited from higher RIN/credit values. A reversal would hit the bottom line.
3. **Midstream segment EBITDA** vs. the $4.5B run-rate path. Strength here supports the 2027 target.
4. **Capital return and net debt.** Check whether second-half buybacks are being accelerated and whether the sub-$16B net debt target still holds.
5. **Q4 guidance and capture rate commentary.** The buy-side will want Q4 throughput, turnaround timing, and any updated 2026 outlook.
