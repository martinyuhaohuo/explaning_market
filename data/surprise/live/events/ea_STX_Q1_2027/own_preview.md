## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Q2 FY2027 revenue and EPS guidance.** This matters more than the Q1 beat, since Q1 was guided. The buy-side will want a sustained step-up.
2. **Gross margin vs. the 52.7% Q4 level.** Expansion supports the mix and pricing story. Contraction would be read as a warning.
3. **Hard-drive vs. mix and pricing commentary.** Watch capacity-based pricing (HAMR / high-capacity drives) and the demand outlook for data-center and AI storage.
4. **Free cash flow and capex.** Watch FCF conversion against FY26's $3.1B and any capital return signals.
5. **Dilution.** The Exchangeable Notes impact on the share count, and any change to the EPS bridge.

## Scenarios

Figures below are my framing around the company's guidance range, not published estimates.

| Scenario | Revenue | Non-GAAP EPS | Key driver | Stock reaction |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Bull | ≥ $4.2B | ≥ $7.50 | Beat on pricing/mix; Q2 guide well above Street; GM up | Strong upside, likely above the options-implied move |

| Bear | ≤ $4.0B | ≤ $7.10 | Revenue shortfall or GM slip; Q2 guide below Street | Sharp downside |

**What management commentary would signal each case:**
- **Bull:** Reiterated multi-year demand visibility, capacity commitments through 2028, raised FY27 outlook.
- **Base:** Reaffirmed cadence, no change to the FY outlook, normal caution on tariffs or Middle East risk.
- **Bear:** Softer nearline or hyperscale orders, pricing pressure, a margin cut, or a higher tax or interest burden.

**Historical context:** Over its last 17 reports, STX closed up 11 times and down 6, with an average close move of ±7.8% (EarningsWatcher, updated Oct 19, 2026). The stock has beaten on guidance but has not always rallied on the day.
