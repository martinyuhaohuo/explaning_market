## 1. The Setup in One Paragraph

Beta Bionics is a single-product (iLet Bionic Pancreas) AID company that has had a brutal 2026: a January 8 preliminary patient-start miss (-37% in a day), a January 29 FDA Warning Letter made public February 24 (shares to <$9, ~-72% from the $31.99 high), and an ongoing securities class action (class period 7/30/25–2/24/26, lead-plaintiff deadline 11/3/26). The narrative then flipped hard: Mint, the reusable/disposable patch pump, was **cleared by FDA on September 14, 2026** — roughly two quarters ahead of the "end of Q2 2027" launch guide given on the Q2 call — and the company simultaneously **cut FY26 revenue guidance to $121–126M from $131–136M**, attributing the entire ~$10M cut to a Q4 Osborne effect as prospective iLet buyers wait for Mint. A $150M gross equity raise priced at $17.25 on 9/15. The stock has since rallied ~25–30% off that offering price and ~+10% on 10/26 alone, and sits above both the 50-dma ($19.13) and 200-dma ($15.52). **So Q3 itself is a de-risked, pre-guided quarter; the print matters mostly for what management says about the size of the Q4 deferral and the Mint launch economics.**

## 3. Key Metrics to Watch — Ranked

1. **FY26 revenue guidance: held, narrowed, or cut again.** This is the whole print. The 9/14 cut was framed as "up to $10M" of *deferral*, recapturable in 2027. If management holds $121–126M, the Osborne thesis is contained. A second cut would mean the deferral is structural, not timing — and would reset the 2027 $183M consensus too.
2. **New patient starts — and whether they give an absolute number.** Q3'25 was 5,334; Q4'25 was 5,592. The company stopped disclosing absolute NPS after January and switched to sequential ranges (Q1'26: -10% to -20% seq; Q2'26: +10% to +20% seq → ~4,900–6,000, mid ~5,500). On the Q2 call management said Q3 NPS would rise sequentially. Implied Q3'26 of ~5,500–6,200 is only **+3% to +16% Y/Y** — a sharp decel from +68% in Q3'25. Resumption of a hard number would itself be a disclosure-quality positive given the litigation backdrop.
3. **Q3 revenue and the DME/PBP mix.** PBP has been the growth engine (Q1: $10.7M, +181%; Q2: $11.6M, +153%) while DME decelerated hard (Q1 +22%, Q2 +9%). Watch for (a) whether DME turns negative Y/Y, and (b) repeat of the Q2 "pharmacy stocking dynamics" (channel destock) that muddied Q2 supply revenue.
4. **Gross margin.** 59.5% in Q1, 59.0% in Q2 vs. 55.5% in Q3'25. The FY guide (58.5–59.5%) implies H2 can't materially expand further. Mint manufacturing start-up costs and the new semi-automated cleanroom begin to weigh — any sub-58% print invites questions about Mint's structural margin.
5. **Mint launch specifics.** Confirmation of **Q1 2027** full US launch, the **1.5M disposable units** 2027 capacity target, pharmacy-exclusive distribution, and payer coverage path (**~40% of insured lives at launch → at least 50% within year one**). Any quantified 2027 Mint revenue framing is the single biggest swing factor for the multiple.
6. **FDA Warning Letter remediation / 3D Intelligence 510(k).** The 1/29/26 Warning Letter (Irvine, CA; complaint handling, MDR definitions/timing, CAPA, corrections & removals) remains the governance overhang. Mint's clearance while the WL was open is meaningful de-risking, but watch for re-inspection status and timing on the **3D Intelligence 510(k)** (submitted 9/14/26), which powers both iLet 3D and Mint 3D.
7. **Cash, burn, and opex ramp.** Q2 ended with $225.2M; +$140.1M net from the September raise, less ~$20–25M of Q3 burn and Mint capex → **~$330–345M** expected. Opex was $44.4M in Q2 (R&D $10.2M, S&M $24.6M, G&A $9.6M) and should step up on the type 2 pivotal (started July 2026, mid-2027 indication target) and Mint pre-launch hiring.

| **Bull (25%)** | $36.5M+ (+34%) | -$0.48 or better | NPS up >15% seq *with an absolute number disclosed*; FY26 raised/narrowed to the high end (≥$126M) implying the Osborne hit is <$10M; GM ≥59.5%; Mint launch pulled to early Q1 with named pharmacy partners and ≥40% covered lives confirmed | **+12% to +20%** |
| **Base (50%)** | $34.5–35.5M (+27–30%) | -$0.51 to -$0.53 | In-line quarter, FY26 $121–126M reiterated, NPS "up sequentially" without a hard number, GM ~59%, Mint on track for Q1'27 with no new quantification | **-4% to +6%** (fades if Mint detail is thin after a 25%+ pre-print run) |

**Operationally, what distinguishes them:** The bull case requires evidence that the *iLet* base business is still compounding independent of Mint — i.e., DME stabilizing and PBP continuing >100% growth. The bear case is that Mint's early clearance cannibalized iLet demand faster than the "up to $10M in Q4" framing implies, which would show up as a weak September and a soft Q4 guide.

**Management tells to listen for:** language shifting from "deferral" to "transition"; refusing to quantify Q4 Mint-related impact; declining again to give absolute NPS; hedging on "at least 1.5 million disposable units"; any mention of re-inspection or expanded FDA scope.

## 6. Catalyst Checklist

1. **Revenue vs. $34.6–35.4M** — the easy bar; a miss here, before Mint can be blamed, breaks the "execution is fine, it's just timing" narrative.
2. **FY26 guide $121–126M held** — the 9/14 cut must be the last one. A second cut is the main downside tail.
3. **Absolute new patient starts** — disclosure itself is a credibility signal with the class action live; the number vs. 5,334 (Q3'25) tells you whether growth is decelerating or stalling.
4. **Quantified 2027 Mint contribution / launch mechanics** — the stock's $1.1B cap and $30 bull targets are underwritten by Mint, not iLet. Pricing, pharmacy partners, covered-lives math, and the recapture of deferred Q4 revenue are the swing.
5. **Warning Letter remediation + 3D Intelligence 510(k) timing** — resolution removes a discount; an FDA setback re-opens the February lows as a reference point.
