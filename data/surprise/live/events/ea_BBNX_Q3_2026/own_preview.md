## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Revenue vs. ~$35.1M.** Q2 grew 38%, and pharmacy-channel revenue was $11.6M (+153%). Whether pharmacy sustains that growth matters most.
2. **FY26 revenue guidance ($131–136M).** Q3 at consensus implies a steep Q4 ramp to hit the midpoint. Reaffirming without a raise may read as a soft tone.
3. **Gross margin vs. the raised 58.5–59.5% range.** Q2 came in at 59.0%.
4. **Adjusted EBITDA and cash burn.** Q2 adjusted EBITDA was -$17.7M against $225.2M in cash and investments as of June 30. Watch the burn rate.
5. **Patient starts / iLet installed base.** New starts drove Q2 growth; the trend here is the leading indicator.
6. **Pipeline updates.** Type 2 expansion (targeted mid-2027, pivotal trial enrolling since July) and the Mint patch pump (full commercialization by end of Q2 2027, with limited initial pharmacy coverage). Any timeline slip would be a negative surprise.

**What would signal each case**
- **Bull:** Management cites accelerating patient starts, a higher FY revenue range, or an earlier Mint launch.
- **Base:** Reaffirmed FY guidance, steady gross margin, and no change to the Type 2 or Mint timelines.
- **Bear:** Pharmacy growth decelerates sharply, gross margin falls below the raised range, or the Mint or Type 2 timelines slip.

**Historical context:** Post-earnings moves in the sources I found ranged from about +10% (Q3 2025, FXEmpire) to about −6% (Q1 2026, OCBJ). The Q1 2026 move is reported inconsistently across sources. Typical earnings-day moves look like 6–10% in either direction, but the data is thin.

## Catalyst Checklist

1. **Revenue vs. ~$35.1M consensus.** Pharmacy-channel growth is the swing factor.
2. **FY26 guidance ($131–136M).** The buy side will want to see whether the range holds, narrows up, or narrows down.
3. **Gross margin vs. the 58.5–59.5% guide.** Sustained margin expansion supports the bull case.
4. **Mint patch-pump timing and pharmacy coverage.** Any further slippage would hit sentiment.
5. **Type 2 pivotal progress and cash burn.** Watch whether adjusted EBITDA losses narrow as revenue scales.
