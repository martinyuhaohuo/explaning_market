## Key Metrics to Watch — Ranked

1. **Q4 guidance (or the refusal to give one).** Werner has historically guided metric ranges, not EPS. The Oct 19 release gave an EPS range — a precedent. If they guide Q4 EPS and it starts with a 2, consensus cuts 25–40%.
2. **Insurance reserve: dollar size, discrete vs. run-rate, and development trend.** On the Q2 call (July 28) management said insurance and claims expense hit its **lowest level since Q3 2024** with preventable accidents down 45% YoY. Twelve weeks later they took an adverse reserve adjustment on *past* claims. Q2 insurance and claims was $41.4M; anything above ~$55M with soft language about "continued development" reprices the whole multiple. Context: Werner carried a $41.3M liability and $79.2M insurance receivable on the Texas nuclear verdict before the Texas Supreme Court reversed it in 2025 — the market knows this tail is live.
3. **Driver recruitment/retention: is it price or permanent?** Truck availability is down ~40% YoY for van. If this is a structural wage reset rather than a one-quarter hiring push, it caps the 2027 margin story. Watch driver pay per mile and the Q2-cut fleet guidance (+16–18%, down from +23–28%) — another cut signals they cannot staff the trucks they own.
4. **Fuel: lag or leak?** Diesel set a weekly record $6.529/gal the week of Sep 21. Rising diesel is normally a *timing* drag that recovers via surcharge. Management must frame this as recoverable; if they can't, it means surcharge programs are underwater — a contract-structure problem, not a fuel problem.
5. **Segment checkables vs. Q2 guidance:** One-Way revenue per mile +10–13% YoY (Q3 guide) · Dedicated RPTPW +3–5% · Logistics adjusted operating margin back to breakeven from −1.3% · equipment gains tracking $10–14M FY · net capex $215–250M.
6. **Balance sheet / capital allocation.** Total debt $841.3M at 6/30/26 (incl. $48.3M finance leases), pro forma net leverage ~2.0x. No buybacks in Q2; 5.0M shares remain authorized. Dividend $0.14/qtr (~2.0% yield) — paid every quarter since July 1987, so a cut is a last resort, but leverage plus the up-to-$35M FirstFleet earnout (on Apr'26–Mar'27 revenue) limits flexibility.

## Catalyst Checklist

1. **Q4'26 EPS guide vs. $0.47 consensus** — the bridge above says $0.25–0.35. A guide below $0.25, or a refusal to guide after having guided on Oct 19, is the bear trigger.
2. **Insurance reserve dollar amount and the word "discrete"** — buy-side needs this quantified. Unquantified = assumed recurring = multiple compression.
3. **Driver cost framing: "investment" vs. "market rate reset"** — determines whether 2027 consensus of ~$2.09–2.18 survives at all. This number is going lower; the question is to $1.50 or $1.80.
4. **Reaffirmation (or withdrawal) of the mid-single-digit adjusted operating margin ambition** — Q3 came in at ~2.3% by my math against a 2H "toward mid-single digits" commitment made in July. Management must either re-anchor the timeline to 2027 or explain the three-month reversal.
5. **Capital allocation signal** — a buyback restart at ~0.9x book-ish levels with 5.0M shares authorized would be the strongest available confidence signal; silence alongside 2.0x leverage reads defensively.
