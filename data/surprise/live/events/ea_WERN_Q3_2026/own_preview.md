## Metrics to Watch (Ranked)

1. **Q4 / full-year outlook.** The buy-side will focus on whether the $0.18 of headwinds persists and whether fuel and driver costs are recovering. The Q4 guide matters more than the Q3 print.
2. **Operating ratio and cost per mile.** Confirm the margin compression from fuel, insurance, and driver pay, and whether any of it is one-time (the reserve adjustment).
3. **Dedicated vs. truckload mix and freight volumes.** Management commentary on demand and pricing. I did not find segment data.
4. **Driver recruitment and retention costs.** Whether the spend is a temporary investment or a structural cost increase.
5. **Free cash flow and capex.** Check for any change to equipment spending or fleet plans.
