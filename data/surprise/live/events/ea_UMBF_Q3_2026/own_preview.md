## Management Guidance (from the Q2 2026 call)

- **Core NIM:** Expected roughly flat from Q2's 3.09% (ex-accretion).
- **Purchase accounting accretion:** About $46M remaining for the rest of 2026 and $77M in 2027. Q2 had $35.9M, including $10.9M accelerated from early payoffs. This is a headwind to reported NII and margin in Q3–Q4.
- **Operating noninterest expense:** About $390M for Q3.
- **Deposits:** Seasonal pressure in Q3. Coverage differs on severity, so watch the tone.
- **2026 effective tax rate:** 20%–22%.

My rough arithmetic: if accretion falls to about $23M per quarter in Q3–Q4, reported NIM could fall toward about 3.2%, down from 3.32% in Q2. This is an estimate, not company guidance.

## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **EPS vs. ~$3.20–$3.23.** The company has beaten in recent quarters, so a modest beat is the baseline expectation.
2. **Core NIM vs. the 3.09% flat guide.** This is the main test of the margin story. Accretion roll-off is the reported-number risk.
3. **Deposit trends and deposit costs.** Watch for seasonal pressure and whether funding costs hold.
4. **Noninterest expense vs. ~$390M.** Any overrun changes the operating leverage story.
5. **Credit quality and provisions.** The results may show a reserve build or release.
6. **Fee income** (trust, payments, and institutional services). I could not find consensus for these lines.
