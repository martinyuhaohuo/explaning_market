1. **Revenue vs. consensus and timing of Brazil projects.** Management said some Brazil projects slipped into H2. Q3 has to show that shift. A Q3 revenue number below ~$970M would suggest the slip is larger than guided.
2. **Free cash flow.** Q2 FCF was –$11M after inventory build. Watch whether Q3 FCF turns positive and whether inventory is coming down.
3. **Gross margin and memory-cost headwind.** Higher memory costs added about $10M to Q2 expenses. Check whether pricing or cost actions offset it in Q3.
4. **Adjusted EBITDA.** Q2 was $120.6M. Q3 should be higher on seasonality. Watch the margin trend.
5. **FY26 guidance.** The company reaffirmed in July. The buy-side will want a reaffirm or a move toward the top of the EPS range ($5.75) to offset the H1 pace. A cut would be the largest negative.
