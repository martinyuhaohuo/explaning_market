## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Scoliosis revenue rebound.** Q2 was −9%. A return to growth would support the capital-placement-timing explanation. Continued decline would undercut the thesis.
2. **Total revenue vs. ~$68.5M.** Q2 ran at $70.5M, so a Q3 print below Q2 would need explaining. Watch the sequential decline.
3. **Trauma & Deformity growth.** It has been carrying the top line at +26%. Any deceleration is a concern.
4. **Gross margin.** Holding about 74% supports the operating leverage story.
5. **Adj. EBITDA and FCF trajectory.** Confirming the path to FY adj. EBITDA of ~$25M and breakeven FCF is the main test for the guidance raise.
6. **FY guidance.** A raise would be the strongest signal. A reiteration at $265–269M is the base case. A cut would be a major negative.
