## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Gross profit and gross profit per unit, by segment.** Q2 detail:
   - **NAST:** Truckload gross profit per mile +2.0%, LTL gross profit per order +19.5%. Volume: truckload +0.5%, LTL +2.0%.
   - **Global Forwarding:** Ocean gross profit per shipment −4.0%. Air gross profit per metric ton +33.5%. Customs gross profit −9.4%.
2. **Operating expense discipline.** Q2 operating expenses were up 4.2% in NAST and down 6.2% in Forwarding. Watch whether the gain in Q2 operating income holds.
3. **Adjusted vs. GAAP gap.** In Q2 it was $1.61 adjusted versus $1.56 GAAP. Watch the restructuring and acquisition-related charges.
4. **Cash flow.** Q2 cash flow dropped, which tempered the reaction to a beat.
5. **Full-year commentary.** Whether the company reaffirms, narrows, or gives any new numeric outlook. The Q2 release gave none beyond tax and capex.
