## 2. Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Industrial Services Adj. EBITDA — target ~$25–27M.** This is the line that destroyed the stock in July. Q2's $34.4M was flattered by extraordinary terminal volumes (6.67Mt, +20% q/q) tied to coal-pricing and Ukraine-related trade flows, plus seasonal slag sales. Management guided volumes back "closer to Q1 levels" (5.64Mt). **Above $30M = the run-rate debate reopens bullishly; below $24M = guidance cut risk.**
2. **Domestic Coke EBITDA/ton — target $55–60.** Needs Middletown's returned turbine (restarted May), insurance recovery proceeds, and the 51–52% coal-to-coke yields to all persist. Watch how much of the beat is **insurance/one-time** vs. operating yield. Volumes ~840kt and capacity utilization ~100% on the post-Haverhill I base (~3.7Mt capacity) are the quality check.
3. **FY26 guidance action.** Raised to >$265M, narrowed inside $250–265M, or trimmed. With H1 at $126.1M, a simple in-line Q3 (~$66M) puts them at $192M with ~$58–73M needed in Q4 — a *narrowing* is the base case, not another raise.
4. **Granite City 2027 recontracting.** The U.S. Steel agreement (~590kt for calendar 2026) **expires Dec 31, 2026** — 9 weeks after this call. Prior extensions have been announced in late January, so silence is normal but increasingly uncomfortable; Nippon's deal terms require Granite City Works stay open near-term, which is supportive. Any 2027 term sheet is the single largest potential narrative unlock.
5. **Cash conversion and deleveraging.** Revolver drawn $160.5M of $325M (plus $500M 4.875% notes due 2029); cash $42.7M; liquidity ~$207M; net debt ~$618M (~2.4x guided EBITDA). A Q3 OCF print below ~$90M makes the $240–260M FY number very hard.
6. **Algoma.** $90.1M impairment taken in Q4 2025 on Algoma's refusal to take Haverhill I tons; enforcement is being pursued. Any recovery is unmodeled optionality.
