## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Consolidated Adj. EBITDA vs. the FY guide.** Does Q3 keep the run-rate that supports the $250M–$265M range? Any raise would be the main positive signal.
2. **Domestic Coke segment.** Coke production, volumes, and pricing. This is the largest guided segment ($172M–$178M).
3. **Industrial Services.** Guided $110M–$115M. Look for signs of stable or improving utilization.
4. **Full-year guidance update.** Whether management reaffirms, raises, or trims the EBITDA, net income, capex, and operating cash flow ranges.
5. **Free cash flow and capex.** Operating cash flow guide of $240M–$260M against capex of $90M–$100M. Watch working capital and any change to the capex range.
