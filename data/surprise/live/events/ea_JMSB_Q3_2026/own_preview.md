## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **NIM and deposit costs.** Q2 NIM expanded 12 bps q/q. Watch whether Q3 holds near 3%. Deposit growth lagged loan growth in Q2, so funding cost is the key risk to margin.
2. **EPS vs. $0.49–0.50, ex one-time items.** A beat with no one-time gain is the higher-quality signal.
3. **Loan growth and pipeline.** CEO Bergstrom said the "pipeline for the third quarter looks strong." Loan growth above Q2's 8.4% annualized would support the thesis.
4. **Deposit growth.** Q2 was only +1.1% annualized. A pickup would ease the funding concern.
5. **Credit quality.** Any move in NPAs or SBA 7(a) charge-offs (three in Q2, $172K) would matter, since the book is otherwise clean.
6. **Efficiency ratio and noninterest income.** Check whether the 50.5% efficiency ratio holds without the equity gain.
