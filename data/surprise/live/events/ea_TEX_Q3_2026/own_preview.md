## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Revenue vs. ~$2.16–2.19B, and organic vs. acquired growth.** The +50% y/y in Q2 is largely the REV Group acquisition. Investors will want the organic component.
2. **Adjusted EPS vs. ~$1.42–1.44, and margin direction.** Compare operating margin with Q2 and with the integration synergy trajectory.
3. **FY26 guidance:** Whether EPS of $4.70–$5.10 is raised, held, or narrowed. A raise above the top end would be the main upside catalyst.
4. **Segment performance:** Materials Processing and Environmental/Utilities, plus REV-related segments. I did not find segment data, so check the press release.
5. **Free cash flow and working capital**, especially integration-related cash costs.
6. **Tariff and input-cost commentary** (steel and aluminum). I did not find current commentary.

## Catalyst Checklist

1. **Revenue vs. ~$2.16–2.19B consensus:** Organic growth is the key test, since most of the headline y/y growth is acquisition-driven.
2. **Adjusted EPS vs. ~$1.42–1.44:** Margin direction matters more than the headline beat.
3. **FY26 guidance vs. $4.70–$5.10 and the $4.85–4.87 consensus:** A raise is the main upside catalyst. A midpoint cut would likely dominate the reaction.
4. **REV Group integration:** Synergy progress, integration costs, and any restructuring or portfolio changes.
5. **Tariffs and input costs:** Any change to margin outlook from steel and aluminum exposure.
