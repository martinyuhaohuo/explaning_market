## Q2 2026 Baseline

- **NIM (FTE):** 3.94%, down 12 bps QoQ. Management guided to a margin "near 4%" for the rest of 2026 and said the month-end June margin was "right at 4%."
- **NII (GAAP):** $109.3M, up from $108.8M
- **Non-interest income:** $19.8M, up from $18.0M
- **Non-interest expense:** $95.0M reported; $83.7M adjusted, up from $81.5M
- **Efficiency ratio:** 72.3% reported; 61.8% adjusted
- **Loans:** $9.77B, up 1.7% QoQ and 30.5% YoY. Full-year 2026 loan growth guidance is 10%.
- **Deposits:** $10.39B, down 0.7% QoQ. Cost of deposits 1.93%.
- **Provision:** $1.5M, down from $4.0M
- **Net charge-offs:** 0.27% annualized
- **No Q3 or FY2026 numeric guidance** appeared in the release.

Sources: [Q2 8-K exhibit (SEC)](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001475841/000110465926085408/nbhc-20260721xex99d1.htm), [MarketBeat Q2 call summary](https://www.marketbeat.com/instant-alerts/national-bank-q2-earnings-call-highlights-2026-07-22/).

## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **NIM vs. the "near 4%" guide.** This is the main earnings driver. A print below ~3.9% would challenge the outlook. Watch the Q3 exit margin and deposit beta.
2. **Adjusted EPS vs. the ~$0.69–0.78 range.** Confirm the consensus basis and check how much of the gap is from acquisition or restructuring charges.
3. **Loan growth vs. the 10% full-year target.** Q2 growth was only 1.7% QoQ, so H2 needs to accelerate.
4. **Deposit trends.** Deposits fell in Q2. Any further outflow pressures funding costs and the margin.
5. **Expense discipline and efficiency.** Adjusted non-interest expense rose in Q2. The adjusted efficiency ratio of ~62% is the key cost metric.
6. **Credit quality.** Provision fell to $1.5M in Q2. Watch the charge-off trend and any reserve build.

## Scenario Analysis

| Scenario | Revenue | EPS (adj.) | Key Driver | Stock Reaction |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Bull | Above ~$136M | Above ~$0.75 | NIM holds at or above 4%, loans re-accelerate toward the 10% target, costs flat | Positive; a clean beat likely lifts the stock several percent |
| Base | ~$130–136M | ~$0.69–0.75 | NIM near 4%, modest loan growth, expenses flat to up | Muted; move likely within ±3% |
| Bear | Below ~$130M | Below ~$0.69 | NIM slips below 3.9%, deposit outflows, loan growth stalls, provisions rise | Negative; a miss likely drops the stock 3–5% |

- **Bull signals:** Management reaffirms or raises the margin outlook and the 10% loan growth target, with a strong Q4 pipeline.
- **Base signals:** A reaffirmed "near 4%" margin and a loan growth commentary that stays vague.
- **Bear signals:** A margin outlook cut, a deposit cost increase, or a credit reserve build.

1. **NIM vs. the "near 4%" outlook.** This determines whether the margin story holds.
