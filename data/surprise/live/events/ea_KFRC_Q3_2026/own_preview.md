## Context from Q2 FY2026 (reported Jul 27)

- Revenue $349.3M, up 4.5% YoY. EPS $0.73, against a ~$0.70 consensus.
- Operating margin 5.4%, up 180 bps sequentially and 90 bps YoY.
- Job orders and new assignment starts both up 18% YoY, per CEO Joe Liberatore ([Barchart](https://www.barchart.com/story/news/3486049/kforce-reports-second-quarter-2026-revenue-of-349-3-million-up-4-5-year-over-year)).
- Management said Q3 revenue would grow sequentially and that YoY growth would accelerate. Q3 has 64 billing days, the same as Q2 and Q3 2025.
- Quarterly dividend of $0.40 declared, payable Sep 25.

## Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Revenue vs. guidance midpoint (~$353M) and consensus.** Does the number land in the upper half, and is the sequential and YoY acceleration confirmed?
2. **Adj. EPS vs. $0.71 guide floor.** Consensus sits near or below the floor, so a beat requires operating margin toward the top of 5.3%–5.7%.
3. **Gross margin, 28.1%–28.3% guide.** Gross-margin mix (Flex vs. Direct Hire, consulting, AI talent) drives operating leverage.
4. **Job orders and starts momentum.** The +18% YoY figure from Q2 is the leading indicator. Check whether it held or accelerated.
5. **Q4 guidance.** Buy-side will focus on whether the margin trajectory and revenue acceleration continue into Q4.
