## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Adj. EPS vs. consensus.** Q2 was a record, so a beat-and-hold would be the base case.
2. **FY EPS guidance ($4.50–$4.70).** Reaffirm, raise, or cut. A raise is the main bull trigger; a narrowing toward the low end is the main bear trigger.
3. **Organic sales growth.** Q2 was +3.5%. Compare Q3 organic growth to that rate, separating M&A from underlying volume.
4. **Water Systems vs. Fueling Systems segment mix.** Water is the core driver of the thesis; check margins and any Fueling weakness.
5. **Gross and operating margin.** Q2 operating income rose faster than sales, so watch whether margins hold as acquisitions are integrated.
6. **Free cash flow and M&A pipeline.** Acquisition spending and leverage matter given the tuck-in strategy.
7. **Commentary on Middle East and South America.** Any change in order trends there.
