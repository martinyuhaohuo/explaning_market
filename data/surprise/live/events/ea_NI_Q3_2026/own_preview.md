## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Adj. EPS vs. ~$0.22.** The main print. Q2 was in line, so a clean beat would be a change in trend.
2. **FY2026 guidance ($2.02–$2.07).** Reaffirmation at the midpoint is the base case. Management said earnings growth is weighted to H2, so Q3 and Q4 matter more than usual.
3. **Weather and usage.** Q2 GAAP EPS of $0.09 (vs. $0.22 a year ago) was hurt by weather, higher O&M, depreciation, and interest. Watch whether these headwinds persisted into Q3.
4. **Data center pipeline.** The 2026–2030 plan is $28.6B: $21.0B base capex and $7.6B strategic data center infrastructure. Any new signed agreements or capex upsizing is the main upside narrative.
5. **Long-term growth.** Reaffirmation of the 9–10% adj. EPS CAGR (2026–2033).
6. **Margins and cash flow:** Not sourced. Check the press release for operating margin and FCF trends against Q2.

## Scenario Analysis

| Scenario | Revenue | Adj. EPS (Q3) | Key Driver | Stock Reaction (my judgment, not sourced) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Bull** | Beat (≥$1.41B) | ≥$0.24 | Weather normalizes; data center wins | +2% to +4% |
| **Base** | In line (~$1.35B) | $0.22–$0.23 | Guidance reaffirmed at $2.02–$2.07 | ±1–2% |
| **Bear** | Miss (<$1.35B) | ≤$0.20 | Weather/O&M drag; guidance trimmed to low end | −2% to −5% |

- **Bull:** Management would signal strong data center execution and possibly raise guidance toward the top of the range. Watch for specifics on signed contracts rather than pipeline language.
- **Base:** Commentary emphasizes H2 weighting and reaffirms guidance. This is the most likely outcome given Q2.
- **Bear:** Language about "timing" of earnings, rising O&M, or cost pressure, plus a cut to the low end of guidance.

**Historical context:** One source puts NI's average absolute Day-0 earnings move at about 1.6%. Q1 2026 was about −1.4% on Day 0. I could not find a reliable list of prior reactions, so these moves are approximate.

## Catalyst Checklist

1. **Q3 adj. EPS vs. ~$0.22:** A beat would likely be read as the H2-weighted story is on track.
2. **FY2026 guidance:** Reaffirm vs. raise vs. cut. Holding $2.02–$2.07 is the expected outcome.
3. **Data center capex and contracts:** The $7.6B strategic investment is the core growth driver. Any upsizing or new signed agreements is a positive catalyst.
4. **Weather normalization:** Whether the Q2 drag reversed. Management commentary on Q4 weather would matter.
5. **Long-term CAGR (9–10%):** Reaffirmation keeps the thesis intact. A cut would matter more than a Q3 miss.
