## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Net long-term flows.** Q2 was a record +$45.1B (8.5% organic growth). Watch whether ETFs/Index (+$30.1B) and QQQ (+$13.8B) hold up. Fundamental Equities had −$7.7B, and that is the main weak spot.
2. **Adjusted operating margin.** Q2 was 37.5%, up from 34.5% in Q1. Management's stated focus is "high 30s." A print at or above 38% supports the bull case.
3. **Average AUM and net revenue yield.** Ending AUM was $2.47T, but average AUM was $2.37T. Q3 revenue depends on average AUM, which carries the Q2 market rally into the quarter. Yield was not disclosed; my derived estimate for Q2 is about 56 bps per quarter.
4. **Cost outlook.** Platform modernization costs are expected at about $15M per quarter in H2 2026. Any upward revision would pressure the margin story.
5. **Capital return and balance sheet.** Net debt fell to $708.6M. Buybacks are up 80% year-to-date. Watch for a larger buyback authorization.

Invesco gave no formal Q3 or FY2026 guidance in Q2, so the call commentary matters more than usual.

## Catalyst Checklist

1. **Q3 flows vs. the Q2 record ($45.1B).** A sequential decline would be read as a slowdown even if it is positive in absolute terms.
2. **Margin vs. the 37.5% Q2 print.** Any step back would be the most likely negative surprise given the Q2 beat.
3. **Commentary on Fundamental Equities outflows.** This is the largest question for the active-management thesis.
4. **Buybacks and capital return.** Net debt is down, so a larger buyback is a credible positive.
5. **Canadian sale and China JV.** The CI Global sale reduced AUM by $11.0B, and China JV equity earnings were $35.1M in Q2. Watch whether both show up in the Q3 run-rate.
