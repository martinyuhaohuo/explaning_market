## 3. Key Metrics to Watch — Ranked

**1. Net revenue yield (the whole print).** Q2 annualized net revenue yield was 22.45bps ($1,329.1M × 4 / $2,368.8B). Consensus $1.385B on $2,510.2B average AUM implies **22.07bps — about 0.4bp of compression baked in.** Hold the yield flat and net revenue is ~$1.41B, roughly 2% above consensus. This single variable is the swing factor, because the asset base is already disclosed.

**2. Adjusted operating margin.** 37.5% in Q2 (up from 34.5% in Q1). Management is "squarely focused" on pushing into the **high 30s**. Constraint: comp guided to ~40% of revenue for FY26 scales with the top line, so leverage must come from non-comp lines — against ~$15M of Alpha/hybrid platform implementation costs plus ~$10M/qtr incremental platform opex in H2.

**3. Mix and the distribution ratio.** Guided to 22.7%–23.0% and "trending toward the high end as the mix shifts toward lower-fee products." Confirmation at the high end validates the compression consensus assumes; a low-end print is the beat.

**4. QQQ competitive position.** BlackRock and State Street are selling Nasdaq-100 exposure at 0.10% against QQQ's 0.18% (post-conversion), with the Nasdaq license alone ~8bps for everyone. The December 2025 UIT→open-end conversion was worth ~4bps / ~$180M annualized to Invesco; this is the second full quarter of that benefit. Management has refused to cut fees, leaning on total-cost-of-ownership and the QQQM barbell (+$75B of inflows without visibly cannibalizing QQQ).

**5. Fundamental equities.** −$7.7B in Q2, and the $1.79B Q4 2025 intangible impairment on U.S. retail mutual funds says management already marked this franchise down. Any worsening undercuts the "mix is fine" argument.

**Base case sits above consensus at ~$0.77.** Bridge from Q2: net revenue +5% q/q to ~$1.40B; comp at ~40% of net revenue (~$558M); other adjusted expenses up ~$16M on platform ramp; ~$15M net interest; 24% tax; ~$44M quarterly preferred dividend on the remaining $3B; ~448M diluted shares. That nets ~$0.76–0.78. The disclosed +6.0% average AUM is doing a lot of work that the $0.74 consensus appears to under-credit.

**Reasons for lower confidence:** the seven-day downward estimate revision suggests a sell-side fee-rate view not visible in public data, and the comp-scales-with-revenue dynamic caps the operating leverage that would otherwise make the beat larger.

**Operational prerequisites by scenario** — Bull needs the September flow stall to have been month-specific (market-driven, not franchise-driven) plus a capital-return announcement. Bear needs the ETF/index and money-market inflows that drove Q3 to have been materially more dilutive to yield than the Street assumes.

## 5. Catalyst Checklist

1. **Net revenue vs. $1.385B** — the only real top-line surprise vector given AUM is pre-released. Watch the implied yield, not the dollar figure.
2. **Adjusted operating margin vs. 37.5% baseline** — the buy-side wants proof of the "high 30s" path, not just a beat.
3. **October month-to-date flow commentary** — after a −$1.1B September, the call Q&A will push hard on whether the long-term flow engine stalled or just hit a market-driven air pocket.
4. **$3B remaining MassMutual preferred** — management guided to "later this year or early next year," contingent on finishing the revolver paydown. At 5.9%, retiring it removes ~$177M/yr of preferred dividend. **This is the most underpriced catalyst on the list** and the one most likely to move the stock independent of the quarter.
5. **Q4/FY27 expense framing** — Alpha implementation is targeted to complete at year-end 2026. A clean "costs roll off" statement is a 2027 EPS unlock; a slip is a credibility hit.

Secondary: buyback pace (only $50M in Q2 against a 60% combined payout target — light, and a visible sore point), private markets traction (INCREF at $6B incl. leverage; Barings and LGT wealth/DC distribution).

## 6. Trading Setup

- **Positioning:** Stock is ~13% off its Aug 27 all-time high of $33.31 but +~9% YTD from ~$26.30, and rallied 2.6% into the print on Oct 26 — some good news is already in the last two sessions.
- **Implied move:** IV30 was **38.7%** as of Oct 23 (Nov 20 ATM 38.7%, up 5% on the day; Dec 18 at 39.0%). The actual event straddle was not obtainable. Mechanically, 38.7%/√252 ≈ 2.4% for an average day; earnings days typically price 1.5–2x that, implying a **rough ±4% expected move**. Treat as derived.
- **Key historical calibration:** last quarter Invesco beat by **+7.2%** ($0.71 vs. $0.666), posted record $45.1B inflows — **and the stock fell 2.3% the next day**, then drifted −1.7% over 90 days. A beat alone has not been enough. Valuation at 9.6x forward is undemanding, but the market has been paying for *fee-rate durability and margin*, not for AUM or headline EPS.
- **Asymmetry:** with AUM known, the EPS distribution is narrower than IV implies, which argues the options are modestly rich into the event. The genuine tail risk is not the quarter — it's a QQQ fee concession or a soft October flow number, neither of which shows up in the numbers table.
