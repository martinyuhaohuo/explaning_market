## 3. Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

**1. Anchor occupancy — the single most important number.** Leased anchor occupancy fell to 98.4% (Q1 and Q2'26) from **99.2%** in Q3'25. Portfolio occupancy: 97.6% → 97.1% → 97.3%. Inline is at a record 95.5%, so inline can't carry much more. Whether anchor boxes re-let (and when rent commences) drives the 2027 NOI bridge. PECO did *not* disclose an SNO/NOI backlog figure in the Q2 supplemental — push for it on the call.

**2. FY guidance action.** After reaffirming on Oct 1, management is unlikely to raise meaningfully. Base case is a **narrow to the upper half** ($2.75–2.79). An actual raise above $2.79 would be a genuine positive surprise; any trim signals anchor/credit stress.

**3. Rent spreads vs. a tough comp.** Q2'26: new 33.7%, renewal 21.2% (YTD new 34.5%). Q3'25 renewal was a **record 23.2%**. Renewal spreads below ~20% would read as decelerating pricing power. Retention was 89.6% in Q2 vs. 93.9% in Q3'25.

**4. JV/recycling accretion math.** $600–700M acquisitions funded partly by $200–250M dispositions + $200–250M net JV contributions. Key questions: cap-rate spread on in vs. out, the recurring fee run-rate (consensus has fees +24.7% YoY), and the timing drag on Q4/1H27 FFO. The JV closed in Q4, so **Q3 results won't reflect it** — only guidance detail will.

**5. Credit/bad debt.** Collectibility guide narrowed to $4–7M. Kimco's Q2 credit loss of 57bps and a narrowed 55–75bps FY guide are a supportive sector read-through; Wells Fargo expects sector-wide FY26 raises on lower bad debt and early rent commencements.

**Secondary:** Net debt/EBITDAre (5.1x in Q2, down from 5.2x at YE25 — watch for drift toward 5.3x+ on acquisition funding); $857.3M liquidity; 95.9% fixed-rate debt at 4.4% WA rate, 5.6-yr maturity.
