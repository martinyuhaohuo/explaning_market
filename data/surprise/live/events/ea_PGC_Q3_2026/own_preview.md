## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **NIM vs. guidance.** Management guided to a 6–9 bp expansion through year-end versus Q1. Q1 NIM was about 3.26%, so that implies roughly 3.32–3.35% by Q4. Q2 already sits at 3.32%, which leaves little room for further expansion. Confirm the exact wording on the call, because the math is tight and the market will test it.
2. **Deposit costs and funding mix.** Management flagged a more competitive deposit market. Watch cost of deposits and the pace of the ~$200M quarterly deposit growth target.
3. **Loan growth and origination yields.** Target is about $300M of loan growth. New originations were just north of 6% in Q2. Yield on new production is the main driver of margin expansion, so watch whether it holds above 6%.
4. **Credit quality.** Provisions, NPLs, and charge-offs. Earlier coverage flagged credit risks as a theme.
5. **Fee income and expenses.** Wealth management fees and non-interest expense. Operating leverage needs expenses to grow more slowly than revenue.
6. **Capital path to targets.** The company says it is on track for 1% ROA and 10% ROE by late 2026 or early 2027. Any change to that timeline would matter a lot.

## Catalyst Checklist

1. **NIM (3.32% in Q2) vs. the implied Q3 path.** The buy side will want evidence that expansion continues. A flat print would be read as a stall in the margin story.
2. **EPS vs. $0.93–0.97.** A miss of a few cents would echo Q2's small miss and could weigh on the stock.
3. **Deposit growth (~$200M target) and deposit cost trends.** Competitive pressure is the main risk to the margin thesis.
4. **Loan growth (~$300M target) and new-origination yields (>6%).** Confirms the earning-asset engine.
5. **Reaffirmation of 1% ROA / 10% ROE timing.** A key narrative point. Any slip would matter more than a small EPS miss.
