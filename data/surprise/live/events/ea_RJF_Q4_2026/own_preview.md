## What to Watch (Ranked)

1. **EPS vs. $3.35 and the mix of the beat.** Q3's beat leaned on asset management fees. Check whether Q4 holds up, given that the Q3 beat was partly fee-driven.
2. **Private Client Group margin (14.9% in Q3).** This is the largest segment. Watch for a margin move, the financial advisor count, and recruiting. Management cited "robust" recruiting.
3. **Capital Markets / investment banking.** The 10.1% margin is the weakest segment. Management cited a "strong" IB pipeline, so a banking rebound would be a positive surprise.
4. **Bank NII and deposit trends.** NII is $560M firmwide. Watch the rate sensitivity and loan growth commentary.
5. **Client assets and net new assets.** These drive asset management and admin fee revenue, which is the largest revenue line (~$2.1B in Q3).
6. **Capital return.** The pace of buybacks at current prices and any dividend commentary.

Management gave **no numerical FY26 Q4 guidance** in Q3, only qualitative "significant momentum." The buy-side will likely focus on the color given for FY27 expense and comp trends.
