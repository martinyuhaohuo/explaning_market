## Company Guidance (reaffirmed July 29)

- GAAP revenue: $1.173B–$1.179B
- Adj. EBITDA: $430M–$438M
- Non-GAAP EPS: $5.15–$5.25 (management expects the high end)
- FCF: $280M–$290M (management expects the high end)
- Management expects to finish in the upper half of all ranges. It gave no explicit Q3 guide.

## Last Quarter (Q2 FY26, reported Jul 29)

- GAAP revenue $290.6M, +3.0%; organic +3.0%
- Recurring revenue $285.3M, +3.3%, 98.2% of total
- Non-GAAP EPS $1.33, up $0.11 y/y
- Adj. EBITDA $110.3M, margin 38.0% (down 70 bps)
- FCF $75.3M, margin 25.9% (up 760 bps); Rule of 40 at 41.0%
- Shares repurchased: just over 6% of shares outstanding in H1

## What to Watch (ranked)

1. **Revenue vs. ~$293M, and organic recurring growth.** Q2 grew 3%. Holding ~3% is the baseline. Any acceleration matters most.
2. **Full-year guide.** Watch for a raise above $5.25 EPS or a move to the high end of FCF. Reaffirmation with "upper half" language is the base case.
3. **Adj. EBITDA margin.** It fell 70 bps in Q2. Confirm whether AI investment is compressing margins.
4. **FCF conversion.** Q2 FCF was strong. Check whether Q3 holds up, since H2 FCF is needed to reach the high end.
5. **AI and "Agents for Good" commentary.** Management said the outlook assumes no meaningful 2026 revenue from these products. Any revenue timing or pricing news would be a narrative shift. The Student First higher-ed partnership and strategic investment are also new.

| **Bull** | ≥$296M (organic ~4%+) | ≥$1.33 | Renewal and retention strength; FY EPS raised above $5.25 | Sharp upside; prior July move was strongly positive |

**Operational signals:** Bull = recurring growth at or above 3.5%, stable margins, and specific AI monetization timing. Base = steady 3% growth and "upper half" language repeated. Bear = recurring growth slipping below 3%, margin guidance cut, or "AI revenue is later" pushed out.

## Catalyst Checklist

1. **Revenue vs. ~$293M consensus:** Organic growth rate is what the buy-side will judge, since the stock is reacting to recovery.
2. **FY26 guide:** Raise, reaffirm, or trim. Management has signaled upper half, so a raise is the upside trigger.
3. **Adj. EBITDA margin trend:** Margin down 70 bps in Q2. A further decline would be a negative.
4. **AI product monetization timing:** Any change to the "no meaningful 2026 revenue" assumption.
5. **Capital return:** Buyback pace after the 6% H1 reduction, and whether it continues.
