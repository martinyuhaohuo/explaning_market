## 2. Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Q4'26 revenue/EPS guide vs. $3.86B / $4.34.** This is the whole print. A midpoint at or below ~$3.80B reads as deceleration into 2027 and validates the Citi bear case; ≥$3.95B re-opens the structural-growth narrative.
2. **Gross margin path toward the 60% 2027 target.** Guide mid is 58.5%; watch whether utilization (tracking to mid-80s) and mix offset substrate/precious-metal input inflation. Any walk-back of 60% is the single biggest de-rating risk.
3. **Automotive y/y and the "company-specific growth drivers" bucket.** Was 47% of auto revenue growing in the low-20s% in Q2. Deceleration here undercuts the content-growth-over-cycle thesis, since reported auto growth is only ~12% (17% ex-MEMS divestiture).
4. **Supply/lead times vs. channel inventory.** Lead times >16 weeks in parts of the portfolio and customer escalations doubled q/q in Q2. Rising lead times with flat 11-week channel inventory = genuine tightness (bullish); but supply can also *cap* upside to the quarter. Watch for any channel re-stock above 11 weeks, which the market will discount as low-quality revenue.
5. **Industrial & IoT / edge-AI pipeline.** ~50% of industrial revenue is already "physical AI"-enabled processors; $1.5B design-win pipeline across 200+ customers. TI's read-across is supportive: industrial +40% y/y, data center +95% y/y (Oct 21).
6. **Secondary:** FCF conversion (>20% of sales), buyback pace (only $104M in Q2 — light), VSMC/ESMC JV capex (~$2.4B committed, 70% of plan), and any 2027 framework given at the call.

## 4. Catalyst Checklist

1. **Q4 revenue midpoint vs. $3.86B** — the sole determinant of whether the "structural, not cyclical" framing survives. Whisper is modestly above consensus (~$3.90B) given TI's upside guide and sector momentum; in-line is likely sold.
2. **Reaffirmation of 60% gross margin and double-digit growth for 2027** — buy-side needs this verbatim. Cantor argues 2028 EPS power is ~$23 vs. $20.49 consensus; that bull case lives or dies on the GM commitment.
3. **Data-center / edge-AI quantification** — Citi downgraded to Neutral (Oct 8, PT $370→$260) explicitly on *limited data-center exposure* and slower estimate revisions. Any first-time sizing of data-center or physical-AI revenue directly rebuts the downgrade.
4. **Volkswagen / S32K5 ramp economics** — VW Group named S32K5 the zonal-controller MCU for its RV Tech-developed SDV architecture (announced Oct 26, first vehicle 2027). Management has said S32N/S32K5 "haven't begun ramping." Dollar content, timing, and whether this is incremental to the existing pipeline are the questions.
5. **Supply tightness vs. demand pull-forward** — lead times >16 weeks and doubling escalations are bullish *if* backed by backlog, bearish if they signal 2027 air-pocket risk from early ordering.
