## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Revenue vs. guide midpoint (~$3.75–3.80B).** Upper half of the range supports the beat narrative.
2. **Q4 revenue and EPS guide vs. consensus.** Probably the main stock driver. The market punished the Q2 print despite a beat, so guidance matters more than the quarter.
3. **Gross margin (Q2 was 58%).** Watch for expansion or compression, plus commentary on cost and supply-chain pressure.
4. **Automotive vs. industrial/IoT vs. mobile split.** Investors flagged limited auto restocking and mobile weakness. Check whether auto recovered.
5. **Book-to-bill and backlog.** Management cited book-to-bill above 1 and growing backlog in Q2. Confirmation supports the recovery thesis.
6. **Free cash flow and capital return.** Not researched in this pass.
