## Context from Q2 2026 (reported July 29)

- **Net loss:** $(1.61) per diluted share, driven by a $256.7M pre-tax securities loss tied to the repositioning. Headline EPS was well below the ~$0.40 estimate, but the loss was a one-time item.
- **NIM:** 3.13%, down 11 bp from 3.24% in Q1. Drivers were nonaccrual interest reversals and higher short-term funding, partly offset by late-quarter core deposit growth.
- **Net interest income:** $250.5M, down $1.1M quarter over quarter.
- **Guidance (from a third-party summary, not verified against the company's release):** NIM rising to about 3.30% once the targeted loan sale closes, a 3.30–3.40% range by year-end, CET1 of 9.45–9.60% by Q3 end, and PTPP income of $125–130M in Q4.
- **Q1 2026 reference:** EPS of $0.39 versus a $0.38 estimate.

## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Closing of the loan sale and the NIM bridge.** Does the company confirm the sale closed and the NIM is moving toward ~3.30%? This is the main thread for the quarter.
2. **Core EPS vs. $0.45 and the GAAP/adjusted gap.** Check how the company defines adjusted earnings and whether the repositioning charges and any residual losses are excluded.
3. **Revenue vs. ~$305M.** Management has missed on revenue in recent quarters, so the reaction may depend on whether NII holds up.
4. **CET1 and tangible book value.** Check whether CET1 is tracking toward 9.45–9.60% and how the loan sale affects capital.
5. **Credit quality.** Nonaccruals, NPAs, provision, and charge-offs. Q2 nonaccrual interest reversals hurt NIM, so any new migration would be a negative.
6. **Deposits and funding costs.** Core deposit growth versus reliance on short-term borrowings.
7. **Q4 PTPP guidance ($125–130M, if confirmed).** Buy-side will focus on whether the 2027 earnings path is credible.
