## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Merger status and pro forma figures.** The Q2 release said the Signature merger closing was scheduled for August 1, 2026. I did not confirm that it closed. Q3 will be the first clean read on the combined entity, so consensus may not be on a like-for-like basis.
2. **Net interest margin.** Q2 NIM was 5.96%, reduced about 10 bps by excess cash. A third-party summary (Quartr) suggests a combined NIM of about 5.45% on day one, improving as litigation assets grow. That figure is unverified against the company's own disclosure.
3. **Litigation finance book.** Q2 balance was $1.29B at an 8.8% blended yield. Growth and credit performance here drive the investment case.
4. **Credit quality.** Q2 included an elevated provision tied to one multifamily nonaccrual loan and a charge-off. Watch for whether it was isolated and whether nonaccruals are rising.
5. **Total loans and deposit growth.** Q2 loans were $1.90B, up $87M linked-quarter. Core deposit growth funded the excess cash.
6. **Efficiency and expenses.** Watch merger-related costs (Q2 had $1.1M pretax) and whether they roll off.
7. **Full-year EPS commentary.** Consensus of $6.77–$7.10 implies a wide range of expectations.
