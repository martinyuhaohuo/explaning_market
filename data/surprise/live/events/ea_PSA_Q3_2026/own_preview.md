## Prior Quarter (Q2 2026, reported Jul 29, 2026)

- **Core FFO:** $4.17/share, down 2.6% YoY, citing higher financing costs and G&A.
- **Same-store revenue:** -0.6% YoY. Same-store NOI: -2.2%. Both beat internal expectations.
- **Occupancy:** 92.5%, up 20 bps YoY.
- **Move-in rents:** +1.6% YoY, the first YoY rise in both move-in rates and occupancy since 2021.
- **Guidance raised:** FY2026 Core FFO to $16.75–$17.05 (midpoint $16.90, up 1.4%). Same-store revenue midpoint -0.2%, NOI midpoint -1.1%. Guidance excludes the NSA and PS Canada deals.

Sources: [AAII](https://www.aaii.com/investingideas/article/471329-what-you-need-to-know-about-public-storages-q2-earnings), [Barchart Q2 release](https://www.barchart.com/story/news/3533725/public-storage-reports-second-quarter-2026-results-and-raises-guidance), [Nasdaq call highlights](https://www.nasdaq.com/articles/public-storage-q2-earnings-call-highlights).

**Source conflict:** Quartr reports 2026 same-store NOI guidance of 0.9%–2.0%. The press-release table shows -2.0% to -0.3%. I trust the press-release table.

## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Core FFO vs. ~$4.27 consensus.** Verify the metric label before using it. Q2 was $4.17, so a print near $4.25 would be roughly flat sequentially.
2. **FY2026 Core FFO guidance** (midpoint $16.90). A raise or a hold at the top of the range matters more than the Q3 beat.
3. **Same-store revenue and NOI trajectory.** Watch whether the -0.2% / -1.1% guidance midpoints hold, given Q2 beat internal plans.
4. **Move-in rate growth vs. occupancy.** Management's guidance assumes low-double-digit move-in rate growth plus +30 bps occupancy. Any deceleration in move-in rates is the main risk.
5. **Financing costs and G&A.** These drove the Q2 Core FFO decline.
6. **Acquisition update.** Closing timing for the $1.2B PS Canada deal, and whether guidance is updated to include it.
