The $80M spread between published revenue consensus figures tells you sell-side models are not aligned on (a) how much of NSA's ~1,100-property portfolio PSA consolidates versus the 313-property, $3.3B JV where OP unitholders hold ~80%, and (b) Canada stub-period timing. **Do not trade the revenue headline.** Trade Core FFO, same-store, and guidance.

That makes the guidance narrowing, not the Q3 number, the primary event.

## 3. Key metrics to watch, ranked

**1. Same-store revenue growth (Q2: −0.6%).** Management guided to sequential improvement with Q4 turning positive. Anything worse than roughly −0.3% in Q3 breaks the "stabilization into recovery" narrative management sold on the Q2 call. Note the 2026 same-store pool **excludes NSA entirely** — so this is a clean organic read.

**2. Move-in rent growth (Q2: +1.6%, first positive since 2021; June +4%).** The single best forward indicator of 2027 revenue. Management guided to continued strength. Sector peers corroborate the direction — Extra Space +~1%, CubeSmart +1.7% on new-customer rates. A deceleration back toward flat would be the most bearish single data point in the release.

**3. Same-store expenses (guide 2.0–3.0%).** Management explicitly pre-announced that **Q3 is the toughest expense comp of the year**, with property tax timing a Q2 headwind and new marketing spend from the April promotional program. Expense upside here is partly telegraphed; the question is whether ML-driven payroll savings (~30% hour reduction over time) offset new PS 4.0 incentive comp.

**4. NSA integration and synergy confirmation.** Guided to **$110–130M run-rate synergies over 3–4 years** and **$0.35–$0.50 Core FFO accretion at stabilization**, but roughly **neutral to 2026 Core FFO** (plus ~$0.02 from financing optimization). Watch the ~14,000 units targeted for reactivation, NSA performance on the PS Next platform, and whether any synergy is pulled forward. The transition moved 265,000 autopay accounts on day one — execution risk is largely behind them, which raises the bar for positive surprise.

**5. Occupancy (Q2: 92.5%, +20bps; guide +30bps; Q3 consensus 92.4% vs 90.7% prior-year pool).** Churn fell 8% in Q2 on customer-experience initiatives. Occupancy plus positive move-in rate simultaneously is the regime change PSA bulls are paying for.

**6. Los Angeles pricing recapture.** Rent restrictions expired July 1, 2026 — Q3 is the first quarter of the "measured and phased" recapture. Guided drag improved from −80bps to −50bps for 2026 and framed as a 2027 tailwind. Any quantification of recapture pace is a genuine 2027 estimate input.

**Secondary:** Sun Belt trajectory (still negative, not expected positive by year-end), ancillary revenue ($92.7M consensus, +8.8%), development pipeline ($692M / 47 projects), leverage (net debt/EBITDA 2.9x; 4.2x incl. preferred; $3.8B liquidity), third-party management (460+ properties), and any first-pass 2027 framing.

| Scenario | Core FFO | Same-store rev | FY26 guide | Key driver | Est. stock reaction |

| **Bear** (25%) | $4.18–4.24 | −0.8% or worse | Cut to **$16.70–$16.90** or midpoint lowered | Move-in rent growth stalls; Sun Belt deteriorates; expenses >3%; NSA share-count dilution outruns synergy capture; cautious 2027 setup | **−5% to −9%** |

**Historical calibration — the skew is negative.** PSA has beaten the Core FFO consensus in 5 of the last 8 quarters, yet the stock has repeatedly sold off on good prints: Q3 2025 beat ($4.31 vs $4.24) with guidance raised and the stock dipped; Q2 2026 (July 29) printed $4.17 and fell **−3.63%** on the day with a **−6.63%** max drawdown, despite guidance being raised $0.22 at the midpoint. **A beat alone has not been sufficient.** The share-count dilution from an all-stock NSA deal (0.14 PSA per NSA share, $41.68/sh consideration) and $900M of OP units for Canada means per-share accretion, not absolute NOI, is what the buy-side is grading.

## 5. Catalyst checklist

1. **FY2026 Core FFO guidance action** — the whole print. Narrowing to a midpoint above ~$16.93 is a positive; anything that lowers the midpoint is a clear negative given the 2H acceleration already embedded.
2. **Move-in rent growth vs. the +1.6% Q2 base** — the buy-side expects continued sequential improvement given June ran +4%. Flat-to-negative is the bear trigger.
3. **Same-store revenue inflection credibility** — Q3 must land at or better than roughly −0.4% for the "Q4 turns positive" guide to stay believable.
4. **NSA accretion timing** — pulling the $0.35–$0.50 stabilized accretion earlier, or a 2027 Core FFO bridge quantifying NSA + Canada + LA recapture, is the most likely source of an upside re-rate.
5. **Expense discipline against the pre-flagged tough comp** — management has already set a low bar; exceeding 3.0% anyway would read as loss of cost control.
