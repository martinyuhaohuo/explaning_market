## Prior Quarter (Q2 2026, reported 7/28/2026)

- **Adj. EPS:** $2.02 vs. $1.99 consensus (beat). Up from $1.92 a year earlier. Reported EPS $1.95.
- **Revenue:** $6.68B vs. $6.71B (slight miss).
- **C&D volume:** −1.8% (−0.4% ex-wildfire cleanup comparison). Residential losses improved 210 bps sequentially.
- **Core price:** +5.7%. C&D yield +3.6%.
- **Recycling + Renewable Energy:** Combined EBITDA up 32.5%, driven by volume, automation, and RNG output.
- **Commodities:** Single-stream recycled prices fell to ~$75/ton (from ~$84). RINs fell to $2.33 (from $2.53). Natural gas fell to $2.23/MMBtu (from $2.81).

**FY2026 guidance (updated):**
- Revenue: cut to $26.275–26.475B (from $26.43–26.63B), mainly on lower volume. Partly offset by energy surcharges.
- Adj. operating EBITDA: $8.15–8.25B (unchanged).
- Adj. EBITDA margin: raised to 31.0–31.2% (from 30.8–31.0%).
- Free cash flow: $3.75–3.85B.
- Volumes: C&D down about 1% for the year.
- No explicit adj. EPS range was in the release.

## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Adj. EPS vs. $2.19.** This is the headline. WM has beaten EPS on recent prints, but the beat margins are small.
2. **Collection & Disposal volume trend.** Is the residential improvement (+210 bps in Q2) continuing? A worse C&D volume figure would pressure the −1% full-year volume assumption.
3. **Adj. EBITDA margin vs. the 31.0–31.2% guide.** Margin expansion is the key support for holding the EBITDA guide despite the revenue cut.
4. **Full-year guidance: revenue, EBITDA, FCF.** Watch for a narrowing or reaffirmation of the $8.15–8.25B EBITDA range and the $3.75–3.85B FCF range. Any cut to EBITDA would be a negative surprise.
5. **Pricing and yield.** Core price was +5.7% in Q2. A deceleration would weigh on the margin story.
6. **Commodity and fuel exposure.** Recycled commodity and RIN prices were lower in Q2. Q3 commentary on these, and on surcharges offsetting fuel, will matter for the Recycling and RNG segments.

## Catalyst Checklist

1. **Adj. EPS vs. $2.19 consensus.** A beat of a few cents is typical. A miss would be a bigger negative than usual given the revenue trend.
2. **Full-year EBITDA and FCF guidance.** The market expects reaffirmation. A raise would be a catalyst. Any cut would dominate the reaction.
3. **C&D volume and residential trend.** Volume is the main weakness in the story. Improvement is needed to support the revenue narrative.
4. **Margin expansion.** The raised margin outlook (31.0–31.2%) is a key bull point. Confirmation or further raise supports the stock.
5. **Commodity and RIN exposure.** Lower recycled and RIN prices are a headwind. Management commentary on hedges or surcharges will matter.
