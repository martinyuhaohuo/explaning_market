## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Organic (core) sales growth.** Q2 was 5.2%. Is it holding, and does it reflect Aerospace strength (13%+) against a stable Process Flow?
2. **Adj. EPS vs. $1.81 and margin trajectory.** Adj. operating margin was a record 21.3% in Q2. Watch for expansion versus contraction, and how much of the beat comes from acquisitions.
3. **FY guide ($6.85–$7.05).** A raise is the main upside lever. Holding the range after a Q3 beat would read as conservative.
4. **Aerospace backlog and orders.** The $1.27B record backlog is the core of the growth thesis. Book-to-bill and any margin commentary matter here.
5. **M&A contribution and integration.** Acquisitions were nearly 20 points of growth in Q2. Watch for new deals and whether the "ahead of plan" language continues.
6. **Tariff recovery and cash flow.** Tariff recoveries are excluded from adjusted EPS, so check how much they add to GAAP and whether they recur.
