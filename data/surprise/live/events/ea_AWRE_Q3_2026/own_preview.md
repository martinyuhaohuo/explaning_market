3. **Biometrics pipeline commentary.** The Q2 coverage highlighted a strong biometric pipeline, which the stock dipped on anyway. Management needs to convert that into booked or recognized revenue.
4. **Gross and operating margin direction.** Check whether margins expand on the revenue base.
5. **Cash, buybacks, and dividend.** Aware's cash balance is a support for the stock. Any change in capital return policy would matter.
