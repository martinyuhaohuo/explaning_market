## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Revenue growth vs. the double-digit Q3 commentary.** A print below ~10% y/y would contradict management's own framing.
2. **Organic case growth and specialty placements.** These show whether share gains are holding.
3. **Gross margin.** Watch for expansion in the mid-25% range. Q4 FY2025 sold off on margin and expense concerns, so compression would matter more than a small EPS miss.
4. **Adj. EBITDA and FY guide reaffirmation or raise.** The $305–315M range is the key number. Expect the buy side to ask whether the raise holds.
5. **Operating expenses and Middle East trends.** Other operating expense rose sharply in Q4 FY2025, and the Middle East business is a drag on the mix.
6. **Free cash flow and debt paydown.** Continued ABL repayment supports the balance-sheet story.
