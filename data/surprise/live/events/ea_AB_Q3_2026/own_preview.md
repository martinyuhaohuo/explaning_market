## Recent Context (Q2 2026)

- **AUM:** Record $905.5B at June 30, 2026. Market appreciation was +$66.1B, and net flows were only +$0.8B.
- **Flows by channel:** Retail +$0.9B, Institutional +$0.6B, Private Wealth −$0.7B.
- **Outflow concentration:** Active equity outflows of about $11B and taxable outflows of about $5B, driven by APAC retail redemptions.
- **Sales:** Management called it the strongest sales quarter in five years. Alternatives/MAS had over $4B of net inflows.
- **Pipeline:** About $26B of institutional pipeline AUM entering H2.
- **Guidance:** Performance fees of $115–135M for FY2026. Full-year effective tax rate of 5–6%. No explicit full-year AUM or net-flow guidance was found.

## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Net flows, total and by channel.** This is the main debate. Q2 was roughly flat, so the question is whether the sales momentum (Alternatives/MAS, institutional pipeline) turns into positive organic growth, and whether APAC retail outflows continue.
2. **Adjusted EPS vs. $0.86.** Consensus is narrow, so a small miss or beat moves the headline. Expense discipline matters at this margin.
3. **Revenue on a consistent definition.** Confirm the basis before reacting to any headline number.
4. **Period-end AUM and market sensitivity.** AUM is at a record, so markets drive it. Separate market gains from organic flows.
5. **Performance fees.** Check the year-to-date run rate against the $115–135M FY guide. This is lumpy and can swing EPS.

**What each scenario needs operationally:**
- **Bull:** Positive total net flows, with retail/APAC outflows stabilizing and institutional funding ahead of the ~$26B pipeline.
- **Base:** Flows around zero, AUM rising with markets, and steady margins.
- **Bear:** Renewed outflows in active equity or taxable products, plus weaker fee income.

**Management commentary to listen for:**
- Bull: "positive organic growth" sustained into Q4, pipeline conversion, Alternatives momentum.
- Base: "stabilizing" APAC flows, reiterated performance fee and tax guidance.
- Bear: continued redemptions, "pressure" on fee rates, or a cut to performance-fee expectations.

## Catalyst Checklist

1. **Net flows vs. zero.** Q2 was +$0.8B. Any return to net outflows would likely dominate the reaction, even with an EPS beat.
2. **Adjusted EPS vs. $0.86.** Narrow consensus. Read it with performance fees and expenses.
3. **Q4 outlook / flow commentary.** The buy side will want the sales-momentum thesis carried into H2, with institutional pipeline conversion as evidence.
4. **APAC retail outflows.** Whether management says they have stabilized.
5. **Performance fee run rate versus the $115–135M FY guide.** A shortfall would be a negative surprise.
