## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Adj. EBITDA vs. the implied $397M–$410M.** This is the cleanest test of the raised guide. A print at the top of the range with a raise would be the bull case.
2. **SKSS pricing and volume.** This is the main swing factor. Q2 EBITDA more than doubled on re-refined oil pricing. Any sign that base-oil pricing is fading would reset expectations for Q4 and FY.
3. **ES margin (Q2: 27.9%).** Watch whether the 17-quarter expansion streak continues. Q3 FY2025 ES margin was 26.8%, so a flat or lower margin would break the streak.
4. **Field Services and Technical Services.** Field Services was up 3% in Q2 against a tough comp. Technical Services was up 18% on PFAS-related filtration work. Emergency-response timing drives Q3.
5. **Free cash flow and FY guide.** A raise to adj. FCF above the $520M–$580M range would support the stock. Holding the range with a weaker Q4 outlook would be a negative.
