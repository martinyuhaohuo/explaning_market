## What to Watch (ranked)

1. **Q3 revenue vs. $5.40B midpoint.** A print above the top of the range ($5.55B) would extend the Q2 beat pattern.
2. **Q3 adj. EPS vs. $2.98 midpoint.** The margin bridge matters most: 8.4% guided operating margin is the key test.
3. **Q4 revenue and EPS guide.** The buy side will judge this against the raised FY26 total. Because I don't have Q1 actuals, I can't back out the implied Q4 step-up, so compute it from the company's Q1 and Q2 figures.
4. **FY26 guidance** ($20.5B / $11.30). A further raise is the bull case; holding it is a neutral-to-negative signal after a raise.
5. **2027 framing.** Management has already said 2027 growth will exceed 2026. Watch for quantification or any change in tone.

- **Bear:** Margins slip below guidance, or the Q4 outlook disappoints the buy side's expectations.

**Historical context:** Day-after moves have been large.
