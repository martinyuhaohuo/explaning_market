## Key Metrics to Watch (Ranked)

1. **AFFO/share vs. consensus.** This is the metric the stock trades on. The Q3 consensus is unverified.
2. **FY26 AFFO/share guide.** Watch for a fourth raise, a reaffirmation, or a narrowing toward the top of $11.00–$11.17. A reaffirmation after two raises could read as a soft signal.
3. **Revenue guide vs. consensus.** Investors already pushed back on the last revenue outlook. This is a likely source of volatility.
4. **Core US leasing and churn.** Watch DISH/EchoStar churn timing, carrier spending on 5G and AI-related densification, and any amended carrier contracts.
5. **CoreSite data center.** Management credited record leasing and outperformance here for the Q2 raise. Watch the leasing pace and any commentary on power and capacity.
6. **International.** Watch India and Africa currency effects. FX helped the Q2 raise.
7. **2027 framework.** Any early color on the mid-to-high single-digit AFFO/share growth that Quartr says is expected to resume next year.

## Scenarios

| Scenario | AFFO/share vs. consensus | FY26 guide | Key driver | Stock reaction (directional) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Bull** | Beat | Fourth raise, toward top of range | Data center and US leasing strength, favorable FX | Up; a clean beat with a raise should lift the stock |
| **Base** | In line to small beat | Reaffirm or narrow | Stable leasing, DISH churn as guided | Muted, roughly flat; the guide and 2027 commentary decide direction |
| **Bear** | Miss, or beat on one-time items | Revenue guide cut or FY AFFO trimmed | DISH churn earlier than expected, weak international, FX reversal | Down; the revenue guide is the most likely trigger |

**What would signal each:**
- **Bull:** Management says leasing is ahead of plan, cites durable carrier demand, and gives constructive 2027 color.
- **Base:** "Reaffirm" language, with 2027 described as on track but not updated.
- **Bear:** Lower churn-adjusted revenue, delayed carrier spend, or a 2027 outlook that is pushed out.

**Historical context:** I did not find a reliable, sourced table of AMT's earnings-day moves. Recent prints show mixed reactions. Reports for Q4 2025 and Q1 2026 showed small moves in either direction despite EPS beats, so beats alone have not reliably moved the stock.

## Catalyst Checklist

1. **AFFO/share vs. consensus.** The beat or miss on this figure matters more than GAAP EPS, which is what Benzinga's table emphasizes. Get the Q3 AFFO/share consensus before the open.
2. **FY26 AFFO/share guidance.** The buy side will want a fourth raise or a clear reaffirmation. Anything below the $11.17 top end without a clear reason is a risk.
3. **Revenue guidance vs. the consensus midpoint.** This was the disappointment last quarter. A repeat would likely weigh on the stock even with an AFFO beat.
4. **DISH/EchoStar churn and carrier spending.** Confirm the timing and size of churn. Any sign of extra carrier pullback would hit the 2027 growth narrative.
5. **2027 framework and data center outlook.** The stock's direction after the print will depend on whether management supports the mid-to-high single-digit growth view and whether CoreSite leasing keeps pace.
