## 1. The Setup in One Paragraph

This is an unusual print: the *numbers* are low-stakes and the *narrative* is high-stakes. FY26 guidance was already raised twice, the quarter is well-telegraphed, and American Tower historically does not issue initial next-year guidance until the Q4 call in February — so there is no formal 2027 number to beat. Meanwhile the stock has re-rated ~18% off its Oct 9 52-week low ($160.06) to a fresh 52-week high ($202.50) almost entirely on SpaceX optionality: the $8B 800MHz spectrum purchase from Grain Management (Oct 9, AMT +7.7%) and the Bloomberg report that SpaceX is negotiating access to ~80,000 U.S. cell sites (Oct 23–24, AMT +~7% to $194.07). **The dominant variable Tuesday is what management says about a prospective fourth national terrestrial entrant, not AFFO/share.** The cautionary precedent is Q3 2025: a clean beat *and* a raise, and the stock fell 3.9%.

## 3. Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **New-entrant / SpaceX commentary (highest beta by far).** Any confirmation of active commercial discussions, master lease agreement structure, or timing. Note the constraint: on the Q2 call management declined to discuss DISH equipment still on towers citing ongoing litigation, and would only say they are "confident" a terrestrial entrant "would partner with us." A repeat of that non-answer is a disappointment relative to what the last three weeks priced.
2. **U.S. & Canada organic tenant billings growth ex-DISH.** Reported OTBG is cosmetically ~0.4–0.7% because one-time DISH churn is a ~1.7pp consolidated / ~4pp U.S.-segment drag. The real number is ~5% ex-DISH in Q2. Watch **new business contribution (~2.5% in Q2)** and the mix shift from amendments toward new co-locations — management's evidence that carriers have entered the 5G densification phase.
3. **CoreSite.** Q2 added more new business than all of 2021 combined; ~12% property revenue growth ex-straight-line. Two tells: (a) does FY data center growth get nudged from ~15% to ~16%, and (b) **pre-leasing on the 36MW under construction, stuck at ~8%** — management is deliberately not underpricing into strong demand, but the bulls need that number moving.
4. **FY26 AFFO guidance action (third raise?).** Midpoint ≥$11.15 is the bull marker; unchanged is neutral-to-negative given the Q3/Q4 math above.
5. **The 2027 bridge, qualitatively.** 2026 is explicitly a trough: DISH churn + a 150bp refinancing headwind (raised from 100bp) + a shrinking services base. Management has pointed to ~6–7% normalized FX-neutral growth and an "earlier-than-expected market repair in Brazil." Watch Latin America OTBG (−2.4% in Q2) for the first inflection, plus net leverage (4.9x vs. 3–5x target) and buyback pace ($200M deployed of the $2B authorization).

**Historical calibration (this is the key risk):** AMT sells off on good prints when positioning is crowded. Q3'25 (Oct 28, 2025): revenue beat 2.3%, AFFO +10.3% YoY, guidance raised across the board → **stock −3.87%** to $182.39, reportedly because investors wanted more from the data center investment. Q2'26 (Jul 28, 2026): AFFO in line, revenue beat, second raise → **+2.85% close, 7.1% max intraday swing.** OptionSlam's Earnings Volatility Rating is **1.8/10** — AMT is structurally a *low* earnings-move name, which is precisely why the current setup is mispriced relative to history.
