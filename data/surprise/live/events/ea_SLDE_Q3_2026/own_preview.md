## Prior Quarter Context (Q2 2026, reported Jul 29, 2026)

- Net income: $134.9M (+92.4% YoY). Diluted EPS: $1.06.
- Gross premiums written: $508.0M (+16.7%).
- Net premiums earned: $360.6M (+47.9%).
- Loss ratio: 30.2% (vs. 37.4%). Combined ratio: about 57.5%.
- Cash: $1.24B.
- **Guidance reaffirmed:** GPW $1.85–1.95B, net income $455–470M. Management called this conservative, citing exposure management and possible reinsurance true-up payments.
- Expansion: approval to enter Rhode Island and New Jersey. California growth is expected to accelerate toward year-end.
- Capital return: about 3M shares repurchased, and the first quarterly dividend of $0.07 was approved.

## Key Metrics to Watch (Ranked)

1. **Loss ratio and reserve development.** Q2's 30.2% was very low. Q1 was also helped by favorable reserve items that may not recur. A reversal toward the mid-30s would hurt the story.
2. **Net premiums earned and GPW growth.** Growth is the main driver of the revenue line. Watch for policy-count growth in new states and whether Citizens depopulation volume tapers.
3. **Reinsurance cost.** The tower was expanded and the first-event return period is about 180 years. Any commentary on true-up payments or reinsurance cost pressure matters for margin.
4. **Full-year guidance.** The buy side will want a raise or a clear reason the reaffirmation is conservative. Simply holding guidance after a strong Q2 could be read as a soft signal.
5. **Capital return and dividend.** Buyback pace and dividend policy matter for the post-print narrative.

## Catalyst Checklist

1. **Loss ratio vs. ~mid-30s run rate.** A repeat of the sub-35% level supports the bull case. Any reserve-driven help should be identified separately.
2. **Guidance (GPW $1.85–1.95B; net income $455–470M).** The buy side expects either a raise or a credible explanation for holding.
3. **Reinsurance true-up and tower cost.** This is the main margin risk in the narrative.
4. **New-state and California growth timing.** Rhode Island and New Jersey entry is a multi-year driver. California acceleration is the near-term one.
5. **Capital return.** Buyback pace and the $0.07 dividend cadence.
