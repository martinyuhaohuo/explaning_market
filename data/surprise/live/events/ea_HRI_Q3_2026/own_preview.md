## Prior Quarter Context (Q2 2026, reported 2026-07-28)

- Total revenue was $1,204M, up 20% year over year. Equipment rental revenue was $1,072M, up 23%.
- Adjusted EBITDA was $487M, up 19%, with a 40% margin.
- GAAP EPS was $0.57. Adjusted EPS was $1.43.
- First-half free cash flow was $202M, versus $103M a year earlier.
- Management raised full-year 2026 guidance and increased net fleet capex to meet demand. Drivers cited were the H&E acquisition, mega projects, and specialty rentals. Fuel inflation was a headwind. Synergies were tracking to plan.
- One secondary source put the full-year adjusted EBITDA guidance midpoint at ~$2.09B, versus ~$2.06B consensus. This is unverified.

## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Full-year guidance raise or reaffirmation.** This matters most, because a Q2 raise sets a high bar. Check whether the revenue range is maintained despite a headline that some sources read as a full-year sales miss versus expectations.
2. **Equipment rental revenue growth and mix.** Watch how much of the 23% Q2 growth came from the H&E acquisition versus organic growth, and whether organic rental growth is slowing.
3. **Adjusted EBITDA margin (~40% in Q2).** Compression would be read as fuel and cost pressure. Expansion would support the synergy story.
4. **Time utilization and rental rate (pricing).** These drive the organic growth quality. Management commentary on mega projects is a useful signal here.
5. **Net fleet capex and free cash flow.** A higher capex plan with strong FCF would confirm demand. A higher capex plan with weaker FCF would raise questions.

## Catalyst Checklist

1. **Adjusted EPS vs ~$3.00 consensus.** This is the headline. Because the Q2 beat was large, a merely in-line print may read as a disappointment.
2. **Full-year 2026 guidance (revenue, adjusted EBITDA, capex).** The buy side will want a raise or reaffirmation with confirmed organic growth. The unverified ~$2.09B EBITDA midpoint is a reference point to check.
3. **Organic versus acquired growth from H&E.** Investors will want to see how much of the top line is durable.
4. **Fuel and margin trajectory.** Fuel was flagged as a Q2 headwind. Confirm whether margins held near 40%.
5. **Capital allocation and synergy progress.** Net fleet capex is rising. Check whether FCF and synergy targets stay on track.
