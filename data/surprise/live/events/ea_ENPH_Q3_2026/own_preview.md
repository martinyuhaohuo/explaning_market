## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Revenue vs. the ~$305M consensus and the $290–320M range.** Q3 includes about $75M of safe harbor revenue, so the quality of non-safe-harbor sales matters.
2. **Non-GAAP gross margin.** Watch whether it holds at 44–47% after the Q2 tariff-refund boost. Stripping out refunds, the underlying trend is the key read.
3. **Q4 revenue guide.** Management said demand softness will persist and that it will under-ship by about $15M in Q3 to clear microinverter inventory. The Q4 setup depends on that.
4. **Global sell-through.** Management guided about +10% QoQ. Confirm it on the call.
5. **Battery volumes (130–150 MWh) and opex discipline ($76–80M).**
