## 2. Prior Quarter and Guidance Baseline

- **Q2 2026 (reported July 28):** Net sales +7.5% to $6.79B. Adjusted EPS $3.70 (+9.5%) vs. $3.52 consensus. Adjusted EBITDA margin 21.5% (+60 bps). GAAP diluted EPS $3.43 (+14.3%).
- **FY2026 guidance raised:** Adjusted EPS $11.80–$12.20 (includes $0.81 Valspar amortization and $0.07 restructuring). GAAP diluted EPS $10.92–$11.32. FY2025 adjusted EPS was $11.43.
- **Sales outlook:** Full-year sales growth in the mid-to-high single digits. **Q3 sales guided to mid-to-high single-digit growth**, which implies roughly $6.68B–$6.93B on the $6.36B base. Consensus sits inside that range.
- **Pricing:** An 8% price increase in Paint Stores Group took effect September 1 to offset raw material, energy, and logistics inflation.
- **Caution from management:** Demand softness is expected to persist in H2.

## 3. Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Organic volume vs. price/mix.** The raise rests more on price than volume. Q3 is the first full quarter that reflects the 8% price increase, so a volume decline would be the main concern.
2. **Paint Stores Group same-store sales** and architectural repaint trends. Housing and commercial repaint have not clearly recovered.
3. **Consumer Brands and Performance Coatings** segment sales, particularly industrial demand.
4. **Adjusted gross and EBITDA margins.** Watch whether price is offsetting raw material inflation.
5. **FY2026 guidance:** Whether the $11.80–$12.20 range is reaffirmed, narrowed, or raised, and whether Q4 sales commentary holds.
6. **Free cash flow and buybacks**, given the Valspar amortization drag on GAAP EPS.

## 4. Scenario Analysis

| Scenario | Revenue | Adj. EPS | Key Driver | Stock Reaction |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Bull | ~$6.90B (+8–9%) | ~$3.85+ | Price increase sticks with flat-to-positive volume; margin expansion | Up; FY guide raised toward or above top of range |
| Base | ~$6.79B (in line) | ~$3.70 | Beat-and-hold pattern; price offsets volume softness; guide reaffirmed | Muted, likely within a few percent |
| Bear | ~$6.65B (+4–5%, below guide) | ~$3.50 | Volume declines offset price; margins pressured; FY guide trimmed to low end | Down; housing/repaint weakness becomes the narrative |

**What would signal each scenario:**
- **Bull:** Management cites improving DIY/pro traffic, backlog or order momentum, and price realization above the 8% headline.
- **Base:** Commentary that H2 demand is "stable but soft," with guidance held.
- **Bear:** Volume down year over year, cautious Q4 language, or a guide cut citing customer destocking or weak housing.

**Historical context:** SHW has beaten EPS consensus in the last two prints (Q2 2026 and Q3 2025), so the bar is set with a pattern of beats. I could not find a reliable recent post-earnings move. ## 5. Catalyst Checklist

1. **Revenue vs. ~$6.79B consensus**, given the 8% price increase. A beat driven by price alone may not be rewarded.
2. **Volume trend** in Paint Stores Group: the buy-side will look for any sign that the H2 softness is easing.
3. **FY2026 adjusted EPS guidance** ($11.80–$12.20): the buy-side will want at least a reaffirmation, and a raise would be a positive surprise.
4. **Q4 sales and margin outlook:** How much of the price increase carries into Q4, and whether margins hold as raw material costs move.
5. **Narrative:** Any update on housing recovery timing, Valspar integration, or capital allocation.
