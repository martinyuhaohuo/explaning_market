## 2. Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **PSG same-store sales and the price/volume split.** Q2 ran +4.2% SSS on mid-single-digit price and low-single-digit volume. PSG is ~57% of sales and ~70%+ of segment profit; this line alone usually sets the tape.
2. **Realization of the September 1 8% PSG price increase.** Only *one of three* Q3 months captures it, and management guided to the historical **60–70% realization** range. Q3 is the read on acceptance; Q4 is where the P&L impact lands. Any sign of volume pushback from "fatigued" contractors is the core bear trigger.
3. **Gross margin vs. high-single-digit H2 raw material inflation.** GM compressed 20bps in Q2 to 49.2%. With inflation accelerating before pricing fully lands, Q3 is the quarter of maximum cost/price mismatch.
4. **FY26 guidance action and the first 2027 frame.** Narrow-to-midpoint is base case; the buy-side is listening for whether 2027 can grow EPS without a housing recovery (store openings guided to the high end of 80–100 net adds starting 2027).
5. **End-market detail:** residential repaint (mid-single in Q2, management's "largest share gains ahead"), new residential (negative single-family starts/completions in 5 of 6 months), commercial (high-single), DIY (muted), protective & marine (8th straight quarter of ≥HSD growth on data centers/semis), and Europe destocking in PCG.
6. **CBG/Suvinil:** adjusted margin held 24.5% in Q2 vs. 22.4% prior year; integration runs through early 2027 and is guided as an immaterial EPS tailwind for 2026.

## 3. Scenario Analysis

| Scenario | Revenue | Adj. EPS | Key driver | Stock reaction |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Bull (25%)** | $6.88B (+8.2%) | $3.85 | PSG SSS +5%+, Sept price increase sticking with no volume loss, GM flat-to-up, FY26 guided to **top half ($12.00–$12.20)** | **+5% to +8%** |
| **Base (50%)** | $6.80B (+6.9%) | $3.70–3.75 | PSG SSS +3–4%, GM down ~20–40bps, FY26 **narrowed to ~$11.90–$12.10**, H2 decel reiterated | **–2% to +3%** |
| **Bear (25%)** | $6.70B (+5.4%) | $3.50–3.58 | PSG volume flat/negative as the 8% increase meets a weakening repaint market, GM down 50bps+, FY26 **cut to ~$11.60–$11.85** | **–7% to –10%** |

**What has to happen operationally:** The bull case requires PSG volume to stay positive *through* the September price action — i.e., share capture continuing to override a dead end-market. The bear case needs volume to roll over in September, which would re-rate the whole "we manufacture our own growth" thesis, not just the quarter.

**Management commentary that signals each:** Bull — raw material inflation described as peaking, realization "at or above the high end" of 60–70%, confidence in 80–100 net new stores for 2027. Bear — hedging on realization, explicit deferral of 2027 framing, or any language about "protecting volume" (code for discounting).

**Historical calibration:** SHW has beaten EPS consensus in **6 of the last 8 quarters**. Q2 FY26 (Jul 28): $3.70 vs. $3.52, stock **+5.9% to +8.1%**. Q3 FY25 (Oct 28, 2025): $3.59 vs. ~$3.45 with a revenue beat, stock **+5.8% pre-market, +4.2% on the close**. The pattern is a high hit rate and outsized *upside* reactions — which is itself a reason the bar is not as low as the recent price-target cuts suggest.

## 4. Catalyst Checklist

1. **PSG same-store sales vs. ~+3.5% whisper** — the de facto score for the quarter.
2. **FY26 adj. EPS guide vs. $11.80–$12.20** — a cut would be the first of 2026 and would validate the UBS/Barclays/Melius housing-downturn call.
3. **Gross margin vs. 49.2% prior-year** — tests whether pricing power survives accelerating inflation.
4. **Implied Q4 EPS (~$2.26) and any 2027 pre-framing** — where the 2027 consensus reset happens.
5. **Capital allocation** — 24.0M shares remain authorized; H1 returned $2.23B. Management said it accelerated buybacks into volatility; an aggressive Q3 repurchase at $290–$310 would be a confidence signal.
