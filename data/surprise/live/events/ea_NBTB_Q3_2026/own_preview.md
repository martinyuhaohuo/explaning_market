## Q2 Context and Guidance (from prior call and release coverage)

- **Net income / EPS:** $53.0M, $1.02 diluted GAAP EPS (adjusted $1.01)
- **NIM:** 3.73% FTE, up 1 bp sequentially and up 14 bp year over year
- **Net interest income:** $137.0M, up 10.3% YoY, helped by the Evans acquisition
- **Loans:** Described as "strong" across commercial and consumer, led by Buffalo. No growth rate was available.
- **Deposits:** About 60% in no- and low-cost checking and savings. Management is de-emphasizing higher-cost time deposits.
- **Fee income:** About 27% of revenue. Growth led by retirement plan administration, wealth management, and insurance.
- **Expenses:** Operating expenses fell sequentially as occupancy costs dropped. Efficiency ratio is below 60%.
- **Outlook:** Operating expense growth guided to 2.5%–3% for the year. Management expects commercial loan growth to continue at a slower pace than Q2. NIM could improve modestly if rates hold, but deposit competition is rising.
- **Dividend:** Raised 8.1% to $0.40 per quarter.

## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **NIM and net interest income.** Is the 3.73% NIM holding or expanding? Beta on deposits and the time-deposit runoff are the swing factors.
2. **EPS vs. $1.09 and revenue vs. ~$194M.** Two straight quarters of small misses make the bar matter.
3. **Loan growth pace.** Management has already signaled deceleration from Q2. Watch for commercial origination detail.
4. **Provision and credit quality.** Not covered in the sources I found. Check NPLs and net charge-offs.
5. **Fee income and expense discipline.** Confirm the 2.5%–3% expense growth guide still holds and the efficiency ratio stays at or below 60%.
6. **Capital and buybacks.** Check CET1 and any changes to the dividend or repurchase plan.

## Catalyst Checklist

1. **NIM vs. the 3.73% run-rate:** Expansion would support the bull case, since the margin is the main earnings driver.
2. **EPS vs. $1.09:** A second consecutive miss would likely weigh on the stock more than the magnitude of the miss suggests.
3. **Q4 and 2027 commentary on loan growth and expenses:** The buy-side will focus on whether growth stays strong while expense guidance holds.
4. **Deposit mix and cost trend:** Evidence that the low-cost deposit base is holding would support the margin view.
5. **Credit quality:** A provision increase would be a negative surprise given the strong loan growth.
