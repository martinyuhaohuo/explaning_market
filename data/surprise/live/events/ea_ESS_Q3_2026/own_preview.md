## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Core FFO vs. $3.99 guide midpoint.** Q2 beat the midpoint by $0.10. A beat near or above $4.05 is the main upside signal.
2. **FY2026 Core FFO guidance.** A raise above $16.25 would signal momentum. Holding at $16.03–$16.25 would be read as a flat quarter.
3. **Same-property revenue and NOI growth.** Q2 was +2.7% and +2.6%. Watch whether the full-year guide holds in the 2.5%–3.1% range.
4. **Northern vs. Southern California.** Northern CA blended rent growth was +6.5% in Q2. Southern CA was +1.4%, with Los Angeles County lagging Orange County. Any LA weakness is the main bear point.
5. **Occupancy and blended lease rate trends into Q4.** Financial occupancy was 96.3% in Q2.

**Bull:** Management sounds confident on Northern California rents and Orange County demand, and guidance rises above the current top end.
**Base:** Results land near the guide midpoint, with same-property growth within the FY range and no new guidance risk.
**Bear:** Management cites LA County softness, or the Q2 one-time property-tax benefit ($0.03) does not repeat. Guidance is cut or narrowed to the low end.

## Catalyst Checklist

1. **Core FFO vs. $3.99 midpoint and ~$4.00 consensus.** Above $4.05 is a clear beat. Below $3.95 is a miss.
2. **FY26 Core FFO guidance change.** The buy-side will watch whether the $16.03–$16.25 range moves up, holds, or narrows down.
3. **Southern California rent trends and LA County outlook.** This is the most likely source of a negative narrative.
4. **Q2 one-time items.** Management should clarify whether the $0.03 property-tax refund recurs or is excluded from run-rate. Also check the Q2 legal-settlement expense cited in one source.
5. **Capital allocation and development pipeline commentary.** Any changes to buybacks, dispositions, or guidance on development spend would be a narrative shift.
