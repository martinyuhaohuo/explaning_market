2. **FY2026 FFO guidance vs. $3.58 midpoint.** Whether they hold, raise, or narrow. The guide includes a -$0.04 dilution from dispositions without reinvestment of excess cash.
3. **Year-end occupancy vs. 86.5%–88.5% guide.** This is the core operating health signal for an office REIT.
4. **Same-property cash NOI vs. -1% to +1% guide.** A break below -1% would be a negative surprise.
