## Recent Track Record

- **Q2 2026 (reported Jul 21):** Adjusted EPS $0.79 vs. $0.67 consensus. Revenue $795.3M vs. $747.6M. GAAP EPS was $0.83, helped by a $74M mark-to-market derivative gain.
- **Q1 2026:** Adjusted EPS $1.52 vs. $1.25 consensus.
- Two straight beats. Q2 realized price including hedges was $3.53/mcfe.

## Operating Context (from Q2 release)

- Production averaged **2.30 Bcfe/d** in Q2, about 67% gas.
- Management guides production to rise ratably to **~2.5 Bcfe/d by year-end**, as gas processing and related infrastructure are commissioned.
- Q2 turn-in-line: 21 wells, ~300k lateral feet. Plan calls for 50 liquids-rich and 18 dry-gas wells in 2026.
- Q2 capital spending was $222M, about 33% of the annual budget. Buybacks were $78M and dividends $24M.
- Q2 pre-hedge NGL realization was $29.10/bbl.

## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Production vs. guidance.** Is Q3 on the ramp toward 2.5 Bcfe/d? Any slip in processing commissioning matters most.
2. **Realized price and hedge impact.** Gas price realizations and hedge losses or gains drive the EPS gap versus consensus. Derivative mark-to-market can distort GAAP EPS, so focus on adjusted EPS.
3. **Unit costs and margins.** LOE, gathering and transport, and G&A per Mcfe. Expansion or compression vs. Q2.
4. **Free cash flow and capital return.** Buyback pace after the $78M in Q2, and whether the dividend or buyback framework changes.
5. **Full-year guidance.** Updated 2026 production, capex, and any 2027 commentary.
