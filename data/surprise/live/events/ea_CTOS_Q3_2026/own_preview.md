## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Revenue vs. ~$517M and vs. the raised FY guide.** Q2 beat by about 10%, so a Q3 beat is likely. The question is whether it holds the FY guide.
2. **Adj. EBITDA margin and trajectory.** The FY guide implies continued margin expansion. The H2 implied margin is what matters.
3. **FY26 guidance.** The buy-side will want another raise or a clear reaffirmation. A narrowed range at the midpoint would likely be read as a hold.
4. **Net leverage and FCF.** Sub-4.0x leverage was a recent milestone. Further deleveraging supports the balance-sheet story.
5. **Rental fleet investment and utilization.** Fleet capex was revised; watch whether growth is funded without FCF strain.
