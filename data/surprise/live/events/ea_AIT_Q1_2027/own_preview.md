## Company Guidance and Recent Context

- **Q1 guidance:** Total sales growth of 6.5–8.5%, organic growth of 6–8%, EBITDA margin of 12.3–12.4%.
- **Q4 FY2026 (reported Aug 13, 2026):** Net sales about $1.4B, up 10.4% (organic +9.7%). EPS $3.17. Record fourth quarter.
- **Management commentary:** Organic sales were tracking up about 7% quarter to date. Management flagged inflation and macro uncertainty, and tougher comparisons later in the year.
- **FY2027 guidance:** Total sales growth of 4.0–6.5%, EBITDA margin of 12.5–12.8%, EPS of $11.65–12.15. Intermediate targets were raised to $7B in sales and a 14% EBITDA margin.
