## 1. Setup in One Paragraph

AIT enters this print with the best fundamental momentum in four years and a stock that has gone sideways-to-down for 74 days. Q4 FY26 (reported Aug 13) delivered 9.7% organic growth — the strongest in three-plus years — with EPS of $3.17 vs. $2.92 consensus (+8.6% beat) and a 13.1% EBITDA margin. Management then guided FY27 EPS to $11.65–$12.15 and told investors Q1 organic was already tracking **~7% through mid-August**, framing a **6–8% organic** Q1. Despite that, shares drifted ~9% lower post-print and sit ~13% below the 52-week high. The asymmetry here is less about the quarter — guidance is pre-telegraphed and the comp math is known — and more about whether management can defend the second-half trajectory against hardening comps while the Engineered Solutions/automation narrative (semis, data centers) carries the multiple.

## 3. Key Metrics to Watch — Ranked

**1. Organic growth vs. the 6–8% pre-announced range (highest importance).**
Management effectively guided this quarter on the Q4 call. Printing **below 6% breaks the credibility of the pre-announcement** and would be read as a September air pocket. Above 8% confirms the "durable recovery" framing. Watch the monthly cadence in the prepared remarks — Q4 ended with June organic above 10%, so any disclosed September deceleration matters more than the quarter average.

**2. Engineered Solutions orders, backlog and book-to-bill.**
Orders rose double digits y/y for three consecutive quarters; July ES orders were up mid-20s with backlog up **both y/y and sequentially — seasonally unusual**. A fourth consecutive quarter of double-digit order growth plus sequential backlog build is the single most bullish thing management can say. Note management's own caveat that project-type orders convert on extended timelines, so backlog growth is not a near-term revenue guarantee.

**3. EBITDA margin vs. the 12.3–12.4% Q1 frame, and the SD&A line.**
This is where the stock has been punished before. In Q2 FY26 (Jan 2026), SD&A rose 11.1% against 2.2% organic growth and shares fell despite an EPS beat. SD&A improved 94bps to 18.6% of sales in Q4 FY26. **The test is whether cost leverage holds while AIT funds technology, shared services and AI investments.** Management targets mid-to-high-teens incremental EBITDA margins at mid-single-digit organic growth.

**4. Price/cost and LIFO.**
Q4 FY26 pricing contributed **250bps**, above the 200bps management had guided. FY27 guidance assumes only **150–200bps**. FY27 LIFO expense is guided to **$24–28M** vs. $21.5M in FY26 — against a Q1 FY26 base of just $2.6M, Q1 FY27 LIFO of ~$6–7M is a ~30–35bp gross margin headwind. If pricing is still running at 250bps, gross margin should absorb it; if pricing has normalized toward 150bps while LIFO steps up, gross margin compresses and the margin story gets harder.

**5. Technology vertical / automation disclosure.**
Automation grew **>20% organically** in Q4 FY26. The technology vertical is now **>15% of Engineered Solutions**, spanning semiconductors and emerging data center work. This is the de-rating/re-rating lever — it is why AIT trades at ~27x forward rather than a traditional distributor multiple. Any quantification of data center revenue or design wins is a positive catalyst; any hint of semi capex digestion is the reverse.

**6. FY27 guidance revision.**
Guidance excludes future M&A and buybacks. AIT has raised intra-year in each of the last two years (FY26: $10.10–10.85 → $10.45–10.75 → $10.64–10.75 → actual $10.95). With Q1 organic pre-announced at 6–8% against a +4.0–6.5% full-year sales guide, **the guide is already conservative** and a raise is the base case, not the bull case.

**7. Capital deployment.**
Net debt is only ~$135M against ~$2B of stated acquisition capacity and 18+ deals since 2018. AIT repurchased $317M (1.2M shares) in FY26, and share count has fallen from 37.41M diluted in Q4 to ~36.7M outstanding. **A deal announced alongside the print is a live possibility** — Thompson Industrial Supply was announced with the Q2 FY26 release.

| Scenario | Revenue | Organic | EBITDA margin | EPS | Key driver | Stock reaction |

| **Bear** (25%) | $1.27–1.28B (+6.0%) | +5.5% | 12.0–12.1% | **$2.72–2.80** | September deceleration; pricing normalizes to ~150bps into a rising LIFO charge; SD&A growth outpaces organic growth; guide reaffirmed with cautious 2H language | **−6% to −11%** |

### What each scenario requires operationally

**Bull.** Service Center holds ≥5% organic (management flagged it may "moderate a little") *and* Engineered Solutions compounds on the +12.9% Q4 against the −0.4% prior-year comp. Requires pricing still near 250bps and incremental EBITDA margins in the high teens. Management language to listen for: backlog up sequentially again, quantified data center pipeline, and an explicit statement that the full-year sales guide is now conservative.

**Base.** Roughly the mechanical outcome of management's own mid-August commentary extended through September. ~$6M LIFO and ~$3M interest expense are absorbed; ~37.0M diluted shares. Guidance moves to the upper half of the range rather than being lifted outright. Signal: "positive momentum sustained," reiteration of the 6–8% first-half framing with explicit caution on second-half comps.

**Bear.** The precedent is concrete, not hypothetical: **January 2026**, when AIT beat on EPS and revenue and still fell ~3% (down ~9% pre-market at the lows) because SD&A grew 11.1% against 2.2% organic growth. The repeat mechanism is a cost-investment step-up plus LIFO landing in a quarter where organic decelerates toward the low end. Signal: hedged language on trade policy, "inflationary headwinds," and deferral of the margin story to the back half.

## 5. Catalyst Checklist

1. **Organic growth ≥6% (vs. pre-announced 6–8%)** — the floor. Below 6% invalidates the pre-announcement and likely triggers the bear case regardless of EPS.
2. **EPS vs. ~$2.98 buy-side bar (not the $2.86 published consensus)** — an in-line-to-$2.90 print reads as a miss to positioned investors given the +4.3% average surprise.
3. **EBITDA margin ≥12.4% with SD&A growth below organic sales growth** — the January 2026 trauma. This single relationship has driven more of AIT's recent earnings-day moves than revenue.
4. **FY27 EPS guidance action** — reaffirmation only, against a pre-announced 6–8% Q1 and a +4.0–6.5% full-year sales guide, would be read as implicit caution on the second half. The bar is a raise.
5. **Engineered Solutions order growth + sequential backlog** — determines whether the 2H deceleration narrative gets deferred or confirmed.
6. **Optional upside: M&A announcement or buyback disclosure** — ~$2B of capacity, an "active and increasingly productive" pipeline, and neither in guidance. Precedent exists for announcing deals with the print.
