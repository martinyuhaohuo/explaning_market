## Recent Track Record

- **Q2 FY26 (Jul 28):** Adj. EPS $1.76 vs. $1.65 est.; revenue $22.8B vs. $21.68B est. GAAP EPS was $0.71 after $891M ($1.05/share) in transformation charges.
- **Amazon glide-down:** Completed. It removed ~2M low-margin pieces/day and ~$4.5B of associated expense. Management said Amazon comparisons will still weigh on 1H27.
- **Stock reaction:** Despite the beat and raise, the stock closed **-6.6%** on Jul 28. A beat-and-raise is not enough on its own.

## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Revenue per piece and U.S. Domestic volume.** Q2 Domestic revenue rose 6.0% on a 9.3% rise in revenue per piece. Watch whether volume is still falling as the Amazon glide-down laps.
2. **Adj. operating margin and cost per piece.** This is the core test of the network reset and the ~$4.5B expense removal. Margin expansion needs to show up in the reported number, not just in the bridge.
3. **Transformation charges and Driver Choice savings.** Check whether the charges are fully disclosed and whether savings are tracking to plan.
4. **International export and Europe.** Some outside coverage flagged weak European and U.S. export demand. Watch for any cut to the international outlook.
5. **FY26 EPS guide ($7.22) and FY26 revenue guide (~$91.2B).** A raise above $7.22 is the bar. A hold after a beat reads as a miss to some holders. Check whether Q4 implied guidance is credible.
6. **Free cash flow and capex.** Watch whether FCF supports the dividend and buybacks.

## Catalyst Checklist

1. **Adj. EPS ($1.63 consensus) and operating margin:** The quality of any beat matters more than the headline. A beat driven by one-time items will be discounted.
2. **FY26 EPS guide vs. $7.22:** The buy-side will want a raise. A reaffirm after a Q3 beat often reads as a miss.
3. **Domestic volume and revenue per piece:** This is the clearest read on whether the Amazon glide-down is behind the business.
4. **2027 commentary on Amazon and cost savings:** The buy-side is looking for a clear 2027 framework, especially for 1H27 comparisons.
5. **Transformation charges and Driver Choice:** Any new restructuring or larger charges would be a negative narrative shift.
