## 2. Last Quarter Baseline (Q2 FY2026, reported Jul 29)

- **Revenue:** $159.5M, +19.7% y/y. Beat revenue consensus only modestly.
- **EPS:** $1.62 vs. ~$1.33 consensus. Large beat.
- **Guidance:** Management said Q3 should **match or beat Q2**. Q2 revenue was $159.5M, so this is a floor near $160M, which sits **about $8M to $11M below the revenue consensus**. This is the key tension.
- **FCF guidance raised** to $56M to $65M (from $50M to $56M). Tax rate 24% to 26%. CapEx $20M to $24M.
- **WTS backlog:** $423M at Jun 30 (incl. confirmed orders), down from $430M in Q1. Ex-confirmed orders, $305M. Management said backlog will normalize after a large unplanned NDA project winds down.
- **Pipeline:** More than $125M of bids pending.
- **Precast:** Order book $61M (up from $55M). Sales fell 4.8% y/y on Texas weather and project delays.

## 3. Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Revenue vs. ~$168M to $171M consensus, and the Q4 outlook.** Q2's "match or beat" guide implies a miss on the street number unless the company beats its own floor by a wide margin.
2. **WTS backlog and new awards.** Watch whether the $125M+ pending bids convert. A drop in backlog ex-confirmed orders would hurt the H2 and FY2027 narrative.
3. **Gross and operating margin trend.** Whether the Q2 EPS beat was driven by margin or one-time items. Q2 EPS of $1.62 on $159.5M revenue implies strong margins, so any compression is a negative.
4. **FCF and the raised $56M to $65M guide.** Reaffirmation or another raise supports the cash story.
5. **Precast recovery.** Order book growth vs. the y/y sales decline.

**Management commentary that signals each case:**
- **Bull:** New awards announced, "backlog normalization" framed as conversion, FY FCF guide raised again.
- **Base:** "Consistent with our prior outlook," with no new award announcements.
- **Bear:** Language about "timing" of awards, project deferrals, or precast weather and delays persisting.

**Historical context:** EPS beats have been frequent (Q1 and Q2 2026 both beat by wide margins). Stock reactions have been inconsistent. Day 0 moves have averaged ~1%, but Day +1 moves have been larger and more volatile. The reaction is not reliably tied to the beat.

## 5. Catalyst Checklist

1. **Revenue vs. ~$168M consensus.** The Q2 guide floor (~$160M) means a revenue miss is plausible even on a good quarter. Why it matters: the street may be too high.
2. **Q4 / FY2026 guidance and the FCF range.** Buy-side expects a reaffirmed or raised FCF target ($56M to $65M) and a clear H2 revenue path.
3. **WTS backlog and the $125M+ pipeline.** Confirmed awards in Q3 or a clear timeline would support the bull case.
4. **Margin trajectory.** Any step-down from Q2 levels would weigh on the EPS story.
5. **Narrative shift.** Watch for any change to the "backlog normalization" language, and any M&A, capacity, or capital-allocation news.
