## Key Metrics to Watch — Ranked

**1. Initial FY2027 Core FFO guidance (highest weight by far).**
DEA has precedent for initiating next-year guidance at the Q3 print. Management's stated long-term algorithm is 2–3% FFO growth; off a $3.10 FY26 midpoint that implies **$3.16–$3.19**. Assume the Street is anchored ~$3.15–$3.20. Anything with a $3.0 handle at the midpoint is a sell-the-print.

**2. FY26 guidance narrowing.** Two consecutive $0.01 midpoint raises (Oct'25 $3.05–$3.12 → Q1 $3.06–$3.12 → Q2 $3.07–$3.13). A narrow to the upper half ($3.10–$3.13) is the tell that Q4 is tracking fine; a cut to the low end on FAA timing would be the surprise.

**3. The FAA lease (expires October 2026).** Management explicitly told investors on the Q2 call *not* to model renewal revenue, while noting FAA "historically had a bit of a move out on time challenge" — i.e. holdover income is possible upside, not base case. Watch for disclosure on holdover status, backfill, or re-lease economics. This is the largest identifiable 2027 revenue headwind.

**4. Cost of capital vs. the accretion threshold.** Management named **$24.50–$25.50** as the share price where acquisitions become meaningfully accretive. The stock is **~$22**, below that line, below its 50-day ($23.52–$23.73) and 200-day ($23.56–$23.84) MAs. DEA issued 796,943 ATM shares at a $23.86 weighted average in Q2 — above today's price. If external growth requires equity below $23, it's dilutive. **The JV answer is the key question on the call:** management has floated sovereign wealth fund and broader JV partnerships (they previously deployed ~$600–700M of VA assets through a JV). A named JV would substantially de-risk the $1.5B pipeline.

**5. Leverage and the investment-grade path.** Adjusted net debt/EBITDA was 7.1x at Q2, net debt/enterprise value 58.4%, total debt $1.7B, WA maturity 4.0 years at 4.6%. Management targets an IG rating by 2027. Watch for progress toward ~6.5x; development completions generate government lump-sum reimbursements that management calls a "natural source of deleveraging."

**6. Dividend coverage.** $0.45/quarter (reaffirmed Oct 22, 2026) against Q2 CAD of $25.8M — roughly 83% of CAD, versus ~58% of Core FFO. Covered, but not comfortably. An 8%+ yield says the market is pricing some doubt. Any softening of language here is a material negative.

**7. Occupancy, WALT, renewal spreads.** 98% occupancy, 9.2-yr WALT, 106 properties / 10.7M leased SF at Q2. WALT has been compressing (9.4 → 9.2 in one quarter). Guidance has been for **mid-to-high-teens net effective rent growth on renewals** against ~$35/sf of TI and building-specific capital — confirm that spread is holding, and watch the 2027 expiration schedule and procurement commentary.

**8. Mix shift.** Target of 50% GSA / 50% alternative (state, local, government-adjacent) new investment to reach a 30% portfolio allocation, which management says adds 60–90bps to same-store growth via 2–3% escalators. The January 2026 Commonwealth of Virginia campus (297,713 SF) was the template. Progress here is the structural bull case.

## Catalyst Checklist

1. **FY2027 Core FFO guidance vs. ~$3.15–$3.20 assumed Street.** The entire print hinges here. A $3.16+ midpoint validates the 2–3% algorithm and the 8% yield gets re-examined as cheap. A sub-$3.10 midpoint confirms that FAA loss + interest expense + dilution overwhelm internal growth, and the stock re-tests $20.
2. **A capital-formation answer for the $1.5B pipeline with the stock at ~$22.** JV, mezz program scale-up (currently a $30–50M target), or asset sales. Absent one, external growth is on hold and the growth story is just 2–3% escalators and three development deliveries.
3. **FAA lease disposition.** Holdover, backfill, or go-dark — plus the 2027 expiration schedule behind it. Management has pre-conditioned expectations low, which creates modest upside optionality.
4. **Leverage trajectory and the IG rating timeline.** 7.1x needs to come down for the 2027 ratings upgrade that management says unlocks pipeline conversion. Reiteration of the 2027 IG target is table stakes; slippage is a negative.
5. **Shutdown/CR commentary.** Government is open under a CR through **December 11, 2026**. GSA rent typically flows on prior appropriations for roughly six weeks into a shutdown, so a mid-December lapse would be a Q1'27 cash-flow issue, not a Q4 one — but any Q&A on contingency planning will move sentiment. Separately, the structural GSA-downsizing overhang (~1,000 leases terminated in the 2025 wave; projections of a 63% reduction in GSA-leased office by 2034) remains the sector's narrative discount. DEA's mitigants: ~96% federal revenue but **zero Washington, D.C. exposure**, mid-90s historical renewal ratio, VA ~24% / FBI ~16% / DEA ~8% of revenue.

**Non-events:** The October 22, 2026 rebrand (new logo, guidelines, easterlyreit.com) carries **no name change, no ticker change, no strategy change** — CEO Darrell Crate: "While our brand has evolved, our focus remains unchanged." Do not read it as a prelude to a strategic review. The October 22 dividend declaration at $0.45 was in line.
