## 3. Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Free cash flow.** Q3 is expected to be positive despite a ~$700M DOJ payment. Reaffirming $1B–$3B for FY26 matters more than the quarterly print.
2. **Commercial deliveries and 737 rate.** Watch for 500 737s and 90–100 787s for 2026, plus rate-increase timing (47 → 52).
3. **BCA operating margin.** Q2 was a loss of $322M. Any sign of a breakeven path is the bullish signal.
4. **Revenue vs. ~$25.0B.** Mostly a delivery-count story.
5. **GAAP EPS vs. ~$0.01.** Charges or one-time items can swing this more than operations.
6. **Backlog.** Reported record of about $715B in Q2. Watch for growth.
7. **Guidance reaffirmation.** Any change to the FCF range or delivery targets.

- **Base:** Reaffirmed guidance, and no new language on charges or the 777X.
- **Bear:** Any hedge on 737 rate, a new charge, or a slip in 787 deliveries.

## 5. Catalyst Checklist

1. **Q3 FCF vs. the ~$700M DOJ payment.** The market wants proof the FY guide holds even with the payment.
2. **737 rate 47 → 52 timing.** Any slip is the most likely negative headline.
3. **BCA margin trajectory.** Narrowing the Q2 operating loss is the key test.
4. **Delivery count and 787 pace.** Watch whether the 90–100 787 target is still intact.
5. **Narrative: DOJ, debt paydown, and any 777X or defense commentary.** Any new legal or charge disclosure can move the stock more than the quarter.
