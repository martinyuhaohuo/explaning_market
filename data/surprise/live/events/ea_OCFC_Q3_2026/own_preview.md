## Key Metrics to Watch (Ranked)

1. **Core EPS vs. ~$0.41.** Check whether the beat or miss is driven by margin or by expenses. Q2 was a miss against the $0.45 estimate, so the bar is not high.
2. **Net interest margin vs. the 3.07%+ guide.** This is the main driver of revenue. A print below 3.07% would be a negative signal.
3. **Operating expenses vs. the $120M–$125M guide.** Post-merger cost cuts are the story. Hitting the guide supports the Q4 expense outlook.
4. **Flushing integration.** Check the deposit and loan balances, the realized cost savings, and any update on the expected deal accretion.
5. **Credit quality.** Acquisition-related loan marks and reserve builds can move the quarter. Check provisions and charge-offs.
6. **Loan and deposit growth vs. the 1%–2% year-end target.** C&I growth is the key indicator.
7. **Q4 guidance and 2027 commentary.** Management's tone on the next year matters more than the quarter itself.
