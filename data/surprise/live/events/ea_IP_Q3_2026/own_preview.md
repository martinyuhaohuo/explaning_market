## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Adj. EBITDA vs. $780–830M guide.** The implied H2 ramp depends on this. Hitting the low end while holding FY guidance would signal the second half is back-end loaded.
2. **Full-year EBITDA guide ($3.20–3.40B).** Reaffirmed last quarter. A cut to the low end is the main bear trigger.
3. **Input cost commentary.** Management's H2 priority is "mitigating rising input costs." Watch price realization versus cost inflation.
4. **Pine Hill mill closure.** Confirm the $85M impact and the restart timeline.
5. **Revenue vs. ~$6.3–6.4B.** Q2 missed on sales, so a second miss would sharpen the volume/price debate.
6. **Free cash flow.** Q2 was negative. Positive Q3 FCF would support the cost-out narrative.
7. **Separation/strategic update.** A search headline questioned the credibility of the separation timeline. I did not verify its details, so check the transcript for any change.
