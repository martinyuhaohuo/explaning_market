## 2. Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Q4 revenue framing vs. "roughly flat sequentially."** Management guided Q4 qualitatively only. Light-vehicle production is forecast **−2% in Q3 and −5% in Q4**; holding Q4 flat on a −5% market requires ~5 pts of outgrowth. Any walk-down toward $940–950M validates the bear case already in the tape.
2. **Adjusted operating margin and the path through 20%.** Q1 18.6% → Q2 19.5% → Q3 guide 19.4–19.6%. Productivity has carried ~50 bps/yr of expansion. Watch whether tariff pass-through (dilutive to margin %, ~30 bps in Q1) and China mix cap progress.
3. **Automotive outgrowth and China mix.** Auto was $544.8M / 24.2% margin in Q2. Management claims ~2 pts of global share gain; CFO flagged "production mix varying quarter-to-quarter based on changing OEM market share," with softening domestic China. Outgrowth is the entire bull thesis — quantify it.
4. **Data center / electrification revenue, with numbers attached.** Five platform concept wins YTD across four hyperscalers; SAM expansion of **1.5–2.5x per megawatt** as racks move to 400–800V; on-site power sensors added ~1 pt of growth in Q2 with acceleration expected in 2027. The market wants a dollar revenue base and a 2027 ramp shape, not a TAM slide.
5. **Free cash flow and the buyback.** 1H FCF $291M (Q2 conversion 130%); capex cut to ~2% of revenue vs. a ~4% historical run-rate. Net leverage hit **2.4x — the 2.5x target, two quarters early** — yet 1H repurchases were only $25.1M. A step-up in buyback is the most available self-help catalyst.
6. **Industrials / ADCE durability.** Industrials $212.1M at 27.1%; Aerospace, Defense & Commercial Equipment $233.7M at 27.8%. On-road truck orders grew double-digits in Q2 supporting an H2 heavy-vehicle recovery; US residential HVAC was recovering modestly. These two segments are ~45% of revenue at ~27%+ margins and are carrying the mix story.
7. **Tariffs / IEEPA.** Q3 assumes a Q2-consistent ~$10M impact and **excludes** any IEEPA refunds. Refund recognition would be a clean, non-operational EPS upside that should be discounted by the buy-side.

| **Bull** (~25%) | $985–995M (at/above high end) | $0.99–1.02 | Heavy-vehicle + ADCE upside and data-center shipments offset auto seasonality; margin ≥19.8%; Q4 guided flat-to-up ($970M+); buyback accelerated | **+7% to +12%** |

| **Bear** (~25%) | $955–965M (at/below low end) | $0.92–0.94 | China mix and auto shutdowns bite; Q4 revenue framed down 3–5% seq (≤$945M); margin ≤19.3%; data-center timing pushed to 2H27 | **−8% to −15%** |

**What signals each:**
- *Bull tell* — management quantifies data-center revenue and the 2027 ramp, raises the outgrowth number, and announces or implies a larger repurchase now that leverage is below target.
- *Base tell* — "modest organic growth for full-year 2026" repeated verbatim with no Q4 dollar range. The stock drifts with the tape.
- *Bear tell* — any of: "pushed to the right" on data-center schedules, renewed China share-loss language, or a Q4 revenue range starting with a $93x/$94x handle.

**Historical calibration:** ~7.1% average absolute post-print move; median ~7.5% over the last eight quarters; 6 beats in 8 (Zacks: 4-for-4 most recently, Q2 was a +5.38% EPS surprise). Despite that, the **Q2 beat produced only ~+2.2% after-hours / ~+2.7%** — the stock has stopped paying for in-line-to-modest beats. The distribution is skewed: beats get ~2–3%, disappointments get the full 7–10%.

## 4. Catalyst Checklist

1. ☐ **Q4 revenue range vs. the ~$945M consensus-implied figure / "roughly flat" guide** — resolves a ~$25M gap between what management said and what the Street modeled.
2. ☐ **Adj. op margin ≥19.6%** (top of guide) — the productivity story is the reason the multiple shouldn't be ~10x.
3. ☐ **Stated auto outgrowth in Q3** vs. LVP −2% — needs to be ≥4 pts to defend the content narrative.
4. ☐ **Data-center revenue disclosed in dollars** plus 2027 ramp commentary — the only line item that can re-rate the stock away from "auto cyclical."
5. ☐ **Capital allocation: buyback sizing** at 2.4x net leverage, ~10.7x forward earnings, and capex at half the historical rate.
6. ☐ **Any FY27 preliminary framing** — management has avoided it; an early bridge would be a narrative shift either way.

**Read-through:** the pre-print drawdown has *not* been accompanied by estimate cuts — only by target cuts and a semis/auto-supplier de-rate. The sell-off is multiple, not numbers. Mechanically, that makes an in-line print with an intact Q4 message the highest-probability path to a relief move, and makes **any Q4 revenue walk-down the asymmetric risk** — it would convert a multiple problem into a numbers problem.

**Positioning view:** risk/reward is skewed modestly positive into the print at ~10.7x with a 4-for-4 beat history and a center-of-guide consensus, but the payoff pattern (small up, large down) argues against naked long exposure through the event. Defined-risk upside expression (call spreads) is preferable to stock for anyone adding here; straddles are not cheap enough to own volatility outright.

*Peer context already on the tape:* Texas Instruments fell ~1.9% on a strong print; Penguin Solutions rallied 13.1%. Analog/auto-exposed semis are being sold on good news — a relevant caution for ST's reaction function.
