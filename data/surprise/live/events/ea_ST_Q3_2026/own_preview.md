## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Adjusted EPS vs. $0.95–0.96.** Sensata beat EPS in each of its last four quarters, and Q2 came in at $0.98 vs. about $0.93 expected. A beat is the base case.
2. **Revenue vs. ~$970M+.** Q3 is seasonally soft because of European automotive shutdowns. Q3 FY25 revenue was $932M, and guidance implies 3%–6% growth.
3. **Adjusted operating margin vs. 19.4%–19.6%.** Watch for margin expansion or compression, and how much of the tariff pass-through (about $10M) affects it. Management says tariffs should not affect adjusted EPS.
4. **Q4 guidance vs. consensus.** The buy-side will focus on the forward view more than the quarter.
5. **Free cash flow and tariff commentary.** H1 FCF was $291M. Guidance excludes potential IEPA tariff refunds and payments from a customer tied to a program termination. Any update on either is a potential surprise.

## Catalyst Checklist

1. **EPS vs. $0.95–0.96:** A beat is expected. The reaction depends on whether the beat comes from operations or one-time items.
2. **Q4 revenue and EPS guidance:** The buy-side is looking for the Q4 range and a reaffirmed or raised FY26 outlook.
3. **Tariff refund and customer payment status:** Excluded from guidance, so any recognition is upside. Any delay or write-down is a risk.
4. **Margin trajectory:** Confirm the 19.4%–19.6% margin holds and that tariff pass-through doesn't dilute it.
5. **Strategic items:** Portfolio moves, M&A, or restructuring commentary. I found none in my sources.
