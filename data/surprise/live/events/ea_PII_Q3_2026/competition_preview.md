## 1. Setup in one paragraph

The bar is low on sentiment and positioning, but *not* low on the actual number.

## 3. Key metrics to watch — ranked

**1. Operational adjusted EPS and the gross margin print.** Strip the refunds. Q2 operational adj. EPS was $1.01 vs. a $0.70–$0.80 target; adj. gross margin was 23.9% (+446 bps). Q3 is a seasonally weaker mix quarter. A clean operational number near or above $0.59 with GM holding ~21–22% is the bull trigger; sub-$0.50 with GM compression says the refund quarter flattered a decelerating base.

**2. Free cash flow and net leverage.** This is the actual bear case. 1H26 FCF was **negative $143.7M** vs. +$343.8M a year ago. Net leverage improved to 2.6x from 3.6x sequentially, and management expects further H2 improvement as working capital normalizes. Q3/Q4 are the seasonal cash-generation window. With the dividend costing roughly $155M/year against a 4.92% yield, a second consecutive quarter of weak conversion puts coverage squarely in the Q&A.

**3. ORV North American retail and share.** Q2 was +5% retail and a **fifth consecutive quarter of share gains**; utility is >70% of Powersports and retailed +10%+; cab units crossed half of ORV retail for the first time. Management guided H2 retail to "flattish, could be up low-single digits if demand holds." Watch the **utility vs. recreational split** — Speetzen already flagged recreational ORV as "pressured." A sixth straight share-gain quarter alongside flat industry retail is the cleanest bull datapoint available.

**4. Dealer inventory, promo, and shipment discipline.** Dealer inventory was -8% YoY with days-sales slightly above 100; promo ran below prior-year and was guided to tick down further in H2. If promo re-accelerates or inventory builds, the "shipments matched to retail" discipline story cracks and 2027 gross margin assumptions go with it.

**5. Tariff/commodity trajectory and the $40M refund option.** China-sourced COGS is tracking from 18% (2024) toward <5% by end-2027, "ahead of schedule." Any of the $40M refund captured is unguided upside. Conversely, USMCA content-rule changes are the live tail risk.

**6. Marine.** +16% in Q2 on premium pontoon (Bennington QX, Godfrey Sanpan), but mid/low-tier remains rate-sensitive and industry pontoon retail was down ~9%. Small segment, outsized margin drag if it deteriorates.

**7. First 2027 framing.** Bristlemoon's short thesis explicitly claims a "flaw in 2027 consensus." Any early 2027 bridge — Indian accretion, commodity relief timing, MY27 product cadence (2027 off-road and snowmobile lineups already unveiled) — will move the stock more than the Q3 number.

## 5. Catalyst checklist

1. **Operational adj. EPS vs. ~$0.50 guide-implied / $0.59 published** — the gap between these two is the whole debate about whether management sandbagged H2 or consensus is stale.
2. **Free cash flow, stated explicitly, plus net leverage vs. 2.6x** — the buy-side wants a FY conversion number and reassurance on the 4.9% dividend. Highest-variance item.
3. **FY26 guidance action** — reaffirm is the base case; a raise is the bull trigger; narrowing sales to the low end functions as a cut.
4. **ORV NA retail + share streak + utility/rec mix** — validates or breaks the "share gains through a flat industry" thesis supporting any 2027 growth number.
5. **First 2027 commentary** — Indian accretion, commodity relief timing, tariff run-rate, MY27 off-road and snow lineups. The explicit short thesis targets this.
6. **Promo and dealer inventory** (secondary) — the tell on whether margin gains are structural or inventory-cycle.

### Bottom line

Expectations are low, the chart is broken, targets were cut into the print, and a short was publicly initiated two weeks ago — a setup that usually rewards a decent number. But the last two beats were sold, and the reason is identifiable and unresolved: **1H FCF of -$143.7M against a 4.9% dividend, and an EPS base flattered by $0.96 of one-time tariff refunds.** The swing factor on Tuesday is not revenue or even EPS. It is whether Polaris puts up a clean positive free-cash-flow quarter with a credible leverage path, and whether the first words on 2027 validate or contradict the consensus a short seller is explicitly calling flawed. Trade the cash flow line, not the headline.
