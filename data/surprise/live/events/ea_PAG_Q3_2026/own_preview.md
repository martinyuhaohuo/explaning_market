# PAG Q3 FY2026 Earnings Preview

**Company:** Penske Automotive Group, Inc. (NYSE: PAG)
**Period:** Q3 FY2026
**Release:** 2026-10-28, 10:58 UTC (about 6:58 a.m. ET, pre-market)
**Knowledge cutoff:** 2026-10-27 20:00 UTC

> **Read this first:** A take-private deal changes how this print matters. Per one report, Penske Corporation and Mitsui & Co. agreed on Oct 15 to buy the public shares for $212.00 in cash. I found only that one source for the $212 price, so confirm it in PAG's SEC filings before acting on it. If the deal is definitive, the stock should trade near the deal price, and the earnings release is unlikely to move it much. The notes below are for the operating picture and for the case where the deal does not close.

## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Retail automotive same-store revenue and gross profit.** Q2 same-store revenue rose 6%. Check whether that holds in Q3.
2. **Service and parts gross margin.** Q2 was up 80 bps. Management expects demand to stay strong from an aging fleet and higher miles driven.
3. **Commercial truck (Class 8) contribution.** Class 8 orders rose sharply in Q2 (orders up ~170%, industry backlog up ~105% to ~186k units). Management expects strong H2 demand.
4. **SG&A as a percent of gross profit.** Management expects the low 70s range.
5. **Q4 outlook and any buyback or dividend change.** The dividend was raised to $1.44 (23rd straight increase). Expect no numeric guidance, only qualitative commentary.

## Scenarios

| Scenario | Revenue | EPS | Key driver | Stock reaction |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Bull | Beats ~$8.3B+ | Beats $3.60+ | Service/parts margin up, truck strength | Limited if deal holds; stronger if deal breaks |
| Base | In line, ~$8.2B | In line, ~$3.45–3.50 | Steady same-store and service | Flat, pinned near deal price |
| Bear | Misses ~$8.0B | Misses <$3.30 | Softer used/new margins, F&I | Modest dip if deal is at risk; little effect if the deal holds |

For each scenario:
- **Bull:** Same-store growth, service margin expansion, and an upbeat truck outlook. Management talks up H2 demand.
- **Base:** Results in line, with management repeating its qualitative outlook and no change to the deal.
- **Bear:** Margin pressure in new vehicles or F&I, or deal-related uncertainty (financing, committee process) raised on the call.

**Historical context:** I found no reliable recent earnings-day reaction data. An older pre-Q2 preview cited an average Day 0 move of ~3.6% and an options-implied move of ~1.9%. Treat both as stale. When the stock jumped 11% to ~$217 on the July $210 proposal, the market was pricing the deal, not operations.

## Catalyst Checklist

1. **Deal status vs. the $212 price:** Any update from the special committee, financing, or shareholder vote matters more than the quarter. Confirm the price and terms first.
2. **Q3 same-store and service growth vs. the 6% / +80 bps run-rate:** A slowdown would be the main operating negative.
3. **Truck (Class 8) commentary:** Confirms or challenges the H2 backlog story.
4. **Dividend and capital return:** Any change to the $1.44 dividend or buyback while a take-private is pending.
5. **Q4 framing:** Qualitative outlook only; watch for "strong demand" language versus caution.

## Trading Setup

- **Stock level:** Post-proposal, the stock traded above $210 (about $217 on the day of the July announcement). I did not find a verified current price. If the $212 deal is definitive, expect the stock to trade just below $212 and stay tight. Check the live quote and the spread to $212.
