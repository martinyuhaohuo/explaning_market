## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Adjusted operating income vs. the $115–175M Q3 guide.** This is the most important number, because it was pre-announced as a range. Landing in the upper half would be read positively.
2. **FY26 adjusted operating profit guidance.** The current range is $1.225–1.375B. Watch for a further cut, or for a narrowing toward the low end.
3. **Packaged Meats volume and margin.** August flagged volume pressure and cautious consumer spending.
4. **Hog Production and Fresh Pork.** Hog-cost volatility drove the August cut. Watch the updated segment ranges.
5. **Margin trend.** Q2 adjusted EBITDA margin was 10.9%. Check whether Q3 holds that level.
6. **Capex and cash flow.** Capex is guided at $350–450M. Check for changes tied to the Sioux Falls project.
7. **Nathan's Famous contribution.** It is excluded from guidance, so check whether management will add it in.
