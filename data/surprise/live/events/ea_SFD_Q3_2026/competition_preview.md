### 4. Key metrics to watch — ranked

**1. FY2026 total adjusted operating profit guidance (highest importance).** Old guide: $1,225–1,375M. Math: H1 actual $638M + Q3 guided $115–175M = **$753–813M**. Even the *low* end of the old FY guide needs Q4 at **$412M+** — essentially equal to Q4 FY25's ~$413M, but with Fresh Pork and Hog Production running hundreds of millions worse YoY. **A cut is near-certain.** My estimate for the reset: **~$1,025–1,125M**, implying FY26 adj. EPS ~**$2.10–2.25** vs. $2.55 in FY25. Anything at or above $1,150M is a genuine positive surprise; below $1,000M is a problem.

**2. Packaged Meats FY reaffirmation and the Q4 implied step-up.** The $1,075–1,150M FY guide requires Q4 PM profit of **$305–380M** (Q4 FY25: $302M). The low end is achievable on holiday ham/seasonal mix; the high end would be +26% YoY and is not credible. If PM gets trimmed, the entire bull case — "PM is the real company, pork is noise" — breaks, and the stock does not hold $18.

**3. Free cash flow and dividend coverage.** H1 FY26 operating cash flow was only **$204M**, against capex guided to $350–450M and a dividend costing roughly **$495M/yr** (394M shares × $1.25). At a **6.8% yield**, the market is already pricing some doubt. Watch for an H2 working-capital unwind, FY26 OCF framing, and any language softening the dividend commitment. Balance sheet is not the issue — 0.4x net leverage, $3.6B liquidity — but the payout-vs.-FCF gap is.

**4. First FY2027 framing.** Lean hogs for December delivery were ~**67¢/lb** in mid-October, roughly **40% off** the March 11, 2026 high; the pork cutout was **$80.68/cwt** for the week ending Oct 16, down $3.83 w/w. USDA sees 2026 production +1.1% and liveweight hog prices averaging $63.79/cwt, with forecasts cut for 1H27. Hog Production plausibly runs at a **loss** in FY27. Any early FY27 bracket above ~$1.1B total op profit would be taken as a win.

**5. Packaged Meats volume and share.** Q2 showed branded packaged lunch meat volume **+9.5%** and share gains in five $1B+ categories. Confirmation that branded volume growth held through a "cautious consumer" quarter is what underwrites the multiple. Watch price/mix vs. volume: growth bought with promotion is worth far less.

**Secondary:** the **Oct 16 8-K** — a $600M senior unsecured delayed-draw term loan (3-year, SOFR + 87.5–137.5bps, drawable to Dec 23, 2026, BofA agent) to redeem the 4.250% 2027 notes. Routine refinancing, but expect a question on FY27 interest expense.

**Operationally, what distinguishes them:** bull requires Packaged Meats to absorb the pork-chain damage via mix and branded pricing. Bear requires PM margin (13.1% in Q2) to crack under freight, fuel and packaging inflation at the same time commodity spreads are compressed — the double-hit scenario the market has been fearing since the August guide-down.

**Historical calibration:** reaction data is thin — only ~7 quarters since the January 2025 IPO. One dataset puts the average earnings-day move at **+3.9%, higher 75% of the time**, but that sample is dominated by the 2025 record-results run and is not a usable prior for a guide-down quarter. More relevant recent prints: **Q4 FY25 (Mar 2026)** EPS $0.83 vs. $0.67, stock **+4.3%**; **Q2 FY26 (Aug 2026)** EPS $0.62 beat by $0.02 and revenue beat by $54M, yet shares fell **–2.3% to $23.88** because guidance was cut. **The last two quarters established the pattern: beats do not matter, guidance does.**

### 6. Catalyst checklist

1. **FY2026 total adj. op profit guide vs. ~$1,050–1,125M** — the single number that sets the move. Reiterating $1,225M+ is not arithmetically supportable; the question is how far below.
2. **Packaged Meats FY2026 range held at $1,075–1,150M** — the bull case's load-bearing wall. A cut here is the bear trigger regardless of the headline EPS.
3. **Q4 FY26 Packaged Meats path** — management must explain a $305M+ Q4. Listen for ham-season volume commitments, distribution wins already shipped, and input-cost relief.
4. **Dividend and FCF language** — explicit coverage framing, or any capex deferral to protect the $1.25. Silence here with weak OCF would be read badly.
5. **FY2027 pre-framing** — whether Hog Production is guided to a loss, and whether management quantifies the cutout/hog-price assumptions behind it.
