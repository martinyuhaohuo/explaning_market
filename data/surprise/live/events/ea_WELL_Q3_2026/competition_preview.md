## 2. Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **SHO same-store NOI growth** — the entire thesis. Q1 +22.1%, Q2 +20.5% (15th straight quarter >20%). The FY guide (18.5–21.5%) *embeds H2 deceleration to roughly 17–19%*. A Q3 print of 19–20% is a modest beat; anything with a "2" handle is a clear beat and forces a FY raise.
2. **Occupancy: average vs. spot-to-spot.** Q2 showed +330bp average occupancy and **+100bp spot-to-spot** — the sequential acceleration signal. Industry data supports it: NIC MAP put national senior housing occupancy at **90.4% in Q3'26** (+60bp q/q, 21st consecutive quarter of gains) with construction down 1.0% to just 16,159 units. If WELL's spot-to-spot doesn't hold ≥~80–100bp, the "uncorrelated demand" narrative takes a hit.
3. **RevPOR vs. ExpPOR spread / NOI margin.** Q2: RevPOR +5.2%, ExpPOR **+0.7%**, margin +300bp to **32.1%** (above pre-pandemic). ExpPOR near zero is not sustainable; the bear case is an ExpPOR reacceleration toward 3%+ on labor. Watch any commentary on **January 2027 rate letters** — the single most important forward datapoint on the call.
4. **FY2026 guidance revision (third consecutive raise?).** Two raises already. The buy-side expects a third; a mere reaffirm at $6.36–$6.44 would be read as a soft negative.
5. **Capital deployment pace and funding mix.** $15.5B pro rata YTD investment activity at Q2 ($9.4B closed, $6.1B under contract), including the C$4.1B / 38-property Amica Senior Lifestyles portfolio (closed April 1). Acquired assets average six years old at ~75% occupancy — the lease-up upside. Watch (a) new announced volume, (b) equity funded vs. leverage (net debt/EBITDA was a fortress-like **2.99x** with ~$9.5B liquidity), and (c) integration/Welltower Business System commentary.
6. **Early framing of 2027.** Formal 2027 guidance comes in February, but any directional comment matters: Street is at **$7.48** for FY27 (+16.3%). Barclays cut its target to $246 on Oct 13 specifically on *tougher 2027 comps*.

## 4. Catalyst Checklist

- [ ] **SHO SSNOI ≥20%** vs. ~18–20% buy-side bar — the 16th straight 20%+ quarter is the whisper; a 17-handle is the first real crack in a four-year streak.
- [ ] **Third FY26 guidance raise to ≥$6.44 low-end** — a reaffirm, not a raise, is the most likely source of a negative surprise given two prior raises conditioned the market.
- [ ] **ExpPOR ≤2% and NOI margin ≥32%** — this is where the "cost pressure from rapid expansion" critique lands; WELL beat revenue but missed EPS in July, raising margin-execution questions.
- [ ] **Spot-to-spot occupancy ≥+100bp** plus any color on **Jan-2027 rate increases** — sets the 2027 RevPOR base before February guidance.
- [ ] **Capital deployment: volume, cap rates, and funding** — pipeline described as "never stronger," but at 37x FFO the market will test whether new deals are still accretive and how much equity is being issued.
