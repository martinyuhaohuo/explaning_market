## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Normalized FFO vs. ~$1.63.** A beat of ~$0.03–0.05 has been the recent pattern. Q2 beat by $0.05.
2. **Same-store NOI growth, especially SHOP/senior housing.** FY guide is 13.75%–16% total and 18.5%–21.5% for SHOP. Any slowdown in the SHOP ramp is the main risk.
3. **FY2026 FFO guidance.** The market is looking for a hold or small raise above the $6.44 top end. A reaffirmation would read as a mild negative given the recent raise.
4. **Occupancy and rate trends in senior housing.** Look for occupancy gains and pricing commentary.
5. **Acquisition and capital deployment pipeline.** Watch funding costs and the cap-rate spread on new deals. Analysts have questioned whether acquisitions can deliver the growth priced in.
6. **Valuation and multiple.** Analysts flag the premium multiple as a source of compression risk if growth slows.

## Bull / Base / Bear Scenarios

| Scenario | Revenue | Normalized FFO | Key Driver | Stock Reaction (judgment) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Bull** | ≥$3.75B | ≥$1.68 | SHOP occupancy and rate beat; FY guide lifted above $6.44 | +3% to +6% |
| **Base** | ~$3.70B | $1.63–$1.66 | In-line to slight beat; guide nudged up or held | −1% to +2% |
| **Bear** | <$3.65B | <$1.60 | SHOP slowdown or higher funding costs; guide held | −3% to −6% |

**Bull signals:** Management raises the SHOP range, reports occupancy gains above seasonal norms, or describes an accretive acquisition pipeline.
**Base signals:** Guide reaffirmed or nudged up within the existing range, with steady SHOP growth.
**Bear signals:** Occupancy plateau, rate-growth deceleration, higher cost of capital, or deal volume pulled forward.

**Historical reaction context:**
- Over the last ~17 reports, the stock closed higher on earnings day about 14 times and lower about 3 times. The average same-day close move is about +2.8%. [EarningsWatcher / ChartRow](https://earnings-watcher.com/wiki/well-stock-after-earnings)
- Next-day moves are more mixed. Trefis counts 10 positive and 8 negative over five years, rising to 73% positive over three years. [Trefis](https://www.trefis.com/stock/well/articles2/597519/how-will-welltower-stock-react-to-its-upcoming-earnings/2026-04-25)
- Average absolute move is about 1.5% on Day 0 and 2.6% on Day +1. [Barchart](https://www.barchart.com/story/news/66729/welltower-stock-outlook-is-wall-street-bullish-or-bearish)
- The July 2026 print was a beat-and-raise, yet the stock fell 1.9%. The market reacted to the raise being only modest and to the premium valuation.

## Catalyst Checklist

1. **FFO of ~$1.63 vs. buy-side whisper.** The whisper is likely a few cents above consensus after the Q2 beat. A print in line may disappoint.
2. **FY2026 guide vs. $6.36–$6.44.** The buy-side expects a raise. Holding the range is the likely negative trigger.
3. **SHOP same-store NOI (guide 18.5%–21.5%).** This is the growth engine. Any cut or hedged language would drive the reaction.
4. **Dividend and capital allocation.** The quarterly dividend was raised 15% to $0.85 in July. Watch for any change in payout or acquisition-funding commentary.
5. **Valuation narrative.** Commentary on growth durability and multiple sustainability will matter more than the quarter itself.

## Bottom Line

The setup is a high-bar, quality-premium name. An in-line FFO print with a held or slightly raised guide is the most likely outcome, and that has tended to produce a flat-to-modest reaction. The main upside requires a clear SHOP acceleration and a guide raise above $6.44. The main downside is a SHOP slowdown or a hold on guidance.
