1. **Core FFO vs. $0.49.** Beats and misses here drive the reaction. Q2 missed by $0.01.
2. **Whether Core FFO guidance moves.** A raise above $1.96 would be a clear positive. Holding the range keeps the stock tied to the $1.99 consensus gap.
3. **Same-property NOI growth vs. the 3.25%–4.25% range.** Q2 printed 4.1%, near the top, so a reaffirmation at the high end is the base case.
4. **Revenue vs. $82.9M–$83.4M.** The two consensus figures differ, so check the number you use.
5. **Acquisitions and non-cash revenue.** Growth from acquisitions is a driver of the Nareit line, and quality of that growth matters for Core.
