## 1. The Setup in One Paragraph

CLW is a ~$310M market-cap pure-play SBS paperboard producer coming off a trough quarter (Q2 adjusted EBITDA of **negative $8M**) and guiding to a sharp snap-back to **$20–30M in Q3**. The whole print is a referendum on one question: **are the two announced $60/ton price increases actually being realized?** Management says they're worth $50–60M annualized ($10–20M in 2026). The complication: **Fastmarkets RISI has not recognized the increases in either September or October** — SBS printed flat month-over-month for two straight months — and roughly **50% of CLW's volume is RISI-indexed**. That is the central tension. Note the stock rose **+23.4%** on the day after last quarter's *miss*, so this is a narrative tape, not a print tape.

**Two estimate-mechanics points that matter more than the headline:**

1. **Consensus revenue implicitly requires ~3–4% sequential price/mix improvement.** Backing out ~$21M of non-paperboard revenue, $388M implies roughly **$1,115/ton vs. $1,077 in Q2**. The RISI index has not validated that. Revenue is the cleanest early read on whether the increases stuck.
2. **Consensus EPS of −$0.155 is effectively the midpoint-to-high end of the EBITDA guide.** Bridging at ~$21.5M quarterly D&A and ~$6.5–7M interest (calibrated off Q3 FY25's $18M EBITDA / −$0.51 adj. EPS), −$0.155 implies **~$24–25M EBITDA**. There is no cushion below the midpoint.

## 3. Key Metrics to Watch — Ranked

**1. Adjusted EBITDA vs. the $20–30M guide.** The only number that matters. Q2 → Q3 bridge: absence of the $22M Lewiston outage (+$22M, the dominant swing), price realization (+$5–10M), better fixed-cost absorption on higher production for inventory rebuild (+$3–5M), Cypress Bend savings ramp (+$2–3M), offset by $3–5M of incremental input-cost headwind (polyethylene, chemicals, freight — guided as a $20–25M FY26 drag). Landing below $20M breaks the self-help story.

**2. Realized price per ton, sequential.** $1,077 in Q2 is the base. Management announced $120/ton cumulative (June + August). With ~50% of volume RISI-indexed and the index flat in Sept/Oct, the open-market half is carrying the load. Quantify: *how much of the $120 has been captured, and what is the Q4 exit rate?*

**3. Q4 guide and the 2027 framing.** Buy-side wants the $50–60M annualized price benefit reaffirmed with a date — management previously said full run-rate by early 2027. Any walk-back of that number or timing is the single most damaging thing they could say.

**4. FCF, net debt, and post-refi interest.** Net debt was $266.8M at 6/30 (−$50M YTD); cash $95.4M. FY26 guide: positive FCF, capex $65–75M, $20–30M working-capital release, aided by $33M insurance recoveries and ~$30M tax refunds. **Watch the pro-forma interest guide on the September 18 refinancing** — a $275M floating-rate AgWest term loan plus $200M revolver replaced the $275M 4.75% 2028 notes. Maturities got pushed out, but cash interest may well go *up*. Also expect GAAP noise from call premium / write-off of unamortized issuance costs.

**5. Volume and capacity.** CLW is **sold out across its ~1.2M-ton network and oversold on extruded**, with management conceding on the Q2 call that the team is "struggling to deliver products on time." That's a pricing-power tell but caps upside. Watch for debottlenecking capex, the Cypress Bend CUK decision (sub-$10M fast path vs. $60M full solution, in trials), and the Circa CRB launch with Green Paper (~40k-ton opportunity).

**6. Industry datapoints.** Last quarter: imports −11%, ~300k tons of industry production removed since the start of 2026, operating rates low-80s (Q1) → 88% (Q2) → 90%+ targeted by year-end. Confirmation of 90%+ is the strongest available argument that pricing eventually sticks regardless of the index lag.

**What each scenario sounds like on the call:**

- **Bull:** "Fully realized," "exit-rate pricing," a Q4 EBITDA number with a 4-handle, explicit tons-and-dollars on the $120/ton capture, and a third price increase floated.
- **Base:** "Beginning to see benefits," "flows through into 2027," heavy emphasis on cost reduction and the $60M+ of fixed cost removed since 2024 — self-help carrying the quarter rather than price.
- **Bear:** "Index lag," "competitive dynamics on open-market volume," margins still "~10% below cycle-through levels," or any softening of the $50–60M annualized figure.

**Historical calibration:** Q2 FY26 (July 28) is the key precedent — CLW *missed* adj. EPS by $0.26 and the stock gapped +8.05% to $18.92 and **closed +23.4%** (max intraday +26.3%) from a $17.51 pre-print close. The market traded the pricing inflection and the balance sheet, not the loss. The asymmetry cuts both ways now: the stock is **+25% since May 31** and only 18% below its 52-week high, so the "worst is behind us" thesis is substantially priced in. A repeat of a forgiven miss is less likely at $19 than it was at $17.50.

## 7. Caveats on This Preview

- Consensus rests on **4 analysts**; dispersion is wide and the "consensus" EPS of −$0.155 is a thin construct. The $20–30M EBITDA guide is the better anchor.
- My EPS bridge assumes **~$21.5M quarterly D&A and ~$6.5–7M interest**, calibrated from Q3 FY25 actuals. The September refinancing makes the interest line genuinely uncertain this quarter — treat the EPS points in the scenario table as EBITDA-driven, ±$0.10.
- **GAAP vs. adjusted will diverge materially again.** Insurance recoveries ($17.5M partial Augusta settlement in Q1, $33M YTD through Q2) have been *excluded* from adjusted figures — which is why Q2 adjusted EPS (−$1.75) was *worse* than GAAP (−$1.33). Expect the same inversion plus refinancing charges. Headline-scanning algos may misread the print in the first minutes.
- RISI SBS pricing for October was reported flat as of the latest Fastmarkets release; a November index print could change the picture after this report.
