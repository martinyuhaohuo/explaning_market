## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Adjusted EPS vs. consensus, and the share-count effect.** Q2 adjusted EPS was $0.72 (vs. $0.70 consensus, $0.75 a year earlier). Diluted shares were up about 19% after the March $700M equity raise, which dilutes per-share comparisons. Watch whether the Q3 beat or miss reflects operations or just the share count.
2. **Revenue vs. consensus.** Q2 revenue was $211M, up from $198M a year earlier. Q3 consensus sits near $260M, so check whether rate and infrastructure growth keep pace.
3. **FY2026 guidance ($3.08–$3.18, standalone).** Management reaffirmed this in July. The buy side will want a reaffirmation or a raise. Note that guidance excludes Quadvest.
4. **Quadvest acquisition and financing.** Close is expected late Q3 or early Q4. Listen for updated accretion, financing terms, and any change in the guidance basis.
5. **Capex progress and Texas regulatory path.** About $207M was spent through June, 43% of the standalone capital budget. The Cibolo Valley deal is expected in Q4, and a combined Texas rate case is planned for early 2027. Watch for rate-case timing and any capex-budget changes.
6. **Long-term growth target.** The company reiterated 6–8% organic EPS growth, with 2026–2030 expected at or above the top of that range. A reaffirmation supports the multiple; any softening would be a negative surprise.
