## Key metrics to watch (ranked)

1. **RP1 commercial launch status.** Did the approval (if confirmed) convert to launch steps, and what are early revenue expectations and pricing?
2. **Cash runway.** A Barchart piece headlined "cash runway ends next quarter unless the FDA reverses course first." Check cash, equivalents, and runway guidance versus the going-concern language risk.
3. **Operating expense and net loss.** Compare the loss to the roughly -$0.70 EPS figure and watch the trend in R&D and G&A.
4. **Regulatory and label commentary.** Post-approval requirements, confirmatory trial design, and label scope.
5. **Financing.** Any equity raise or partnering deal, which often accompanies a print like this and can dominate the reaction.

- RP1 (vusolimogene oderparepvec) with nivolumab for advanced melanoma received two prior complete response letters (July 2025 and April 2026).
