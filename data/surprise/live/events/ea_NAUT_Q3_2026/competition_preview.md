## 1. The Setup in One Paragraph

NAUT is a pre-commercial proteomics platform company with **$190K of TTM revenue and a $323M market cap** (P/S ~1,700x). The stock has gone near-vertical — **+164% in one month, +112% in a quarter**, from $0.97 on September 16 to a 52-week high of $4.31 — on a *Nature Methods* tau-proteoform publication (Sept 4), two bullish initiations, and momentum/retail flow. It then gave back 22% today on no news, after a +24.6% unexplained surge on Friday Oct 23. Critically, the company filed a **$300M shelf on Sept 11 and launched a $125M ATM with TD Cowen on Sept 18 — when the stock was under $1.00.** The ATM is now worth ~39% of the share count at today's price. **The quarterly P&L is nearly irrelevant to this print; the ATM disclosure, the cash balance, and the Voyager commercialization dates are the whole event.**

## 3. Key Metrics to Watch — Ranked by What Actually Moves the Stock

**1. ATM usage / subsequent-events disclosure (dominant).** The $125M ATM went live Sept 18 at ~$1.00 and the stock quadrupled within four weeks on 13M+ shares/day of liquidity. Two very different realities:
   - *Good dilution:* they sold into $3–4 in October, raising $50–100M, pushing runway toward 2029 and clearing the financing overhang. Dilutive but strongly accretive to survival odds.
   - *Bad dilution:* they sold meaningful stock in late September at $1.00–1.50, destroying value before the re-rate.
   - *No disclosure:* overhang persists, and the market assumes a raise is still coming. Watch the 10-Q cover-page share count (dated ~Oct 20+) versus 127.2M at June 30 — that single number may reveal the answer before the call.

**2. Cash balance and runway language.** Last guided to "into Q1 2028" (~7 quarters). Any softening, or a cash number below ~$110M, resets the burn math. Management said on the Q2 call they expect to raise debt/equity "between now and mid-2027" but were "not in a rush."

**3. Voyager commercial timeline — three hard dates.** Beta unit placement **in 2026** (one quarter left), pre-orders opening **early 2027**, instrument shipments **mid-2027** at ~$1M/instrument with assay kits at "a few thousand dollars per sample." Slippage on the beta placement is the most likely negative surprise, since the window is nearly closed.

**4. AKT1 oncology assay into Early Access — promised for "late 2026."** This is the proof that the "new assay every few months" cadence toward ~20 proteoform assays by mid-2028 is real, and the first expansion beyond neuro. A slip to 2027 undercuts the entire roadmap that supports the $3.50–4.00 targets.

**5. Early Access Program customer count and revenue mix.** Only a "small number" of paying projects as of Q2 (Baylor College of Medicine first). FY26 revenue of ~$0.5M is mostly Michael J. Fox Foundation grant money — look for how much is genuine commercial service revenue versus grant.

**6. Operating expense trajectory.** The "<10% FY26 growth" guide leaves room for a 2H step-up to ~$18–20M/quarter as the commercial build starts. A Q3 opex print above ~$17.5M is narrative-positive (they're building to launch) but burn-negative. Which way the market reads it depends entirely on whether the cash question (#1) was answered well.

**What management commentary signals each:** Bull = specific names, dates and unit counts ("beta unit is installed at X"). Base = "on track," "later this year," no new specifics. **Historical calibration:** NAUT earnings prints have not historically been the catalyst — the 8-of-8 EPS beat streak has produced no durable moves. Every large move in this name over the past two months came from *non-earnings* news: the Sept 4 *Nature Methods* paper (130 tau proteoform groups resolved, <5.5% median CV, 0.1% sample sensitivity), the Sept 30–Oct 2 analyst initiations (+12.8% on Oct 2), and Roth's $3.50 target (Oct 19). This argues the print itself is a lower-probability catalyst than the options market is pricing — **unless** the ATM question gets answered.

## 7. Bottom Line

**The asymmetry is negative into this print.** Not because the numbers will be bad — they will almost certainly be fine, with a 1–3 cent EPS beat and in-line revenue — but because **a $323M valuation on $190K of revenue has priced in a successful 2027 commercial launch, while a $125M ATM sits live and unexercised against a 74M-share float.** Good news is largely in the price after a 164% month; the ATM is a structural cap on upside, because every rally is a selling opportunity for the company itself.

The genuine two-way risk is concentrated in a single disclosure: if management reveals they opportunistically raised $50–100M in October at $3–4, that is a materially better company — runway into 2029, overhang cleared, and dilution taken at a price nobody would have underwritten six weeks ago. If they reveal they sold stock at $1.00 in late September, or reveal nothing at all, the overhang and the burn math both remain, and the stock has no support until the $2.00 put wall.

**Sizing note:** with a 15% ATR and 142% implied vol, conventional position sizing does not apply. This is a milestone-driven, pre-revenue name where the quarterly report is a liquidity event, not an information event.
