## 3. Key Metrics to Watch — Ranked

1. **FY26 guidance raise magnitude.** Third raise of the year is expected. Taking revenue to ~$6.36B+ and EPS to ~$8.20+ is the neutral-to-good outcome; a mere narrowing of the range is a sell signal given the depressed implied Q4.
2. **Backlog / cRPO.** $8.1B is the anchor. Backlog is lumpy and 2026 is a low-renewal year, so a modest sequential dip is defensible — but sub-$7.8B with soft commentary breaks the "AI-driven structural demand" narrative.
3. **Non-GAAP operating margin + first 2027 framing.** Guide is 43.5–44.5% vs. 47.6% LY. Any *initial* 2027 color (consensus wants a path back toward 46%+) likely matters more to the stock than the Q3 print itself.
4. **IP segment durability.** +40% y/y in Q2 against an easier base. Synopsys' Design IP grew only ~11% y/y in its July quarter — if Cadence IP decelerates toward the teens, the share-gain story weakens.
5. **Agentic AI monetization specifics.** ChipStack (>20 engagements) and ViraStack (~25) plus today's **Maestro** domain-specialized model launch (NVIDIA Nemotron-based; claims ~30% higher quality, up to 5x token efficiency). CFO John Wall has explicitly said "we're not assuming a sudden step function" — look for whether AI is showing up in *recurring* revenue rather than press releases.
6. **China (15% of revenue) and export controls.** Guidance assumes rules remain "substantially similar." Any change in that language is a first-order risk.
7. **Hardware (Palladium Z3 / Protium X3) supply.** Record quarter in Q2 but supply-constrained; watch whether constraint is easing or capping upside.
8. **Intel Foundry 14A DTCO partnership** — management called it "100% additional." Any quantification of 2027 contribution is new information.

## 5. Trading Setup

- **Realized has undershot implied four straight quarters:** Jul'26 6.5% implied / 4.4% actual · Apr'26 7.0% / 4.6% · Feb'26 6.3% / 4.8% · Oct'25 8.1% / 4.7%. Over eight quarters the stock exceeded the implied move only half the time. **The straddle has been a losing buy recently** — structurally this favors premium selling or defined-risk spreads over outright straddles.
- **Reaction history (5yr, Trefis):** 10 positive / 10 negative 1-day moves (50%); median up-move **+5.0%** (max +12.5%), median down-move **–3.1%** (worst –8.8%). Over the last 3 years the hit rate falls to **45%**, and the average post-earnings move is **–0.69%** — i.e., beats have been consistently sold.

The EDA tape is supportive; the open question is whether Cadence's premium growth (and premium multiple) holds.

## 6. The Setup in One Line

Cadence is a **de-rating story fighting an accelerating fundamental story**: 20%+ revenue growth, record $8.1B backlog and +55% H1 bookings, against a stock down ~6% y/y because margin dilution has capped EPS growth at ~6% and because the market has pre-paid for the beat. The print itself is very likely fine. **The stock's reaction will be decided by the FY26 raise size and any first look at 2027 margins** — not by the Q3 numbers. Given a 6.2% implied move that has overstated realized moves four quarters running, and a three-year pattern of beats being sold, the risk/reward favors **fading extremes and selling volatility** over directional positioning into the print.

### Primary risks to this view
- **Upside gap risk:** today's Maestro/NVIDIA launch plus the Intel 14A DTCO deal give management an unusually rich narrative; a quantified 2027 framework could produce a >10% move that breaks the "beats get sold" pattern.
- **Downside gap risk:** China is 15% of revenue on an explicit "rules stay the same" assumption. That assumption changing is the single largest tail risk and would not be priced by a 6.2% straddle.
