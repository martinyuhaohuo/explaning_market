## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Revenue vs. the top of guidance ($1,625M).** A print above $1.62B signals demand strength and a likely FY raise.
2. **Non-GAAP EPS vs. $2.07 (top of range) and ~$1.96 consensus.** Consensus is stale or inconsistent, so the guidance range is the more reliable anchor.
3. **Backlog and bookings.** Q2 backlog was a record $8.1B. Whether it grows again matters more than the quarter's revenue for the multi-year story.
4. **Full-year guidance.** Watch for a raise above $8.05–$8.15 EPS and $6.26B–$6.34B revenue. Two raises already happened in 2026, so the bar is high.
5. **Margin trajectory and Hexagon D&E dilution.** The Hexagon D&E business is about $160M of 2026 revenue and roughly $0.28 of EPS dilution, expected to turn accretive in 2027. Commentary on the 2027 path matters.
6. **Recurring revenue mix and IP growth** (roughly 80% recurring; IP growing >40%). Sustained IP strength supports the AI/hardware-design thesis.

## Scenarios

These are my estimates, not published forecasts.

| Scenario | Revenue | EPS | Key driver | Likely stock reaction |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Bull** | >$1.63B | >$2.10 | Strong IP and bookings, backlog growth, FY EPS raised above $8.15 | Sharp gain, plausibly in the high single digits to low double digits |
| **Base** | $1.61B–$1.62B | $2.05–$2.08 | Beat at the top of guidance, modest FY raise, margins in range | Modest gain, low to mid single digits |
| **Bear** | ~$1.60B | ~$2.01–$2.03 | Revenue at the low end, FY reiterated, margin pressure or softer bookings | Flat to down, with a gap-down risk if backlog slows |

**What management commentary signals each case:**
- **Bull:** raised FY revenue and EPS, backlog up sequentially, AI-driven demand language, a confident 2027 outlook.
- **Base:** a small FY raise, in-line Q4 guide, steady margin language.
- **Bear:** FY held or cut, cautious Q4 guide, bookings flat, Hexagon integration costs, softer hardware commentary.

**Historical context:** Beat-and-raise prints have generally produced gains of about 2% to 13% on the day. Q3 2024 rose about 13% on a revenue beat. Q4 2025 rallied strongly after the company eased AI-disruption fears. Q2 2026 rose only about 2% in the morning session despite a beat and a raise. The sample is small, so treat these ranges as calibration, not forecasts.

## Catalyst Checklist

1. **Revenue vs. $1,625M top of guide and ~$1.61B consensus.** Clearing the top of the range is the main signal of upside.
2. **EPS vs. $2.07 top of guide.** A beat here with a raise is the base expectation the buy side will hold the stock to.
3. **Full-year guide raise.** The buy side will want a raise above $8.15 EPS. Holding the range would likely be read as a disappointment after two prior raises.
4. **Backlog and bookings trajectory.** The record $8.1B backlog is the core of the bull case. Sequential growth is the test.
5. **Narrative shift: Hexagon D&E and 2027 framing.** Any update on accretion timing, margin targets, or AI-related demand.
