## Q2 2026 Context (last reported, Jul 30)

- Revenue $5.39B, down 5.6% YoY, and below the ~$5.70B estimate. Management blamed "consumer affordability issues."
- Adj. EPS $9.61, down from $11.52, and below the ~$11.06 estimate.
- Gross margin 16.0% (vs. 16.4%). Adj. SG&A 70.8% of gross profit (vs. 68.7%). Adj. operating margin 4.1% (vs. 4.7%).
- New vehicle gross margin 6.7% (vs. 7.3%). Used retail gross margin 4.8% (vs. 5.2%). Parts & service stable at 56.2% margin, with revenue down 3.6%.
- F&I gross profit per retail unit $2,030 (down 1%). F&I revenue down 8.8%.
- No buybacks in Q2. YTD repurchases total $72.4M at $353.08 average. $306M authorization remains.
- No FY2026 guidance in the release. Pending: Hennessy Atlanta (10 stores, ~$1.7B annual revenue, expected to close by year-end 2026). Also a $50M annualized US expense cut and three UK Geely stores.

## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Same-store new and used retail units and revenue.** Q2 was down about 5% in total revenue. Consensus appears to assume roughly flat YoY revenue, so any further decline is the main risk.
2. **SG&A as % of gross profit.** The key read on whether the $50M cost program is showing up. Anything above Q2's 70.8% adj. ratio signals continued deleverage.
3. **New vehicle gross margin.** Q2 was 6.7%. Watch for stabilization or further compression from incentives and affordability pressure.
4. **F&I gross profit per unit and penetration.** Q2 was $2,030. Flat or down is the consensus-consistent case.
5. **Parts & service growth and margin.** Defensive revenue. Watch for growth turning positive.
6. **Capital allocation.** Buyback resumption at lower prices, Hennessy deal timing, and whether FY guidance (if given) is reaffirmed.

## Scenario Analysis

These are my estimates, not consensus.

| Scenario | Revenue | Adj. EPS | Key driver | Stock reaction |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Bull** | ≥ $5.8B | ≥ $11.50 | Gross margins hold, SG&A ratio improves on cost cuts, buyback restarts | +5% to +8% |
| **Base** | $5.5B to $5.7B | $10.00 to $11.00 | Revenue roughly flat to down low-single digits, margins stable, guidance held | ±3% to ±5% |
| **Bear** | ~$5.4B to $5.5B | $8.50 to $9.50 | Revenue down ~5% YoY again, new vehicle margin compresses, SG&A deleverages | -8% to -15% |

**What would need to happen:**
- **Bull:** Management cites stabilizing affordability, improving used margins, and early savings from the expense program. A buyback or raised commentary on the Hennessy deal would reinforce it.
- **Base:** Commentary is cautious but not worse than Q2. Expect "navigating a challenging environment" language and no new guidance cut.
- **Bear:** Management says the affordability headwind is persisting into Q4. Watch for remarks on incentive spend, inventory aging, or a guidance cut.

**Historical reactions:** Per one tracker, the average post-earnings move is about ±5.8%. After the Jul 2026 report, the stock fell about 17% despite a miss. Prior moves: -8.4% (Jan 2026), -6.4% (Oct 2025). Sample sizes are small and sources differ.

**My read:** Consensus appears to assume a much better trend than Q2 showed. If the YoY decline held at Q2's pace, revenue would be roughly $5.5B and adj. EPS well under $10.74. That gap makes the bear case more likely than the bull case on the numbers alone. Positive offsets are the cost program and a possible buyback.
