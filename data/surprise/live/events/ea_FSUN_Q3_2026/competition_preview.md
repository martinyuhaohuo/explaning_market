## 1. The setup in one paragraph

Q3 is the first *reportable* quarter in which FirstSun's First Foundation (FFWM) acquisition can be judged on operating merit rather than on merger accounting. The deal closed April 1, 2026, roughly doubling the bank to $15.7B assets, and Q2 was effectively unanalyzable: a $(0.49) GAAP loss on $57.6M of merger expense and $42.4M of net charge-offs, against $0.45 of adjusted EPS. Management then laid out an unusually specific bridge — NIM "slightly above" the 3.76% June exit rate in Q3, efficiency in the mid-to-low 60s in H2, H2 net charge-offs in the "mid-teens" bps, and 2027 EPS "north of a flat 5." Q3 is the first checkpoint on all four, and the core system conversion was scheduled for late September, inside the quarter. The stock has round-tripped to flat over 52 weeks and sits at 1.12x tangible book with a $45.75 average target, so the print is being asked to validate the bridge, not re-rate the story.

## 3. Key metrics to watch, ranked

1. **Net interest margin vs. the 3.76%+ guide.** The whole 2027 >$5.00 EPS case rests on deposit repricing: brokered deposits were carried at ~4.80%, with $300M maturing in H2 2026 at a 4.77% weighted rate. The complication is macro — the Fed *hiked* 25bp on September 16 to 3.75–4.00%, with a hawkish hold expected October 28 and most participants seeing another increase by year-end. A hiking bias compresses the repricing benefit and raises deposit competition. NIM at or above 3.78% keeps the bridge intact; 3.65–3.70% breaks it.
2. **Credit — net charge-offs and NPA direction.** Q2 NCOs were 1.45% annualized, $35M of which came from two C&I names: a ~$22M fraudulent-borrower loss and a ~$12.9M technology-company deterioration. Management called them isolated and guided H2 to mid-teens bps. NPAs at 1.32% of assets (up from 0.82% in Q1) is the number that must stop rising. A third idiosyncratic C&I loss would move this from "integration noise" to "underwriting question," and the sector is unforgiving on that distinction right now after the Tricolor/First Brands-style nonbank blowups put NDFI and borrower-fraud disclosure at the center of bank earnings calls.
3. **Core conversion execution and the expense run-rate.** 65% of the original $68M cost-save target was already realized at Q2, with the conversion slated for late September — inside this quarter. Watch (a) whether conversion completed on schedule, (b) the adjusted efficiency ratio (mid-to-low 60s is the H2 promise; low-60s is the Q4 promise), and (c) any service-disruption or deposit-attrition commentary.
4. **Loan growth vs. the "low double-digit annualized" H2 guide.** Organic loans *declined* ~6% annualized in Q2. Getting to low double digits is a sharp reversal, made harder by deliberate multifamily runoff (~$100M targeted in H2, $285M in 2027, on top of the $890M performing multifamily portfolio already sold to Brookfield-affiliated entities). Watch gross C&I production versus CRE/multifamily attrition — mix matters more than the net number.
5. **Capital, buyback and TBVPS accretion.** TBVPS fell to $35.16 from $38.57 on deal marks; CET1 11.95%, TCE/TA 10.59%. The $150M repurchase authorization was to be executed over four quarters starting August, so expect ~$35–40M (~1M shares) in Q3. At 1.12x tangible book, buybacks are mildly TBV-dilutive — look for TBVPS to still accrete to roughly $35.8–36.3 on retained earnings.

## 5. Catalyst checklist

- [ ] **Adjusted EPS vs. ~$0.98** — and, equally, whether the company's adjusted definition matches what the sell side modeled. Mis-mapping, not fundamentals, drove the Q2 headline chaos.
- [ ] **NIM ≥ 3.76%** (the June exit rate) with the Q4 "mid-380s" and Q1 2027 "high-380s" path reaffirmed under a *hiking* rather than cutting Fed path.
- [ ] **NCOs in the mid-teens bps and NPAs flat-to-down from 1.32%.** Highest-variance item and the one the market will extrapolate hardest.
- [ ] **Conversion completed on schedule; adjusted efficiency ≤62%** with a credible Q4 step-down. Also watch whether total realized cost saves move above the $68M target, as management hinted.
- [ ] **2027 EPS "north of $5.00" reaffirmed** against $4.66–4.70 consensus. That ~7% gap is the real valuation lever.
- [ ] **Share count / repurchase disclosure** confirming the $150M program is actually being executed.

## 7. How to frame it

The bar is a *bridge*, not a number. Consensus $0.98 is almost exactly what management's own Q2 guidance arithmetic produces, so an in-line print is not informative on its own. The quarter is decided by two things consensus can't model: whether Q2's $35M of C&I losses were genuinely idiosyncratic, and whether the late-September conversion went clean enough to let the Q4/Q1 efficiency step-down happen on schedule. Get both, and the 2027 >$5.00 number becomes defensible against $4.66 consensus, which is the actual re-rating catalyst. Miss either, and a 1.12x TBV multiple on a bank with 1.32% NPAs and a six-month-old integration has room to compress.

**Two risks to this preview itself:** (1) the EPS bar is genuinely uncertain, with vendor estimates spanning $0.89–0.99 and the GAAP/adjusted split unresolved until the release — a narrow beat/miss is not tradable; (2) no usable options market means the ±6% expected move is backward-looking from three data points, not a market-derived number.
