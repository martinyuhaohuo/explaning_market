## Reference Points from Q2 2026

- **Revenue:** $937.7M, +42% YoY. Beat the estimate (sources cite $883M–$901M).
- **Adj. EPS:** $0.08 vs. $0.05–0.06 estimate.
- **Adj. EBITDA:** $172.3M, +20% YoY.
- **GAAP net loss:** $(16.9)M, including $81.2M of one-off IPO and public-readiness costs.
- **Equipment orders:** $2.3B, +316% YoY. Nearly $1.5B came from data centers.
- **Equipment backlog:** $6.6B at 6/30, +279% YoY.
- **Guidance commentary:** Equipment about 65% of 2026 revenue. Margin improvement expected in H2 as capacity ramps. Plan to nearly triple production capacity to 10 GW by 2030, self-funded.

## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Revenue vs. ~$1.04–1.06B.** This is the first test of whether Q2's beat is repeatable. Check whether the H1 run-rate supports FY guide of $3.8–3.9B. Q2 alone was $938M, so Q3 at consensus implies continued sequential growth.
2. **Equipment orders and backlog conversion.** A backlog of $6.6B against a ~$2.3B quarterly order intake is the core bull thesis. Watch the book-to-bill, the data-center mix, and any cancellations or pushouts.
3. **Adj. EBITDA margin and H2 ramp.** Management guided to H2 margin expansion. Any slip in margin would call the ramp into question.
4. **FY26 guidance: raise, reaffirm, or cut.** Given the strong Q2 beat, a raise is plausible but not confirmed. Any reaffirmation alone may read as a negative.
5. **Capacity and capex.** Progress on the 10 GW by 2030 plan, funding (self-funded claim), and any change in capex.
