## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Adj. gross and operating margin vs. Q2 levels.** The stock fell on margin pressure last quarter. Management has already guided Q3 margin slightly lower, so the question is whether the guide holds or worsens.
2. **Core revenue growth vs. the ~4% FY target.** Check whether the raised core growth holds in H2 and by segment (Industrial Technologies vs. Intelligent Operating Solutions, if reported).
3. **Adj. EPS vs. $0.71 and the FY26 range.** Watch for a raise above $3.05 or a reaffirmation at the midpoint.
4. **FCF and buyback pace.** FCF conversion and continued ~$200M/quarter repurchases support the EPS story.
5. **Orders, book-to-bill, and pricing vs. volume**, if disclosed. These are the industrial demand signals behind the core growth guide.
6. **Tariff, FX, and geopolitical commentary.** The company flagged these as FY risks.

## Scenario Analysis

| Scenario | Revenue | Adj. EPS | Key Driver | Stock Reaction (directional) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Bull** | ≥$1.09B | ≥$0.74 | Gross margin stable or up YoY; core growth ≥5%; FY EPS guide raised above $3.05 | Relief rally; the reaction is sized by how much the margin concern is cleared |
| **Base** | $1.06–1.08B | $0.71–0.73 | In-line revenue; margin slightly below Q2 as guided; FY guide reaffirmed or nudged up | Muted to modestly positive, depending on the tone of H2 commentary |
| **Bear** | <$1.05B | ≤$0.69 | Margin compression worse than guided; core growth slows; FY guide held at the low end or cut | Sharp decline; another margin-driven selloff is possible |

**What would signal each case**
- **Bull:** Management says mix headwinds are easing, pricing is holding, and demand is durable into 2027. The FY guide is raised again.
- **Base:** Management reaffirms guidance, says margins are "in line with expectations," and gives no new H2 risks.
- **Bear:** Language shifts from "upper half" to "low end," or management cites weaker orders, tariff costs, or FX drag.

**Historical context:** Last quarter was a beat-and-raise, yet the stock fell about 6.5% on the day, per Investing.com. (Sources disagree on the exact figure; MarketBeat cited roughly 8–9% intraday.) Options had priced a ~2.65% move. The lesson is that the bar is high and margin is the swing factor. I did not find a reliable multi-quarter earnings-reaction history.

## Catalyst Checklist

1. **Adj. gross margin vs. Q2 and vs. the "slightly below" guide.** This matters most, since it drove last quarter's selloff.
2. **FY26 EPS guide: raise, reaffirm, or cut.** The buy side expects at least a reaffirmation at the midpoint, with upside if the beat is sustained.
3. **Core revenue growth vs. the ~4% target.** A miss here would undercut the raised outlook.
4. **Capital allocation.** Buyback pace and any change in leverage or M&A appetite.
5. **Narrative and tariff/FX commentary**, including any changes to the segment structure or portfolio.
