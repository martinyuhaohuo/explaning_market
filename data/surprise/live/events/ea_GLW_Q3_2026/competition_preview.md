## The Setup in One Paragraph

Corning has become an AI-infrastructure proxy, and it is being repriced as one. The stock closed at a record ~$255 on June 29, 2026 and has since lost roughly half its value — down 18.4% in the past month alone — even though the business is executing: Q2 core sales +17% to $4.74B, core EPS +30% to $0.78, core operating margin +190bps to 20.9%. The de-rating is about multiple compression (trailing P/E from ~95x at the peak to ~59x; forward ~29x 2027 consensus), a $2B at-the-market equity authorization filed September 11, and sector-wide anxiety about hyperscaler capex durability. That inversion matters for the setup: the fundamental bar is low (guidance is conservative, consensus sits a hair above the top end), but the *narrative* bar is high. GLW has fallen on each of its last three earnings days — −12.1%, −8.9%, −5.0% — despite beating every time. Eight consecutive EPS beats have not been enough.

## Key Metrics to Watch — Ranked

**1. Does core sales print ≥ $5.0B? (The $20B run-rate question.)**
This is the single most important number. Springboard targets a **$20B annualized run rate by Q4 2026**; on the Q2 call management said growth had improved enough that they "could hit it a quarter early" — i.e., in Q3. A $5.0B+ core sales quarter *is* the $20B run rate, delivered ahead of schedule, and it's the cleanest possible rebuttal to the bear case. Consensus $5.02B already assumes it. Note the uncomfortable detail: Corning has missed consensus revenue in each of the last four quarters while beating on EPS. A $4.95B print is "in guidance" and still a revenue miss.

**2. Q4 guidance and the shape of the 2027 ramp.**
More important than the Q3 print. Q4 consensus is ~$5.02B / $0.88 — which, taken literally, implies no sequential growth, so the Street's Q4 model is likely stale and will be reset by whatever management guides. Springboard calls for a **19% sales CAGR from Q4'26 to Q4'30** ($30B run rate by end-2028, $40B by end-2030). Watch for any softening of the "orders are accelerating" language, or any reframing of the timing of optical scale-up in AI factories — management explicitly flagged on the Q2 call that the *timing* of new optical technology penetration is hard to predict. That caveat is the bear case's entry point.

**3. Optical Communications growth rate and mix — the 30% line.**
OC was $2.072B (+32%) with net income +77% in Q2; Enterprise +65%, Carrier only +1%. The Street needs ~$2.23B (+35%) and sustained 30%+ growth to support the valuation. Two sub-questions: (a) is Carrier finally inflecting, given the Verizon agreement (80M+ fiber miles, 2027–2032) and AT&T's $3B+ deal — these are mostly 2027 revenue, so Carrier may stay soft; (b) how much of Enterprise growth is Gen-AI product (higher margin) vs. commodity fiber.

**4. Margins through the capacity ramp.**
Core GM 39.6% / core OM 20.9% in Q2. Corning is building three new Nvidia-dedicated plants (North Carolina, Texas) and expanding US optical connectivity capacity tenfold. Start-up costs, plus rising helium and energy costs, are the realistic path to a margin disappointment that EPS can't absorb. Also watch **Solar**: a $7M loss in Q2 on +90% sales, with management guiding to improvement in Q3. A Solar breakeven is a tidy, underappreciated EPS tailwind.

**5. Capital structure and the dilution overhang.**
The $2B ATM (announced Sept 11, ~2% of market cap) triggered a 14% drop and remains an open wound at a halved share price. Questions to expect: how much has been drawn, and at what prices? Separately, the Nvidia partnership carries a right to invest up to $3.2B in Corning — clarity on whether that equity comes in, and on what terms, would substantially reduce the need for ATM issuance. Any hint of an *upsized* or accelerated offering is the worst outcome on the tape.

**6. The non-AI drags.** Handheld units guided down mid-teens for the full year on memory-price-driven device pricing; global vehicle sales −2%; Life Sciences still loss-making. These are ~40% of revenue and the reason total growth is 17% while OC is 32%.

| **Bear** (25%) | $4.88–4.95B | $0.85–0.87 | ≤$5.10B / ≤$0.91 | Revenue misses for a 5th straight quarter; OC decelerates toward high-20s; start-up costs/helium compress GM; order *timing* caveats expand; incremental equity issuance | **−11% to −19%** |

**What would need to happen operationally.** Bull requires Enterprise/Gen-AI optical to keep compounding at 60%+ *and* either Carrier to inflect early or Solar to stop bleeding — the beat has to come from volume, not cost. Bear doesn't require demand destruction; it only requires *timing slippage*, which management has already pre-flagged as unpredictable. That asymmetry — a bear case that needs no fundamental break — is why the distribution is wider to the downside than the implied move suggests.

**What management commentary signals which.** Bull tell: naming the $20B run rate as achieved, plus a quantified 2027 capacity-sold-out statement. Bear tell: any shift from "orders are accelerating" to "customers are optimizing deployment schedules," or deferring the $30B/2028 milestone discussion.

**Historical calibration.** Three consecutive post-earnings declines on three consecutive beats (−12.1% in July on light Q3 guidance, −8.9%, −5.0%). The pattern is unambiguous: for GLW, **guidance has been the print**. The July 28 reaction is the template — a clean beat, conservative guide, 12% drawdown, and it led a sector-wide rout in optical names.

## Catalyst Checklist

1. **Core sales ≥$5.0B** vs. consensus $5.02B / guide top-end $5.00B — delivers the $20B run rate early; a sub-$4.95B print makes it five straight revenue misses.
2. **Q4 core sales guide ≥$5.3B** — the buy-side whisper is a Q4 guide that validates the 19% 2027 CAGR; anything starting with a "5.0" or "5.1" reads as deceleration regardless of the Q3 beat.
3. **Optical Communications ≥+35% YoY with core OM ≥21%** — growth *and* margin together; either alone won't hold the stock.
4. **ATM/Nvidia equity disclosure** — shares issued to date and whether the $3.2B Nvidia investment right removes the dilution overhang.
5. **Springboard milestone language** — reaffirmation (or quiet softening) of $30B by end-2028 and $40B by end-2030.
