## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Optical Communications sales and guide.** Q2 was $2.07B (+32%), with Enterprise Networks +65%. This segment drives the story and the stock's sensitivity to AI/data-center optics sentiment.
2. **Q4 guidance vs. consensus.** Q3 guidance was only in line on EPS and slightly light on sales, and it triggered a sharp selloff. The Q4 outlook is likely to matter more than the Q3 print.
3. **Gross and operating margin.** Q2 gross margin was 36.1% and operating margin 15.5%. Watch whether margins hold as Optical volume grows.
4. **Solar segment.** Q2 sales were $438M (+90%) with a $7M net loss. Management guided to better Q3 profitability after a wafer-facility maintenance shutdown and upgrade. Confirm this recovery.
5. **Springboard Plan progress.** The annualized run-rate target is $20B by end-2026, $30B by 2028, $40B by 2030. Any reaffirmation or slip is a narrative signal.
6. **Free cash flow and capital returns.** Q2 operating cash flow was $1.72B. Check whether it supports buybacks and dividends.

**What would signal each:**
- **Bull:** Management raises the Optical outlook, cites new hyperscaler or enterprise wins, and says Solar is back to profit.
- **Base:** "Reaffirm" language, and Optical growth described as steady.
- **Bear:** Pushback on Optical timing, capacity constraints, or customer concentration, plus any Springboard target delay.

**Historical context:** Reactions have swung both ways. Jan 2019 was about +11% on a Q4 beat. Apr 2019 fell on guidance despite a beat. Oct 2025 was roughly −5% and then +5% the next day. Jul 2026 was reported at −14% to −16% premarket on the Q3 outlook, but sources conflict (see below). Guidance and sector sentiment have often mattered more than the headline beat.
