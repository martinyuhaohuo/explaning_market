## 1. The Setup in One Paragraph

Intapp is the single most expectation-loaded name in its peer set right now. The stock is up **~148% off its 52-week low** and sits **~4% below its all-time high**, having run **~40% since the Q4 FY26 print** (from $32.74 pre-print to ~$46). Critically, **sell-side price targets now sit below the share price** — consensus targets cluster $39–$42 against a $46 spot, and the rating mix is a *Hold* (4 buy / 4 hold / 2 sell). That is an unusual configuration: the market has repriced the Celeste "Firm AI" narrative faster than analysts have repriced their models. The practical consequence is **asymmetric downside**: a routine 1–2% revenue beat with an in-line guidance nudge — Intapp's modal outcome for six straight quarters — is already in the price. To move the stock up, management needs to show *monetization*, not just adoption.

## 4. Key Metrics to Watch — Ranked by Stock Impact

**1. Cloud ARR growth rate (highest weight).** The whole bull case. $401.4M → $495.7M over FY26 at a steady **+29–30%**. Q1 needs **≥$518M to hold 29%**. My base is ~$520M. Anything printing a **27-handle** breaks the "durable 30% cloud compounder" story the 40% re-rating was built on, and the stock will not forgive it at 5.1x EV/sales with targets below spot. Note the comp gets no easier from here.

**2. Celeste / AI share of net new bookings.** Ran 15% → >20% across Q3→Q4 FY26. This is the first *full* quarter post-GA (July 15) and the single most important qualitative disclosure. **>25% = thesis validation; 20–22% = stall signal.** Watch for a shift in how it's disclosed — if management stops quoting the percentage, or pivots to logo counts and "pipeline," treat that as a negative tell. Also listen for whether platform fees or *usage* are driving it; usage-based revenue is the proof that firms are actually running Celeste, not just buying seats.

**3. FY27 guidance revision magnitude.** Intapp has beaten EPS in **7 of its last 8 quarters** and habitually raises. With the stock at $46, a mechanical flow-through raise is not enough. The bar is a raise of **≥$4–6M above the current $660.5M high end**, signalling Celeste is additive rather than already embedded.

**4. Cloud NRR.** Pinned at 123% for four quarters. Celeste is the natural expansion vector, so **124–125% would be the cleanest single proof point** that AI is monetizing inside the installed base. A slip to 121–122% directly contradicts the narrative.

**5. Gross margin and operating leverage.** Q1 guide implies **21.2% non-GAAP OI margin vs. 22.5% in Q4 — ~130bps of sequential compression.** Some is normal Q1 seasonality (payroll resets, sales kickoff), but Celeste inference/token costs run through COGS against a 75–76% gross margin. **Watch gross margin specifically.** If it compresses more than ~50bps, the "AI is margin-accretive" claim weakens and the FY27 +200bps expansion guide comes into question. Management has pre-framed this with a model-agnostic, architecture-driven cost-mitigation story — see whether the numbers back it.

**6. $1M+ client cohort.** 142 at year-end, +30% YoY. Look for **148+**. This cohort is the best large-deal and pricing-power proxy.

**7. License revenue trajectory.** −25% in Q4. FY27 guidance embeds license + services falling ~17% ($155.0M → ~$127.8M). This is *designed* shrinkage that funds cloud conversion, but it's why total revenue grows 14% while cloud ARR grows 29%. **If license declines faster than planned, total revenue misses even on a cloud beat** — the most likely mechanism for an "optically bad, structurally fine" print. Also watch RPO: management already flagged on-prem RPO as a headwind from end-of-life migration dynamics, so a weak headline RPO number needs to be read net of that.

## 5. Scenario Analysis

| Scenario | Prob. | Total Rev | Non-GAAP EPS | Cloud ARR | Key Driver | Stock Reaction |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Bull** | 30% | $164.5M (+18.3%) | $0.49 | ~$526M (+31%) | Celeste >28% of net new bookings; NRR to 124–125%; FY27 revenue raised to ~$668M+, EPS to $1.70+ | **+8% to +15%** |
| **Base** | 45% | $162.0M (+16.5%) | $0.45 | ~$520M (+29.5%) | Standard ~1.5% beat; Celeste ~23–25% of bookings; FY27 nudged to $662–665M / $1.63–1.67 | **−5% to +4%** |
| **Bear** | 25% | $159.5M (+14.7%) | $0.40 | ~$512M (+27.5%) | Cloud ARR decelerates to a 27-handle; license drag worse than planned; FY27 merely reaffirmed; gross margin down on inference costs | **−15% to −25%** |

### Bull — what must happen
Cloud ARR adds **~$30M sequentially** (vs. ~$25M typical) — Celeste pulling net-new logos *and* expansion simultaneously. Operationally: usage-based Celeste revenue material enough for management to quantify it; several of the 30+ signed migrations converting to live cloud ARR early; the $1M+ cohort jumping to 150+. **Management tell:** a *specific, quantified* Celeste contribution — ARR dollars, attach rate, or usage revenue — rather than a bookings percentage. Giving a Celeste ARR number for the first time would be the strongest possible signal. Also bullish: raising the FY27 **margin** guide alongside revenue, which would kill the inference-cost bear argument outright.

### Base — what must happen
The six-quarter pattern repeats: ~1.5–2% revenue beat, $0.04–0.05 EPS beat, cloud ARR +29%, NRR 123%, Celeste tracking "ahead of plan" qualitatively, guidance raised by roughly the beat. **Management tell:** Celeste framed as "early innings" with FY28 as the monetization year. This is the trap in the current setup — *a genuinely good quarter that does nothing for the stock*, because targets at $39–42 imply the sell side thinks fair value is 10–15% lower. Expect an initial pop that fades, mirroring Q3 FY26 (solid beat, raised guide, **+0.7% after hours**).

### Bear — what must happen
Two credible paths. **(a) Deceleration:** cloud ARR +27–28%, NRR 121–122%, Celeste bookings flat ~20%. The read-through is that February's announcement pulled forward demand and GA didn't broaden the funnel. **(b) Mix/margin:** cloud fine but license falls off faster than the plan, total revenue lands at the guide low end, *and* gross margin compresses on Celeste inference. Path (b) is the sneakier risk — it's structurally benign but reads as a miss on the headline, and at this valuation the market sells first. **Management tell:** vaguer Celeste disclosure, "lumpy enterprise deal timing," or any reframing of FY27 as an "investment year." Barclays' Underweight (target raised to $38 on Oct 12 — raised, still ~17% below spot) is the explicit articulation of this case.

### Historical reaction calibration
- **Q4 FY26 (Aug 4, 2026):** Beat ($0.41 vs. $0.36; $152.5M vs. $149.5M) + FY27 guide → **+12.8%** next day, then **+24.4% drift** over the following 84 days. Prints in a range of $34.41–$48.19 post-event.
- **Q3 FY26 (May 2026):** EPS $0.29 vs. $0.23 (+26% surprise), guided accelerating SaaS → **+0.7% after hours.** Essentially no reward.
- **Pattern:** 7 of 8 EPS beats. Reaction is **almost entirely a function of positioning going in, not the beat magnitude.** August worked because the stock was at $32.74 with a depressed multiple. Today's $46 entry resembles the May setup far more than the August one — which is the core reason I weight the bear tail at 25% rather than the ~15% a pure fundamental read would suggest.

**Options-implied move:** not available from public sources — MarketChameleon gates the straddle data behind a subscription, and no other outlet publishes it for INTA. **Pull the ATM straddle on the nearest weekly before the close.** My working estimate from realized history is **~10–13%**, anchored on the +12.8% August move and INTA's typical post-print range expansion.

**Reading the implied move against my scenarios.** My probability-weighted absolute move is roughly **9–10%**. So:
- **Implied ≤9%:** options are *cheap* relative to the distribution. The bear tail (−15% to −25%) is materially fatter than a 9% straddle prices, given the valuation and targets-below-spot configuration. Long volatility — specifically **put spreads or a put-skewed strangle** — is the better expression than directional equity.
- **Implied ≥13%:** options are rich; the base case (−5% to +4%) is the modal outcome and premium sellers get paid. Note that base-case *realized* moves of ~1–5% have happened twice in the last four prints.

**Net view.** Fundamental momentum is genuinely strong — 29% cloud ARR, 123% NRR, 200bps of guided margin expansion, Celeste bookings doubling sequentially, and a clean balance sheet with $162.8M net cash and 25% FCF margins. But **the setup, not the fundamentals, is the binding constraint.** With the stock 4% from its high, at ~28.5x forward earnings, with every published target below spot and estimates unmoved for two months, the risk/reward into the print is unattractive to own outright. The distribution is negatively skewed: the bull case pays +8–15%, the bear case costs −15–25%, and the most likely single outcome (a good quarter, modest raise) pays roughly nothing. **Prefer defined-risk downside or a post-print entry over pre-print length.** For holders, the August precedent cuts both ways — the +24% drift after Q4 shows this stock trends hard on validated narrative, so a confirmed Celeste monetization datapoint is worth chasing *after* the fact rather than anticipating.
