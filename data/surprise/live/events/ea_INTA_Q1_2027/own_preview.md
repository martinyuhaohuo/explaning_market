## Catalyst Checklist

1. **Cloud ARR vs. expectations.** The buy-side will want to see growth hold near 29%. A miss on net new ARR matters more than a revenue beat.
2. **FY27 guide: raise or hold.** The Aug guide already sits above the old consensus. The question is whether the Street has moved up enough. A raise is the bull trigger.
3. **EPS quality and margins.** Check whether the EPS beat comes from operating leverage or from one-time items.
