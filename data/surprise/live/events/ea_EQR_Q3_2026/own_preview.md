## Recent Context

- **Q2 2026 (reported July 22):** Normalized FFO $1.02, up 3% y/y, at the top of the $0.98–$1.02 guide. GAAP EPS $0.30. Same-store revenue up 1.9% to ~$748M. Revenue missed consensus. [Chartmill](https://www.chartmill.com/news/EQR/Chartmill-51397-Equity-Residential-NYSEEQR-Reports-Mixed-Q2-Results-Strong-NFFO-Beat-Overshadowed-by-Revenue-Miss-and-Merger-Uncertainty)
- **Same-store guidance raised:** Revenue growth to 2.1–2.7% (from 1.2–3.2%), NOI growth to 1.5–2.1% (from 0.5–2.5%). Physical occupancy trimmed to 96.3% (from 96.4%). [Webull](https://www.webull.com.au/news-detail/15417821242434560)
- **Merger:** All-stock merger of equals with AvalonBay Communities, announced May 21, 2026. This is the dominant narrative and the reason guidance is absent.
- **Beat history:** Recent quarters have mostly beaten EPS estimates, though bases differ across sources. [Public.com](https://public.com/stocks/eqr/earnings)

## What to Watch (ranked)

1. **Normalized FFO vs. ~$1.03.** A beat has been the pattern. A meet or miss would matter more now that there is no formal guide.
2. **Same-store revenue and NOI trajectory.** Does the raised full-year revenue range (2.1–2.7%) hold? Watch the implied Q4 deceleration or acceleration.
3. **Physical occupancy and blended lease rate spreads.** Occupancy was trimmed, so any further softness is a negative signal.
4. **Merger status.** Timing, shareholder votes, regulatory steps, exchange ratio, and any break-risk language. This could override the quarterly numbers.
5. **Revenue vs. ~$794M.** Q2 revenue missed, so a repeat would draw attention.

1. **FFO vs. ~$1.03 consensus.** The beat cadence is the main signal. Buy-side whisper is likely near or slightly above consensus, but I did not find a verified whisper number.
2. **Guidance language.** With the FY guide withdrawn, buy-side will look for qualitative reaffirmation of the same-store revenue range.
3. **Occupancy and blended spreads.** Trimmed occupancy guidance makes this the sharpest operational check.
4. **Merger timeline and terms.** Any change in expected close or exchange ratio dominates.
5. **Capital allocation and buybacks.** Restraint during a pending merger is expected. Any change is a narrative shift.
