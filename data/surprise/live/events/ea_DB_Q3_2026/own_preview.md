2. **Cost/income ratio:** H1 was 60.9% (one source says 61%); Q2 was 63.0%. Watch progress toward the sub-60% 2028 target against the €21B cost guide.
3. **NII trajectory:** Q2 NII was ~€3.6B. Any further raise to the ~€14B guide is a positive signal.
4. **EPS vs. consensus:** Benzinga shows a Q2 EPS miss ($0.66 vs. $0.91), but another source reports €0.84 with a large YoY gain. The source conflict makes the beat/miss read unreliable.
5. **Capital return and CET1:** Q2 announced a €500M buyback. Look for CET1 at or above the 13.5–14.0% year-end range and any change to capital return.
