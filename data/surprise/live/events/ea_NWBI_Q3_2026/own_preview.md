## Recent Context (Q2 FY2026, reported July 27, 2026)

- GAAP net income: $54M, or $0.36 per diluted share. Adjusted EPS: $0.37, versus a $0.34 estimate.
- NIM: 3.75%, the fourth consecutive quarter of expansion.
- Loans: up $174M to $13.2B. Annualized net charge-offs: 0.15%.
- **2026 guidance:**
  - NIM of 3.73%–3.75%, assuming a stable Fed funds rate
  - Low-to-mid single-digit loan growth
  - Low single-digit deposit growth
  - Noninterest expense of $420–430M, excluding merger costs
  - Tax rate of 23%–24%

## Key Metrics to Watch (Bank Framework, Ranked)

1. **NIM vs. the 3.73%–3.75% guide.** The main driver of the quarter. Holding or beating it supports a reaffirmation or raise.
2. **Credit quality.** Net charge-offs (Q2: 0.15%), provision expense, and any migration in criticals or nonaccruals.
3. **Loan and deposit growth.** Confirm the low-to-mid single-digit pace is intact. Watch deposit costs, since the deposit beta drives NIM.
4. **Noninterest expense vs. the $420–430M guide**, and whether merger-related costs are excluded consistently.
5. **Fee income** and the tax rate (23%–24% guide).
6. **Capital and buybacks** and whether the 2026 outlook is reaffirmed, raised, or cut.

## Catalyst Checklist

1. **NIM vs. 3.73%–3.75% guide.** Stock-moving if it lands above 3.78% or falls below 3.70%.
2. **2026 guidance reaffirmation or raise** on NIM, loan growth, and expenses. The buy-side expects a reaffirmation at minimum after four quarters of NIM expansion.
3. **Credit trends.** Net charge-offs, nonaccruals, and provision. Any surprise here outweighs a small EPS beat.
4. **Deposit costs and beta.** The main risk to the NIM story.
5. **Capital return and M&A.** Management's posture on buybacks and any acquisition or merger-cost commentary. The guide excludes merger costs, so any new deal would reset the expense outlook.
