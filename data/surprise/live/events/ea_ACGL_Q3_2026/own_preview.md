2. **Operating EPS vs. consensus.** Settle the basis question first.

4. **Book value per share growth.** Compare with the $68.04 Q2 level. Buybacks reduce share count, so the book value trend is a cleaner signal than EPS.

6. **Capital deployment.** Pace of buybacks and any commentary on excess capital.
