## 2. Context from Q2 2026 (reported 29 Jul 2026)

- **Net profit:** ~$2.8B. PBT $3.6B; underlying PBT $3.9B.
- **Return on CET1:** 15.4% reported, 16.4% underlying. The 2026 exit-rate target is ~15%, and management says it is well positioned to beat it.
- **CET1 ratio:** 14.4% (guidance ~14%). Leverage ratio 4.4% (guidance >4.0%).
- **Capital return:** New $3B buyback to finish by end Q2 2027. At least $1B to be repurchased in the next three months. Dividend growth accrual in the mid-teens. A $3.0B reserve for future buybacks cut CET1 capital by $0.8B in the quarter.
- **Guidance:** Cost/income exit-rate below 70%. CHF 750M integration costs in H2 2026. GWM net interest income growth of ~10% for 2026.
- **Open item:** Swiss Parliament's treatment of capitalizing foreign subsidiaries. This could set the pace of buybacks.

Sources: [UBS 2Q26 media release](https://www.ubs.com/content/dam/assets/cc/investor-relations/quarterlies/2026/2q26/2q26-media-release-en.pdf), [UBS 2Q26 results page](https://www.ubs.com/global/en/media/display-page-ndp/en-20260729-2q26-quarterly-result.html), [Wealth Briefing Asia](https://wealthbriefingasia.com/printarticle.php?id=208385).

## 3. Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Return on CET1 and PBT vs. the ~15% exit-rate target.** This is the headline for the 2026 target. Watch whether management says it is on track to beat it.
2. **Capital return pace.** Buyback execution against the $1B / 3-month commitment and any change to the dividend or buyback size, especially if the Swiss capital debate moves.
3. **Cost/income ratio vs. <70% exit target.** Check whether the H2 integration costs (CHF 750M) change the path.
4. **Global Wealth Management.** Net new fee-generating assets, net interest income (NII growth ~10% guided), and deposit trends.
5. **Investment Bank.** Fee and trading revenue vs. the peer group. Management will comment on the IB pipeline into Q4.
6. **CET1 ratio vs. ~14% guidance.** Any drift below guidance would be a negative surprise.
7. **Swiss regulatory update.** Commentary on capitalizing foreign subsidiaries and the timing of its resolution.
