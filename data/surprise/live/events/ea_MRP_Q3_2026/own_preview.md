## Recent Results

- **Q2 2026 (reported Aug 4):** Revenue $196.9M against a ~$204.7M estimate, a miss of about 4%. EPS $0.76, roughly in line with $0.75. AFFO $0.77/share, with the quarterly run rate at $0.80, the "high end of guidance."
- **Q2 detail:** Option fees $195.4M. Total homesites under option $9.7B. Total homesites 143,771 across 877 communities in 30 states. Portfolio yield 9.2%. Invested capital $8.8B, including $5.9B under the Lennar Master Program and $2.8B under other agreements.
- **Dividend:** $0.77/share, the sixth consecutive increase.
- **Q1 2026:** One source reports EPS of $2.44 on $600.5M revenue. That is implausible against the Q2 run rate, so I did not use it. Verify against the 10-Q.

## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **AFFO/share vs. ~$0.77–0.80:** Is the run rate sustained at the high end?
2. **Revenue vs. consensus:** Q2 missed. Whether option fees and development loan income recover is the main question.
3. **Homesite sales and net cash proceeds:** Q2 net proceeds were $1.0B. Pace shows whether the Lennar-linked model is converting.
4. **Invested capital growth and yield:** Watch whether the 9.2% portfolio yield holds as capital outside Lennar grows.
5. **Full-year guidance and dividend:** Q2 gave no explicit Q3 or FY AFFO guidance. Any new explicit range will matter more than the quarter itself.
