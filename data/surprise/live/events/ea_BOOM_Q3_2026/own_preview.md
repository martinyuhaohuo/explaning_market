## Prior Quarter (Q2 FY2026, reported Jul 29, 2026)
- Sales $157.0M, flat year over year, up 16% sequentially. Above the $148M–$158M guide, and above the ~$149M consensus cited by one source.
- Adj. EBITDA attributable to DMC $10.7M, down 21% year over year, up 174% sequentially.
- EPS sources conflict: $0.10 diluted GAAP (net income $0.5M) per one summary, versus $0.04 adjusted per others.
- A $1.5M one-time tariff refund boosted DynaEnergetics margins in Q2 and is excluded from Q3 guidance. Expect a margin optic to look worse sequentially even if operations are stable.

## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)
1. **Revenue vs. $158M guidance low end:** Both the guide and the consensus sit near here, so a miss of the low end would be a clear negative.
2. **Adj. EBITDA vs. $10M–$13M:** Q2's $10.7M was flat-to-down year over year. Margin direction matters more than the headline.
3. **DynaEnergetics pricing and mix:** Management cited price pressure and unfavorable mix in Q2. Any improvement or further erosion will be the key segment signal.
4. **Arcadia:** Management called it the Q2 bright spot. Watch whether growth carried into Q3.
5. **Aluminum input costs:** Management flagged volatility. Watch the commentary on pass-through and margin protection.
6. **Q4 / full-year commentary:** I did not find a full-year 2026 outlook in the Q2 materials, so any Q4 guidance will be new information.
