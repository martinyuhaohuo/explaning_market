## Recent Context

- **Q3 FY26 (reported Jul 1):** EPS $0.60 on net earnings of $19M. Revenue about $577M. Gross margin 14.1%, up 230 bps sequentially. Deliveries 3,600 units, orders 2,200 units. Backlog 13,800 units (~$2.0B). Owned lease fleet 20,600 units, 99% utilization.
- **Guidance cut at Q3:** Aggregate gross margin lowered to 13.8–14.2% (from 14.8–15.2%). EPS narrowed to $3.00–3.15 (from $3.00–3.50). Deliveries narrowed to the 15,650–15,850 range, including about 1,500 units tied to Brazil.
- **Demand:** Management is assessing deferred North American railcar demand. Some manufacturing activity is shifting into FY27, with a positive 2027 pipeline cited.

## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Manufacturing gross margin and FY26 margin landing.** The 13.8–14.2% range is the main test. Q4 margin is the swing factor for whether the FY EPS range holds.
2. **FY26 EPS within $3.00–3.15.** Q4 EPS must be compared with the FY range. Compute the implied Q4 from the company's Q1–Q3 EPS. I did not verify those quarterly figures, so do this before the call.
3. **Q4 deliveries and orders.** Check whether deliveries hit the FY range (15,650–15,850). Orders and backlog trend (~13,800 units) point to FY27 volumes.
4. **FY27 commentary.** Any early outlook on deliveries, margin, and the shift of activity from FY26 into FY27. This matters more than the Q4 print for the stock.
5. **Leasing and capital allocation.** Lease fleet growth, utilization (99%), lease-rate trends, and any buyback or dividend change.

**What would signal each case**
- **Bull:** "Margin above guidance," "deferred demand converting," and a firm FY27 delivery outlook.
- **Base:** Reaffirmed FY ranges, "continued engagement with customers," and no new timing caveats.
- **Bear:** Words like "further deferrals," "softer pricing," or "Brazil timing risk," plus a lower FY27 framing.
