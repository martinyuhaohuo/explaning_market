## The Setup in One Paragraph

This print has an unusually narrow surprise surface, and that is the whole point. Greenbrier **already pre-announced Q4 orders** on Sep 22 (3,400 units / ~$600M), so the order number is known. Management's FY26 guidance brackets Q4 revenue, deliveries, and EPS tightly. What is *not* known — and what will set the stock — is **FY2027 guidance**, issued by a CEO who hands over the job ten weeks later. The stock is at a 52-week low ($37.02 low vs. $38.23 now), down ~35% from its $59.19 high, on 11.4x forward earnings with a 3.6% yield, after **two guidance cuts in FY26** and a sell-side consensus of Sell/Reduce. Expectations are washed out; the risk is an FY27 reset rather than a Q4 miss.

## Key Metrics to Watch — Ranked

**1. FY2027 guidance (deliveries, revenue, EPS).** The only genuinely unknown number. Management explicitly declined to guide FY27 on the Q3 call while noting visibility into "the first part of the next fiscal year." The 11.4x forward multiple implies a market FY27 EPS near **~$3.35**. A guide starting with a 3 is survivable; a 2-handle re-rates the stock.

**2. Q4 aggregate gross margin.** The dominant EPS lever. At ~$605M revenue, **each 100bps of aggregate gross margin ≈ $0.145 of EPS** (~$0.07 per 50bps). By contrast, a 200-unit delivery swing is worth only ~$0.08. Margin, not volume, decides the headline.

**3. Deliveries — did the deferred cars actually ship?** The entire FY26 story was deliveries sliding right "into early fiscal 2027" on order *timing*, not demand. Hitting 3,850–4,050 (vs. 3,600 in Q3) validates the ramp; another slip makes "deferred" look like "cancelled by another name" and puts the FY27 guide in question before it is even digested.

**4. Backlog and book-to-bill.** With orders known, focus on the backlog print. My bridge: 13,800 units + 3,400 orders − ~3,950 deliveries ≈ **13,150–13,350 units, ~$2.0B** — flat in dollars, down ~20% in units from 16,600 a year ago. Q4 book-to-bill ≈ **0.86x**, the best of FY26 (Q1 0.84x, Q2 0.76x, Q3 0.61x) but a **fourth straight quarter below 1.0x**; FY26 ≈ 0.77x. *Price is the offset:* Q4 orders priced at ~$176K/car vs. ~$155K in Q3 and ~$145K for the existing backlog — richer tank-car and international mix (780 units for Saudi Railway, including Greenbrier's first intermodal sale to SAR). Backlog *value* holding at $2.0B on fewer units is the bull's best data point.

**5. Leasing & Fleet Management — and the leverage behind it.** The owned fleet jumped 23% sequentially to 20,600 units at 99% utilization, with $285M of FY26 leasing capex and fleet leverage at 85%. Leasing ran a 60.3% gross margin and $29.2M of operating earnings in Q3 — nearly matching Manufacturing's $30.4M on one-eleventh the revenue. Watch whether recurring leasing earnings keep backfilling manufacturing weakness, and at what cost: **total debt was $1,805.6M against $273.7M cash and a $1.18B market cap.** Net debt exceeds the equity value, so the stock is a levered call on the railcar cycle. The split between recourse and non-recourse (a new $425M non-recourse term loan) matters.

**6. CEO transition.** Lorie Tekorius retires Jan 6, 2027; Brian Comstock (EVP, President–Americas) succeeds. An outgoing CEO issuing the FY27 guide creates two-sided risk: a conservative hand-off number, or a kitchen-sink reset. Any strategic reframing — further European rationalization, lease-fleet monetization, capital-allocation change — lands here. Watch also for the 50th consecutive quarterly dividend ($0.34).

## Scenario Analysis

| Scenario | Revenue | EPS | Key Driver | Stock Reaction |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Bull** (~25%) | $630M+ | $0.95+ | Full delivery ramp (4,000+), Q4 agg. GM ≥16%, FY27 EPS guide ≥$3.50 and deliveries ≥17,000 | **+10% to +16%** ($42–44) |
| **Base** (~45%) | $590–615M | $0.82–0.90 | Deliveries ~3,850–3,950, agg. GM 14.5–15.5%, FY26 EPS lands in guide; FY27 guided wide/cautiously (~$3.00–3.60) or deferred | **−3% to +5%** ($37–40) |
| **Bear** (~30%) | <$560M | <$0.75 | Deliveries slip again, agg. GM ≤14%, FY26 EPS below $3.00; FY27 EPS guide with a 2-handle or no guide at all | **−10% to −18%** ($31–34) |

**Bull — what must happen:** The deferred FY26 cars ship, and Q4 mix carries the $176K/car pricing into margin. Commentary signal: management quantifies FY27 deliveries *above* FY26 and frames 2027–28 industry replacement demand (third-party forecasts point to ~30,000 industry cars in 2027 and 40,000+ in 2028 versus 20,000–23,000 in 2026) as contracted rather than hoped-for. A backlog *value* print above $2.1B would confirm pricing is more than offsetting unit decline. At 52-week lows with a Sell-skewed sell-side, positioning is the fuel here.

**Bear — what must happen:** A third consecutive guidance disappointment. Commentary signal: "continued customer deliberation," another timing shift, or an FY27 range so wide it concedes no visibility. The tell would be backlog units falling below ~13,000 alongside a sub-14% Q4 margin — that combination says the ramp was never real and FY27 absorption gets worse, not better. Given net debt above market cap, multiple compression is amplified in the equity.

**Historical calibration:** reactions have been muted on the day and punishing afterward. Q3 (Jul 1, 2026) — EPS in line at $0.60, revenue missed by 5.9% — closed **−0.7%** the next day with a −5.2% max intraday swing, then **drifted −19.3% over the following 117 days**. Across the last 18 reports, the average earnings-day move is roughly +0.4%, −0.2% at five days and +1.7% at 30 days, with only ~43% positive. OptionSlam scores GBX's earnings volatility at 4.5/10. **Translation: the day-one move is usually smaller than the options market prices, and the durable damage comes from the guidance trajectory, not the headline.** That argues against paying up for a one-day event and in favor of expressing the view on the FY27 guide.

## Catalyst Checklist

1. **FY2027 EPS/delivery guidance vs. the ~$3.35 implied forward number** — the single determinant of the move. Buy-side needs deliveries ≥ FY26 and an EPS floor at/above $3.00.
2. **Q4 aggregate gross margin vs. ~15%** — the guidance-implied bar; worth $0.145 of EPS per 100bps and the proof point on whether cost actions are structural or mix-driven.
3. **Deliveries vs. 3,850–4,050** — the credibility test on "deferred, not lost."
4. **Backlog vs. ~13,200 units / ~$2.0B, and $/car** — flat dollars on fewer units at higher prices is the bull case; sub-$2.0B value breaks it.
5. **Leasing trajectory and fleet leverage (85%)** — how much recurring earnings offset, and whether the balance sheet supports another year of $285M leasing capex.
6. **Comstock's framing** — any strategic reset, portfolio action, or capital-allocation change from the incoming CEO.
