## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Q3 revenue vs. the $935–945M guide and vs. consensus.** The Q2 revenue cut and the weak Q3 guide are the main concern. Consensus at $970M looks stale against guidance, so the gap to the true whisper matters.
2. **Full-year revenue guide ($3.715–3.755B).** Does management hold, cut again, or narrow? Another cut would confirm the trend.
3. **Adj. EBITDA margin.** The $190–210M Q3 range implies a margin in the low 20s on guided revenue. Hitting the top half would support the "profitability inflection" narrative.
4. **Homes.com and Apartments.com trends.** Watch the Homes.com sales-force changes and Apartments.com pricing discipline cited as revenue drags.
5. **Ten-X restructuring.** Check the cost and timing of the restructuring and whether it drives further revenue impact.
6. **Buyback pace.** Progress against the $700M target.
