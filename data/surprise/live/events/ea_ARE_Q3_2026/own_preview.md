## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Operating occupancy vs. guidance.** Was 86.9% at June 30. Year-end guide is 86.2%–87.8%. The low end of the range is the key risk.
2. **FFO/share vs. $1.52 and the H2 implied run-rate.** Any change to the FY range is the main stock driver.
3. **Same-property NOI.** Fell 10.6% in Q2 and 11.5% in 1H. Watch whether the decline is narrowing as lease expirations roll through.
4. **Leasing volume and rent spreads.** Q2 leasing was 1.04M RSF, up 60% from Q1. Sustaining that pace supports the occupancy recovery.
5. **Asset sales and capital plan.** About $2.9B of dispositions planned, with no common equity issuance assumed in 2026. Progress on sales, pricing, and any dividend commentary matters.
6. **Impairments.** Q2 real estate impairments were $222.5M. Additional charges would hit GAAP results, though FFO excludes them.
7. **Liquidity.** $3.60B at June 30.
