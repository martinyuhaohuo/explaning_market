## 2. Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **FY26 FFO guidance action** — the single biggest driver. On Aug 3, ARE *beat* on revenue and FFO ($1.73 vs. estimates) and the stock still fell 4.1% intraday and ~7.8% the next session purely on the guidance narrowing. The print matters less than the Q4 bridge.
2. **Net debt / Adjusted EBITDA vs. the 5.6–6.2x 4Q26 target** — 2Q26 ran **7.0x annualized**. Closing that gap in two quarters depends entirely on disposition proceeds landing. A 3Q print still near 6.8–7.0x alongside a maintained target strains credibility; investment-grade rating headlines are the tail risk.
3. **Disposition execution** — $2.9B target; 46% complete/pending and 38% "in process" as of August, with September closings expected. Slippage breaks the deleveraging math *and* the ~$3.15B capex plan funding through 2027. The Long Island City sale closed at a steep discount to its 2018 cost — pricing marks matter as much as volume.
4. **Occupancy and the SLNYO bridge** — 2Q26 operating occupancy 86.9%, or 90.9% including signed-leases-not-yet-occupied. Only ~64% of that 1.4M RSF was expected to commence by year-end, averaging **November** — so 3Q gets little benefit while ~450k RSF of 2H26 expirations bite. Street at 85.9–86.1%.
5. **Leasing volume and renewal spreads** — management guided ~**950k RSF** for 3Q (vs. 1.039M RSF in 2Q, +60% q/q). Renewal spreads were **−7.4% straight-line / −9.6% cash** in 2Q against full-year guidance of −9.0% to −1.0%. Watch tenant mix: public biotech was just **6% of 2Q leasing** despite being 21% of annual rent, with life science tools/services (~40%) and advanced technology (~30%) filling the gap.
6. **Fresh impairments** — $222.5M in 2Q, concentrated in San Diego submarkets outside Torrey Pines/UTC. Another large charge signals the mark-down cycle isn't finished.
7. **2027 pre-commentary** — formal 2027 guidance is deferred to the **Dec 2, 2026 Investor Day**, but any directional framing on the 1.4M RSF / **$100.5M of expiring rent** with downtime already extended to **12–24 months** (from 6–18) will move the stock.

**Sector backdrop:** national life science vacancy ~24%, with some major markets above 30% after the speculative build cycle. ARE recently canceled a Boston office-to-lab conversion citing vacancy. Management's counter-argument is Megacampus outperformance — 80% of annual rent, 8–12 points of occupancy outperformance vs. market, and 1.7x/2.0x market-share leasing in San Diego and the Bay Area respectively. Development pipeline: 1.4M RSF under construction, 71% leased.

## 4. Catalyst Checklist

1. **FY26 FFO guidance vs. the $6.40 midpoint** — a cut is the clearest path to a double-digit drawdown; the Aug 3 reaction proved guidance dominates the print even on a beat.
2. **4Q26 net debt/EBITDA reaffirmation (5.6–6.2x) plus disposition closings** — the bridge from 7.0x is the credibility test for the entire deleveraging story.
3. **Occupancy trough call** — the buy side wants management to say occupancy bottoms in 3Q/4Q26 as the 1.4M RSF SLNYO backlog commences; a "the bottom is later than we thought" admission is the bear trigger.
4. **Dividend durability** — $2.88 is ~45% of FFO but far higher against AFFO after heavy TI and lease-up capex (payout screens near 2.0x on some cash measures). Any ambiguity reopens the cut debate five weeks before Investor Day, and ARE has already cut once (Dec 3, 2025, $1.32 → $0.72).
5. **2027 framing / Dec 2 Investor Day setup** — BMO models 2027 core FFO **−26% YoY** with possible further erosion in 2028. If management declines to push back, the stock trades off the 2027 number, not the 2026 one.
