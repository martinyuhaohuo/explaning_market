## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Q3 EPS vs. ~$0.96–1.00.** Management flagged roughly a $0.03/share headwind in the September quarter from the U.S. Value team wind-down. Check whether consensus already reflects it. If it doesn't, the EPS number could look like a miss even with an in-line business.
2. **Net flows and AUM.** Q2 ended at about $183.4B AUM after $10.5B of net outflows, with $6.4B from U.S. value and $2.8B from growth. Watch for continued equity outflows and whether credit and alternatives inflows hold.
3. **Management fee rate and performance fees.** These drive revenue beyond AUM levels.
4. **Expense guidance.** Full-year fixed expense growth is guided to mid-single digits, excluding Grandview and long-term incentive compensation. A reaffirmation is the base case. Any increase would be negative.
5. **Capital return.** The dividend and buyback pace matter for a stock that has been weak.

## Catalyst Checklist

1. **EPS vs. $0.96–$1.00 after the ~$0.03 wind-down hit:** This determines whether the beat/miss headline is positive or negative.
2. **Net outflows vs. the Q2 pace (−$10.5B):** A smaller outflow would be the clearest positive.
3. **Expense guidance reaffirmation:** The buy-side expects mid-single-digit growth to hold.

5. **Value team wind-down scope:** Confirm the remaining AUM and fee impact.
