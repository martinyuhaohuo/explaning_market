## What to watch (ranked)

1. **RASM growth vs. the +12.5% to +16.5% guide.** This is the core revenue driver. The range is wide and high, so the print will show whether revenue management is delivering.
2. **CASM ex-fuel vs. the +2.5% to +4.5% guide.** Cost control is the main margin lever.
3. **Fuel vs. $3.49/gal.** Brent has moved since the July 10 curve, and fuel is a large swing factor for the loss.
4. **Q4 and FY guidance changes.** Watch the FY operating margin range (-2% to -5%) and whether the 2028 EPS target holds.
5. **Fleet and engines.** Aircraft are out of service due to Pratt & Whitney groundings. Watch for delivery timing (12 A220s in 2026, 6 delivered by June 30) and any capacity impact.
6. **Liquidity and cash burn.** Capex of ~$850M for 2026 is significant against a net loss of $247M in Q2. Liquidity figures were not in the investor update, so check the release.
