**Quality-of-earnings flags:**
- About $241K of Q2 interest income was non-recurring, from bond calls and acceleration of a purchased credit-deteriorated loan mark. That is roughly 3–4 bp of NIM, so the underlying Q2 margin is about 3.86%.
- Management attributed much of the margin gain to lower deposit costs.
- Q2 charge-off disclosures conflict between presentation versions (~$1.4M vs ~$0.7M related to one customer bankruptcy). Check the Q3 10-Q for the final figure.

## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Net interest margin.** Does it hold near 3.85–3.90% after the Q2 one-time items fall away? A drop toward 3.8% would signal that the deposit-cost tailwind is fading.
2. **EPS vs. consensus.** Q2 was $0.86. Anything in the high-$0.80s is roughly in line; a clear beat needs well above $0.90.
3. **Net interest income and loan growth.** Check balance-sheet growth alongside margin, since margin gains alone can mask flat earning assets.
4. **Credit quality.** Watch the provision, net charge-offs, and nonperforming loans, especially the bankruptcy-related losses.
5. **Noninterest income and expense.** Track fee income and efficiency ratio trends.
6. **Capital and buybacks.** Any capital return commentary matters for a stock trading on earnings quality.
