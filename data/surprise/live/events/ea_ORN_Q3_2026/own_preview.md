- Opportunity pipeline: about $27B

## Key metrics to watch (ranked)

1. **Marine revenue and margin recovery.** Q2 was the weak spot. Watch for project start-up timing and equipment utilization. Nearly 90% of Marine work is reportedly under contract.
2. **Q3 revenue vs. $246M.** Q2 missed slightly, so a second miss would raise questions about the back-half ramp needed to hit the $900–950M range.
3. **Adjusted EBITDA vs. the $50–54M full-year range.** Confirm whether management holds the range or narrows it.
4. **Concrete growth durability.** Strong growth is in the segment carrying the most momentum.
5. **Backlog and new awards.** Watch Marine backlog ($554M) and whether book-to-bill stays above 1.0x.
6. **Cash flow and liquidity.** Not yet researched.
