## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Revenue vs. guide, especially the top half.** Q2 guidance implies roughly 19% growth. Beating the top of $8.25B would support the raised FY outlook.
2. **EPS vs. $1.00–$1.07.** The upper end is the bar if the company is beating; a print near $1.00 reads as in-line.
3. **Operating margin.** Adjusted operating income of $535M–$565M implies margin expansion. Watch for contraction from mix or component costs.
4. **FY2027 guidance raise.** A second raise would signal AI infrastructure demand is holding. Holding the range would read as neutral-to-negative given the stock's sensitivity to demand durability.
5. **Spin-off commentary.** Watch for timing, capital structure, and standalone-company targets. This is the main narrative variable.
