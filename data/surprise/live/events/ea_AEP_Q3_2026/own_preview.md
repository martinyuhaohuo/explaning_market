## Recent Context

- **Q2 2026 (reported Jul 30):** Operating EPS $1.36 vs. $1.43 a year earlier. Earnings Whispers consensus was $1.49 and AAII's was $1.48, so this was a miss. GAAP EPS was $1.31. Revenue was $5.445B vs. $5.087B a year earlier.
- **Year to date:** Operating EPS $3.01 (vs. $2.98 in the prior year), per Quartr.
- **Implied H2 math:** Full-year guide of $6.25–$6.55 less $3.01 YTD implies $3.24–$3.54 for Q3 + Q4. At a ~$1.95 Q3 consensus, Q4 would need roughly $1.29–$1.59. The low end of the range looks achievable; the high end needs a strong Q4.
- **Long-term:** Operating EPS growth of 7–9% through 2030 (greater than 9% CAGR as a stated expectation), backed by a $78B five-year capital plan. Expected load additions are 69 GW through 2030, and about 13 GW of gas turbine capacity is secured.
- **Prior quarter commentary:** Management attributed the guidance raise to first-half performance. The GAAP guide was $6.16–$6.46, after a mark-to-market hedging impact and a West Virginia PSC order.

## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Operating EPS vs. ~$1.92–$1.98.** A beat of several cents supports the guidance raise. A miss after Q2 would raise doubt about the H2 ramp.
2. **FY2026 guidance.** Watch whether the $6.25–$6.55 range is reaffirmed, narrowed toward the top, or raised again. Given the Q2 pattern, a narrowing toward the upper half is the most likely constructive outcome.
3. **Load growth and large-customer pipeline.** Any change to the 69 GW load-addition figure, signed large-load agreements, or data-center contracts. This is the main long-term growth driver the market is pricing.
4. **Regulatory outcomes and rate cases.** Ohio, Texas, Indiana, and West Virginia decisions, plus any affordability pushback. The West Virginia PSC order already hit GAAP results.
5. **Capex and financing.** Any change to the $78B plan, equity needs, and the FFO/debt trajectory.

1. **EPS vs. ~$1.95:** A beat supports the raised guide; a miss after the Q2 miss would be a credibility issue.
2. **FY26 guide ($6.25–$6.55):** Expect narrowing or reaffirmation. The buy-side wants confidence in the H2 ramp.
