## Q2 Guidance to Test Against Q3

- **Net interest margin:** 3.98% in Q2. Management guided to a stable margin, but sources disagree on the range (3.96%–4.04% vs. 3.96%–4.01%). Check the transcript for the exact figure.
- **Loan growth:** Mid-single-digit annualized.
- **Fee income:** $74M–$77M.
- **Non-interest expense:** $149M–$154M per one source; another gives $149M–$152M.
- **M&A:** Agreed to acquire Finward Bancorp for about $208M, expected to be roughly 5% accretive to EPS.

## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Revenue vs. ~$272M.** Q2 missed on revenue, so this is the main risk. Check net interest income, not just the total.
2. **NIM vs. the guided range.** Loan accretion income declined in Q2, and the margin depends on funding costs holding.
3. **Fee income vs. $74M–$77M.** A miss here would compound a revenue shortfall.
4. **Non-interest expense vs. guidance.** Watch for merger-related costs tied to Finward.
5. **Credit quality.** Provision, net charge-offs, and non-performing loans. I did not check these data points.
6. **Loan growth.** Confirm it is tracking mid-single-digit annualized.
7. **Q4 guidance and Finward timing and accretion.**
