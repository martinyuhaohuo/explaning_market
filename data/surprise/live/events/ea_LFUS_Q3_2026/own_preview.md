1. **Bookings and book-to-bill:** Is it still above 1.0, and does it confirm Q4 demand? This matters most for an industrial/electrification name guiding +21% organic.
2. **Organic growth vs. the 21% implied:** Whether the beat is volume-driven or price-driven.
3. **Adj. EBITDA / operating margin:** Can it hold or expand from 23.6% with the Basler integration?
4. **Segment mix:** Electronics, Automotive, and Industrial. Automotive exposure to EV/auto cycles is the main risk.
5. **Q4 2026 guidance:** The buy-side will judge this against the Q3 run-rate.
6. **Tax rate, FX, and free cash flow:** Secondary, but they can move EPS quality.
