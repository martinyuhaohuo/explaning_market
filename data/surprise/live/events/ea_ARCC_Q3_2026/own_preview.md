## Key Metrics to Watch (ranked)

1. **Core EPS vs. $0.48.** The dividend is $0.48, so a beat or miss shows whether the payout is covered by earnings.
2. **Dividend outlook for Q4.** Any change to the $0.48 run-rate, or commentary on spillover use, will drive the reaction.
3. **NAV per share vs. $19.35.** Direction tells you whether marks are stabilizing after several quarters of decline.
4. **Non-accruals and credit quality.** Watch the 2.4% at-cost level and any new names moving to non-accrual.
5. **Investment income vs. ~$776M–$788M.** The consensus spread is wide, so reconcile against the press release.
6. **Portfolio activity and leverage.** Net deployment, repayments, and the debt-to-equity ratio.
7. **GAAP vs. core gap.** Unrealized gains or losses drive GAAP EPS, which matters less for the dividend but moves headlines.
